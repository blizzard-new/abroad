國際短期課程

本校針對學生進行之國際短期交流包含寒暑期固定甄選本校學生約20名出團至韓國首爾教育大學參訪，甄選期間約4、5月，請同學再留意網站資訊。另外，在每年12月、1月以及3月皆會安排由本校接待來自歐美、韓國、日本等大學，提供本校學生與國際交流之機會，相關資訊皆會不定期刊登於網站上。不定期之寒暑假研習團活動：包含日本、韓國、大陸或其他國家會不定期舉辦冬、夏令營提供本校師生更多出國交流機會。

 

🌍 International Short-Term Programs at NTUE

  • Korea Study Tour
    ▸ Every summer & winter, ~20 students are selected for a study tour at Seoul National University of Education.
    ▸ Selection period: April–May (details will be announced on the OIA website).

  • On-Campus Exchange Opportunities
    ▸ NTUE regularly hosts students from Europe, the U.S., Korea, and Japan in Dec, Jan, and Mar, giving NTUE students chances to connect globally without leaving campus.

  • Seasonal Camps & Study Tours
    ▸ Short-term programs in Japan, Korea, Mainland China, and beyond during summer and winter breaks.
    ▸ Expand global perspectives, experience diverse cultures, and build lifelong friendships.

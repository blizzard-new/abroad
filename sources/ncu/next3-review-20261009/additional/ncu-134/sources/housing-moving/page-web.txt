Moving In and Out
Moving into the International House
Residents must submit the Notification of Acceptance of Residency, whch is emailed  to an applicant once it is approved, to the International House office. The procedures for movin-in must be taken between 9:00am and 4:00pm on weekdays. These procedures will not be handled on Saturdays, Sundays or national holidays.
・Carry out the moving procedures at the Office located at the Building A
（Contact）
Office in Building A: TEL: 096-338-4575
Move-in Information (Students)
Move-in Information (Researchers)
Moving out the International House
When you decide the date of moving-out from the International House, be sure to submit the Notice of Moving-out to the Office at least one month prior to the date of moving-out so that the rent can be prorated on a daily basis. If you fail to submit this notice at least one month in advance, you will be charged another month's rent. Procedures for moving-out shall be taken at the International House office between 9:00am and 3:00pm on weekdays only.

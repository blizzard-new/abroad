Japan Studies 107 (Business)
Introduction to Corporate Governance and Japanʼs Business Environment
Bishwa Raj Kandel（ﾋﾞｼｭﾜ ﾗｽﾞｶﾝﾃﾞﾙ）
This course aims to equip international students with the necessary Japanese language knowledge and fundamental
communication skills for working in Japanese companies. Through lectures, students will further develop their analytical
skills and understanding of global business in Japan, while strengthening cross-cultural competence in Japanese society
and corporate workplaces. Students will also gain an understanding of various challenges faced in professional
environments in Japan.
We explore various aspects of Japanese business society and the work environment; therefore, active learning and
creativity are essential. In every class, students are assigned topics and are required to prepare PowerPoint
presentations. The course mainly focuses on understanding fundamental and appropriate Japanese business culture and
workplace practices.
In addition, students visit small and medium-sized companies to observe Japanese-style management.
The main topics to be covered in this class are as follows:
1回 Guidance
2回 About Globalization in Japan
3回 Big Companies and Corporate Governance
4回 Family Business and small Japanese companies  
5回 Case Studies : Field Visit in Aichi ken (Small Companies)
6回 Case Studies : Field Visit in Aichi ken (Small Companies)
7回 Mid-term presentation (Part 1)
8回 Mid-term presentation (Part 2)
9回 JAPANESE MANAGEMENT STYLE
10回 About Toyota System
11回 PROBLEMS OF JAPANESE Corporate Governance
12回 CORPORATE Scandals
13回 Final presentation(Part 1)
14回 Final presentation(Part 2)
Grades will be based on class participation (20%), mid-term presentation (40%), and ﬁnal presentation (40%).

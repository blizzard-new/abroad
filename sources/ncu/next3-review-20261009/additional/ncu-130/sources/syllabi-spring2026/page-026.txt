Japan Studies 903 (Special Course)
Japanese Society and the World - Report with a Journalistic Approach
⼸削 雅⼈（ﾕｹﾞ ﾏｻﾋﾄ）、櫻井 省吾（ｻｸﾗｲ ｼｮｳｺﾞ）
《Course Objectives and Outline》
(1) The goal of this course is to capture the actual image of Japan from various aspects, including its diversifying society,
culture, lifestyle, its globalizing economy, and its increasingly advanced information technology, and to research and
understand its origin and background in order to close in on the essence of the current Japanese society.
(2) In this process, international comparisons based on perspectives from around the world and a journalistic approach
that analyzes things from multiple viewpoints will be used.
(3) The class will be divided into groups and each group will conduct research on its own unique theme. In the process,
ﬁeldwork, including interviews with appropriate organizations and individuals, will be required.
(4) The results of the investigation and research will be summarized in the style of a newspaper article. The results of
the whole class will be published in the university's English-language newspaper, "The NUFS Times."
《Recognition required for the course and speciﬁc course procedures》
(1) This course adopts an active learning approach. Students are not expected to be taught passively, but to actively
engage in the assigned tasks.
(2) This course requires active and friendly cooperation between domestic and exchange students. A cooperative and
complementary attitude is expected in speciﬁc tasks such as selection of article topics, research and investigation, and
article writing.
(3) This course is a practical learning experience that includes the creation of articles of The NUFS Times. The NUFS
Times will be distributed on/oﬀ campus.
(4) The ﬁnal decision as to whether the content and quality of the articles prepared by the students are appropriate for
publication in The NUFS Times will be left to the discretion of the instructors.
(5) Time will be set aside for analysis and overview of the Japanese media and journalistic perspectives, and students
are welcome to raise any issues they wish.
(6) Students will receive comments and feedback from the instructors during the reporting and writing stages of the
article and will rewrite and improve their reports. There will be no exam or paper submission, so basically the evaluation
points will be based on the improvements and results of the comments and feedback received.
(7) Google Classroom will be used to download and upload materials and assignments. An invitation to Google
Classroom will be sent to your NUFS email address at the beginning of the semester.
【Generative AI】
The use of text generated by generative AI is not permitted in the creation of the article of “The NUFS Times.” However,
the use of generative AI during the creation process is not prohibited. In such cases, the type of AI used and the prompt
employed must be clearly stated.
This policy will be explained during the ﬁrst class orientation and will be reviewed periodically during the course.

Week 1 Orientation

Week 3 Lecture: Journalistic approach and structure of newspaper articles (basics)
Week 4 Discussion on research and coverage plan
Week 5 Presentation of research progress
Week 6 Practice: Mock interview and writing article
Week 7 Lecture: Appropriate article style in the NUFS Times (practical use)
Week 8 Feedback on ﬁrst draft of article
Week 9 Additional Instructions for article writing
Week 10 Feedback on the second draft of the article
Week 11 Check and additional writing for the third draft of article
Week 12 Feedback on the third draft of the article
Week 13 Finish the article
Week 14 Editorial adjustments to the newspaper and conﬁrmation of the article
Your grades will be decided based on your improvement eﬀorts, results, and cooperation with the group. Articles do not
have to be perfect, but improvement eﬀorts and the reﬂection of instructor's feedback will be evaluated.
Newspaper articles: 60%
Class participation: 30%
Reﬂection paper: 10%
【Generative AI】
The use of text generated by generative AI in the creation of the article of “The NUFS Times” is not permitted. Violations
will result in measures such as denial of course credit.

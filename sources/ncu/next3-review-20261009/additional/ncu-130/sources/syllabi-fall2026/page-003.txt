Japan Studies 105 (Business）
The contemporary Japan Business Landscape : summarizing the key point
TAKITA Yoichi(ﾀｷﾀ ﾖｳｲﾁ)
This course explores the business environment surrounding Japanese companies as well as actual business practices in
Japan.
Students will acquire foundational knowledge of corporations and management while also studying the economic and
social contexts that inﬂuence businesses. Lectures by external experts will provide valuable real-world insights into
current developments and help deepen understanding of what is actually happening in the ﬁeld.
This course will be conducted entirely in English. Students are required to attend all classes and deliver presentations in
English.
1.      Introduction: Japan's current business environment as of 2026 序説
2.      Macroeconomic Situation: Escape from deﬂationary stagnation マクロ経済
3.      Business: Companies shifting to oﬀense 企業活動の全体像
4.      The political landscape surrounding companies 政治・⾏政環境
5.      Theoretical considerations and historical background 理論・歴史考察
6.      Women's empowerment and transformation of corporate culture ⼥性進出
7.      Corporate governance and management reform 企業統治改⾰
8.      Midterm presentation 中間報告
9.      Theoretical considerations and historical background 理論・歴史考察
10.  Global expansion and risk management 海外活動・リスク管理
11.  Adaptation to changes in the international environment 国際環境対応
12.  Enhancement of corporate ﬁnancial eﬃciency 財務改⾰
13.  Final presentation 最終報告

Japan Studies 223 (Language)
Introduction to Japanese Linguistics
SAKURAI Shogo(ｻｸﾗｲ ｼｮｳｺﾞ)
This course introduces the basics of Japanese linguistics. By taking this course, students will become familiar with the
following aspects of Japanese linguistic properties and be able to understand the unique characteristics of each by
comparing with the ones of their native language. The class will be conducted entirely in English; thus, all students
should possess either 500 on TOEFL ITP or equivalent English proﬁciency, and/or at least one semester of study abroad
in an English-speaking country. Exchange students must be enrolled in at least IJLE Level 2 or above Japanese language
classes.
1. Phonetics
2. Phonology
3. Morphology
4. Syntacs
This course will be conducted in-person on the Nisshin campus throughout the semester. Students are expected to read
assigned material before coming to the class, take weekly quizzes, participate in class/group discussion, and take the
ﬁnal exam at the end of the semester.
The detailed syllabus will be handed out in the ﬁrst class meeting.

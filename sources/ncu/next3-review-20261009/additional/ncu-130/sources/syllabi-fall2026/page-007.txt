Japan Studies 208 (Culture)
Geography of Japan from the Perspective of Culture, Society, and Tourism
Jakub E. Marszalenko(ﾔｺﾌﾞﾏﾙｼｬﾚﾝｺ)
The objective of this course is to equip students with knowledge about the Japanese Archipelago from the perspective of
culture, society, history, and tourism. In each class, a diﬀerent one of the nine regions* of Japan will be covered,
introducing its unique aspects. After completing the course, the students will be well versed in Japanʼs geography, with
their knowledge going being stereotypes and commonly known trivia.
*The Kyushu Region has been divided into “Kyushu” and “Okinawa and the Ryukyus” due to the cultural and historical
diﬀerences between the present-day Okinawa Prefecture and “mainland” Japan.

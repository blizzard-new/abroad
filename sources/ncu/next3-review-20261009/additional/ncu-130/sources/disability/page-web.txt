Physical, Medical, and Sensory Disability Accommodations
Process for Requesting Accommodations
To begin a review of your eligibility for accommodations, you will need to submit a request for accommodations.
Basically, NUFS can support you with the same or part of the support you receive at your university. However, please note that some types of disability may not be accommodated by NUFS due to equipment or other problems.
You will need to submit a document stating the support you are receiving at your university. It must be issued by your university. NUFS faculty will review it and let you know what support is available at NUFS.
Please make sure to request when you apply to NUFS
, as it may not be possible to respond if you mention it after you have been accepted or after you have arrived in Japan.

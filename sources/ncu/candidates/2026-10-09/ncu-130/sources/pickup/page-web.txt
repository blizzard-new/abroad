Pick-up Service on arrival
Pick-up Service on arrival
NUFS operates a pick-up bus from Central Japan International Airport (Centrair) on designated arrival days. Departure times are
2pm
and
9:30pm
. (Times are subject to change, see acceptance package for correct times).
It is advisable to take the pick-up bus time into account when arranging your flight tickets. If you have some time before the bus departs, there are plenty of restaurants, souvenir shops and hot springs in Centrair, so you won't have a lot of spare time.

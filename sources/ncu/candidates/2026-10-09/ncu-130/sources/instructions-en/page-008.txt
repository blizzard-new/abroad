as well. 
 
For students who apply for the NUFS scholarship 
The minimum required balance is 40,000 yen per month. 
■ One semester: 40,000 yen x 5 months = 200,000 yen 
■ One academic year: 40,000 yen x 10 months = 400,000 yen 
 
For students who do NOT apply for the NUFS scholarship 
The minimum required balance is 100,000 yen per month. 
■ One semester: 100,000 yen x 5 months = 500,000 yen 
■ One academic year: 100,000 yen x 10 months = 1,000,000 yen

Housing Information -Dormitories-
All exchange students are required to live in our dormitories. Please go through the information on each housing facility and choose the dormitory that best suits your needs!
You will be asked which dormitory you wish to stay in at the time of application. However the International Office will decide which dormitory you will be placed in and will not accept requests for changes afterwards. We will do our best to allocate you to your first or second choice, but there is a possibility that you may be allocated to a third choice due to capacity.
NUFS International House (I-House)
There are about 60 rooms in a two-storey building with a central hall and courtyard. All rooms are private and each room has its own bathroom. The kitchen is shared. As every step out of a private room opens onto the hall, interaction between residents is easy and active.
Specifics
Type: University dormitory for international students
Application and maintenance fee: ￥10,000 (upon move-in only, Non-refundable)
Room Rent: ￥35,000 (including utilities)*
Rental Bedding: Available (￥6,000+tax /once only)*
Kitchen: Communal (tableware not included)
Bathroom: Not Communal (shower and toilet in each room)
Internet: Free Wi-Fi
Laundry Facilities: Communal (￥100 per cycle)
Furniture: Fully furnished
To/From NUFS: 5 minutes’ walk
Address: 37-39 Takenoyama, Iwasaki, Nisshin, Aichi, 470-0131
*May be subject to change
NUFS Global Village
It is an eight-storey building with about 100 rooms. All are private rooms and each room has its own bathroom. There is a shared kitchen on each floor and a common room on the ground floor.

Type: University dormitory for international students
Application and maintenance fee: ￥10,000 (upon move-in only, Non-refundable)
Room Rent: ￥35,000 (including utilities)*
Rental Bedding: Available (￥6,000+tax/once only)*
Kitchen: Communal (tableware not included)
Bathroom: Not Communal (shower and toilet in each room)
Internet: Free Wi-Fi
Laundry Facilities: Communal (￥100 per cycle)
Furniture: Fully furnished
To/From NUFS: 5 minutes’ walk
Address: 1-1701 Takenoyama, Nisshin, Aichi, 470-0136
*May be subject to change
NUFS-NUAS Residence
It is an eight-storey building with about 55 rooms. All are private rooms with a bathroom and a mini-kitchen in each room. The common room is located on the third floor. As the kitchens are in private rooms, this dormitory is recommended for students who value their privacy.
Specifics
Type: University dormitory for international students
Application and maintenance fee: ￥10,000 (upon move-in only, Non-refundable)
Room Rent: ￥35,000 (including utilities)*
Rental Bedding: Available (￥6,000+tax/once only)*
Kitchen: Not Communal (kitchen in each room, kitchenware and tableware not included)
Bathroom: Not Communal (shower and toilet in each room)
Internet: Free Wi-Fi
Laundry Facilities: Not Communal (washing machine in each room)
Furniture: Fully furnished
To/From NUFS: 5 minutes’ walk
Address: 1-301 Takenoyama, Nisshin, Aichi, 470-0136
*May be subject to change

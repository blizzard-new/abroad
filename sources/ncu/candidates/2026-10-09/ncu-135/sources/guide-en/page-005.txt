② Registration for Regular Undergraduate Faculty Classes Only                                              
 
This option applies to Exchange Students who, based on the conditions set forth in the Student Exchange Agreement 
concluded between specific Faculties at both universities, may only register for classes in the Faculty to which they 
belong. (Ryukoku University Faculty of Economics and Dalian University of Foreign Languages School of Japanese 
Studies） 
 
③ Registration for Regular Graduate School Classes Only                                          
 
This option applies to Exchange Students who, based on the conditions set forth in a Student Exchange Agreement 
concluded between specific Graduate Schools at both universities, may only  register for classes in the Graduate 
School to which they belong. (Agreements concluded with our Graduate School of Advanced Science and 
Technology). 
 
 
 
 
●First, the student should confirm with the Study Abroad Coordinator at their home university regarding whether 
exchange student is possible under the existing Student Exchange Agreement. In some cases, due to agreements 
concluded between specific Faculties or Graduate Schools, the student may be limited to registering for subjects 
only within a specific major.  
● If the student selects the English Track, they will not be able to register for subjects conducted in Japanese.  
If you indicate this selection (JSP-I) in your application, you will be placed in a Japanese language class at the  
JSP-I level. In principle, it is not possible to change to JSP-II or a higher-level class after submitting your application, 
so please consider your choice carefully before applying.  However, exchange students with a one-year exchange 
term may, if they wish, take a Japanese language placement test after the end of their first semester.  If the se 
placement test results indicate that the student possesses Japanese language proficiency equivalent to JSP -II or 
above, they may be assigned to JSP-II or another appropriate higher-level class in their second semester. 
Please note that the final class placement will be determined by Ryukoku University based on the placement test 
results and the student's overall Japanese language proficiency. 
●For the Japanese track, it is recommended that the student has completed at least 300 hours of Japanese language 
study or has Japanese proficiency equivalent to JLPT N4 or higher.  
●Based on the results of the Japanese placement test, if the student’s Japanese level is determined to be lower than 
that required for entry into JSP-II, they may be reassigned to JSP-I (English Track)

Please complete your application process while following the procedures below. 
 
Qualifications for Application 
Only students who are enrolled at universities which have a Student Exchange Agreement with Ryukoku University, 
and who have been nominated by their home university, are eligible to apply. 
 
Application Procedures 
The Home University Coordinator must complete the online form titled <2027 年度交換留学ノミネーシ
ョン／Spring 2027 Exchange Program Nomination Form>. 
Nomination Period: October 6th ~ November 9th, 2026 23:59 (Japan Time) 
 
The Nominated Students must receive a link from the Home University Coordinator for the online form titled 
<Exchange Application Spring 2027 Entry> and complete the form within the following period: 
Application Period: October 6th ~ November 9th, 2026 23:59 (Japan Time) 
 
① Notification of Acceptance or Non-Acceptance 
Those who have completed the above procedures by the deadline will be notified of their acceptance or non-
acceptance in approximately the middle of December. 
 
② Issuance of the Official Acceptance Letter and COE (Certificate of Eligibility) 
These documents will be sent to accepted students at approximately the beginning of February. 
 
 
 
 
 
To receive a student visa from the Japanese Embassy or Consulate, you need a Certificate of Eligibility (COE). The 
Ryukoku University Center for the Promotion of Global Education (R-Globe) Office will apply for this (in Japan) 
on your behalf, after we rece ive all your required application documents. It generally takes six to eight weeks for 
the Certificate to be issued, so be sure to submit all your materials as soon as possible to avoid any possible delay. 
Once we receive the Certificate of Eligibility, we  will send it together with the Letter of Acceptance to you in a 
“Welcome Package”, addressed to your home university. Once you receive these documents, please take the 
Certificate of Eligibility and the Letter of Acceptance to the Japanese Embassy in your own country to apply for 
your student visa.

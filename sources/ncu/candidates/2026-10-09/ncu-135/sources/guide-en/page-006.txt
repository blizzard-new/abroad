The JSP curriculum is structured to include <Core Required Subjects> (for which credit acquisition is mandatory), 
<Required Enrollment Subjects> (which all students must take but credit acquisition is not mandatory), and 
<Elective Subjects>. Elective Subjects are divided into three clusters: Subject Cluster A ~ Subject Cluster C.  
 
Subject Cluster A Japanese Language 
A group of subjects specializing in Japanese language study. Offers 
diverse learning related to the Japanese language, ranging from JLPT 
(Japanese Language Proficiency Test) preparation to linguistics. 
Subject Cluster B 
Intercultural 
Collaborative 
Learning (PBL) 
A group of subjects taken alongside Ryukoku University's domestic 
students. These are conducted in a small  group, project-based 
Learning (PBL) format. 
Subject Cluster C 
Japanese Society, 
Culture, and 
Industry 
A group of  subjects focused on Japanese society and culture, 
economics, business, and other topics. This category offers a diverse 
lineup, ranging from subjects  easily accessible for the purpose of 
experiential learning and cultural exchange , to more specialized 
academic subjects. 
 
● There are subjects offered in both Japanese and English, allowing you to select subjects that match your individual 
needs and language ability. 
● Japanese language subjects are be organized into classes based on proficiency level.

Regardless of whether you are insured in your home country, students who will study in Japan for more than three 
months (i.e., three months and one day or longer) are required to enroll in Japan’s National Health Insurance system. 
The insurance premium is approximately 1,500 to 2,000 yen per month. Enrollment procedures must be completed 
at the ward office near the university after admission.  
 
Before traveling to Japan, you must enroll in overseas travel insurance or accident insurance that covers the entire 
duration of your exchange program. In particular, choosing a plan that includes personal liability coverage can help 
reduce financial burdens in the event of unexpected accidents. 
 
To ensure a safe and secure study abroad experience, please make sure to enroll in appropriate insurance and review 
the coverage details prior to your departure.

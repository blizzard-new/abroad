To complete the JSP program, students must earn credits from the JSP courses listed below. 
Students who meet the completion requirements will be awarded a JSP Certificate of Completion (Digital Badge). 
- For students enrolled for one semester: Completion of 15 or more JSP course credits 
- For students enrolled for two semesters: Completion of 30 or more JSP course credits 
 
 
 
 
 
●You are required to register for a minimum of 15 JSP credits per semester (30 JSP credits per year). (Please note 
that this minimum registered credit requirement is subject to change.) Be sure to confirm with the responsible 
staff or faculty member at your home university regarding which fields of study you must complete studies in at 
Ryukoku University. 
● Students in JSP-IV and JSP-V may enroll in selected undergraduate and graduate school subjects in addition to 
their JSP subjects. Students in JSP I ~ III may only enroll in selected English-taught undergraduate and graduate 
courses outside of their JSP subjects. 
●You cannot enroll in certain subjects with enrollment restrictions, such as those that have a prerequisite system (先
修制) or are small-group classes like seminars (ゼミ) in the regular undergraduate and graduate programs. Prior 
permission to enroll is required for these courses. 
● You can view the Ryukoku University online syllabus at the following link. Please use it for your reference. 
https://syllabus.ws.ryukoku.ac.jp/acrsw/CSylNoSSO/CNoSSO.do

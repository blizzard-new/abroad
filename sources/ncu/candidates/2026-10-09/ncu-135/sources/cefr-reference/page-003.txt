JSP-Ⅳ B1.1 
With preparation, is able to give a detailed presentation. Can converse concretely 
on topics of interest. Is able to read and understand short and easy sentences. 
Can write short sentences about familiar content and topics of interest. Is able to 
understand and respect both similarities and differences between one’s own 
culture, that of Japan, and that of other countries. 
JSP-Ⅲ A2.2 
Can understand regularly used sentences and expressions regarding topics 
directly related to oneself such as personal information, family information, 
shopping, one’s neighborhood and work, etc. Can exchange information about 
short and regular, everyday matters. Can explain, in easy words, necessary 
information about one’s background and daily circumstances. 
JSP-Ⅱ A2.1 
On short and familiar topics, can attain limited information by reading and 
listening. Can make a simple presentation and write a report with short 
sentences. Can converse and answer questions on simple, everyday topics. 
 
Class placement is determined comprehensively based on the results of the “Self -Assessment of Japanese 
Language Proficiency” and the “SPOT Test.

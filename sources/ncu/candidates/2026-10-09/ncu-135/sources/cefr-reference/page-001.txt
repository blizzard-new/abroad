Explanation for Self-Assessment of Japanese Language Skills 
 
 
Ryukoku University launches the Ryukoku Japan Studies Program (JSP) for international exchange students 
in the 2026 Academic Year.  
 
The goal of this program is to take advantage of the benefits of living in Japan in order to ① Gain a variety of 
experiences both within and outside of Ryukoku University and ② Improve one’s Japanese language skills 
and gain insights into the societies and cultures of various countries through interactions with Japanese students 
and other international students.  
 
To achieve this, the program incorporates project -based learning in Japanese classes, such as through 
interviews and surveys. It also makes use of Japan’s social and cultural settings – especially the unique location 
of Kyoto – by including activities such as the observation of festivals and other traditional events.

Application Method
〈International House Residence Management System〉
Please apply through your academic supervisor who is eligible to download the "Application Format" from the International Residence Management System. International students before enrolling in Kumamoto University are not eligible to access to the System.
Your application will complete when the "Application Format" is registered on the International House Residence Management System.
International House Residence Management System (Accessing is available only on campus, and please use a browser other than Firefox)
URL：https://fgc.jimu.kumamoto-u.ac.jp/kokusai_kaikan/
International Students
Application period is twice a year; around January for spring semester and July for fall semester. Now we are accepting applications for the International House for academic year 2026 (fall semester). International students who request to reside in the International House are advised to carefully read Application Guideline, then submit the application through your academic supervisor by
July 17, 2026
.
*Please take note there would not be enough rooms for all applicants.
※Regarding an application beyond the application period, please contact Internation Student Office by email.
gji-ryugaku@jimu.kumamoto-u.ac.jp

International Researchers
Applications are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis 2 months before moving-in. Please apply through your academic supervisor who is eligible to  download the "Application Fromat" from the Internation Residence Management System.
<NOTE> Such a international researchers whose contract with Kumamoto University is for more than one year is not eligible for the application of International House.

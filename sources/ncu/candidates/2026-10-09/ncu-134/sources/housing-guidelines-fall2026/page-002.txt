*The above move -out deadlines of February and August are set to prepare  for new international students en roll ing in April 
and October of the following year, respectively. Please check with the academic affairs office of your respective department

* Applicants, who wish to have a specific period of residence, are advised to consult with  the International Students Office 
individually.

* Depending on the student's affiliation and status , a different period of stay may be set.

the room fee s for C, D and E and common  service fee s by 8% to 10% starting from  April 2027.  
We will notify residents via the International  House  website or email as soon as the decision is made.

  （3）All residents must rent Futon (Bedding) during each permitted residency period.

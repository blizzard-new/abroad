From Kumamoto Station:
By Sanko-Bus: take a bus for Ozu (via Sanrigi and Jinnai) and get off at the "Ozeki-bashi Bus Stop."
By JR train: From Kumamoto Station, take the Hohi Line and get off at "Tatsudaguchi Station" and then walk to the International House on foot.
By Taxi: Approximately 15 minutes.
From Sakuramachi Bus Terminal:
By Sanko-Bus: Take a bus for Ozu or Musashigaoka and get off at the "Ozekibashi bus stop."
By Kumaden bus: Take a bus for Tatsudaguchi/Kusunoki-danchi and get off at the "Ozekibashi bus stop."
By Taxi: Approximately 15 minutes.
From Kumamoto Airport:
By Sanko Bus: Get on the Limousine Bus headed for the Sakuramachi Bus Terminal and get off at the terminal. Take a Kumamoto-Shiei Bus for Tatsudaguchi/Kurokami-danchi or the Sanko-Bus for Ozu/Musashigaoka. Get off at the "Ozekibashi bus stop."
By Taxi: Approximately 40 minutes.

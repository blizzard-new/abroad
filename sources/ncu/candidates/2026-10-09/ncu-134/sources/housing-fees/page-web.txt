Rental Fees and Charges
Due to the recent increase in maintenance costs and other expenses caused by inflation, we plan to raise the room fees for C, D and E and common service fees by 8% to 10% starting from April 2027.
We will notify residents via the International House website or email as soon as the decision is made.
The increased room fees and common service fees are expected to be the amounts listed in the table below.
1. Payment of rent, property usage charges, electricity and water expenses
(1) Residents of Building A and B
International Students
The invoices for rent, expenses for common service, electricity and water charges are put into each mailbox around the 15th of every month. Residents must pay these charges at the Kokaibashi Branch of Higo Bank by the end of the month. Couple and family rooms are equipped with a gas range. Residents need to initiate a contract. For details on how to apply, use and pay for gas, follow the instructions provided by the gas company and the International House office.
Researchers
The invoices for property usage charges, expenses for common service, electricity and water charges are put into each mailbox around the 15th of every month. Residents must pay these charges at the Kokaibashi Branch of Higo Bank by the end of the month. Since the rooms for researchers are equipped with a gas range, residents need to initiate a contract. For details on how to apply for, use and pay for gas, follow the instructions provided by the gas company and the International House office.
(2) Residents of Building C and D
International Students
The invoices for rent, expenses for common service, electricity and water charges are put into each mailbox around the 15th of every month. Residents must pay these charges at the Kokaibashi Branch of Higo Bank by the end of the month.
(3) Residents of Building E
International Students
The invoices for rent and expenses for common service are put into each mailbox around the 15th of every month. Residents must pay these charges to the Kokaibashi Branch of Higo Bank by the end of the month. Since electricity and water must be contracted by the residents directly with the utility companies, follow the instructions of the International Student Office and the International House office for these.

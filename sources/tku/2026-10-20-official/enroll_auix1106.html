2027-2028 年赴姊妹校交換生聯合甄選說明會

iCalendar

活動內容：

本處將針對各地區姊妹校交換甄選作業流程進行說明，以幫助學生了解如何申請交換，亦邀請回國學長姐分享交換生經驗。
由於場地座位有限，將優先保留給完成報名者，請於活動當日 18:10 前完成報到，18:10 後抵達者可能無法保留座位。
如報名額滿，仍可於活動當日現場報到並排隊候補，工作人員將於 18:10 後視現場狀況酌情安排入座或站區位置。
OICSA will explain the application procedures for exchange programs with partner universities in different regions to help students understand how to apply. In addition, returned exchange students will be invited to share their experiences.
As seating is limited, priority will be given to registered participants. Please complete check-in before 18:10 on the event day; seats may not be guaranteed for those arriving after 18:10.
Walk-in participants may queue for standby entry, and OICSA staff will arrange after 18:10 depending on seat availability.

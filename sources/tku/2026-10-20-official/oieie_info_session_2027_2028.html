國際處於115年11月6日（五）舉辦「2027-2028年赴姊妹校交換生聯合甄選說明會」，請踴躍報名參加。

2026-10-19
陳靜

說明：

一、旨揭說明會將於115年11月6日（五）下午6時整於驚聲國際會議廳舉行，本處將針對各地區姊妹校交換甄選作業流程進行說明，以幫助學生了解如何申請交換，亦邀請回國學長姐分享交換生經驗。

二、由於活動場地座位有限，將優先保留給完成報名者，請於115年11月5日（四）前逕至活動報名系統報名: https://enroll.tku.edu.tw/course.aspx?cid=auix1106

三、如對於活動內容有任何問題，請洽國交流組陳靜，校內分機3669。

四、本活動與聯合國永續發展目標SDG4(優質教育)、SDG8(尊嚴就業與經濟發展)及SDG17(夥伴關係)連結。

Program Description:

1.The session will be held at 6:00 p.m. on November 6, 2026 (Friday) at the Ching-sheng International Conference Hall. OICSA will explain the application procedures for exchange programs with partner universities in different regions to help students understand how to apply. In addition, returned exchange students will be invited to share their experiences.

2.As seating at the venue is limited, priority will be given to registered participants. Please complete your registration through the event registration system by November 5, 2026: https://enroll.tku.edu.tw/course.aspx?cid=auix1106

3.If you have any questions, please contact 02-2621-5656 #3669.

4.The activity is connected with Sustainable Development Goals: SDG4 (Quality Education), SDG8 (Decent Work And Economic Growth) & SDG17 (Partnerships For The Goals)

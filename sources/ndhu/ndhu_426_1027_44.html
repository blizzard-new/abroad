【德州大學阿靈頓分校】

Located in the heart of the Dallas/Fort Worth Metroplex, the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA) stands adjacent to many of the world’s top Fortune 500 companies. Featuring strategic course content and experiential learning options, UTA offers an authentic glimpse into the current business world and prepares students to be lasting contributors in the global workforce. Through a cooperative partnership, National Dong Hwa University students have the unique opportunity to complete an AACSB-accredited MBA program abroad in just one year and 27 credit-hours while expanding their business acumen in one of the largest metropolitan business centers in the world.

 

【Partnership Program】

Double Degree Master of Business Administration (MBA) – 1 Year: Fall/Spring Admissions

9 Credit Hours in NDHU, 27 Credit Hours in UTA | Completion in 1 Year

  • Curriculum

  1. Joint Coursework from National Dong Hwa (9 Credit Hours):
    • Business Statistics
    • Financial Management
    • Measurement & Analysis for Business Decision Making

  1. Core Coursework (24 Credit Hours):
    • Marketing
    • Leadership and Teams
    • Decisions and Strategy
    • Advanced Business Communication
    • Sales and Sales Management
    • Negotiations and Conflict Management
    • Ethics and the Business Environment
    • Entrepreneurship
  2.  Capstone Course (3 Credit Hours)
    • Strategic Management

  • Admission Requirements

    1. Unconditional
      • 3.0 GPA (last 60 hours of undergraduate degree) AND
      • GMAT score of 500+ (Verbal & Quantitative scores in 30th percentile or better)
    2. Unconditional (GMAT/GRE Waiver)
      • 3.5 GPA (cumulative and last 60 hours of undergraduate degree) from an AACSB accredited school within the last 7
      academic years OR
      • Earned graduate degree or graduate certificate (min. 9 hours) from a regionally and/or professionally accredited school with
      a minimum 3.0 GPA for all coursework.
    3. Probationary
      • 3.0 GPA (last 60 hour of undergraduate degree)
      • Successful completion of up to 3 business background course modules

  • Language Proficiency (either one)

  1. TOEFL Minimum Score: 79
  2. IELTS Minimum Score: 6.5
  3. Duolingo Minimum Score: 100

  • Application Information:

  1. Complete the international student application via Apply Texas at www.goapplytexas.org. Apply Texas is a common application used by all public institutions in the state of Texas so students will need to select the University of Texas at Arlington from the appropriate drop down menu when prompted. Additionally, students will need to select “M.B.A. (Flexible Format-Main Campus)” when asked to choose their major.
  2. Priority Application Deadlines:
    • Fall semesters: March 15th
    • Spring semesters: August 15th
  3. International Application Fee: $90
  4. Submit official transcripts from all prior institutions attended. Information on how to submit transcripts can be found here. Students who are still completing coursework may submit unofficial transcripts for initial admission purposes but will be required to submit official transcripts no later than the 12th class day of their first semester.
  5. After completing all requirements, an admission decision is typically rendered within 2-3 business days and the student will be notified of their admission via email. Additionally, the Office of Admissions will begin contacting the student regarding the documents required for them to issue an I-20 so the student can travel to the US.

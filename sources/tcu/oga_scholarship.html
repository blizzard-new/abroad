Scholarships

At Tzu Chi University (TCU), we are committed to making high-quality education accessible to students from around the world. Through a range of scholarships, financial aid opportunities, and transparent tuition information, we aim to support students in pursuing their academic goals while studying in Taiwan.

The sections below provide an overview of available scholarships, financial assistance, tuition fees, and estimated living expenses to help you plan your academic journey at TCU with confidence.

Our scholarships are designed to support academic excellence, encourage personal growth, and make higher education more accessible to talented and motivated students from diverse backgrounds

International Student Scholarships

This scholarship provides financial support to pursue their studies at TCU, including coverage for tuition and miscellaneous fees, on campus accommodation, and monthly allowance of up to NT$5,000 is provided for a maximum of 4.5 months per semester (excluding summer and winter breaks). The exact duration and amount are subject to the university’s annual budget and scholarship regulations

Financial Aid for International Students

TCU’s Financial Aid for International Students is designed toensure that talented and motivated students can pursue their studies in Taiwan without financial barriers. This support covers tuition and miscellaneous fees, on-campus accommodation, and a monthly allowance of up to NT$5,000 for up to 4.5 months per semester, excluding breaks

Scholarship and Financial Assistance for International Nursing Students

This program is for students in the Nursing program at TCU who face financial hardship or special circumstances. It provides coverage for tuition and miscellaneous fees, on campus accommodation, and a monthly allowance of up to NT$5,000 for up to 4.5 months per semester, excluding breaks.

English-Taught Biomedical Program Scholarship

This scholarship supports students enrolled in TCU’s English Taught Biomedical Science graduate program through three categories: full scholarships, tuition and fee waivers, and living allowances. It provides comprehensive financial support to help awardees focus fully on their studies and research

TCU Graduate Student

Scholarship provides financial support for TCU students pursuing graduate or Ph.D. degrees, helping them focus on their studies and research. Master’s students receive a monthly stipend of NT$3,000 for up to 22 months, while Ph.D. students receive NT$10,000 per month for up to 60 months, with an additional NT$5,000 per month after passing their qualification exam.

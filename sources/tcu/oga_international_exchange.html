International Exchange

Outbound

Take your learning beyond the classroom through Tzu Chi University’s outbound exchange programs. Experience different cultures, expand your global perspective, and gain valuable international academic experience while continuing your studies.

An outbound exchange is more than studying abroad, it is an opportunity to challenge yourself, build lifelong connections, and develop the knowledge and skills needed to succeed in a global community.

Learn More About Our Outbound Program

Inbound

Experience life beyond your home campus through Tzu Chi University’s inbound exchange program. Study in Taiwan, explore a vibrant multicultural environment, and gain valuable international academic experience while becoming part of the TCU community.

Your exchange experience at Tzu Chi University goes beyond the classroom. Discover Taiwanese culture, build meaningful connections with students from around the world, and develop the knowledge, confidence, and global perspective to thrive in an interconnected world.

Learn More About Inbound Program

Dual/Triple Degree

Advance your education through Tzu Chi University’s Dual and Triple Degree Programs. In collaboration with international partner universities, these programs allow students to study across multiple institutions and earn two or more internationally recognized degrees.

Designed to enhance global learning and academic mobility, our Dual and Triple Degree Programs provide students with international experience, cross-cultural perspectives, and a competitive advantage for graduate study and global careers.

Learn More About Dual/Triple Outbound Program

Partner Universities

Tzu Chi University collaborates with international partner universities worldwide to promote academic excellence, global learning, and cross-cultural exchange. Through these partnerships, students can participate in exchange programs, collaborative research, and other international opportunities that broaden their academic experience and global perspective.

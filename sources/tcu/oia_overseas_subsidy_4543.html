申請條件 Registration Requirements

-本校行政主管及教師至姊妹校（含學術約定）進行學術與行政交流者。
The administrative leader and faculty members of the school sent to sister schools (including academic agreements) for academic and administrative exchanges
-本校教師帶領學生至姊妹校（含學術約定）進行學術、社團及文化交流者。
Faculty members leading students to sister schools (including academic agreements) for academic and exchanges
-本校行政主管及教師帶隊前往海外進行國際競賽者。
Faculty members who lead overseas for international competitions
-本校行政主管及教師帶隊前往海外進行志工服務者。
Faculty members who lead overseas for volunteer services
-本校各系（所）開設之課程因教學需要，須全程或部分移往海外地區之講授、教學活動者。
Courses offered by various departments (institutes) of the school that must be conducted whole or partly overseas for teaching purposes
-本校各單位所提其他交流計畫經專案核准者。
Other exchange plans proposed by various units of the school that is approved by the project

補助項目及金額 Qualified Expense Subsidies

-交通費：
1.符合適用對象第一至三款交流者，補助國際來回經濟艙機票(實報實銷)；符合第四款帶隊前往海外進行志工服務者，補助國際來回經濟艙機票，至多二萬元整。
2.國內交通火車費用、機場捷運費用。
Transportation: Those who meet the first to third category of applicable exchanges will be subsidized with international round-trip economy class air tickets (actual reimbursement); those who meet the fourth category of leading a team to perform volunteer services overseas will be subsidized with international round-trip economy class air tickets, up to NT$20,000 and also TRA transportation fees and airport MRT fees.
-生活費：住宿、交通、保險等，實報實銷，每人每天最高核發金額不得超過人事室「慈濟大學教職員工差假及請假作業要點」支付標準；帶隊前往海外進行志工服務者，補助國際來回經濟艙機票至多二萬元整，不予補助生活費。
Living expenses: Accommodation, transportation, insurance, etc. will be reimbursed in real terms, and the maximum amount approved per person per day shall not exceed the payment standards of the “Tzu Chi University Faculty Members Leave and Leave Application Regulations” issued by the Office of Personnel; International round-trip economy class tickets will be subsidized for those who lead a team to perform volunteer services overseas. Air tickets are capped at NT$20,000, and living expenses will not be subsidized.

申請條件及補助 Application Requirements and Subsidies

以本校業務單位核可參加者為限，補助金額依年度計畫所編列之預算決定，每名學生在學期間總補助金額上限三萬元，僅補助往返經濟艙機票；若以同案獲校外機關（單位）補助者，補助金額由學生海外交流審查委員會審定。
Only those approved by the respective administrative unit of the university are eligible to participate. The subsidy amount is determined based on the budget allocated in the annual plan, with a maximum total subsidy of NT$30,000 per student during their period of study. The subsidy covers only round-trip economy class airfare. If the same project receives funding from external organizations, the subsidy amount will be determined by the University Overseas Exchange Review Committee.

補助下列屬性之活動 Eligible activities applicable for subsidies

-姊妹校(含學術交流學校)之交流活動，包括由姊妹校舉辦之國際學術研討會。
Exchange activities between sister schools (including academic exchange schools), including international academic seminars organized by sister schools.
-學生社團交流活動：每學年補助由學生事務處推薦足以代表本校之學生社團.
Student club exchange activities: Each academic year, the subsidy is recommended by the Student Affairs Office to student clubs that are representative of the school.
-國際學生聯合會交流活動。International Student Association exchange activities.
-志願服務學習交流活動。Volunteer service, learning and exchange activities.
-短期課程與實驗/實習活動。Short-term courses and experimental/internship activities.
-國際比賽、國際會議。International competitions and international conferences.
-海外移地教學。Overseas teaching program.
-其他：依實際情況審定。Others: reviewed and determined based on actual circumstances.

上述各款交流活動依以下標準進行審核 Review Criteria

(一)計畫完整性40%。Project completion: 40%.
(二)學習成效40%(包含服務學習訓練時數) 。Learning outcomes: 40% (including service-learning training hours).
(三)交流風險性20%。Communication risk: 20%.

語言能力證明 Proof of language proficiency

1、至非使用華語地區交流，以檢附英語能力證明者為優先；至非使用華語地區使用華語交流，請提供佐證文件則不需檢附語言能力證明。
If you want to communicate in a non-Chinese-speaking area, priority will be given to those who submit a certificate of English proficiency; if you are going to communicate in a non-Chinese-speaking area using Chinese, please provide supporting documents and there is no need to submit a proof of language proficiency. 
2、其他地區：依當年度審核結果而訂。
Other regions: determined based on the annual review results.
3、參加國際競賽、國際會議及海外移地教學之學生，需提供全民英檢中級複試或全民英檢中高級初試或等同其他英語能力檢定成績之證明文件。其英語能力證明文件可為全民英檢、多益、托福、IELTS、GET等。

Students who participate in international competitions, international conferences, and overseas teaching program must provide documents proving the results of the General English Proficiency Test(GEPT) Intermediate Retest or the General English Proficiency Test(GEPT) Intermediate and Advanced Preliminary Examination or equivalent to other English proficiency tests. The English proficiency certificates can be GEPT, TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS, GET, etc.

4、海外見實習及志工服務依當地機構要求而訂

Overseas internships and volunteer services are arranged according to the requirements of local institutions

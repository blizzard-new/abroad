STUDY ABROAD PROGRAMS

Here you can find the list of study abroad programs available for MCU students. In case the information is revised, please follow the announcement issued by the Study Abroad Center.

Detailed introduction of Overseas Study Abroad programs can be found in "MCU STUDY ABROAD PROGRAM" HANDBOOK, available in IEE office. Electronic version is available for download in the "INFORMATION DOWNLOAD" web link.

MCU Study Abroad Info Sheet  
download The file clearly indicates the host universities, available exchange quotas, language proficiency requirements, and eligible colleges.

★ Applicants for undergraduate programs are recommended to research the websites of universities of interest before you start applying.

★ Applicants for graduate programs are recommended to research the websites of universities of interest for the application documents and relevant requirements. A complete package of Postgraduate Application shall be handed to Study Abroad Center in time before the deadline set by the overseas universities

*Facts from 2026 Fall:

  • University Housing Availability: Due to a significant increase in demand, we are unable to guarantee university housing to all incoming exchange students. This shortage is particularly acute during Semester 1 (September – December).

    • Recommendation: Housing pressure is greatly reduced during Semester 2 (January – May), and we recommend trying to redirect some students to come during this period if possible.

  • Faculty of Business and Economics (FBE) Course Enrolment: Courses offered by FBE have become increasingly competitive. 

    • No Guarantees: Enrolment in any specific FBE course cannot be guaranteed.
    • Course Load: It is unlikely that students will be able to take a full course load focused entirely on a single discipline within this Faculty.

    • Academic Flexibility & Alternative Options: We strongly encourage students to be flexible with their course planning and to have backup plans ready. Furthermore, we highly recommend that students consider applying to other faculties where possible.

  • Common Core (CC) Courses: We highly recommend that exchange students consider enrolling in CC courses. These interdisciplinary courses cover a wide range of global and local topics. Students may take up to two CC courses from a selective menu, with each course chosen from a different Area of Inquiry: 'Scientific and Technological Literacy', 'Humanities', 'Global Issues' and 'China: Culture, State and Society'. They are an excellent way for students to earn credits while exploring subjects outside their primary major, and they often provide more flexible enrolment opportunities.

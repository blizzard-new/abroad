校級出訪交換生甄選常見問題

FAQ for Outbound Exchange Student Selection

▼ 甄選 Application and Selection
1. 交換生的申請資格為何？Who can apply for the exchange program?

關於交換生申請資格，請至國合處網站"最新消息"處，查閱最新一期之甄選公告作為參考，公告標題通常為「（預計出訪學期）校級暨國際兩岸交換學生！」，內容包含「校內甄選簡章」及「姊妹校名額表」。 由於每次的甄選規定、姊妹校要求及提供名額皆可能調整，請務必以您報名當學期公告之內容為準，並於申請期間持續留意該公告是否有名額或資格異動。 其中：

1.「校內甄選簡章」為本校校內甄選之申請資格、規定與作業流程。

2.「姊妹校名額表」則為各姊妹校之申請條件與甄選門檻。

因此，申請前請務必詳閱上述兩項文件，並確認自身符合其所列之相關資格與條件。

以下列出常見的基本規範，其餘細節與特定要求，請務必詳閱當期簡章內容：

(1) 學制、年級及學籍規定：

  • 申請時須為本校完成註冊並繳清本校全額學雜費之在學學生，且符合下列年級資格：
    • 專科部：三年級下學期（含）以上。
    • 四技部、進修部四技：一年級下學期（含）以上。
    • 二技部：本校五專直升者須為三年級上學期（含）以上；非本校五專直升者須為三年級下學期（含）以上。
  • 出訪期間仍須為本校完成註冊並繳清本校全額學雜費(進修部以18學分計)之在學學生，且符合下列年級資格：
    • 專科部：四年級（含）以上。
    • 四技部、進修部四技：二年級（含）以上。
    • 二技部：本校五專直升者須為三年級下學期（含）以上；非本校五專直升者須為四年級上學期（含）以上。
  • 五專部學生不得於五專教育階段報名，並於二技教育階段出訪；出訪期間須與報名時為同一教育階段。
  • 研究所學生（不含在職專班）及轉學生，申請時須已於本校就讀滿一學期，並符合申請資格。

(2) 學業成績：歷年學業總平均 70 分以上；申請語言學習組赴語言中心者，歷年學業總平均 65 分以上。

(3) 操行成績：歷年操行總平均 80 分以上。

(4) 外語能力：外語能力檢定須依各交換學校規定辦理並繳交相關正本證明文件；語言學習組雖部分姊妹校未訂定語言能力檢定門檻，仍須具備該語言之基本能力（如曾修習相關語言課程或具備基礎溝通能力）。

(5) 交換次數限制：學生於同一教育階段擔任交換學生以兩次為限，不得申請赴相同國家研修，且兩次研修期程相加不得超過一學年。

(6) 國籍限制：外籍學生、僑生及陸生不得申請赴其母國之學校進行交換。

1. Who can apply for the exchange program?

Regarding eligibility for exchange student applications, please refer to the latest exchange program announcement available under the “Latest News” section of the OICC website. The announcement title is usually “(Expected Exchange Semester) 校級暨國際兩岸交換學生！” and includes the “Selection Guidelines” and the “Partner University Quota List.”
 

As the selection regulations, partner university requirements, and available quotas may vary for each application cycle, applicants must follow the information announced for the semester in which they apply and continue to check the announcement for any updates regarding quotas or eligibility requirements during the application period.
Among these documents:

1. The “Selection Guidelines” outline Wenzao’s internal application eligibility, regulations, and procedures for the exchange student selection process.

2. The “Partner University Quota List” specifies the application requirements and selection criteria set by each partner university.

Therefore, applicants must carefully review both documents and confirm that they meet all relevant qualifications and requirements before applying.

 

Below are some commonly applied basic requirements. For further details and specific conditions, please carefully review the official guidelines for the semester in which they apply.

(1) Academic Level and Enrollment Requirements
Applicants must be registered students at Wenzao, have completed registration, and have paid the full tuition and fees at the time of application.
At the time of application, applicants must meet the following academic level requirements:
•     5-year Program: At least the second semester of the 3rd year.
•     4-year Program (including Continuing Education Division): At least the second semester of the 1st year.
•     2-year Program:
•      - Wenzao students directly admitted from the 5-year Program: At least the first semester of the 3rd year.
•      - Students admitted from other institutions: At least the second semester of the 3rd year.
 

During the exchange period, students must remain registered at Wenzao, have completed registration, have paid the full tuition and fees (For students in the Continuing Education Division, full tuition and fees are based on 18 credits.), and meet the following academic level requirements:
•    5-year Program: At least the 4th year.
•    4-year Program (including Continuing Education Division): At least the 2nd year.
•    2-year Program:
•      - Wenzao students directly admitted from the 5-year Program: At least the second semester of the 3rd year.
•      - Students admitted from other institutions: At least the first semester of the 4th year.
 

Additional requirements:
•     Students in the 5-year Program may not apply during the 5-year Program and participate in the exchange program after advancing to the 2-year Program. The academic level at the time of application must be the same as that during the exchange period.
•    Graduate students (excluding in-service master's programs) and transfer students must have completed at least one semester of study at Wenzao before applying and must meet all eligibility requirements.
 

(2) Academic Performance
•    A minimum cumulative academic average of 70/100 is required.
•    Applicants to the Language Learning Exchange Student category whose host institution is a language center must have a minimum cumulative academic average of 65/100.
 

(3) Conduct Grade
•    A minimum cumulative conduct score of 80/100 is required.
 

(4) Language Proficiency
Language proficiency requirements vary depending on the host university. Applicants must submit the required offcial and original language proficiency in accordance with each partner university's regulations.
Although some partner universities in the Language Learning Exchange Student category do not require an official language proficiency test, applicants are still expected to possess basic proficiency in the target language (e.g., completion of relevant language courses or basic communication skills).
 

(5) Exchange Limits
•    Students may participate in exchange programs up to two times within the same academic level.
•    They may not apply to study in the same country more than once, and the total duration of all exchange periods may not exceed one academic year.
 

(6) Restrictions for International Students
International students, overseas Chinese students, and students from mainland China may not apply to partner universities in their home countries.

2. 請問應屆畢業生或延修生可以申請嗎？Can graduating students or students extending their studies apply?

可以。申請及出國交換當學期須尚未符合畢業資格，並持續具有本校在學學籍及符合簡章相關規定，並於出訪當學期繳交本校全額學雜費。

原則上，申請交換前一學期及申請當學期修習學分數均不得少於10學分（研究所學生除外）。如修習學分數未達10學分，申請前一學期、申請當學期仍須完成註冊並繳交本校全額學雜費；進修部全額學雜費以18學分計。

此外，應屆畢業生如有服兵役的義務，須考量能否在出國前取得緩役的許可，方可如期出國交流，建議事先可向學生事務處生輔組或戶籍所在地區公所詢問相關程序，以免影響申請計畫。

2. Can graduating students or students extending their studies apply?

Yes. You may apply as long as you have not yet met the graduation requirements during the application and exchange semesters, remain enrolled at Wenzao, and meet all eligibility requirements stated in the application guidelines. Students are required to pay full tuition and fees to Wenzao during the exchange semester.

In principle, students must take at least 10 credits in both the semester prior to application and the semester of application (except graduate students). If taking fewer than 10 credits in either semester, students must remain fully registered and pay full tuition and fees for that semester. For students in the Division of Continuing Education, full tuition and fees are calculated based on 18 credits.

Graduating students who need to complete military service should check if they can get approval for deferment before going abroad. It is strongly recommended to ask the Student Affairs Office or the District Office in advance.

3. 如何申請交換生計畫? How do I apply for the exchange program?

出訪交換生每年辦理兩次甄選，通常於寒假及暑假期間公告甄選資訊與簡章。

第一學期約於 7～8 月（暑假）公告甄選資訊，辦理該學年度第二學期（約隔年 2 月）出訪之交換學生甄選；第二學期則約於 1～2 月（寒假）公告，辦理下一學年度（約當年 9 月）出訪之交換學生甄選。

屆時請留意本組網頁「最新消息」之交換生甄選公告，公告標題通常為「（預計出訪學期）校級暨國際兩岸交換學生！」，內容包含甄選簡章、姊妹校名額表及報名連結。

此外，申請期間亦請持續留意該公告是否有名額、資格條件或其他相關資訊異動。

報名截止後，亦請持續留意國合處網站公告之後續甄選資訊，如書面審查通過公告、面試公告及撕榜公告等。

3. How do I apply for the exchange program?

Wenzao conducts outbound exchange student selection twice every academic year, with application announcements and guidelines usually released during the winter and summer breaks.

For the first semester selection, announcements are generally released during July–August (summer break) for exchange programs starting in the second semester of the same academic year (approximately February of the following year).

For the second semester selection, announcements are generally released during January–February (winter break) for exchange programs starting in the following academic year (approximately September).

Please check the “Latest News” section on the OICC website for exchange student application announcements. The announcement title is usually “(Expected Exchange Semester) 校級暨國際兩岸交換學生！” and includes the Selection Guidelines, Partner University Quota List, and application link.

Applicants should also continue to check the announcement during the application period for any updates regarding quotas, eligibility requirements, or related information.

After the application deadline, applicants must continue checking the OICC website for subsequent selection announcements, such as document reviewing results, interview announcements, and school selection announcements.

4. 面試是用哪種語言 ? What language is used in the interview?

除了基本中、英文，面試主要依據所申請交換之語組應答，例如申請法語組則以法語為主試語言；德語組以德語為主試語言；西語組以西語為主試語言；日語組則以日語為主試語言。

4. What language is used in the interview?

In addition to Chinese and English, the interview can also be conducted in the language based on the language group you apply for. For example, French will be used in the interview of French group; German will be used in the interview of German group; Spanish will be spoken in Spanish group interview; and Japanese will be needed in the Japanese group interview.

5. 不是該語系(例：日文系)學生，是否可以至該語系國家(例：日本)研修? If I am not a student majoring a particular language, can I still study in the countries where the language is spoken?

只要符合本校簡章規定以及申請學校之資格(註:部分姐妹校僅受理二技四或四技學生)、語檢條件等規定即可報名該語組。
*需注意部分科目可能無法抵免而影響畢業時程，請於提出申請之前至所屬科系確認有關交換生之課程抵免問題。

5. If I am not a student majoring a particular language, can I still study in the countries where the language is spoken?

Yes. You may apply if you:
• Meet the "Selection Guidelines for exchange program" requirements
• Meet the host university’s requirements
(Some partner universities only accept certain academic programs.)

• Meet the language proficiency requirement

⚠ Important note:
Some courses may not be transferred back for credit. This may delay graduation.
Please check credit transfer rules with your department before you apply.

6. 我成績只差一點點怎麼辦？What if my grades are slightly below the requirement?

交換生代表本校赴國外交流，因此訂定選拔標準，期望同學在國外的學習能獲得預期的成效，同時拓展國際視野。如果目前還不符合規定門檻，無論是校內成績或是語言檢定，只須持續努力，定能爭取成為交換生。

6. What if my grades are slightly below the requirement?

Exchange students represent Wenzao. The selection rules help students live and study abroad successfully. If you do not meet the requirements now, please continue to improve your grades or language ability and apply again in the future.

7. 為什麼每學期公布的交換名額表會有所不同？Why does the list of available host universities change every semester?

每學期國合處都會與各語組老師進行調查，確認姊妹校最新的合約狀態與收生意願，僅將確認可前往的學校列入名額表，以降低同學申請後無法成行的風險。因此，請務必以「申請當學期公告」為準，並在報名期間密切注意名額表是否有更新。

7. Why does the list of available host universities change every semester?

To minimize the risk of students being unable to study abroad after selection, the OICC coordinates with Professors for International Cooperation each semester to confirm the latest contract status and student quotas for each sister school. Only confirmed institutions are included in the list.
Please ensure you refer to the latest announcements during the application period and keep a close eye on the most up-to-date vacancy table.

8. 申請期間需要注意哪些資訊？What information should I pay attention to during the application period?

申請期間請隨時留意國合處國際交流組網站公告及電子郵件通知。若申請資料需修正，將以Email另行通知。 甄選各階段資訊將公告於網站，包括：甄選公告（姊妹校資訊隨時更新）、報名成功名單、書面審查通過名單、面試公告及撕榜順序公告等。請務必留意各階段截止期限，逾時恕不受理。

8. What information should I pay attention to during the application period?

During the application process, applicants should regularly check the OICC website announcements and the email. If any corrections are required, notifications will be sent via email.
Information for each stage of the selection process will be announced on the website, including selection announcements (with updated partner university information), list of successful applicants, document review results, interview announcements, and ranking/placement order announcements. Please pay close attention to all deadlines, as late participation and submission will not be accepted.

9.填寫申請資料時需要注意什麼？What should I pay attention to when submitting my application?

請務必報名截止期限，逾時恕不受理。填寫報名表時，請詳閱各欄位說明，並依規定格式填寫及繳交資料。報名表繳交後，基於公平性原則，恕不接受補件或修改，請務必確認無誤後再送出。

9. What should I pay attention to when submitting my application?

Please pay close attention to the application deadline, as late submissions will not be accepted.

When completing the application form, please carefully read the instructions for each section and follow the required format when submitting all materials.

Once the application form has been submitted, no revisions or additional documents will be accepted due to fairness considerations. Please ensure that all information is complete and accurate before submission.

Some departments may separately offer department-level exchange programs, with application and selection procedures handled by the respective departments. 11. 通過校內甄選並完成選校（撕榜）後，就代表一定能出國交換嗎？Does passing the selection and choosing a school (ranking selection) guarantee my placement?

通過校內甄選並撕榜後，同學將取得本校「交換學生候選人」資格，後續由國合處及各語組老師協助向姊妹校進行提名。

請注意：即便通過校內甄選，提名及申請是否被接受，仍須由各姊妹校進行最終審核，最終錄取決定權在交換學校。

10. Does passing the selection and choosing a school (ranking selection) guarantee my placement?

No. Passing the internal selection of Wenzao and completing the school choice process grants you the status of "Exchange Student Candidate." The OICC and professors for International Cooperation will then assist in your nomination to the host university.
⚠ Final Decision Authority:
Please be aware that the final decision to accept a nomination rests entirely with the host institution. Even after passing our internal selection, your admission is subject to their final review and approval.

▼ 獎學金及學費 Scholarships and Tuition
1. 交換學生有獎學金嗎? Are there scholarships for exchange students?

本校設有校內獎學金，交換生完成交換返國後於期限內繳交所需文件即可獲得該獎學金，金額以每年度本校預算為準。
除校內獎學金，若出訪交換生成績優異可爭取學海飛颺獎學金，國合處亦會協助經濟弱勢同學申請教育部學海惜珠獎學金。每年度獎學金以教育部核撥金額為主。
有些姊妹校亦設有獎學金，提名時姊妹校即會提供資訊，國合處或負責之國際交流合作教師皆會協助同學申請或辦理。

請注意：校內獎學金、學海飛颺、學海惜珠僅能擇一申請。

1. Are there scholarships for exchange students?

Yes.
Wenzao offers internal exchange scholarships. Students can receive this scholarship after they return and submit the required documents on time. Scholarship amounts depend on Wenzao's budget each year.
Some partner universities also offer scholarships. Information is usually provided during nomination.
(因外籍學生不符合學海系列獎學金資格，因此英文版未說明學海飛颺及學海惜珠獎學金。)

(Note: As international students are not eligible for the Ministry of Education's “Study Abroad Scholarship Program,” the English version does not include information on MOE Study Abroad Scholarships.)

2. 何時能確定所獲學海獎學金之金額核發時間? When will the scholarship amount and disbursement schedule for the Ministry of Education's “Study Abroad Scholarship Program,”be confirmed?

教育部於每年5月公布「學海飛颺」及「學海惜珠」獎學金核定結果，故上學期出訪之學生，約於當年6月可獲知結果，下學期出訪之學生，可於11月左右得知是否獲得獎學金。

3. 有關交換費用，交換學生需要繳交本校學費或研修學校的學費嗎? Do I need to pay tuition or any other fee?

凡具備交換生資格者，皆須繳交文藻全額學雜費以維持在學學籍。在「互惠對等」的基礎下，多數姊妹校提供交換生免繳對方學費的待遇，即「繳交文藻學雜費，免繳對方學費」。然而，部分學校因交流條件不同（例如修習語言中心課程或已載明為「自費」項目），同學仍須額外繳交對方的課程費用，請務必詳閱甄選當學期的公告確認各校的互惠條件。此外，除學費外的所有開銷，包含但不限於交通、住宿、生活、保險、書籍雜費等，均須由學生自理。建議同學可了解當地物價再行評估。

3. Do I need to pay tuition?

Yes. All exchange students must pay full tuition and fees to Wenzao to maintain their student status. Based on the principle of reciprocity, this is generally considered equivalent to paying tuition at the host university, meaning no additional tuition is required by most partner schools.

⚠ Exceptions & Self-funded Programs:
Some programs (such as those at language centers or marked as "self-funded") require students to pay extra tuition to the host institution. Please refer to the latest exchange program announcement for the specific terms of each school.

Additional Expenses:
Students are responsible for all non-tuition costs, including but not limited to transportation, housing, living expenses, insurance, and books. We recommend evaluating the local cost of living before applying.

▼ 出國前準備 Before Departure
1. 錄取後，須辦什麼手續? What do I need to do after I pass the selection?

(1) 於規定之時間內繳交「交換意願切結書」。
(2) 由各學系之國際交流老師及國合處協助進行交換學校之入學申請。
(3) 取得交換學校之入學許可後，自行申請簽證、預訂機票以及出國準備。
(4) 準備欲於研修學校選修的課程資料與其所屬科系負責學分抵免相關師長討論確認。
(5) 如獲得學校核發之獎學金者，須繳交「行政契約書」及填寫獎學金之所需資料。
(6) 參加交換學生行前說明會。
(7) 最遲於出國前完成繳交「文藻外語大學國際交換學生行前程序單」，未如期完成即出國研修者視同未辦妥出國手續，本處得視情節嚴重性做適當懲處，或追回獎助學金。

1. What do I need to do after I pass the selection?

(1) Submit the Exchange Participation Commitment Form.
(2) Apply to the host university with the assistance of the International Cooperation professor of each department.
(3) After receiving the admission offer from the host university, students should proceed with visa application, flight booking, and other pre-departure preparations on their own.
(4) Prepare course information and discuss credit transfer with International Cooperation professor of your department.
(5) If you receive a scholarship, submit the required contract and forms.
(6) Attend the pre-departure orientation.
(7) Submit the Exchange Student Pre-departure Checklist before leaving

If you leave without completing the required steps, you may face penalties or be asked to return the scholarship.

2. 研修學校會安排接機嗎? Will the host university pick me up at the airport?

不一定。若交換學校提供接機服務，可能有以下三種情況：
(1) 若該校提供接機服務，建議主動查詢該校官方網站相關資訊，或於取得國合老師同意後，主動詢問該校，並於期限內提出申請及提供班機資訊。
(2) 有提供機場或車站至學校的接駁車，需事先預約，且有固定班次，如抵達時間已無接駁車，仍須自行搭其他交通工具到校報到。
(3) 有提供，但需自行付費申請。可能學校派車，或需自行與接駁車或巴士公司預約，並於搭車時付清車資。

若該校未提供接機服務，則需自行安排交通。

2. Will the host university pick me up at the airport?

Not always. Generally, if the host university offers airport pick-up services, there are three possible situations:
• Free pick-up is available after students submit flight information. Students are advised to check the official website of sister school for relevant information. After completing the application and obtaining approval from International Cooperation Professor, students may also contact the host university directly, submit a request within the designated deadline, and provide their flight details.
• Shuttle service is available at fixed times (reservation required).
• Pick-up is available, but students must pay an extra fee

If no airport pick-up service is provided, students are responsible for arranging their own transportation.

3. 姊妹校一定會提供宿舍嗎？Will partner universities provide accommodation?

不一定。

各姊妹校之住宿政策不同，有些姊妹校會提供住宿（可能為校內宿舍或校外合作住宿），有些則不提供住宿。無論是否提供住宿，住宿費均須由學生自行負擔。

即使姊妹校提供宿舍申請，也不代表一定能申請上。由於各校宿舍名額有限，且須與來自世界各地的交換學生共同申請，仍可能因名額不足而未獲分配，屆時須由學生自行安排校外住宿。

若未分配到宿舍，可向姊妹校詢問是否提供校外住宿資訊、合作宿舍或推薦租屋名單；也建議同學於出國前先行了解當地住宿市場及租屋方式，預先規劃住宿備案。只要提早蒐集資訊、依序處理，通常都能逐步找到適合的住宿安排。

住宿資訊仍應以各姊妹校當學期提供之最新資訊為準。

提醒：交換期間可能遇到住宿、選課、簽證等各種需自行規劃與處理之事項，因此建議申請交換前，先評估自身是否具備獨立規劃、蒐集資訊及解決問題的能力，並做好相關準備。培養面對問題、尋找資源及獨立解決問題的能力，也是交換學習的重要收穫之一。

3. Will partner universities provide accommodation?

Not necessarily.

Accommodation policies vary among partner universities. Some universities provide accommodation (either on-campus housing or off-campus affiliated housing), while others do not. Regardless of whether accommodation is provided, all accommodation expenses are the student's responsibility.

Even if accommodation is available, it is not guaranteed. Since dormitory spaces are limited and are allocated to exchange students from around the world, students may not be assigned a room due to limited availability. In such cases, students will need to arrange off-campus accommodation on their own.

If on-campus accommodation is unavailable, students may ask the partner university whether it can provide off-campus housing information, affiliated accommodation, or recommended rental options. Students are also strongly encouraged to research the local housing market and rental procedures in advance and prepare alternative accommodation plans before departure.

Accommodation information is subject to the partner university's announcements for the semester of exchange. After nomination, students should complete the accommodation application according to the partner university's instructions and within the specified deadlines to avoid affecting their accommodation arrangements.

Reminder: During the exchange period, students may need to independently handle various matters such as accommodation, course registration, and visa applications. Therefore, applicants are encouraged to assess whether they are prepared to independently plan, gather information, and solve problems before applying for the exchange program.

4. 學校會幫忙辦簽證及訂機票嗎? Will the home university help with visas or flight tickets?

不會。辦理簽證、預定機票及交換當地住宿皆為準交換生義務，若無法承擔此任務，不建議申請交換生計畫。各校入學許可寄達時間、簽證辦理期程、個人準備進度不一，建議務必依個人所申請學校建議之時程即早準備。
(1) 簽證事項可至該國駐台辦事處網站查詢，訂機票可請旅行社代辦，也可自行處理，依個人選擇而定。
(2) 請務必辦理正確目的及效期的簽證，若有任何準備上之疑問，可隨時詢問本處或各系協助之國際交流教師。

4. Will the home university help with visas or flight tickets?

No. Exchange students must apply for visas, book flights, and arrange accommodation by themselves.
Students who cannot manage these tasks independently are advised not to apply.

Visa information is available on the foreign representative office websites in Taiwan. Flight tickets can be booked through travel agencies if needed.

Students must ensure that they apply for the correct type of visa and that its validity period covers the duration of their stay. If there are any questions during the preparation process, students may contact our International Cooperation Professor for assistance.

5. 於研修學校可修什麼課程? What courses can I take at the host university?

視各姊妹校規定而定。部分學校開設之課程可供交換學生自由選修，不受年級或科系限制；惟學校仍可能依學生之語言能力及實際修課情況，對可修習之課程或學分數進行規範。

有些學校會特別開設供國際學生或交換學生修習之課程；亦有學校考量交換學生之語言能力限制，規定學生僅能選修特定科系（院）或與原就讀科系相對應之課程。

此外，部分姊妹校係透過特定科系（院）與本校建立交流合作，因此本校學生可能僅能修習該系（院）所開設之課程。

5. What courses can I take at the host university?

This depends on the regulations of each host university.

Some universities allow exchange students to freely enroll in courses across different departments. However, the host university may still determine the courses or credit load available to students based on their language proficiency and academic performance.

Certain universities offer courses specifically designed for international or exchange students. Others may impose restrictions due to language requirements, allowing exchange students to take courses only within certain departments/faculties or within departments related to their home major.

In addition, some partner universities maintain exchange agreements through specific departments or faculties; therefore, students from Wenzao may be limited to taking courses offered by those particular departments or faculties only.

6. 於研修學校所選之課程是否都可抵免? Can all courses taken in host university be transferred for credit?

不是所有課程都可以抵免。出訪交換生學分抵免相關規定建議請參考法規：教務處的文藻外語大學學生科目學分免修與抵免辦法

註：學分抵免由學生所屬之科系，依科系依「課程領域、課程名稱、授課內容、學分數/上課時數」等為原則自行認定之。

6. Can all courses taken in host university be transferred for credit?

Not all courses can be transferred.

For regulations regarding credit transfer for outbound exchange students, please refer to the “Regulations Governing Credit Waiver and Credit Transfer,” as announced by the Office of Academic Affairs.

Note: Credit transfer decisions are subject to review and approval by the student’s home department, based on criteria such as course field, course title, course content, and the number of credits/contact hours.

• Help with exchange-related activities in their department

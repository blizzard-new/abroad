Scholarships for degree students

為促進本校國際化，鼓勵就讀本校之優秀外國籍學生，獎勵在學期間學習優良者順利完成學業，訂定「國立臺北商業大學外國籍學生獎學金要點」。     

      To promote the school’s internationalization, and to encourage and reward outstanding international students at NTUB who complete their studies here, NTUB has established the Scholarship Program for International Students. The Program is sponsored by the Ministry of Education Award and NTUB School Affairs Fund. Up to 50% of the school’s income from international student’s tuition fees and other miscellaneous fees can be used to sponsor the Program.

補助對象Eligibility

已經就讀本校大學部、碩士班及博士班之外國籍在校生(不含延修生、僑生及陸生)。

Overseas Chinese and Mainland Chinese students are not eligible. Applicants must be:

Any undergraduate or postgraduate international student currently enrolled at NTUB but not in a period of extension for graduation.

Students cannot apply for the scholarship under the Program if they already have a scholarship.

獎學金分配Scholarship Allocation

(一)每學期核發一次。

(二)符合資格者:博士生2萬五千元、碩士生2萬元、學士生1萬5千元。

(三)視當年度申請人數及經費機動調整。

(四)核發順序依據學生學制核發，博士生優先，其次為碩士生，最後為學士生。

(i)Scholarship disbursed once per semester.

(ii)Eligible recipients: PhD students NT$25,000, Master's students NT$20,000, Undergraduate students NT$15,000.

(iii)The number of recipients for each semester is subject to the number of applicants and the funding available for sponsorship in that academic year.

(iiii)The priority of allocation is based on the student's academic level, with PhD students given priority, followed by Master's students, and finally Undergraduate students.

申請資格Qualifying Applications

一、具有本校正式學籍之外國籍在校生符合下列規定之一者可提出申請。

(一)就讀本大學部滿一學期，歷年成績班級名次百分比為前50%，操行達A(含)以上，且無不良紀錄者。

(二)就讀本碩士班滿一學期，歷年成績學業班級名次百分比為前75%，操行達A(含)以上，且無不良紀錄者。

(三)就讀本博士班滿一學期，前一學期學期成績85分(含)以上，操行達A(含)以上，且無不良紀錄者。

(i) Any international student not in their first year, but who has official academic records at NTUB and is one of the following:

1. An undergraduate student at NTUB who has completed at least one full semester, with a cumulative academic ranking within the top 50% of the class and a conduct grade of A or above, and with no record of misconduct.

2. A postgraduate student at NTUB who has completed at least one full semester, with a cumulative academic ranking within the top 75% of the class and a conduct grade of A or above, and with no record of misconduct.; or

3. Those who have completed one semester of the doctoral program, achieved an average grade of 85 or above in the previous semester, have a conduct of A or above, and have no bad records.

申請時間Application Deadline

每年上學期於10月底前遞件，下學期於3月底前遞件。

  • First semester: Apply by the end of October each year.
  • Second semester: Apply by the end of March each year.

申請文件Requried Documents

外國籍在校生須檢附申請表、學生證影本、居留證影本、存摺影本及歷年成績單。

International students must submit the following documents to the Office of International Affairs. 

  • Completed application form
  • A copy of the student ID
  • A copy of the residence permit
  • A copy of the bankbook
  • A transcript of academic records

Exchange Student

Requirements

Students who want to apply for the exchange program need:

1. Nomination

  • You need to be first nominated by the home university before applying for the NTUB exchange student program.

  • If your university does not have a signed exchange agreement with NTUB. We are not able to accept your application.

  • OIA is only responsible for the application of the exchange program. If you are applying for a degree student, please contact the Office of Academic Affairs at ntubadmis@ntub.edu.tw

2. Language Proficiency

          You should meet either English or Chinese language proficiency at 

  • English language proficiency of at least CEFR B2 or

  • Chinese as a Foreign Language TOCFL Band B Level, or Chinese Proficiency  HSK Level 5

 

Course Information

  • According to NTUB regulations, exchange students must take at least one course per semester. Furthermore, students should also follow the requirements of their home university.
  • Students whose native language is Chinese may enroll in Chinese-taught classes with the instructors' approval.
  • The course information for the Fall semester is available in August; the Spring semester is available in January.
  • Before course information is announced, you may refer to our file to search for courses offered in the past years.

 

Accommodation

  • NTUB offers off-campus dormitories for overseas students. Travel time between the dormitories and NTUB is approximately 40-60 minutes by MRT.
  • Accommodation is guaranteed for fall intake but not for spring intake.
  • Costs for sharing rooms in dormitories range between NTD3,800-NTD6,000(approx, USD120-USD190)per month.
  • Rental contracts are for the entire duration of the official exchange semester (6 months), NON-REFUNDABLE for early checkout.
  • For self-arranged accommodation, a reasonable monthly rental price in Taipei ranges from NTD12,000 to NTD25,000 (as reference)

 

Scholarships

  • Scholarships are generally for degree students

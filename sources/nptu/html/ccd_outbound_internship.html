  2. 出國實習 Overseas Internship Programs

本校同學海外實習補助可透過教育部學海計畫，無論選擇學海築夢計畫(非新南向國家) 或新南向學海築夢計畫(新南向國家)，皆須由系所老師作為計畫主持人統一申請。有意至海外實習者，可留意系所老師招募海外實習成員之訊息。

Overseas internship subsidies for our students can be obtained through the Ministry of Education's "Ministry of Education (MOE) Overseas Exchange Student Financial Assistance Grant." Whether choosing the "Overseas Intership Program" (for non-New Southbound Policy countries) or the "New Southbound Policy Overseas Internship Program" (for New Southbound Policy countries), the application must be submitted by a faculty member from your department as the Principal Investigator. Those interested in overseas internships should pay attention to information from their department regarding recruitment of overseas internship participants.

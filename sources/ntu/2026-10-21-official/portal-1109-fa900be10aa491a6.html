蒙納許大學 Monash University

此校開放予第二次出國交換之同學選填
申請資格
一般組
  1. 本校大二以上/碩一以上(不含博士生)學生。（以報名交換學生甄選時，本校教務處學籍系統上之年級為準），前述「以上」包含該年級 (The term ‘at least’ includes the specified year itself.)。
  2. GPA 達3.30分以上。
  3. IELTS 6.5 (聽 6.0 讀 6.0 寫 6.0 說 6.0)，該校部分系所可能有更高語檢要求，選填志願前請務必主動確認  (Some departments may have higher language proficiency requirements. It is your responsibility to check before listing your preferences.)。
  4. 不接受法律學院、醫學院、管理學院會計學研究所、管理學院財務金融學研究所、管理學院國際企業學研究所、管理學院資訊管理學研究所、管理學院商學研究所、管理學院管理學院企業管理碩士專班之學生申請。

一學期2名。
(限定交換第一學期人數 2 人)(人數限2名，於本校第一學期出國，可選擇蒙納許大學的 Semester 2 only)

⚠️ 注意：上述申請資格及名額尚待交換學校確認，適用於116學年度第1學期之資訊將於甄選開跑前更新完成 (Please note that the eligibility requirements and exchange quota listed above are subject to confirmation by the partner university. Information applicable to Semester 1, 2027/2028AY will be updated before the application period begins.)

Campuses in Australia
You can study your units across the Clayton and the Caulfield campuses. If you want, however, to study units taught at the Peninsula or Parkville campuses, you must take all of your units at that campus.
  • Clayton: This is the main campus, where most disciplines are offered and only where Engineering, Science, and undergraduate Law are offered.
  • Caulfield: The only campus where Art, Design, and Architecture are offered but within easy commuting distance to Clayton, and students studying Arts, Business or IT can study between the two campuses, timetable permitting. They must still be nominated to a primary campus.
  • Melbourne Law Chambers: Postgraduate law students only. (Not for exchange students)
  • Parkville: Pharmacy students only. (Not for exchange students)
  • Peninsula: Not available in the application portal but a student with a particular reason to study there can be allocated there Monash University manually.
簽證資訊

We would like to firmly discourage students from applying for Working Holiday Visas. We understand that, since the significant increases to the price of student visas, the Working Holiday has become a popular option. Since May 2026, however, the Australia visa landscape has changed significantly students have experienced an unprecedented rate of visa refusals and delayed processing timelines that have led to the late cancellations of their program.

To minimise the risk of disruption to students' plans, we strongly recommend that students intending to study with us apply for the Student Visa (Subclass 500) rather than a Working Holiday Visa. Please refer to the Australian Department of Home Affairs website for the latest visa information and requirements.

住宿資訊
不保證住宿，更多資訊請見此連結

On-campus accommodation
  1. Information about on-campus options can be viewed here. Students do not require an offer for Exchange or Study Abroad to apply for on-campus accommodation; there is no fee to apply. Once applications open, students can go to the website to register, and complete an application. During the application process students must ensure they answer that they are a Monash Abroad applicant. As rooms are limited and offered on a first-come, first-served basis, we recommend students apply as soon as possible.
  2. Questions not answered on the website can be directed to Monash Residential Services.

Off-campus accommodation
  1. If students choose to live off-campus, they can receive assistance/advice on searching for rental properties, setting up a share house, things to look out for before you sign a contract, an idea of comparable rental costs, how to budget, and how to get the essentials like internet, gas and electricity connected.

UC Chile in numbers

UC Chile (Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile) seeks excellence in the creation and
transfer of knowledge and in the provision of a Catholic- based education to form people at
the service of society.

Founded in 1888, theuniversity currently has nearly33,000 students and
3,500professors. Nearly 1,000international come toUC Chile experience amobility program.

Our university has four campuses in Santiago and one in Villarrica, located in the
south of Chile.

We maintain rigorous academic standards. As aresult, we are ranked the
bestuniversity in Latin America (QS University Rankings 2023) and 93rd world 2024).

It has 18 faculties, 6 interdisciplinary academic units and the UC Chile College
Program.

Currently, we offer more than 60 undergraduate programs with 110 careers,105
master’s programs and 39 PhD programs.

More than 1.800 international students come to UC Chile to participate in a mobility
program and improve their global competences.

Migratory Process

Exchange students who come to Chilefor less than 180 days
may enter thecountry with a Temporary Stay permit.While
those who come for a year mustcompulsorily apply for
TemporaryResidence (student subcategory).

National Immigration Service website

Insurance and Health

Exchange students must have insurance that includes
medical expenses for at least USD 30,000; health and
funeral repatriation services, and must cover the entire
period of stay in Chile.

You can check some options here.

Arrival and Orientation

Upon arrival at UC Chile, the international student has the
support of the Office of the Vice President for International
Affairs and of several groups of students willing to help in
all aspects of their exchange. In addition, at the beginning of
each semester, an orientation session is held to ensure that
students are aware of all the details regarding course
registration, the services offered by the university, and other
relevant topics.

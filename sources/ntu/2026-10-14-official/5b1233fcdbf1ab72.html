此校開放予第二次出國交換之同學選填
申請資格
一般組
  1. 本校大二以上/碩一以上/博一以上學生。（以報名交換學生甄選時，本校教務處學籍系統上之年級為準），前述「以上」包含該年級 (The term ‘at least’ includes the specified year itself.)。
  2. 學士生 GPA 達3.00分以上，管理學院之學生前學年 GPA 達 3.30 分以上 (NTU CoM student GPA should be higher than 3.30)；
    研究生 GPA 達3.00分以上。
  3. 學士生 TOEFL iBT 4.0 (不接受 Home Edition) 或 TOEFL iBT 80 (不接受 Home Edition) 或 IELTS 6.5 或 全民英檢中高級，該校部分系所可能有更高語檢要求，選填志願前請務必主動確認 (Some departments may have higher language proficiency requirements. It is your responsibility to check before listing your preferences.)；
    研究生 TOEFL iBT 4.0 (不接受 Home Edition) 或 TOEFL iBT 80 (不接受 Home Edition) 或 IELTS 6.5，該校部分系所可能有更高語檢要求，選填志願前請務必主動確認 (Some departments may have higher language proficiency requirements. It is your responsibility to check before listing your preferences.)；。
  4. 不接受法律學院、醫學院之學生申請。

ELI Courses
If the student's English test score is below the level required to waive the ELI Placement Test and they do not qualify for an exemption, the student must take the ELI Placement test before the semester starts. This is mandatory. Depending on the ELI Placement Test results, the student may be placed into 1 to 3 ELI classes (intermediate or advanced). These classes will be taken with their other university courses.
 
Does the "three ELI courses" refer to a total of three classes for the semester, or are they a semester-long course that counts for credits?

Depending on ELI Placement test results, the student may be required to enroll in 1-3 ELI classes. Each ELI class has lectures throughout the semester. Here is information on what they can expect from the ELI courses: https://www.hawaii.edu/eli/courses/eli-courses/
 
If it's a semester-long course, how many credits does it count for? And is it included in the minimum 8 credits and maximum 16 credits required for graduate studies?

Each ELI course is the equivalent of 3 credits for registration and U.S. immigration enrollment requirements for F-1 students. However, any ELI courses numbered below 100 count as 0 credits on the final transcript report and may not be considered transferable back to their home university. Please find more information on the credit policy here: https://www.hawaii.edu/eli/courses/credit/
 
If the three courses exceed 8 credits, can I simply take the ELI courses and skip the other courses?

If the student wants to do that, it's fine with us. However it may mean that they have no academic credit transferable back to their home university. The grades on their transcript would appear as "CR" for credit received or "NC" for no credit but will not indicate any GPA.

校內外獎學金

scholarships both within and outside the university

本校為鼓勵學生參與國外大專校院、機構或企業之學習、研究或見/實習等活動，以提昇本校學生國際競爭力，提供多樣獎學金申請選項。學生有機會申請教育部籌辦相關獎學金、本校自辦之見實習獎勵，或其他政府/校外機構辦理之獎補助計畫。詳情請續見本頁面資訊。

Our university offers diverse scholarship options to encourage students to engage in international learning, research, and internships, enhancing their competitiveness. These include Ministry of Education scholarships, university scholarship, and other external funding opportunities. See page for details.

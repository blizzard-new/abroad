【出國交換】116學年(2027秋季、2028春季) 赴國外非大陸交換生甄選簡章暨學海補助申請，1/13(三)報名截止，歡迎本校學生報名 [Overseas Exchange] Announcement of 116th Academic Year (Fall 2027& Spring 2028) Exchange Student Selection and Financial Assistance Grant Application (excluding Mainland China). Application deadline: January 13. Students of our university are welcome to apply.

一、甄選116學年(2027秋季、2028春季)赴非大陸出國交換暨學海補助申請

*詳閱附件"甄選簡章、學海飛颺、學海惜珠補助辦法"及所有附件相關規定後，再提出申請。

二、出國期間：116學年第1學期、第2學期 (國外2027-秋/冬季、2028春/夏季班)

三、報名收件截止日: 2027年1月13日(三)下午3:00截止
(欲申請學海補助者，無論於116-1、116-2、116-1&2出國，皆須於此期限內繳完所須資料)
※如年度交換有剩餘名額才辦理第 2 學期補選，欲出國交換者請踴躍參與第 1 學期之甄選。

*國外姊妹校申請條件及名額僅為參考，各姊妹校之名額、語言能力要求及在校成績等若有新規定，一律依姊妹校最新公告辦理。

四、申請資料：

  1. 甄選申請表 (表單1 如附件，以電腦打字後列印紙本，送家長、導師、系所主管等各單位簽名核章，接受電子簽名)
  2. 中文歷年成績單(正本)：含歷年學業總成績平均、全班人數及班排名
    (碩班須至教務處蓋章含:學業成績總平均+全班排名+班排百分比)
  3. 英文歷年成績單(正本)：含GPA成績
  4. 外語檢定證明(影本) :2年內有效，不採多益校園考成績，IELTS雅思採學術組成績
  5. 英文在學證明 (約5天作業時間)
  6. 家長同意書 (表單2 如附件)
  7. 出國研習計畫 (含簡歷、自傳、申請動機*先以第一志願撰寫、讀書計畫*先以第一志願撰寫等，中文、外文各1,000字以上，格式自訂)
  8. 健康自述表 (表單3 如附件)
  9. 有利審查資料
    (1)本校教師英文推薦信 (格式自訂，請先以第一志願交換校撰寫，無須彌封)
    (2)修習全英課程 (附課程大綱，下載區連結)
    (3)    具有具體獲獎事蹟或參與國際性競賽獲獎證明文件

*若為師培公費生須先詢問師培中心實習組相關流程。

 ※繳件方式：（恕不收缺件）

  1. 申請表須由家長、導師、系所、院主管核章(可電子簽章)，並放首頁；其餘申請資料1-9項，依順序排列，並編頁碼，
    將上述全部文件，掃成1個PDF檔Email至    outgoing@gm.ncue.edu.tw，檔名：國家+校名(第一志願)-學生姓名。
  2. 繳交紙本原稿正本1份 (申請資料1-6項單面列印、7-9項雙面列印，以透明資料袋裝妥，不需裝訂）
    收件單位：校級甄選送至國際處辦公室（白沙大樓4樓）林九汝專員，分機5125
                      若為系、院級甄選，須多送1份至系、院辦承辦人。         
  3. 學海補助申請表，Email至outgoing@gm.ncue.edu.tw，檔名：學海補助申請表-學生姓名。
  4. 填寫google表單 https://forms.gle/8ZAo5x42CTiHNs769

115/7/9公告

 

2027–2028 Outbound Exchange Program (Non-Mainland China) & MOE Study Abroad Scholarship (Xue Hai Program)

1. Application for the 2027–2028 Outbound Exchange Program (Non-Mainland China) and MOE Xue Hai Scholarship

Please read the attached Application Guidelines, MOE Xue Hai Fei Yang Scholarship Regulations, MOE Xue Hai Xi Zhu Scholarship Regulations, and all related documents carefully before submitting your application.

2. Exchange Period

Academic Year 2027–2028 (AY 2027–2028)

  • Fall Semester 2027 / Winter Term 2027
  • Spring Semester 2028 / Summer Term 2028

Applicants may apply for:

  • Fall Semester only
  • Spring Semester only
  • Full Academic Year (Fall + Spring)

3. Application Deadline

Deadline: Wednesday, January 13, 2027, at 3:00 PM

Applicants who wish to apply for the MOE Xue Hai Scholarship (Xue Hai Fei Yang or Xue Hai Xi Zhu) must submit all required documents by this deadline, regardless of whether they plan to study abroad in Fall 2027, Spring 2028, or the full academic year.

Note: A second-round selection for the Spring Semester will only be held if exchange vacancies remain after the first-round selection. Students planning to study abroad are therefore strongly encouraged to apply during the first-round selection.

The admission requirements, language proficiency requirements, GPA requirements, and available quotas of partner universities listed are for reference only. Any updates announced by the partner universities shall prevail.

4. Required Application Documents

  1. Application Form (Form 1, attached)
    • Complete electronically and print.
    • Obtain signatures/approvals from your parent or guardian, academic advisor, department chair, and other required authorities.
    • Electronic signatures are accepted.
  2. Official Chinese Academic Transcript
    • Must include cumulative academic average, class size, and class ranking.
    • Graduate students must obtain the Academic Affairs Office seal showing:
      • Overall academic average
      • Class ranking
      • Ranking percentile
  3. Official English Academic Transcript
    • Must include GPA.
  4. Language Proficiency Certificate (Photocopy)
    • Test results must be valid within the past two years.
    • TOEIC Institutional Program (IP) scores are not accepted.
    • IELTS Academic module scores only.
  5. Certificate of Enrollment in English
    • Processing time: approximately five working days.
  6. Parental Consent Form (Form 2, attached)

  7. Study Abroad Proposal
    The proposal should include:

    • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
    • Autobiography
    • Statement of Purpose (based on your first-choice university)
    • Study Plan (based on your first-choice university)

    Both Chinese and English versions are required, with at least 1,000 words each. The format is at the applicant's discretion.

  8. Health Declaration Form (Form 3, attached)
  9. Supporting Documents (Optional but Recommended)
    • Recommendation letter in English from an NCUE faculty member (format at the applicant's discretion; addressed to the first-choice partner university; no sealed envelope required)
    • Proof of completion of English-taught courses (with course syllabus; see download section)
    • Certificates of awards, honors, or achievements in international competitions

Students receiving government-funded teacher education scholarships should consult the Teacher Education Center (Internship Division) regarding the required procedures before applying.

Submission Instructions

Incomplete applications will not be accepted.

A. Electronic Submission

  • The signed application form must be placed as the first page.
  • Arrange all remaining application documents (Items 1–9) in the required order and number all pages.
  • Combine all documents into one PDF file.
  • Email the PDF to:

outgoing@gm.ncue.edu.tw

File name format:

(First-choice Country)(University Name)(Applicant Name).pdf

B. Hard Copy Submission

Submit one original hard-copy set.

Printing requirements:

  • Documents 1–6: single-sided
  • Documents 7–9: double-sided

Place all documents in a transparent document folder. Stapling or binding is not required.

Submit to:

Student Exchange Division, Office of International and Cross-Strait Affairs (OICA)
4F, Baisha Building, National Changhua University of Education (NCUE)

Contact Person:
Laura Lin
Extension: 5125

Applicants participating in department- or college-level screening must also submit one additional copy to their department or college office.

C. Google Form

Please complete the online registration form:

https://forms.gle/8ZAo5x42CTiHNs769

5. MOE Xue Hai Scholarships

Applicants wishing to apply for the MOE Xue Hai Fei Yang Scholarship or MOE Xue Hai Xi Zhu Scholarship must submit their scholarship application together with their exchange application.

Please read the scholarship regulations carefully before submission.

6. Partner Universities and Available Quotas

Please refer to the list of partner universities and exchange quotas.

The information provided is for reference only. Admission requirements, language proficiency requirements, quotas, and other regulations are subject to the latest announcements issued by each partner university.

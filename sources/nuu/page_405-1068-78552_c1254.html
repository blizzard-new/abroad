Japanese KOSEN Internship & International Exchange Program日本高專實習暨國際交流活動

字體大小調整 Font Size 小 中 大
國立聯合大學於115年3月接待來自日本6所高專，包括宇部高專（Ube KOSEN）、小山高專（Oyama KOSEN）、大分高專（Oita KOSEN）、函館高專（Hakodate KOSEN）、高知高專（Kochi KOSEN）及釧路高專（Kushiro KOSEN）之學生來校進行為期一個月之實習交流，展現本校推動國際學術交流之成果。

本次活動於3月3日舉辦「相見歡暨歡迎會」，由本校校長侯帝光、國際及兩岸事務組組長溫如梅教授，以及各系師長共同出席；並邀請日本宇部高專副校長畑村學（Hatamura Manabu）蒞校參與交流。活動現場氣氛熱絡，透過師長與學生之互動，協助來訪學生快速融入校園生活。

實習期間，日本學生分別進入各系實驗室，在教師指導下進行專題研究與實作學習，深入體驗臺灣高等教育之教學與研究環境，同時也與本校學生進行交流互動，拓展國際視野。

活動於3月27日舉行成果發表會，邀請日本高專師長蒞臨指導，包括宇部高專副校長畑村學，以及小山高專教授岡田晃、大分高專教授菊川裕規及新居濱高專教授野田善弘等貴賓出席。學生以英語進行成果簡報，展現實習期間之學習成果，並由校方頒發實習證書，為本次國際實習活動畫下圓滿句點。

National United University welcomed students from six Japanese KOSEN institutions—Ube, Oyama, Oita, Hakodate, Kochi, and Kushiro KOSEN—for a one-month internship program in March 2026, demonstrating the university’s commitment to international academic exchange.

The program began with a welcome event held on March 3, attended by President Ti-Kuang Hou, Prof. Ju-May Wen (Head of the International Affairs Division), and faculty members from various departments. Vice President Hatamura Manabu from Ube KOSEN was invited as the distinguished guest. The event provided a warm environment for students to quickly adapt to campus life.

During the internship, Japanese students conducted laboratory-based research under faculty supervision, gaining hands-on academic experience while interacting with Taiwanese students and broadening their global perspectives.

The program concluded with a final presentation on March 27, attended by distinguished guests from Japanese KOSEN institutions, including Vice President Hatamura Manabu (Ube KOSEN), Professor Akira Okada (Oyama KOSEN), Professor Hironori Kikugawa (Oita KOSEN), and Professor Yoshihiro Noda (Niihama KOSEN). Students presented their work in English and received certificates, marking a successful completion of the program.

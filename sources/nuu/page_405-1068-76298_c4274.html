【公告】修正「受聘僱外國人健康檢查管理辦法」及外國技術人力健康檢查新制相關資訊，115年1月1日生效

字體大小調整 Font Size 小 中 大
一、依教育部函轉衛生福利部修正「受聘僱外國人健康檢查管理辦法」，並新增外國技術人力健康檢查新制圖卡，以及「各類受聘僱外國人健康檢查問答輯」等相關作業文件。

二、本案係依衛生福利部114年12月31日衛授疾字第1140101304號函，及衛生福利部疾病管制署114年12月31日疾管檢字第1142100385號函辦理。

三、相關問答輯、作業規範及宣導圖卡，請至衛生福利部疾病管制署網站查詢下載：
🔗 www.cdc.gov.tw

（路徑：國際旅遊與健康 → 外國人健康管理 → 最新消息）
或縮網址：https://gov.tw/fHQ

四、檢附相關來文及附件，請本校師生及有需求者自行參閱運用。

In accordance with the letter forwarded by the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Health and Welfare has revised the Regulations on Health Examinations for Employed Foreign Nationals, and has released new infographics on health check requirements for foreign professionals, as well as the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on Health Examinations for Various Categories of Employed Foreign Nationals and other related documents.

This matter is handled pursuant to the letter issued by the Ministry of Health and Welfare on December 31, 2025 (Ref. No. Wei-Shou-Ji-Zi No. 1140101304), and the letter issued by the Taiwan Centers for Disease Control on December 31, 2025 (Ref. No. Ji-Guan-Jian-Zi No. 1142100385).

The relevant FAQs, operational guidelines, and promotional infographics are available for download on the website of the Taiwan Centers for Disease Control:
🔗 www.cdc.gov.tw

(Path: International Travel and Health → Health Management for Foreign Nationals → Latest News)
or via the shortened URL: https://gov.tw/fHQ

The related official letters and attachments are enclosed for reference by faculty members, students, and other interested parties.

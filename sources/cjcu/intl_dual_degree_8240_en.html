Dual Degree Program

Dual Degree Program:

Through the Dual Degree Program, students can spend four years in undergraduate studies or two years in a master's program divided between our university and a partnered overseas institution. Upon completing the required courses at both institutions, students will receive degrees from both schools. For those interested, please contact the relevant department.

【澳洲 Australia】昆士蘭⼤學 The University of Queensland

學校基本資料 School Information

學校網站 Official Website：https://www.uq.edu.au/

國家：澳洲
Country：Australia

錄取名額：無限制，限本校碩士研究生
Admission Quota：No Limitation，Only For Master's Degree Students

 

交換期程 Exchange Period
一學年
One Academic Year

 

適合科系 Eligible Students

全校學生 All NKUHT students

 

語言門檻 Language Requirement

學術雅思Overall 6.5 以上 (每項不得低於6.0分)
Academic IELTS overall score of 6.5 or higher (With no individual band lower than 6.0)

托福iBT總分 87 (聽力19/閱讓19/寫作21/口說19
TOEFL iBT total score of 87 (Listening 19 / Reading 19 / Writing 21 / Speaking 19)
 

預估費用 Estimated Costs

學費：澳幣 27,048 / 學期
Tution：AUD 27,048 / semester

住宿費：澳幣 559 - 752 / 週 (無提供宿舍) 
Accommodation： AUD 559 - 752 / week (Dormitory is not provided)

保險費：澳幣 500/ 學期
Insurance：AUD 500 / semester

 

實習時間/薪資 Internship Duration / Salary

實習時間：每週以學生簽證可在當地打工20小時
Internship Duration：Students may work up to 20 hours per week under a student visa.


*實際費用請依澳洲學校說明會為主*
*Please refer to The University of Queensland ’s information session for details regarding the financial costs.*

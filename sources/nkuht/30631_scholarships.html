海外研修獎學金 Study Abroad Scholarships

「海外研修暨實習」獎學金有兩類，包含：
There are two categories of scholarships for study abroad， including: 

  • 教育部學海獎學金（學海飛颺、學海惜珠）
    MOE Scholarship for Excellent Students to Study Abroad or Overseas Internship Training
  • 高餐大「探索之航：發現新天地」出國獎學金
    NKUHT"Voyage of Discovery Scholarship": Uncover New Frontier for Study Abroad

注意：已領取教育部學海獎學金者，不得申請探索之航獎學金。

教育部學海飛颺 MOE Feiyang Scholarship(Click Me)

申請資格 Eligibility

  • 四技二年級、五專四年級以上在學學生。
    2nd-year undergraduate students and above; 4th-year junior college students and above.
  • 中華民國籍且在臺灣設有戶籍。
    R.O.C. nationality with household registration in Taiwan.
  • 前三學期操行平均達70 (含)以上。
    70 or above on average conduct scores in the previous 3 semesters.
  • 出國一學期或一學年，獲得國際處海外研修暨實習方案入學許可者。 
    Students who are admitted to the Office of International Affairs’ overseas study & internship program for one semester or one academic year.

獎助金額 Amount

新臺幣 50,000 元至 300,000 元（依當年度核定金額而定）
NT$50,000 – NT$300,000 (depending on the approved amount for the current year).

申請期間 Application Period

每年 1–2 月
Every January – February

教育部學海惜珠 MOE XiZhu Scholarship(Click Me)

申請資格 Eligibility

  • 四技二年級、五專四年級以上在學學生。
    2nd-year undergraduate students and above; 4th-year junior college students and above.
  • 中華民國籍且在臺灣設有戶籍。
    R.O.C. nationality with household registration in Taiwan.
  • 前三學期操行平均達70 (含)以上。
    70 or above on average conduct scores in the previous 3 semesters.
  • 出國一學期或一學年，獲得國際處海外研修暨實習方案入學許可者。
    Students who are admitted to the Office of International Affairs’ overseas study & internship program for one semester or one academic year.
  • 具低收入、中低收入戶或其他符合惜珠計畫補助資格之經濟條件者。
    Students from low- or middle-low-income families who meet the XiZhu Scholarship requirements.

獎助金額 Amount

依教育部專案審查核定金額為準。
The approved amount is subject to review and approval by the MOE.

申請期間 Application Period

每年 1–2 月
Every January – February

國立高雄餐旅大學 探索之航獎學金 
NKUHT Voyage of Discovery Scholarship(Click Me)

申請資格 Eligibility

  • 本校在學學生。
    Current enrolled students.
  • 通過國際處海外研修暨實習方案或雙聯學位甄選，獲得入學許可者。
    Students who have been selected through the Office of International Affairs’ overseas study and internship program or dual-degree selection and have received admission
  • 不得同時領取教育部或其他國內外獎學金。
    Students are not allowed to concurrently receive scholarships from the Ministry of Education or other domestic and international sources.  
  • 首次參加國際處海外研修暨實習方案或雙聯學位學程者。
    Students participating for the first time in the Office of International Affairs’ overseas study and internship program or dual-degree program.

獎助金額 Amount

  • 出國一學期：最多4萬 A semester: up to NT$40,000
  • 出國一學年：最多6萬 An academic year: up to NT$60,000

申請期間 Application Period

每年 1–2 月
Every January – February

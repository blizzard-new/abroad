About National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism (NKUHT)

Welcome to National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism (NKUHT)
Your Gateway to Excellence in Hospitality and Tourism Education

Located in the vibrant city of Kaohsiung, Taiwan’s second-largest city, NKUHT is the only public university in Taiwan exclusively dedicated to hospitality and tourism education. With a stellar reputation both locally and internationally, NKUHT has been ranked among the world’s Top 50 universities in the QS World University Rankings by Subject for “Hospitality and Leisure Management” for three consecutive years, achieving and maintaining 38th place in 2023 and 2024.

Since its establishment in 1995, NKUHT has been at the forefront of combining academic excellence with practical learning. Offering Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Ph.D. degrees, the university provides hands-on training through internships, global skill competitions, and partnerships with industry leaders around the world.

International Programs for Global Learners

At NKUHT, international students can choose from a variety of English-taught programs, including:

  • International Ph.D. in Tourism Management
  • International Master’s Program in Tourism and Hospitality
  • International Bachelor Program in Tourism Management
  • International Bachelor Program in Culinary Arts

Additionally, two bilingual undergraduate programs, the 4-Year Bilingual Program in  Hotel Management  and the 4-Year Bilingual Program in  Leisure and Recreation Management, offer 70% of their courses in English, providing an immersive bilingual learning environment.

A Truly Global Campus

Home to 4,437 students and 309 faculty members, NKUHT prides itself on its diverse and inclusive community, with over 10% of students and 6% of faculty coming from international backgrounds. Convenient public transportation—including bikes, buses, MRT, and a light rail system—makes exploring Kaohsiung and beyond easy. International students also enjoy priority access to on-campus dormitories, ensuring a smooth transition into university life.

With a network of over 100 international partner universities in 27 countries, NKUHT offers extensive opportunities for exchange programs and overseas internships, allowing students to gain invaluable global experience.

Mandarin Learning and Foundation Program

For students interested in learning Mandarin, NKUHT provides tailored  Mandarin language courses  for international students and professionals. The innovative  International Foundation Program (1+4 & 1+2)  allows students to focus on Mandarin learning during their first year. Upon achieving TOCFL Level A2, students can seamlessly transition into their chosen bachelor’s degree programs.

Scholarships and Financial Support

International students at NKUHT can benefit from various financial aid options, including:

  • Government scholarships
  • NKUHT scholarships
  • Teaching assistantships

Connect with Us!

Explore more about NKUHT and stay updated on our latest achievements and opportunities. Follow us on  Facebook ,  Instagram, and  YouTube  to discover why NKUHT is the perfect choice for your global education journey.

University information

If you’re an international student who’s interested in studying at the National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism, you will need to submit the following documents:

  •  Two copies of the completed admission application form
  • Photocopy of educational qualification, verified by a ROC overseas representative office or local authentication agencies
  •   One photocopy of the official transcript, verified by a ROC overseas representative office or local authentication agencies
  • Financial statement and financial guarantee statement, verified by a ROC representative office
  • Photocopy of passport or other proof of nationality
  • Study plan
  • TOCFL test, SAT Subject Test in Chinese with listening result
  • Photocopy of supporting proof of English proficiency
     

Please note, you will be required to pay a fee to obtain the Alien Resident Certificate.

For more information about VISA / ARC (Alien Resident Certificate).

Scholarships are also available for home and international students across all degree levels. These can be awarded for outstanding academic performance and financial-need.

Counselling and assistance

National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism has its very own professional student counselling team who provide mental health education classes, offer professional counselling, as well as several other services.
In addition, the university closely monitors students’ attendance and grades in order to assist with their learning progress. There is an advisor supervision system where teaching staff can review students’ academic performance and develop a teaching plan to help with the students’ learning and offer additional support should they need it.
In addition, students who undertake an internship out of school will receive visits from academic advisors to monitor their progress. Students will need to keep weekly journals and submit a report after they complete their internship.

Special services are also available for international students.

Student life

Kaohsiung City itself is a vibrant metropolis, characterized by its friendly people and safe society. With its fancy malls, restaurants and cultural events, Kaohsiung is a fascinating place to both visit and live whilst it also offers a wide range of opportunities and activities for students to enjoy.

The city offers excellent public transport links and is only a 10 minute drive from Kaohsiung international airport.   

Due to the regulating effects of the marine climate, Kaohsiung is generally sunny and enjoys pleasant weather year-round.

When it comes to student life at NKUGT, it’s a truly international experience. Approximately 5 percent of students are international, representing a multitude of countries including Malaysia, Indonesia, Korea, Japan, Australia, France, Paraguay, the US, Thailand, Czech, Vietnam, Philippines, and Singapore.

Students have access to over 50 student clubs, such as:

    … and many more! There are also other popular activities such as parties for homestay families or multi-cultural experience and training courses, celebration events for international days on campus, as well as the first-year student volleyball game.


Student accommodation

Students have the option to live either on- or off-campus. The National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism has its very own on-campus student accommodation which offers safe, convenient and comfortable living. Plus, it’s only a five minute walk from the university campus, so there’s no need to worry about being late!

All first-year students are required to live on-campus during the first year of their studies, allowing them to become more easily acquainted with the campus and the city. It’s also a good way of making new friends!

Students who live in the NKUHT student accommodation have access to a number of amenities, including freshwater dispensers, a laundry room, and a local convenient store that’s located on the ground floor.

Employability

Within few months of graduating, 85.9 percent of NKUHT graduates were in employment. The other 14.1 percent were enrolled in further education, or in military service.

To prepare for the international job market as talented and qualified graduates, the National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism offers students the opportunity to attend and take part in career fairs, as well as domestic and overseas internships which provide students with real-life work experience in the hospitality and tourism industries.

 

Alumni network

Students can be part of a truly international alumni network that can take them to all four corners of the globe.  


Career seminars

The National Kaohsiung University of Hospitality and Tourism regularly organizes career expositions and industrial recruitment seminars for graduating students in order to support students in their career development. In addition to career support and employment seminars, industrial lecturers, experts and alumni are invited to share their experiences with current students. Students will then receive support to help build their own careers.


e-Portfolio platform

The university has established the e-Portfolio platform, on which every student can keep a record of personal learning until graduation. From the personal portfolio, students can review their strength and weakness, and gradually work to find their career position. This platform will help them easily write up resumes, and create and market their personal image.

NKUHT Scholarship

 

There are scholarships for undergraduate students, Master Program students, and Ph.D. students.

Applicants can apply for it during the admission application process.

 

For undergraduate students, there are 3 kinds of scholarships:

(1) Tuition and miscellaneous fees waived.

(2) Half of tuition and miscellaneous fees waived.

(3) Dormitory fees waived. The maximum duration is for 4 years.

(4) Teaching Assistantship

 

For Master students, there are 2 kinds of scholarships:

(1) Tuition, miscellaneous fees and credit fees waived.

(2) Tuition and miscellaneous fees waived. The maximum duration is for 2 years.

(3) Teaching Assistantship

 

For Ph.D. students there are 2 kinds of scholarships:

(1) Tuition, miscellaneous fees, credit fees and dormitory fees waived. 

(2) Tuition and miscellaneous fees waived. The maximum duration is for 3 years.

(3) Teaching Assistantship

 

*Scholarship specifications and regulations are subject to change.

VISA information

International students should obtain a student visa or entry permit at a Taiwan overseas representative office before coming to Taiwan. Two kinds of visas are available: visitor visa and resident visa. For the latest visa information, please visit the Taiwan Representative Office or Embassy in your country or check the following website:

Bureau of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Republic of China

boca.gov.tw

 

(1) Visitor visa

International students with valid passports or other legal documents who intend to stay for no more than six months in Taiwan can apply for a visitor visa.

 

(2) Resident visa

International students with valid passports or other legal documents who intend to stay for longer than six months in Taiwan can apply for a resident visa.

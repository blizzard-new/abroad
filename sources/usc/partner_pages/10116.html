瑞士飯店管理大學 Hotel and Tourism Management Institute in Switzerland

瑞士飯店管理大學

Hotel and Tourism Management Institute in Switzerland

學校介紹 School Introduction

HTMi 是以優良的教育及訓練品質享譽國際。學生在此上課、住宿和實習，學校即飯店，充份體現了理論與實踐的結合。宗旨為「來的時候是一位學生，畢業時是一位專業經理人。」學生畢業後可獲得由本校所頒發的瑞士文憑及英國歐斯特大學所頒發之學士學位；研究所方面則有由英國愛丁堡龍比亞大學與本校分別頒發之雙碩士學位。HTMi 所有課程皆由EDUQUA 瑞士政府官方教育品質的認證機構所認證。

The HTMi Vision is: To be a leading Hotel Management Institute in the World.
To achieve this, they take a three-pronged, multi-faceted training and development approach.
This includes, high quality and high-profile industrial partners that helps to keep our programmes up to date with contemporary working requirements in the leading-edge hotels of the world.

Secondly, they employ a highly experienced teaching faculty who have a broad mix of both industrial and academic skills and who between them have worked in over 30 countries. These backgrounds ensure students have top quality experiences and feel ready to embrace industrial internships and the workplace once their studies have been completed. An HTMi graduate is also guaranteed opportunities to apply for management positions with our sister company hotel brand, Swisstouches Hotels and Resorts in China, Vietnam or Switzerland.

計畫說明 Program

3+1雙聯學制計畫 (雙學士)

  1. 申請資格: 限民生學院餐管系、文創學院觀管系與休產系
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)68分以上/雅思5.5級分以上
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 於姊妹校修讀18週，另於瑞士當地飯店(或姊妹校安排機構)帶薪實習24週，完成其他學業規定後可取得姊妹校學士學位

1+1雙聯學制計畫 (三邊碩士)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院碩士班與民生學院餐飲產業創新碩士班
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)79分以上/雅思6級分以上
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 於姊妹校修讀18週，另於瑞士當地飯店(或姊妹校安排機構)帶薪實習24週，完成其他學業規定後可取得姊妹校(HTMi)MBA以及愛丁堡龍比亞(Edinburgh Napier University)Ms碩士學位

3+1 Dual Bachelor Degree Program (One-year)

  1. Eligibility: Dept. of Food and Beverage Management, Dept. of Tourism Management and Dept. of Recreation Management
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 68 / IELTS 5.5
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at HTMi for 18 weeks and take an internship at a hotel or institution in Switzerland for 24 weeks

1+1 Trilateral Master Degree Program (One-year)

  1. Eligibility: College of Management Master’s Program and Innovation Management in the Food Service Industry Master’s program
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 79 / IELTS 6
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at HTMi for 18 weeks and take an internship at a hotel or institution in Switzerland for 24 weeks. After fulfilling the academic requirement, students can be awarded MSc in Hospitality and Tourism Management from Edinburgh Napier University (ENU) and MBA in Hospitality Management from HTMi

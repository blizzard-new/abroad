約翰內姆應用科技大學 FH Joanneum University of Applied Sciences

約翰內姆應用科技大學

FH Joanneum University of Applied Sciences

學校介紹 School Introduction

約翰內姆應用科技大學創立於1995 年，為奧地利最大的應用科技大學之一，學生人數約四千多人。主校區位於格拉茲，另外兩校區位於卡普芬貝格及巴特格萊興貝格。約翰內姆應用科技大學約有45 個學位學程，教學的重點是能應用於未來的就業的學習，並促進跨學科、跨領域的合作。而約翰內姆應用科技大學也和全世界200 多間學校為合作夥伴。

FH Joanneum is the second-largest university of applied sciences in Austria. It has about 4,200 students and about 651 employees.The main campus is located in Graz, while there are two other locations in Kapfenberg and Bad Gleichenberg. All three are situated in the province of Styria, Austria. FH Joanneum offers 47 degree programs in a variety of areas including business, technology, design, media, architecture, health and social services. The programs are practice-oriented, project-based and interdisciplinary.

計畫說明 Program

單學期交換計畫 (僅修讀學分)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院、國際學程、商資學院、民生學院餐管系、食保系、文創學院觀管系與休產系
  2. 申請名額: 3名 (免繳姊妹校學費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)61分以上/雅思5級分以上/多益600分以上
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 僅Health & Tourism Management開放申請

One-semester Exchange Program  (Credits Transfer Only)

  1. Eligibility: College of Management, International College, College of Business and Information, Dept. of Restaurant and Institutional Management,  Dept. of Food Science, Nutrition, and Nutraceutical Biotechnology, Dept. of Tourism Management and Dept. of Recreation Management.
  2. Quota: 3 students (Tuition waiver)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 61 / IELTS 5 / TOEIC 600
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Only Health & Tourism Management is available.

威斯康辛大學-河瀑分校 University of Wisconsin – River Falls

威斯康辛大學-河瀑分校

University of Wisconsin – River Falls

學校介紹 School Introduction

威斯康辛大學河瀑校區創建於1874 年，坐落於美國威斯康辛州北部的聖克羅伊河谷，距離明尼蘇達州雙城約30 分鐘。校園面積226 英畝，並有佔地150 英畝和290 英畝的實驗農場。擁有學生6000 名，師生比為1：20。目前設置四個學院：文理學院、教育及專業學院、商業及經濟學院、農業、食品及環境科學學院，總計超過40 個專業學程。

University of Wisconsin – River Falls was founded in 1874, it is located on the Kinnickinnic River in the St. Croix River Valley. The 226-acre (91 ha) campus is situated on the Kinnickinnic River in the St. Croix River Valley. The university has 32 major buildings and two laboratory farms, with a total of 440 acres (178 ha) of land. UWRF offers over 40 undergraduate majors. Some of these programs are accredited by specialized accreditors, including the National Association of Schools of Music, the Council on Social Work Education, the Association for Education in Journalism and Mass Communication, the National Association of School Psychologists, the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business, the Council for Academic Accreditation in Audiology and Speech-Language Pathology (CAA), the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics, the American Chemical Society, and the American Society of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering.

計畫說明 Program

單學期交換計畫 (僅修讀學分)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院、國際學程、商資學院及民生學院家兒系。
  2. 申請名額: 不限額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)78分以上/雅思6級分以上/多益800分以上
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75分以上

3+1雙聯學制計畫 (雙學士)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院、國際學程及商資學院之學系(應外/英系可赴該校修讀TESOL 學士學位)
  2. 申請名額: 不限額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)78分以上/雅思6級分以上
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 大四時前往姊妹校修讀一年，修滿最低30學分並符合其他畢業相關規定，即可取得姊妹校學士學位

1+1雙聯學制計畫 (雙碩士)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院國際企業英語碩士學位學程
  2. 申請名額: 不限額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)80分以上/雅思6.5級分以上
  4. 學業成績:歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上。
  5. 其他: 碩二時前往姊妹校修讀一年，修滿規定學分並符合其他畢業相關規定，即可取得姊妹校碩士學位

One-semester Exchange Program (Credits Transfer only)

  1. Eligibility : College of Management, International College, College of Business and Information, and Dept. of Family Studies and Child Development
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 78 / IELTS 6 / TOEIC 800
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75

3+1 Dual Bachelor Degree Program (One year)

  1. Eligibility: College of Management, International College and College of Business and Information (Applied Foreign Language & Applied English can apply for TESOL degree)
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 78 / IELTS 6
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at UW-RF in senior year, and should fulfill minimum 30 credits and UW-RF graduation requirements.

1+1 Dual Master Degree Program (One year)

  1. Eligibility: English Taught Program in International Business (Graduate only)
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 80 / IELTS 6.5
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at UW-RF in the second year, and should fulfill required credits and graduation requirements.

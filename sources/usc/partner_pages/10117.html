計畫說明 Program

3+1雙聯學制計畫 (雙學士)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院、商資學院、民生學院餐管系、國際學程
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)60分以上(寫作口說需17分以上；聽力閱讀需11分以上)/雅思6.0以上(各項皆需5.5以上)/PTE 50 with no communicative score less than 46(未達英文入學門檻可就讀學術英文銜接課程，順利完成語言課程並通過校內評估即可直接入讀正式學位。語言課程週數將視學生提交的英文成績評估。)
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 大四時前往姊妹校修讀一年，修滿規定學分並符合其他畢業相關規定，即可取得姊妹校學士學位
  6. 可修讀學士系別：
    • Bachelor of Business in Business
    • Bachelor of Business in Accounting 

3+1+1雙聯學制計畫 (雙學士/碩士)

  1. 申請資格: 限管理學院、商資學院、民生學院餐管系、國際學程
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福(iBT)60分以上(寫作口說需17分以上；聽力閱讀需11分以上)/雅思6.0以上(各項皆需5.5以上)/PTE 50 with no communicative score less than 42(未達英文入學門檻可就讀學術英文銜接課程，順利完成且通過校內評估即可直接入讀正式學位。語言課程週數將視學生提交的英文成績評估。)
  4. 學業成績: 歷年學業及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 大四時前往姊妹校修讀一年，修滿規定學分並符合其他畢業相關規定，即可取得姊妹校學士學位；再於姊妹校修讀一年並滿足相關學業規定，即可取得姊妹校碩士學位
  6. 可修讀碩士系所：
    • MBA

3+1 Dual Bachelor Degree Program (One-year)

  1. Eligibility: College of Management, College of Business and Information and Dept. of Food and Beverage Managment, International College
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 60 (Speaking and Writing should be above 17; Reading and Listening should be above 11) / IELTS 6.0 (Each subject should be above 5.5)/PTE 50 with no communicative score less than 46 (If students don’t not meet the English requirements can enroll in a pathway English course to transition into the formal degree program. The number of weeks required for the language course will be determined based on the English proficiency scores provided by the student.)
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at SCU in senior year, and should fulfill required credits and graduation requirements
  6. Bachelor Degree Program available:
    • Bachelor of Business in Business
    • Bachelor of Business in in Accounting 

3+1+1 Dual Bachelor/ Master Degree Program (Two-year)

  1. Eligibility: College of Management, College of Business and Information and Dept. of Food and Beverage Managment, International College
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency: TOEFL iBT 60 (Speaking and Writing should be above 17; Reading and Listening should be above 11) / IELTS 6.0 (Each subject should be above 5.5)/PTE 50 with no communicative score less than 42 (If students don’t not meet the English requirements can enroll in a pathway English course to transition into the formal degree program. The number of weeks required for the language course will be determined based on the English proficiency scores provided by the student.)
  4. Academic Requirement: The academic & conduct average grade should be over 75
  5. Note: Study at SCU in senior year, and should fulfill required credits and graduation requirements; study the Master’s program at SCU for another year and should fulfill the academic requirement
  6.  Master Degree Program available:
    • MBA

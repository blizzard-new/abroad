Griffith University

學校介紹 School Introduction

格里菲斯大學（Griffith University，簡稱GU）位於昆士蘭，是一所全球排名前2%的公立研究型大學，亦為澳洲創新研究大學聯盟（IRU-Australia）及亞太國際貿易研究聯盟（PACIBER）的成員之一。該校以卓越的教學與研究聞名，在2023年泰晤士高等教育年輕大學排名中位列第46位，並且根據聯合國17項可持續發展目標（SDGs），在2021至2023年間的泰晤士高等教育影響力排名中名列全球前100名。GU擁有超過55,000名學生，校區分布於黃金海岸、洛根、內森、格拉瓦特山和南岸，並於2018年設立數位校區，提供一系列線上學位。學校的課程設計符合行業需求，著眼於未來發展，同時以社會影響為核心，這使得GU在全球大學排名中始終位列前2%。

Griffith University (GU), located in Queensland, is a public research university ranked in the top 2% globally. It is also a member of the Innovative Research Universities (IRU-Australia) and the Pacific Basin Economic Council for International Business Education and Research (PACIBER). Renowned for its excellence in teaching and research, GU was ranked 46th in the 2023 Times Higher Education Young University Rankings. Additionally, based on the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), GU was ranked among the top 100 universities globally in the Times Higher Education Impact Rankings from 2021 to 2023.

GU serves over 55,000 students across campuses located in Gold Coast, Logan, Nathan, Mount Gravatt, and South Bank. In 2018, GU established a digital campus offering a range of online degrees. The university’s programs are designed to meet industry needs, focusing on future development and societal impact, which ensures its consistent ranking among the top 2% of universities worldwide.

計畫說明 Program

2+2 雙聯學制計畫   (學士)

  1. 申請資格: 限國際學程(限ETPIB)、文創學院(限觀光管理學系)及商資學院(限商科學系)
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福575/托福(iBT)79分以上(每科不得低於19)/雅思均分6.5以上(每科不低於6.0)/PTE58分以上(每科不得低於50)
  4. 學業成績: 大一跟大二平均成績60分以上及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 需於實踐大學修讀完成學士兩年課程，完成後可取得實踐大學與格里菲斯大學雙學位。

3+1+1 雙聯學制計畫  (學士+碩士)

  1. 申請資格: 限國際學程(限ETPIB)、文創學院(限觀光管理學系)及商資學院(限商科學系)
  2. 申請名額: 不限申請名額 (需繳交姊妹校學雜費)
  3. 語言能力: 托福575/托福(iBT)79分以上(每科不得低於19)/雅思均分6.5以上(每科不低於6.0)/PTE58分以上(每科不得低於50)
  4. 學業成績: 大一至大三平均成績60分以上及操行平均成績75 分以上
  5. 其他: 需於實踐大學修讀完成學士三年課程，完成後可申請格里菲斯大學一年碩士Study Abroad Program (SAP)，完成SAP課程後，可同時取得實踐大學學士學位，再完成最後一年格里菲斯大學碩士學位，即可取得。

2+2 Dual Bachelor Degree Program (Two-year)

  1. Eligibility: Interantional College (Only ETPIB), College of Culture and Creative (Only Dept. of Tourism Management), College of Business and Information(Only Business Dept.)
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency:  TOEFL 575/ TOEFL iBT 79 (Each subject should be above 19) / IELTS 6.5 (Each subject should be above 6.0) / PTE58 (Each subject should be above 50)
  4. Academic Requirement: First and second-year average grades must be above 60 & conduct average grade should be over 75.
  5. Note: Completion of two years at Shih Chien University is required. After completion, will receive dual degrees from both Shih Chien University and Griffith University.

3+1+1 Dual Bachelor/ Master Degree Program (Two-year)

  1. Eligibility: Interantional College (Only ETPIB), College of Culture and Creative (Only Dept. of Tourism Management), College of Business and Information(Only Business Dept.)
  2. Quota: No limitation (Fee-paying)
  3. Language Proficiency:  TOEFL 575/ TOEFL iBT 79 (Each subject should be above 19) / IELTS 6.5 (Each subject should be above 6.0) / PTE58 (Each subject should be above 50)
  4. Academic Requirement: First to third-year average grades must be above 60 & conduct average grade should be over 75.
  5. Note: Complete a three-year bachelor's program at Shih Chien University and apply for Griffith University's one-year Study Abroad Program (SAP). After completing SAP, will receive a bachelor's degree from Shih Chien University and a master's degree from Griffith University.

4. SECURITY, SAFETY & LEGAL RESPONSIBILITIES
Students must respect and abide by the Constitution and Laws of Vietnam, including local (Thai
Nguyen province) and university regulations.
Any student involved in legal or disciplinary matters must cooperate with the university and
relevant authorities to resolve the issue properly.
In the case of emergencies, conflicts, or legal incidents, students are required to:
Inform the AEP Office or designated university contact.
Avoid posting sensitive or unresolved legal issues publicly or on social media without
clearance.
Students will bear full responsibility before the law for violations of any of the above rules or the
terms of their student visa.
5. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY AND CONDUCT
All students are expected to:
Attend classes regularly and meet academic requirements.
Respect instructors, staff, and fellow students.
Avoid cheating, plagiarism, or any form of academic dishonesty.
Disciplinary actions may be taken for violations of academic or behavioral policies, including
warnings, suspension, or expulsion.
6. COMMUNICATION & CONTACT
Students must maintain updated contact information with the AEP Office (including phone
number or ZALO number, email, and address).
In case of travel, illness, emergencies, or absence from campus, students are expected to
inform the AEP Office or student assistant staff.
Students should check emails and official channels regularly for important announcements.
RULES AND
REGULATIONS

Students are expected to take full personal responsibility for complying with all of the above
commitments. Violations may result in disciplinary action by the university, involvement of local
authorities, or other legal consequences as applicable under Vietnamese law.

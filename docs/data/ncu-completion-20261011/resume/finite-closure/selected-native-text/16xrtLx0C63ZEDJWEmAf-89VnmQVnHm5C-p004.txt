
8. Proof of English Proficiency
 Applicants must demonstrate English proficiency equivalent to CEFR B2.
 Acceptable evidence includes:
•
Official English test scores listed in the application guidelines; or
•
A CEFR B2 English Proficiency Certification Letter issued by the home institution.
•
Documentation from the home institution certifying that the applicant is enrolled in a degree
program taught entirely in English.
 Please refer to the latest application guidelines for detailed requirements and accepted
documentation.

WITHDRAWING FROM THE PROGRAMME
Before arriving at NTU
You must inform your home university in writing of
your decision to withdraw, with NTU's Office of
Global Education and Mobility (OGEM) in copy.
After arriving at NTU
You must inform your home university in writing of
your decision to withdraw, with NTU's Office of
Global Education and Mobility (OGEM) in copy,
before firming up your departure travel plans.
Withdrawals requested ‌after arriving at NTU‌ will be
counted as a utilised exchange spot.
KEY THINGS TO KNOW:
ARRIVAL & DEPARTURE
All inbound students are expected to‌
attend classes from the first day of the‌
semester.‌ ‌You are strongly encouraged to
arrive early in the week before classes
begin, which will give you time to settle
in, adjust to campus life, and take part in
Welcome Week activities organised for
inbound students. The inbound team will
share more detailed guidance on the
recommended arrival date closer to the
start of the semester. Arriving after the
semester has begun is strongly
discouraged.
Course changes are not permitted after
the add/drop period (Teaching Weeks 1
and 2) under any circumstances, and
remote learning options are not available.‌
If you are unable to arrive by the
recommended time, please contact OGEM
promptly with valid reasons so that we
can advise you accordingly.
When planning your return travel, please
ensure that your flight is scheduled‌ ‌after‌
the official end of the semester‌, as
examinations may be held on the final
day. Examination dates cannot be
rescheduled. Students who miss an exam
without valid supporting documentation
will be deemed to have failed the course,
and an “F” grade will be reflected on the
transcript.
OFFICIAL TRANSCRIPTS
Official NTU transcripts will be made available to
your home university's exchange office within two
months of completing the exchange programme.
There is no need to request these transcripts.
ACADEMIC UNITS AND GRADING SYSTEM
NTU's courses are assigned credits based on
academic units. Most courses offered in NTU are
worth three to four AUs.
The AU is a measure of the student's workload
associated with both class attendance and
preparation. For a typical one-semester course, the
number of AU is calculated as follows:‌ ‌
1 ‌hour of lecture/tutorial per week : 1 AU‌ ‌
3 hours laboratory/fieldwork per week : 1 AU
​NTU uses the Grade Point Average (GPA) system in‌
awarding grades. The Pass/Fail option is not‌
applicable to exchange students.

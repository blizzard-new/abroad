APPENDIX
Proof of English proficiency is required where English is ‌not ‌the medium of instruction in the home
institution. These guidelines are in place to ensure that exchange students are suited to cope with the
rigorous curriculum conducted in English.
We accept the following English proficiency tests and indicative grades:‌
IELTS/IELTS Indicator: a minimum 6 (Overall) and 6 (Writing)‌
TOEFL/TOEFL iBT® ‌(taken before 2026)‌: a minimum of 570 (paper) or 90 (internet) or 237 (computer)‌
TOEFL/TOEFL iBT® ‌(taken from January 2026)‌: a minimum 4.5 (Overall) and 4.5 (Speaking)
Note:‌
TOEFL MyBest® scores are not accepted.‌
For IELTS and TOEFL, only scores obtained in the 2 years leading up to the application deadline will be
considered.

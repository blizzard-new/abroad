Moving out
1. Moving out procedure
1) Submit ‘Application for Leaving/ Refund’ form
① Visit the Dormitory office and get the ‘Application for Leaving/ Refund’ form
② Clean up the room → fill in the check list → sign in
③ ‌Submit the application for leaving / refund form to the Dormitory office on
your check-out date
2) Check-out Report
① Room condition check-up with office staff members
② Return all provided items (ex: Room key, Laundry basket)
③ ‌Check-out Completed (The deposit refund procedure might take more than
2 weeks.)

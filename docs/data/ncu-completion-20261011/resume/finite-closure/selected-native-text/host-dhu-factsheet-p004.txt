Insurance (mandatory)
You may purchase the international insurance at home country. However, you are REQUIRED to purchase the
designated insurance -- Guoren Property & Casualty Insurance Co., Ltd. for Foreigners Staying in China –
before the enrollment (CNY400/semester). For those who want to get their X1 visa to Residence Permit (RP)
are required to pay for two semesters’ insurance fee (CNY 800 in total) at a lump sum according to the
regulations on visa/residence permit. For more information about this insurance, you may refer to
https://english.dhu.edu.cn/insurance/list.htm .

Accommodation:
1. International Student Apartment (ISAs) – West Yan’an Road campus
⚫ According to the exchange agreements, students can only choose Double Room (shared by 2 students, 1
bathroom).
There will be two types of room: CNY 60/night; CNY 75/night. For more information, please check on
https://english.dhu.edu.cn/2014/1113/c5274a325021/page.htm
Payment: the accommodation fee will be confirmed and need to be paid in one lump sum upon check-in.
⚫ Each room is equipped with private bathroom, basic furniture, air conditioner, fridge and internet jack. In
the public areas, there are washing machines, microwave oven, hot & cold-water dispenser,
electromagnetic stoves, professional laundry service, self-study lounge, etc.
⚫ Residences in ISA should afford the internet use on their own.

2. Off-campus residence:
Students can choose off-campus apartment with the recommendation from friends or from the local real
estate agency. The specific residential address information should be submitted to the office upon
enrollment.

Financial Aid
Since these programs are based on exchange agreements between Tohoku University and the students' home institutions,
tuition fees are waived for exchange students.  Although the students themselves should bear the cost of living, they are eligible
to apply for the JASSO Student Exchange Support Program (Scholarship for Short-term Study in Japan), which provides an
allowance of 80,000 yen/month for the duration of study.  Applicants will be asked to indicate their intention at the time of
application.

Double Degree Programs
Double Degree Programs are based on memorandums of understanding between Tohoku University and the following
partner institutions.  Under this program, students can undergo research guidance at Tohoku University and their home
institution and receive master's degrees from both universities.
International Joint Graduate Programs
Collaborating with leading universities abroad, International Joint Graduate Programs enhance the students' competitive
edge in fields where Tohoku University has strengths. Supervised by both home and host universities, students receive a
degree from their home university, along with a certificate of joint-supervision.

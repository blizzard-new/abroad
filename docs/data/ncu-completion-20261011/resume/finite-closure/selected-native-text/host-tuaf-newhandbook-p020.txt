COURSE
REGISTRATION
To ensure a smooth enrollment and registration process each semester, students
must follow the steps below:
1. ACADEMIC PLAN DISTRIBUTION
At the beginning of each academic semester or year, the Advanced Education Program
(AEP) Office will send an official academic plan to all students via email. This plan
outlines the course offerings, credit requirements, and study schedule for the upcoming
term. Students are encouraged to review this document carefully to plan their semester
accordingly.
2. SUBMISSION OF ENROLLMENT FORMS
After receiving the academic plan, students must complete and return the official
Enrollment Form to the AEP Office no later than one (1) week before the start of the
academic term. This form allows the AEP to gather course preferences and necessary
information for course registration. Late submissions may result in delays or limited
course options.
3. REGISTRATION AND CONFIRMATION
For international students and those needing assistance, AEP will facilitate the
registration process. Once enrollment is processed, a confirmation email will be sent to
each student to verify the registered subjects. Students should double-check their
registration details upon receiving the confirmation and report any discrepancies
immediately.
4. RELEASE OF TIMETABLES
After all registrations are finalized, AEP will provide students with a personalized class
timetable. This includes course schedules, classroom assignments, and instructor
information. Timetables are sent via email and made available on the academic portal, if
applicable.
5. ADD/DROP PERIOD
Students are allowed to revise their course selections during the first two (2) weeks of
the semester. During this period, they may add or drop subjects by sending a formal
request via email to the AEP Office. After the add/drop deadline, changes to the schedule
will not be permitted unless under special circumstances approved by the Academic
Affairs Office.

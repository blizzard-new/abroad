Important Changes and Notes Regarding the SDW: Sandwich
Program (Coursework-Based Exchange)
  NEW / Important Changes
1. Graduate-Level Course Enrollment Restriction (Effective Spring 2027)
 From Spring Semester 2027, undergraduate students participating in the SDW Program will
no longer be allowed to enroll in graduate-level courses.
 Students should consider this change when preparing study plans and discussing credit
transfer arrangements with their home institution.
 Until the Spring 2027 course list is published, students should refer to the current
timetable for reference.

2. Special Provision for Students from Five-Year Integrated Degree Programs
 Students enrolled in integrated five-year degree programs may be considered academically
equivalent to master's students during their senior years.
 Eligible students may continue to enroll in graduate-level courses.
 Partner institutions must explain which year(s) of their program are considered master's-
level equivalent.
 Supporting documentation must be submitted through the TAO application system by the
nominator.

3. Mandatory Submission of Financial Support Documentation
 Due to stricter immigration screening procedures, all student visa applicants must provide
proof of financial capability.
 Applicants must demonstrate sufficient financial resources to cover their living expenses
during their stay. Details regarding the estimated monthly living expenses will be provided in
the application system.
 Supporting documents must be uploaded through the TAO application system.

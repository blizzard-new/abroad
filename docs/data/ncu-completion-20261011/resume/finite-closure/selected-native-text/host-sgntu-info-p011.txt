NTU STUDENT INSURANCE COVERAGE
PERSONAL INSURANCE
Enrolment in NTU’s student insurance schemes‌
(comprising Group Personal Accident Insurance,‌
Group Hospitalisation & Surgical Insurance, and‌
Medical Scheme) is included in the fees payable by‌
exchange students. Coverage under these schemes is‌
mandatory, regardless of any existing insurance you‌
may already hold‌. You should also purchase
comprehensive travel insurance for your trip to and
from Singapore and any travel during the semester.
BANKING FACILITIES ON CAMPUS
OCBC bank on campus offers banking services, with
an early closure fee for accounts closed within six
months. ATMs are available across campus for cash
withdrawals.

MEDICAL AND WELLBEING SERVICES
Medical, dental, and counselling services are available
on campus to support your wellbeing. For after-hours
care, visit a nearby clinic, or in emergencies, go to Ng
Teng Fong General Hospital.
GETTING TO CAMPUS
NTU is located in the western part of Singapore and is
accessible from Changi Airport via MRT, taxi, or ride-
hailing (Grab, GoJek, TADA, etc). Our pre-arrival guide
will provide more information.
To ensure that your wellbeing is addressed, declare all
existing conditions (medical, physical/mobility,
sensory, neurological, learning disabilities, etc.) in
your application. You may be asked to provide medical
records or specialist reports for evaluation.

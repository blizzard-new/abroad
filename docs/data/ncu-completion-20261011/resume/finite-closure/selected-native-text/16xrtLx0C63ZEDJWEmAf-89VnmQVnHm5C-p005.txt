Important Changes and Notes Regarding the REP
(Research Exchange Program)
Please ensure that the following important information is communicated to students prior to
nomination and application for the REP Program.

  NEW / Important Changes

1. Simultaneous Participation in the SDW Program (Coursework-Based Exchange Program) Is
Not Permitted （NEW）

Due to the increasing number of SDW participants, SIT expects that available course capacity
will need to be prioritized for students officially enrolled in the SDW Program. As a result, it has
become difficult to accommodate requests from REP students who wish to enroll in courses in
addition to their research activities. Although REP students staying for one semester were
previously allowed to take courses upon request, this arrangement will no longer be available.
Accordingly:
•
Students participating in the REP (Research Exchange Program) are not eligible to
participate in the SDW Program (coursework-based exchange program) at the same
time.
•
Students admitted to the REP Program will engage exclusively in research activities.
•
Course enrollment will not be permitted for REP students.


2. Mandatory Submission of Financial Support Documentation
Beginning in July 2026, the Immigration Services Agency of Japan will implement stricter
screening procedures for student visa applications. As a result, all students applying for a
student visa will be required to provide documentation demonstrating sufficient financial
resources to support themselves during their stay in Japan.
Accordingly:
•
All student visa applicants must submit financial support documentation.
•
Applicants must demonstrate sufficient financial resources to cover their living expenses
during their stay. Details regarding the estimated monthly living expenses will be
provided in the application system.
•
Detailed instructions regarding the required documents will be provided through the TAO
Online Application System. Applicants will be required to upload the designated
supporting documents through TAO.

Kick off your exchange journey with ‌Welcome Week‌—a
vibrant line-up of activities designed to help you settle in,
make friends, and feel at home on campus from day one. Join
the Orientation Briefing, explore NTU and the city through
guided tours, and meet fellow exchange students from around
the world. This is your first step towards starting your
semester with confidence, community, and plenty of
memorable moments.
You will also be introduced to GEM Club, NTU’s student-led
exchange community. Through the GEM Buddies Programme,
you will be paired with a friendly NTU student who can help
you navigate campus life, answer questions, and ease your
transition into university life.

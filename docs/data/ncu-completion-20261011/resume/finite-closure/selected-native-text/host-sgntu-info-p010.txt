CAMPUS HOUSING
Campus housing is ‌subject to availability and is not‌
guaranteed‌. Places are allocated through a random
computer ballot. Fees are billed per semester, with
refunds for early withdrawal granted only under
extenuating circumstances, subject to the Office of
Campus Housing's (OCH) assessment and approval.
Eligibility
Exchange students taking courses may apply. Research
exchange students are not eligible because of varying
project dates.
Application & Allocation
After accepting your offer, apply through the GEM
Trailblazer Exchange/Study Abroad Portal. ‌Do not‌
apply directly to the Office of Campus Housing‌. If you
are not allocated a room or apply late, you will need to
arrange off-campus housing.
OFF-CAMPUS ACCOMMODATION
Students seeking off-campus accommodation should
begin their search well in advance of arriving in
Singapore. We recommend focusing on areas near
NTU, such as Boon Lay, Jurong West, and Jurong East.
ALTERNATIVE HOUSING OPTIONS
STUDENT'S PASS (STP)
You must hold a valid Student’s Pass (STP) from
Singapore’s Immigration & Checkpoints Authority
(ICA) for the duration of your programme. Application
guidance will be provided after you accept the offer.
Travel should only be arranged after your STP is
approved.
Fees (subject to changes):‌
Processing fee: S$45‌
Issuance fee: S$60‌
Multiple-journey fee (if applicable): S$30
Note:‌
If your NTU programme exceeds six months, you
must undergo a health screening as per the STP
requirements.‌
Exchange students holding the STP are ‌not‌
‌allowed‌ ‌to engage in work activities, whether paid
or unpaid, for the duration of their stay.‌

ENTRY VISAS
Students needing a visa to enter Singapore do not
need to apply separately for one. Once the Student’s
Pass (STP) is approved, the In-Principle Approval
(IPA) letter issued will include a single-entry visa for
entry to Singapore.
ADMINISTRATIVE FEE
As a nominated exchange student, you do not pay‌
tuition fees to NTU. However, a mandatory fee of‌
about S$410 (Undergraduate) or S$460‌
(Postgraduate) applies.‌  ‌This administrative fee‌
covers student insurance, programmes and amenities,‌
IT services, academic resources, administrative‌
support, and social activities. Miscellaneous fees for‌
the second semester will also apply for students‌
nominated for full year exchange.
Paym‌ent is billed before the semester starts and must‌
be settled by the second week of the semester.
* Fees are mandatory and non-refundable, include GST at the

ACCOMODATION
TUAF offers a range of accommodation options through its system of 6 dormitory
buildings (K1–K6). AEP students are typically housed in the well-equipped K3 and
K5 buildings, known for their better facilities, spacious rooms, and convenient
amenities.
Dormitory Fees
The monthly cost for a dormitory room ranges from $10 to $65, depending on the
type of room selected. Luxury rooms are optional and may cost more.
Important Notes
For your safety, please inform the AEP Student Affairs Office before moving out
of the dormitory.
If you have not been assigned a dorm room, contact the AEP Office for
accommodation assistance.

ACADEMIC INFORMATION
STUDY LEVEL
The GEM Trailblazer Exchange Programme is open to
both Undergraduates (UG) and Graduates (PG).
PROGRAMME DURATION
Study at NTU for one or two semesters. Research has a
minimum duration of 3 months.
MEDIUM OF INSTRUCTION
All courses, except language courses, are conducted in
English.
SEMESTER WORKLOAD
A typical course load for UG students is between three
to five courses per semester, subject to approval from
both their home university and NTU. PG students may
only read postgraduate-level courses. Each course is
generally worth three or four Academic Units (AUs).‌
Students must maintain enrolment in at least one
course throughout the programme, with a ‌maximum‌
allowable workload of 20 AUs‌ per semester.
DIRECT MASTERS STUDENTS
Only Direct (Integrated) Masters students who have
completed at least‌ ‌seven semesters of continuous‌
study‌ at their home university are eligible to apply
under the UG category, to have access to advanced UG
courses in addition to PG courses.‌
MODE OF STUDY
You can either read courses, conduct research, or opt
for a combination of both throughout the exchange.
COURSE SELECTION AND RESTRICTIONS
NTU offers a wide range of undergraduate courses and
limited selection of postgraduate courses. Exchange
students are ‌strictly not allowed‌ ‌to apply or appeal for
restricted courses. ‌(Refer to 'Course Matters' on page 5‌
for more.)

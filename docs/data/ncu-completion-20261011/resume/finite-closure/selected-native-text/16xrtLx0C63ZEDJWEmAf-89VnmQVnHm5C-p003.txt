6. Course Load Requirement for Student Visa Eligibility
To satisfy the requirements for obtaining and maintaining a student visa, students must attend
classes totaling at least 600 minutes per week throughout the semester.


7. Course Availability and Enrollment
 Course offerings and enrollment capacities vary from year to year.
 Enrollment in specific courses, including graduate-level courses, cannot be guaranteed.
 Students should carefully consult with their home institution when planning their study
abroad program.
 This is particularly important for students who have:
•
specific degree requirements,
•
credit transfer objectives, or
•
graduation timelines that depend on particular courses.

 The Spring Semester 2027 timetable is scheduled to be released in March 2027.
 Until then, students should use the 2026 timetables as a reference when preparing
preliminary study plans.

Courses with Enrollment Caps
 Some courses have limited enrollment capacity.
 Even if a course is open to exchange students, enrollment is not guaranteed.
 Students are strongly encouraged to complete degree-critical courses at their home
institution whenever possible.

Architecture-Related Courses
 Architecture-related courses are particularly competitive.
 Enrollment capacity is often very limited.
 Students may not be able to enroll in their preferred architecture courses even if those
courses are listed as available.

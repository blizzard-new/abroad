4. Research Activities for SDW Participants
Eligibility
 Only students enrolled in the SDW Program for two semesters (one academic year) are
eligible.

Requirements
Students wishing to conduct research activities must:
 Apply during the same application period as the REP (Research Exchange Program).
 Obtain prior approval from the prospective academic supervisor (laboratory faculty
member).
 Submit the same application documents required for the REP Program, including:
o Curriculum Vitae (CV)
o Research Proposal
o Any other required supporting documents
 Pay the facility usage fee specified in the REP Program application guidelines.

Important Notes
 Research activities will only be permitted after all required application procedures have
been completed and approved.
 Final approval is subject to review by both the University and the prospective academic
supervisor.


5. No Extension of the SDW Program During Enrollment

 Effective from Fall 2026, students who enroll for one semester are not permitted to extend
their participation in the Sandwich Program during their period of enrollment. Applicants
must carefully decide at the time of application whether to register for one semester or
two semesters.

Please note that students who enroll for one semester are not permitted to switch from the
Sandwich Program to the REP Program, as stated in Item 4 above.

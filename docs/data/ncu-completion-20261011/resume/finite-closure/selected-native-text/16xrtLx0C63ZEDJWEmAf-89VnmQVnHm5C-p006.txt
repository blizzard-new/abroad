
3. Extension of the Study Period
Students Entering Japan with a Short-Term Visa
 Students who participate in the program under a short-term visa are not eligible to extend
their study period under any circumstances.
 Students should carefully determine their intended period of study before submitting their
application.

Students Holding a Student Visa

Students who wish to extend their study period while holding a student visa must notify the
International Office at least two months prior to their scheduled completion date.

Applicants seeking an extension are required to submit the prescribed application documents.

If the extension results in a total study period of 182 days or longer, an additional facility
usage fee of JPY 47,000 will be charged.

Approval of an extension is subject to the University's review and procedures.

4. Selection of a Prospective Academic Supervisor
SIT does not publish a directory of faculty contact information. In addition, the application
process differs depending on the applicant's circumstances. Applicants should apply according
to one of the following categories.
■ Case 1: Applicants Who Have Obtained Prior Consent from a Faculty Member
•
This category applies to students who already have an academic connection with an SIT
faculty member and have discussed the possibility of supervision in advance.
•
Applicants may designate that faculty member as their Prospective Academic Supervisor
in their application.
■ Case 2: Applicants Without a Prospective Academic Supervisor
•
This category applies to students who do not have an existing connection with an SIT
faculty member.
•
Applicants may refer to the faculty list for applicants without a designated supervisor,
which is available on the SIT website.
•
Applicants may indicate up to three preferred faculty members in order of preference in
their application.
•
Based on the applicant's preferences and academic background, SIT will conduct the
supervisor matching process.

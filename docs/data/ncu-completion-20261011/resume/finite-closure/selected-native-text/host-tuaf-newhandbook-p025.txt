To support a safe, respectful, and law-abiding learning environment at Thai Nguyen University of
Agriculture and Forestry (TUAF), and to contribute to the prevention of crime, drug abuse, and
traffic accidents, all students are expected to commit to the following regulations:
1. VISA & IMMIGRATION REGULATIONS
Students must strictly follow Vietnamese immigration laws and the stated purpose of their visa
(education).
All students must submit visa extension requests no later than 30 days before expiration. Late
submission may result in fines or visa overstay penalties.
Students are not allowed to change visa purpose or status without notifying the AEP Office and
receiving proper approval.
The AEP Office will assist with visa-related matters, but students are responsible for tracking
their own visa validity.
2. Dormitory & Housing Rules
Students must comply with the internal regulations of the dormitory/boarding area as issued by
the university or housing management.
Overnight absences from the dormitory must be reported in advance to the dormitory
manager, AEP officer and the assigned student affairs assistant.
Any security or disciplinary incidents occurring in the dormitory area must be reported
immediately to the dorm manager or local security authority.
Students are expected to maintain cleanliness, respect quiet hours, and avoid any behavior that
disrupts the safety or well-being of others.
Visitors to the dorm must be registered and approved in accordance with local dorm policies.
Overnight guests are not permitted unless explicitly authorized.

3. MOTORBIKE USE & TRAFFIC RULES
Students who operate motorbikes in Vietnam must:
Hold a valid Vietnamese driving license or an approved international driving permit (IDP).
Wear a helmet at all times while driving or riding.
            Strictly observe the traffic laws of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
Avoid reckless driving, speeding, or driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
Students are encouraged to learn about local traffic norms and practice safe transportation
at all times.

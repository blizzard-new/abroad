CAMPUS ARRIVAL
Your first few weeks at Advanced Education Program (AEP-TUAF) are going to be busiest ones.
There are few things that you need to keep in mind:
Be aware of the time and date to show-up at (AEP) Office to officially confirm your arrival at
TUAF (within 24 working hour upon arrival).
Make sure you bring both originals and copies of all required documents listed in the
registration guidelines.
Know the location of AEP Office which is shown on the map.
 If you haven’t received any e-mail from AEP Office, please inform AEP to correct your e-
mail by sending an email to aep.admissions@tuaf.edu.vn with the corresponding
information: full name, date of birth, country of origin.

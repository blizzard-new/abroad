ABOUT
TUAF-AEP
Thai Nguyen University of Agriculture and Forestry (TUAF),
established in 1969, is one of Vietnam’s leading institutions in the
fields of agriculture and forestry. TUAF is proud to have the largest
campus in Northern Vietnam, covering 261.8 hectares across three
provinces: Thai Nguyen, Tuyen Quang, and Cao Bang.
The university is home to 8 faculties, with 57% of academic staff
holding doctoral degrees, and offers 40 training programs across
various educational levels. TUAF is recognized for its outstanding
research achievements and fosters a multicultural environment for
both learning and working, welcoming students and scholars from
around the world.
The Advanced Education Program (AEP) is a specialized program office under TUAF, established in 2010. It
has been recognized by the Vietnamese government as one of the top advanced education programs in
the country. AEP has developed strong international collaborations with prestigious institutions such as UC
Davis (USA), the University of New England (Australia), Tamkang University (Taiwan), and the University of
Greifswald (Germany). These partnerships focus on key academic fields including Environmental Science,
Agricultural Economics, Food Technology, and International Tourism Management.
At the graduate level, AEP offers international Master's programs in Environmental Science and
Biotechnology. All programs are conducted in English, providing students with a globally oriented
education.

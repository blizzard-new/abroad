5. English Language Proficiency Requirement
The required English proficiency documentation differs depending on whether the applicant has
already obtained preliminary acceptance from a prospective academic supervisor.

■ A: Applicants Who Have Obtained Preliminary Acceptance from a Prospective Academic
Supervisor

The applicant must submit either one of the following:
•
An official English-language proficiency test score listed in the application guidelines; or
•
An email from the prospective academic supervisor confirming:
o their preliminary acceptance of the applicant; and
o that the applicant possesses sufficient English proficiency to conduct research
activities.


■ B: Applicants Without a Designated Prospective Academic Supervisor
The applicant must submit:
•
An official English proficiency test score demonstrating CEFR B2 level or above (e.g.,
TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS, or another test specified in the application guidelines).


Notes
•
English proficiency certificates issued independently by universities or other educational
institutions (e.g., CEFR B2 certification letters) will not be accepted.
•
Please note that this requirement differs from the English proficiency requirements for
the SDW Program.
•
Applicants whose native language is English or who are enrolled in a degree program taught
entirely in English must submit:
Letter issued by their home institution confirming this status
This requirement applies regardless of whether the applicant falls under Category A or
Category B.

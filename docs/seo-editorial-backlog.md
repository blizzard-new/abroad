## Candidate intentions for later batches

Prioritize three distinct questions after checking existing/open PR content and current official sources. These are candidates, not prewritten or scheduled-to-publish pages. Update an existing guide if a topic is too close to it.

- Choosing between one semester and one academic year: calendar, graduation and cost tradeoffs.
- Comparing exchange versus visiting/self-funded study: agreement, fees and admissions distinctions.
- Preparing a recommendation request: evidence pack, recommender workflow, truthful sample email (do not send it).
- Evaluating an English-taught exchange course list when local-language proficiency is limited.
- Organizing an exchange interview: official format differences and clearly hypothetical practice questions.
- Planning arrival week: campus registration, housing, communication and emergency contacts without universal legal claims.
- Tracking scholarship and university obligations during exchange: documents and return deadlines.
- Managing timetable conflicts and course changes with a practical decision worksheet.
- Reading a partner-school fact sheet: version, nomination versus application, academic calendar and contact scopes.
- Comparing total commuting cost and time across campus/housing options.
- Planning exchange with a limited budget: explicit hypothetical scenarios and tradeoffs, not “guaranteed cheapest schools”.
- Returning from exchange: document handover, portfolio and next-semester planning.

Do not launch near-duplicate country articles that repeat the three cost guides. More destinations need actual country-specific official sources and relevant UniWay partner options. Avoid unsupported “best school” rankings, fabricated student stories and generic city lists with no decision value.

## AI 輔助申請與英文準備：新增候選方向（2026-10-20）

使用者新增五個構想：用 Claude／ChatGPT／Gemini 做出好看的讀書計畫或自我介紹、用 Prompt 寫出沒有「AI 味」的文章、用 AI 學英文、托福滿分目標、多益準備建議。以下依學生任務統整為四個方向、八個候選題目。這是使用者構想與編輯規劃，不是搜尋量、排名或成效證據；也不是已寫好或已排定發布日期的文章。

### 選題與製作原則

- 保留原有交換準備候選清單，將上述方向納入同一候選池；先從 AI 申請文件、自然寫作 Prompt、AI 英文學習評估首輪題目，再安排托福與多益，不硬性固定每週的類別配額。
- 每批仍選三個有獨立搜尋意圖的問題；檢查 main、現有文章與待審 PR。更新既有指南不能被當作新的近似頁，依原批次規則另選獨立題目補足。
- 每篇 AI 教學應提供實際任務、可複製且有填寫欄位的 Prompt、具體輸入／輸出或修改前後範例、操作步驟與人工檢查表。示範人物與經歷明示為假設，不捏造申請者成果。
- 「好看」同時指內容清楚與版面易讀；分開說明模型協助內容整理與最終文件排版，保留校方規定格式與真實申請資料。
- 申請文件以本人真實經歷為基礎，依目的校當期規定確認 AI 使用與揭露要求；不宣稱所有學校一律接受或禁止。
- 「沒有 AI 味」指具體、有個人聲音、少套話且可核對的寫作；不提供通過 AI 偵測的保證，不把偵測分數當成原創或品質證明。
- 工具功能、方案、價格與地區可用性在寫作時查核官方資料並標示日期；未實測不得聲稱某工具絕對最好或捏造比較結果。AI 回饋與模擬分數不可冒充官方語言能力評量。
- 托福與多益內容依發稿時可核對的官方考試版本、題型與計分規則撰寫；以高分目標與可執行方法承接搜尋需求，避免「保證滿分」或「幾天必拿高分」。

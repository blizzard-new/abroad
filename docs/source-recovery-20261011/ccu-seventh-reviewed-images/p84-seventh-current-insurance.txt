MEDICAL INSURANCE – MEDICAL ASSISTANCE  
 
Medical Insurance  
All EU/EEA country students must have the European Health Insurance  
Card in order to be able to receive medical care at all public hospitals when  
and if they need it.   
Students who are third country students and European students who do  
not have a European Health Card must have a medical insurance from  
their home countries with coverage in Cyprus. The home universities may  
be contacted for more information.

ENTRY PERMIT REGULATIONS FOR NON-EU ERASMUS STUDENTS WHO STUDY IN EU 
COUNTRIES 
{Please check the next highlighted title on this page if you are an International Erasmus student 
(Non-EU student studying in a Non-EU country)} 
Please ensure you read the following instructions carefully, as they contain important 
information 
1. Students who hold a valid permit issued by a first Member State, other than Cyprus, and who 
are covered by a Union or multilateral program that comprises mobility measures or by an 
agreement between two or more higher education institutions, shall be entitled to enter and 
stay in order to carry out part of their studies in a higher education institution in Cyprus for a 
period up to 360 days. 
2. Cyprus requires the higher education institution in the first Member State, to notify the 
competent authorities of the first Member State and of Cyprus of the intention of the student to 
carry out part of the studies in the higher education institution in Cyprus. In this case the 
notification takes place either: 
(a)  at the time of the application in the first Member State, where the mobility to Cyprus is 
already envisaged at that stage or 
(b)  after the student was admitted to the first Member State, as soon as the intended mobility to 
Cyprus is known. 
3. In case that Cyprus does not object to the mobility of the student to its territory within 30 days 
from having received the complete notification, the mobility of the student to Cyprus, to carry 
out part of the studies, shall be allowed at any moment within the period of validity of the 
permit. 
4. The notification shall include the valid travel document and the valid permit issued by the first 
Member State covering the total period of the mobility. 
5. The notification shall include the transmission of the following documents and information: 
(a) evidence that the student carries out part of the studies in Cyprus in the framework of a 
Union or multilateral program that comprises mobility measures or of an agreement between 
two or more higher education institutions and evidence that the student has been accepted by a 
higher education institution in Cyprus, 
(b) the planned duration and dates of the mobility 
(c) evidence that the student has health insurance for all the risks normally covered for Cypriot 
nationals 
(d) evidence that during the stay the student will have sufficient resources to cover subsistence 
costs without having recourse to Cyprus social assistance system, study costs, as well as the 
travel costs to the first Member State 
(e) evidence that the fees charged by the higher education institution have been paid, where 
applicable. 
All supporting documents and information shall be submitted in Greek or English language.

6.  Based on the notification referred above, Cyprus may object to the mobility of the student to 
its territory within 30 days from having received the complete notification where the conditions 
of the law are not met or the maximum duration of stay referred to in paragraph 1 has been 
reached. 
7. Students who are considered to pose a threat to public policy, public security or public health 
shall not be allowed to enter or to stay on the territory of Cyprus as a second member state. 
8. The competent authority, without delay, informs the competent authorities of the first 
Member State and the education institution in the first Member State, in writing about its 
objection to the mobility. Where Cyprus objects to the mobility, the student shall not be allowed 
to carry out part of the studies in the higher education institution in Cyprus. 
9. If your application is not rejected within 30 days you can proceed with the mobility, without 
any further steps.

All non-EU students applying to the UCY are required an Entry Permit visa to enter the Republic 
of Cyprus. Provided that all the required documents and payments have been received, the 
University will submit the student’s application to the Migration Authorities for evaluation. 
The student should submit the application form MEDUMEDUM2, (please fill in only your 
personal details in Part I – page 2. It is not necessary to sign) along with the supporting 
documents at the International Students and Staff Office of the University of Cyprus. 
Please note that document number 7 from the list of supporting documents will be prepared by 
the International Students and Staff Office: 
               Bank Guarantee either from the student or from the Higher Education Institution for repatriation expenses. 
The payment for the registration and the entry visa fees, for the amount of €140 (if you already 
have an Alien Registration Certificate in Cyprus the amount is €70) should be made via JCC 
SMART, through the website  https://www.jccsmart.com/businesses/9356296 (Application will 
not be processed without this non-refundable fee). 
Please be aware that in case your country is not a member of the Hague Convention, your 
documents will have to be attested at the Ministry of Exterior in Cyprus too. We will arrange the 
attestation and inform you regarding the exact amount which you will have to pay, after we 
check all the attested documents. 
 
IMPORTANT – ATTESTATION OF DOCUMENTS: 
Some supporting documents must be officially translated (if necessary) to English and 
attested by the relevant authorities in the applicant´s country. The translated document 
should be attested. 
Documents to be attested: 
1. Copy of valid passport (only the page with the personal data) or other travel document 
2. Evidence that during the stay the student will have sufficient resources to cover 
subsistence costs without having recourse to Cyprus social assistance system, study 
costs, as well as the travel costs or a certificate for granting a scholarship by the 
Organization granting it. Bank account statement (attested) unless if all the expenses 
are covered by the Erasmus programme, in which case we need a verification from the 
Erasmus Office. 
3. Original Certificate of a clear Criminal Record from the country of origin (if the third-
country national is residing in a country other than the country of origin, the certificate 
may be issued from the country of residence). 
4. Οriginal medical examinations for Hepatitis B and C, AIDS/HIV , Syphilis and report of 
chest X-ray for Tuberculosis from the country of origin.

Please note that the APOSTILLE stamp does not need to be translated. 
• The attestation process differs between countries that have signed the Hague 
Convention of 1961 (Apostille) and those that have not. The Attested documents must 
be in English. 
• Students applying from countries that have signed the Hague Convention of 1961 
(Apostille) must have the APOSTILLE stamp. If any document doesn’t have the 
APOSTILLE stamp the application will be rejected. Double certification from the 
Ministry of Exterior and the Embassy/Consulate of Cyprus in the country will NOT 
be accepted for documents issued in the member states.  
Students must have their documents attested by the designated, competent government 
authority which is authorized to affix the Apostille on their documents. The Ministry of 
Justice or Ministry of Foreign Affairs is usually the government body that issues the Apostille. 
• Students applying from countries that have not signed the Hague Convention of 1961 
(Apostille) but have a Cyprus Representation in their home country must have all their 
documents attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in their home country and then 
further attested by the Cyprus Embassy, High Commission or Consulate. 
• Students applying from Russia and Serbia: Based on bilateral agreements between 
Cyprus and Russia and between Cyprus and Serbia, students from these countries are 
exempt from the above procedures, provided all their documents are officially signed 
and stamped by the issuing authority. 
{Also detailed information about the attestation can be found on the Deputy Ministry of 
Migration and International Protection}.

TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMIT REQULATIONS FOR NON-EU ERASMUS STUDENTS 
COMING FROM NON-EU UNIVERSITIES 
Within 7-10 days from the date of entry into the Republic of Cyprus, the non-EU student 
should submit the “after arrival” documents at the International Students and Staff Office.  
IMPORTANT 
Once the requested documents are ready, you should visit the International Students and 
Staff Office (ISSO) to stamp the documents, and arrange for you an appointment with the 
Migration Department to provide them with your biometrics. 
You can visit the Migration Department only after an appointment, arranged between the 
University of Cyprus and the Migration Department.  
Required documents for the Cyprus Residence Permit card: 
1.  Copy of the passport’s or other travel document’s page with a stamp that shows the last 
arrival in Cyprus. 
2.  Original Entry Permit (Blue slip). 
3.  Original medical examinations for Hepatitis B and C, HIV and Syphilis as well as Chest 
X-Ray (testing for Tuberculosis). Upon arrival in Cyprus, each student must undergo 
these medical     examinations. 
4.   Certificate of Health Insurance for medical care that covers inpatient and outpatient 
care and transportation of corpse (Plan A). Valid for one year (unless the duration of your 
stay in Cyprus is less) 
5.   Confirmation from a bank in the Republic of Cyprus that the student has deposited a 
satisfactory amount to cover living expenses and tuition fees for the period that the 
residence permit is applied for (minimum balance €800). 
6.   Copy of the Rental Agreement/contract certified by the President of a Community 
Council (your landlord will be able to tell you where you can find the Community Council 
Area President) or title deed. Valid for one year (unless the duration of your stay in

Cyprus is less). 
  Please note that the Rental Agreement must be also signed by two Cypriot 
witnesses (ID and Telephone number required). This is needed by the Cyprus Banks, 
for the purpose of opening a        bank account in Cyprus. 
7.   €55 fee for the Application Form (paid at the Migration Department). 
Important Notes: 
• The ISSO will inform you when your Temporary Residence Permit card is ready (the 
procedure takes approximately 4 months). You can find out whether your application 
has been examined after 3-4 months by following the instructions at the SMS 
Notifications’ website.  
• Your Temporary Residence Permit will be automatically cancelled if you stay abroad for 
a period of more than three months. The ISSO must be informed in order to apply for 
your re-entry permit. 
• Please do not make any money transactions in the northern occupied part of Cyprus. 
This is considered illegal practice and the Migration department will reject your 
application. You should also avoid using the airport in the northern part of Cyprus as it is 
an illegal point of entry. 
• Please provide the ISSO with your contact details in Cyprus (telephone, address and 
email) and also with the receipt you will get from the Migration office after the 
appointment.  
RENEWAL OF VISA/TEMPORARY RESIDENCE PERMIT REQULATIONS FOR NON-EU 
STUDENTS 
Your Temporary Residence Permit lasts for one year, so it is very important to renew your 
temporary residence permit every year until the end of your studies. 
Please see below the required documents in order to get your Renewal Temporary 
Residence Permit: 
1. Application form MEDU2/MEDUM2, duly completed and signed (by the student and the 
ISSO). 
2. Copy of your passport, valid for at least 2 years or with a validity that covers at least the 
duration of the intended stay. 
3. Copy of valid residence permit. 
4. Original Transcripts for all Semesters (until the time of submission of the application). 
5. Confirmation statement from a bank in the Republic of Cyprus, confirming that the 
student has deposited a satisfactory amount of currency to cover living expenses and 
tuition fees for the period that the residence permit is applied for (minimum balance 
€800).

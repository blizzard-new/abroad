Saint Petersburg Electrotechnical University «LETI» is one of Russia’s leading higher education institutions. The research fields of the university are radio engineering, electronics and computer science. With over 135 years of successful experience in tertiary education, LETI is the oldest electrotechnical university in the world.

The top-class faculty and staff
. ETU is proud of its' science and research team than joins 5 corresponding members of the Russian Academy of Sciences, 20 prizewinners of national and international awards and more than 200 PhDs.
Wide range of majors and courses
. University offers educational programs in radio engineering, telecommunications, electrical engineering, electronics, information technology, solid state physics and material science along with ones in humanities, economics and management.
Location
. The university is located in very center of St. Petersburg, the second largest city and cultural capital of Russia, famous for its'architecture masterpieces, museums, theaters, and events.

ERASMUS+ PROGRAMME 
LANGUAGE OF TEACHING AT THE UNIVERSITY OF CYPRUS 
Language of teaching 
The official language of teaching at the University of Cyprus is Greek.  
It is advisable that students have at least basic knowledge in Greek.  
Students STRONLGY advised to attend the Greek intensive courses offered at the School of 
Modern Greek of the University.  
The requires knowledge of English is B2.  
All students must send along with their application documents, official examination certificates 
as  
• A TOEFL certificate 
• An IELTS certificate 
• A TOEIC certificate 
• A Cambridge certificate 
• IGCSE 
• ESOL 
• Oxford Test of English 
• DAAD 
• Any other internationally recognized English language certificates corresponding to CEFR 
Level B2 or higher. 
Certificates issued by the home universities and the OLS certificates will not be accepted.  
In the case the student is a native a native speaker of English we will need a copy of the identity 
card/passport. 
 
If a student has successfully finished a study programme of at least one year which was 
completely taught in English, we can accept a certificate from the Director of the Study 
programme indicating this information. 
 
 A GPA of 6.5/10 is required only for students nominated at the Department of Computer 
Science. 
 
 
The School of Economics is the only School of the University currently offering a large number of 
courses in English. Most of the other departments of the University offer few courses in English

but not a full programme. Students may combine courses from departments of the same School 
and may also choose 1 -2 courses from other Schools provided the majority of the courses is 
selected from the department with which the Erasmus + agreement exists.  
In departments where there are no courses in English Erasmus students have the possibility to 
do assignments or even tutorials in English, provided they agree this with the academics in 
their departments.  
There is bibliography in English and hence students should ask the academics teaching the 
subject they are interested in to provide titles in English if available. 
Communication language  
Almost all people in the University and in the country in general speak English and nobody 
should have any communication problems.

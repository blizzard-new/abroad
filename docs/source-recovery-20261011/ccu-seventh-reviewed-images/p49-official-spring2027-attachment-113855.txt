Qualification of Students
Students who meet the following requirements are eligible to apply 
(including male students):
1. Students who have been nominated by their home university
2. Semester Requirement: Students who have completed at least two 
semesters (at least one semester for graduate students)
3. Academic Requirement: Students who meet or exceed the academic 
standards required by their home university for nomination
4. Language Requirement: Korean or English proficiency (submission of an 
official language test score is not mandatory)
※ Available courses may vary depending on the student’s language 
proficiency.
※ Recommended Language Proficiency: TOPIK Level 3 or above, or TOEFL 
iBT 3.5 / TOEFL iBT: 59 or above for tests taken by January 20, 2026.
Major Selection
Students will be registered in the same or a related major as their home 
university.
When applying through the portal, select a department that is the same 
as or most similar to your current major at your home university. If it 
does not align with your field of study, the university will change it to a 
more appropriate department during the administrative process.

Health Insurance 
Private Health Insurance (Mandatory)
Ÿ As required by the Korean Ministry of Education, you are required to be 
covered by Korean private health insurance.
Ÿ For a one-time payment of around KRW 80,000, coverage is provided for 
six months (one semester).
Ÿ The purpose of this private insurance is to cover medical gaps that may 
arise from the time you enter the country until your National Health 
Insurance coverage begins, which can take several months.
Ÿ The Certificate of Admission will be issued only after the payment has 
been confirmed.  Further details will be provided to students who have 
completed the application.
 National Health Insurance (Mandatory)
Ÿ National Health Insurance is mandated by the law.
Ÿ National Health Insurance is applied upon the issuance of the Residence Card.
Ÿ Details about registration and fees are TBA.
Ÿ Fees must be paid on a monthly basis. (₩80,000‒100,000 per month)
Ÿ Students with Japanese or French nationality are eligible to apply for 
the exemption. (Students must apply for the exemption individually)
Student Visa
(D-2)
All incoming international students at Sungshin must obtain a D-2 student visa.
Ÿ Required documents provided by Sungshin Women's University:
  1. Certificate of Admission
  2. Business Registration Certificate of Sungshin Women’s University
Ÿ Documents Sending Date:
 - Spring semester: End of Dec ember
 - Fall semester: End of June
u Note
 - After receiving the Certificate of Admission and the Business Registration 
Certificate from us via email, you can apply for a D-2 student visa at the 
Korean Embassy in your home country. Please contact the relevant 
diplomatic mission for specific procedures, required forms, and 
processing time, as they may vary by country.
 - The D-2 student visa is valid for a single entry to Korea. Without an 
ARC, you cannot re-enter Korea on the same student visa. Be sure to 
obtain your ARC before departure during your exchange period.
 - Please note that your D-2 visa will be used upon your first entry into 
Korea and will be invalidated when you depart from Korea in the 
absence of a valid Registration Card. Should you wish to go ahead with 
the above planned itinerary, you will need to apply for D-2 visa again 
for your exchange program.

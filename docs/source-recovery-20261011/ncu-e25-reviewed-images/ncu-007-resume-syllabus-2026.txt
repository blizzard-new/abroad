    Urban planning knowledge and practice                                                        158h    13



    Urban planning theory and practice
    Main lecturer : Pedro Gomes

    The module’s objectives are to provide insight on the main urban planning instruments
    and objectives in France and in the Paris region in particular ; to link existing planning
    documents, policies and practices to theoretical debates ; to develop comparative             18h     4
    thought between France and other national contexts.
    The module is lecture-based and built around a weekly reading assignment. Students

    semester.

    Socio-ecological Transitions in Urban Planning                                                 42h     6


    Urban planning and transitions in Paris - workshop
    Coordinating lecturer: John Bingham-Hall

    During a whole week, students actively and critically engage with transition initiatives in   18h      2
    the Paris region. The week’s’ format varies, including workhsops built around site visits
    with urban professionals, to fieldwork- or project-based ones. The workshop may be
    jointly led with students and faculty from other institutions in France and abroad. In
    2026, students will critically engage with the city’s bioclimatic urbanism), in partnership
    with the Paris School of Archtecture.


    Urban natures
    Main lecturer: Lou Gomez
    Effective urban transitions must rethink the place of nature in urban areas. This course      12h      2
    introduces students to the issue, providing a theoretical background and practical
    examples in the Paris region. Course topics include i) the major conceptual approaches
    to nature and natural processes in cities, including ecosystem services and urban
    metabolism; ii) planning and design approaches to green, blue and brown infrastructure
    for ecosystem preservation and risk management; iii) environmental justice, i.e.
    unequal access and vulnerability to urban natures.


    Urban mobilities
    Main lecturers: Abel Girard and Marie Hue

    Decarbonizing urban mobilities is a key factor in fighting climate change, promoting
    urban health and struggling for spatial justice. This course’s main objective is to
    introduce students to the relationships between urban space, urban and transportation          12h     2
    planning and mobilities. Course topics are i) main concepts in transportation and
    mobility planning; ii) the mobility-urban planning nexus at different scales; iii) low
3   carbon mobilities, cyclable and walkable cities; iv) urban mobilities and inequality.
    Examples from the Paris region will be used, through visits and/or case studies.

    Representing the City                                                                        36h     6



    Models for urban ecodesign
    Main lecturer: Florence Jacquinod

    In this course, students learn to collect and use 2D and 3D geodata to analyse some
    aspects of an urban site and to represent them in a GIS tool. Each session is divided
    into two parts: a short introduction with conceptual notions and then practical exercises    18h     3
    using GIS tools and real world data. In this second part, quizzes are used to outline key
    aspects in assessing the relevance of geodata and GIS tools for specific urban site
    analyses. The course assignment contributes to data analysis within the Urban
    Planning Studio.




    International video workshop

    The workshop unites planning, engineering, architecture and arts students from
    institutions in Chile, France, Italy and in the USA. During one week, students shoot,

    Languages and culture                                                                       36h     5



    French foreign language
    Main lecturer: Andréia Dias Rodrigues

    By the end of the semester, students will have achieved level A1 of the Common              18h     2
    European Framework of Reference for Languages. This includes knowledge of the
    main grammatical structures of the language and the vocabulary needed for basic
    everyday interactions. Assessment includes weekly quizzes, written exams and an oral
    presentation about the students’ everyday life in Paris.




    Cultural diversity and intercultural management
    Coordinating lecturer: Derek Mainwaring
                                                                                                18h     3
    This course aims to boost students’ cross-cultural competency and ability to work in a
    multi-cultural context. Throughout the semester, students learn about cultural concepts,
    teambuilding and management, as well as teamwork and culture. Students are
    assessed through an empirical analysis of an intercultural work situation.

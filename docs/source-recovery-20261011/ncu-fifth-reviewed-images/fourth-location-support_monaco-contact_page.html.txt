Getting Here
Motorway and Road Network
By motorway A8 exit Monaco. From Nice airport, the Principality may be reached in less than 30 minutes.
By Road
Between Nice and Monaco, one may take three roads:
The “Basse Corniche” (Low Coast-Road – Highway 98), along the seaside
The “Moyenne Corniche” (Middle Coast-Road – Highway N7), going through Eze-Village
The “Grande Corniche” (Great Coast-Road), going through La Turbie and Col d’Eze
Railways
All trains stop at Monaco/Monte-Carlo Station which is located 2mn away from the International University of Monaco.
Bus
Bus 110 stops at MONACO “Place d’Armes” which is located 200m away from IUM.
Airlines
The nearest airport is located in Nice, France. Bus, helicopter (Heli Air Monaco or Monacair) and taxis are available to help you get to Monaco. For more information, please

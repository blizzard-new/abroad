10. Notes
1. The classes conducted in the 2027AY have yet to be fixed at the time of the application since they will be finalized in late
 March 2027. The available classes for exchange students will be informed during the orientation week after their arrival. If
 applicants need to earn many credits during the mobility, please consult the International Student Office before the
 application period starts.


2. If an applicant requires special assistance when studying at Meiji University, please make sure to inquire with the International
 Student Office before your application. Meiji University will consider whether assistance is available. We may request
 additional documents if ever needed. Any inquiry/request about special assistance after your application, or in cases when we
 could not receive the necessary documents on time, we will not be able to give special assistance.


3. Reapplication for the next semester during the stay at Meiji is not permitted. After completing the exchange period selected
  at the time of application, students are required to return to their home country promptly.

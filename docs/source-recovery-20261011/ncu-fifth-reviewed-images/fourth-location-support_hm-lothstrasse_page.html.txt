Lothstraße Campus
Lothstraße Campus
With 12,502 students, the campus Lothstraße located north-west of the main railway station, is the largest site of Hochschule München. All central administrative departments, nine departments, the Library, as well as the CAREER Centre, the E-Learning Center and the Centre for Continuing Education are based here.

How to get there by public transport
As there are very few parking spaces available at Hochschule München, we recommend travelling by public transport. From Munich Central Station, you can reach the university’s campuses using the following services:
Lothstraße Campus (Departments 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 09, 12, 13)
Take tram lines 20 (Moosach station), 21 (Westfriedhof) or 29 (Hochschule München) to the “Hochschule München (Lothstraße)” stop.
Lothstraße Campus (Department 14)
Take tram lines 20 (Moosach Bf.), 21 (Westfriedhof) or 29 (Hochschule München) to the “Hochschule München (Lothstraße)” stop. From there, walk north along Dachauer Straße, turn left into Lazarettstraße, right into Heldstraße, left into Pfänderstraße and then left into Schachenmeierstraße until you reach the university.
Alternatively, take the U1 (Olympia-Einkaufszentrum) to the “Maillingerstraße” stop. From there, walk along Nymphenburgerstraße, turn right into Lazarettstraße, then left into Ernst-Henle-Straße, following the allotments, and then left again into Schachenmeierstraße until you reach the university.
Timetable information from the Munich Transport Association (MVV):
www.mvv-muenchen.de

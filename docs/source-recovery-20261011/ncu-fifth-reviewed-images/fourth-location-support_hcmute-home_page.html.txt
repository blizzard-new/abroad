HCM-UTE Signed MOU with the VNU-HCM Dormitory and Urban Area Management Center to Strengthen Student Support
On the morning of August 13, 2026, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Engineering (HCM-UTE, formerly Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education) held a signing ceremony for a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) and an exchange session on experience in space leasing and utilization with the Dormitory and Urban Area Management Center of Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (VNU-HCM).
HCM-UTE – Mahasarakham University (Thailand) Academic Exchange Program Concludes with Research for Sustainable Development
As part of the international cooperation program between Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Engineering (HCM-UTE, formerly Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education) and Mahasarakham University (MSU), Thailand, the Faculty of Foreign Languages at HCM-UTE, in collaboration with several academic faculties, organized an academic exchange program for 30 students from Mahasarakham University from July 6–9, 2026.
HCMUTE Alumni Sports Festival 2025: A Gathering of Sportsmanship and Connection with HCMUTE

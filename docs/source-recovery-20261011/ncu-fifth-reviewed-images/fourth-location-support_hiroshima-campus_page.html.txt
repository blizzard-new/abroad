)
Higashi-Hiroshima campus comprises of the following:
Administration Bureau
Schools
Integrated Arts and Sciences, Letters, Education, Economics (Daytime course), Science, Engineering, Applied Biological Science, Informatics and Data Science
Graduate Schools
Integrated Arts and Sciences, Letters, Education, Social Sciences (Department of Law and Politics, Department of Economics), Science, Advanced Sciences of Matter, Engineering, International Development and Cooperation, Integrated Sciences for Life

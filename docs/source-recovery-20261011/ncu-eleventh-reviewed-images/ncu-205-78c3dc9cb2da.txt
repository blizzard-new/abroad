The Universitas Airlangga research community advances knowledge across diverse fields, from tropical diseases and stem cell science to the green economy and patient safety. Guided by its vision of “Excellence with Morality,” UNAIR transforms knowledge into practical solutions that address society’s most pressing challenges. Through excellent, innovative, and impactful research, UNAIR contributes to empowering communities and advancing global well-being.

Impact-Driven
UNAIR
Through the Ksatria Airlangga Hospital Ship (RSKKA), Universitas Airlangga extends essential healthcare services to communities in island regions and other areas with limited access to medical care. Supported by UNAIR healthcare professionals and the university community, the 2026 RSKKA Community Service Mission continues to deliver medical services, health education, and community outreach, reflecting UNAIR’s commitment to improving the health and well-being of underserved island communities.

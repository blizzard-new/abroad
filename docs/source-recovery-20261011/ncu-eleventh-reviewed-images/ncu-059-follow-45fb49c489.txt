Instruction document on travel safety for incoming staff  and students to ZHAW 
Zurich University of Applied Sciences  
 
1. Travel preparation 
− Provide your secretary or a member of your family with a photocopy of your travel 
plan, passport and credit card numbers and emergency contact details.  
− Carry copies of the following documents with you: Passport and visa, tickets, credit 
card numbers and emergency numbers, contact details of your ZHAW host or the In-
ternational Office, as well as the local diplomatic mission.  
 
2. Security on site 
Police and ambulance telephone numbers should be saved in your mobile phones 
(find them below).  
 
3. Emergency 
− In the event of a robbery, hand over your belongings to the perpetrator without de-
fending yourself (self -protection comes first) and report the incident to the local po-
lice immediately.  
− After an accident, contact the police and your insurance company.  
 
4. Kidnapping/hostage taking 
− Follow the instructions exactly, avoid direct eye contact and do not do anything with-
out first asking the hostage -takers/kidnappers for permission.  
− Be alert and be prepared for rescue operations or an escalation of violence.  
− If you are questioned, your answers must m atch the information contained in your 
papers. Be polite but firm.  
− In the event of a rescue operation, stay as close to the ground as possible.  
− Follow the instructions of the perpetrators and do not try to resist.  
 
5. Contact points 
− Contact ZHAW host or th e International Office.  
− Contact your diplomatic mission.

7. Health Insurance 
− EU citizens don't need to obtain Swiss health insurance if they have public health in-
surance in their home country.  
− Non-EU citizens must  obtain Swiss health insurance coverage.  
We recommend Swisscare ( www.swisscare.com ) or Scorestudies 
(www.scorestudies.com).

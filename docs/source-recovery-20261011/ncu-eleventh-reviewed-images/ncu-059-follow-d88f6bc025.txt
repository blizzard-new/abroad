Study in the heart 
of Europe
The ZHAW School of Engineering is
located in the city centre of Winterthur. 
The main campus building was built  
150 years ago, when the ZHAW School  
of Engineering was founded. Winterthur 
offers a high standard of living, with its 
historic city, green parks, and plenty to 
do. There are ZHAW events, such as the 
Tailcoat Parade and the Night of Techno-
logy, and very renowned festivals, such 
as the Winterthur Music Festival.
Winterthur is just a 20-minute train ride 
from Zurich and a 15-minute ride to the 
airport. Zurich, where the ZHAW School of 
Engineering also has a campus, is Switzer-
land’s largest city and economic centre.
Switzerland is a perfect starting point to 
explore Europe, by night train or by plane. 
Distances are short and famous cities are 
easily reached within a few hours. But 
there is also a lot to discover within Switzer-
land: its diverse regions and four national 
languages, its mountains for hiking or 
skiing, its lakes and rivers for a swim or a 
boat tour, and many picturesque cities.
ZHAW School of Engineering
Founded in 1874 as the Technikum Winter-
thur, the ZHAW School of Engineering  
has a longstanding tradition of educating 
engineers. Today, it still educates almost 
a quarter of all engineers in Switzerland. 
The ZHAW School of Engineering has 
very close links to companies in Switzer-
land and abroad.

Accommodation
In Winterthur and Zurich, various organisations offer 
reasonably priced accommodation for ZHAW stu- 
dents. You can find further information on our website.
Financial Support 
Incoming students from European partner univer-
sities receive a financial contribution ranging from 
CHF 1900 to CHF 2200 per semester. In addition, 
they can apply for a ZHAW School of Engineering 
scholarship of up to CHF 1000 per semester. 
Overseas students can apply for a ZHAW scholar-
ship of up to CHF 3000 per semester.
Health insurance
Basic health insurance is required by law in Switzer-
land. Students from the European Union are exempt 
from this obligation if they hold a valid European 
Health Insurance Card (EHIC). The International 
Office assists all non-European students in obtain-
ing affordable Swiss health insurance cover.

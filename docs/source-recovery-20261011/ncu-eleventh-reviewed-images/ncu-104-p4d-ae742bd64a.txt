REQUIRED INF ORMATION:
Nominations require partners to include the following for each student individually:
• Partner institution name
• Partner institution contact
• Student biographical information (full name, date of birth, gender)
• Student contact information (email address)
• Student degree level/academic level
• Student field of study
• Verification of English Proficiency
Please note, information will not be saved until you submit the nomination. Please have the above information 
available while completing the nomination.

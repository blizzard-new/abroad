The Visitors’ Service
offers guided tours in and around the University Park in Aarhus, lectures and a general introduction to the university’s courses, history, organisation and architecture.

Incorrect data in the map
We continuously work to ensure that the information on the map is accurate. If you notice missing or incorrect information, please contact the relevant function depending on where the issue occurs.
Errors in Apple Maps data
If you notice errors or missing information in the map itself, such as paths, tunnels, roads or other geographical features, this data originates from Apple Maps and cannot be corrected by Aarhus University.
These issues must therefore be reported directly to Apple:
iPhone:
Report an issue in Apple Maps
Mac:
Report an issue in Apple Maps
Errors in the underlying building data
Data on building locations, addresses, building numbers and special locations are retrieved from Dalux and maintained by AU Buildings.
Contact:
Building Documentation, AU Buildings –
[email protected]
Errors in the map display on the website or in the AU Find app
Errors related to the technical display of the building map on au.dk or in the AU Find app – including the integration with Dalux and the rendering in Apple Maps – are handled by the TYPO3 team.
Contact:
TYPO3 Team –
[email protected]
Errors in static maps (PDF maps)
Corrections and questions regarding the static maps are handled by the Web Editorial Team in the Senior Management Team.
Contact:
Web Editorial Team –
[email protected]
AU for visitors
If you are interested in visiting Aarhus University, the Visitors’ Service offers free guided tours for groups of the Aarhus campus. You can also visit several university museums and grab a bite to eat in at one of the canteens.

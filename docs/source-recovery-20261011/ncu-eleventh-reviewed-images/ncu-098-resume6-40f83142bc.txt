Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility KA171 (2024 Project) 
Selection Procedures for Incoming Students 
Education Mobility 
-This Program provides 2 months (minimum) and 5 months -long (maximum) student 
mobility for studies (SMS) exchange financed by the SDU KA171 budget. 
Traineeship Mobility 
-Internship mobility may include related research activities of doctoral candidates. 
-This Program provides 2 months (minimum) and 5 months -long (maximum) student 
mobility for traineeship (SMP) exchange financed by the SDU KA171 budget. 
Short-Term Doctoral Mobility 
- It is a type of activity in which doctoral candidates can carry out short -term study or 
internship mobility abroad.  
-This Program provides 5 days (minimum) and 30 days  (maximum) student mobility for 
studies/ internship exchange financed by the SDU KA171 budget. 
Scholarship 
-Incoming students (Education/ Traineeship) are paid 800 Euro per month. 
-Incoming students (Short-Term Doctoral Mobility) are paid 5 -14 days 79 Euro per day; 
15-30 days 56 Euro per day. 
- Selected students (for education and short-time doctoral education) will participate to the 
program in Fall (traineeship/ short-term doctoral mobility), Spring (Education/traineeship/ 
short-term doctoral mobility), Summer (Traineeship/ short -term doctoral mobility)  
Semester of 2026-2027 academic year. 
- Selected students (for traineeship and short -time doctoral traineeship) will participate 
from December 2026 till 31 July 2027. 
- All the short-listed candidate incoming students  are paid one-time travel grant depending 
on the distance between Süleyman Demirel University (City: Isparta) and the partner 
institution’s location ( https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/resources-and-tools/distance-
calculator). 
-Selected students entitled to the program can waive their grant if they prefer.

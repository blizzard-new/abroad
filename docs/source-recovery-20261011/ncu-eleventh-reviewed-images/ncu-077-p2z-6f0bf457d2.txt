ACADEMIC CALENDAR 2026/2027 
 
PLEASE NOTE: The information reported below may be subject to change. 
For up-to-date information, please refer to the academic calendar available on the University website : Academic Calendar. 
 
The academic year is divided into two semesters. For departments in the economic, management, and humanities fields, each semester is further divided into two terms. 
Students enrolled in semester-long courses can take exams on any available exam date during the exam sessions scheduled at the end of the  semester. Students enrolled in 
term-long courses can take their exams on any available exam date during the exam sessions at the end of each term, as well as at the end of the semester.

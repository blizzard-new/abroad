Le Havre campus
Le Havre campus
Want to study on the waterfront? Only 2 hours from Paris, Le Havre is an international metropolis with one of the largest ports in Europe, but also a charming seaside resort in the city. Between land and sea, the city offers an ideal living environment to study and enjoy the coastline.
Your Business School in Le Havre
The Le Havre campus is the place par excellence for specialisations linked to logistics, maritime and port activities and is also the totem of EM Normandie's digital transformation. A 12,100 m² open and futuristic campus inaugurated in 2020 offering numerous training and living spaces, "full digital" and modular classrooms to encourage interaction and creativity.

Discover Le Havre city
A powerful port city
Le Havre has established itself as an international maritime metropolis: as
the leading French port
and the
5th European port
for container traffic, the
HAROPA PORT
is also the leading national port for foreign trade.
Between the Nordic waters and the Atlantic Ocean, its port aims to become the
gateway to Europe
within a few years. The Port of Le Havre has a high-performance logistics system and solid,
internationally recognised expertise
in maritime and port logistics.
An economic and cultural dynamism
The
largest city
in Normandy with over 200,000 inhabitants, Le Havre enjoys a privileged geographical location, with its
2 km long beach in the city centre
.
The city has managed to diversify by launching
centres of excellence
and
major economic projects
: petrochemicals, aeronautics, aeronautics, mechanics, etc. It has established itself as a competitive and attractive city.
The city has been a
UNESCO World Heritage Site
since 2005. It is also a city of art and history, with many
cultural and sporting activities
.
In the Top Student Cities
The city benefits from a
dynamic student environment
and is regularly ranked as one of the
best cities to live and study in
.
In the L'Etudiant 2022-2023 ranking, more than
82%
of students recommend this city, notably for its
proximity to the sea
, the possibility of
walking to the city centre
and its rather
affordable cost
of living.
Indeed, living expenses average 800€ per month including accommodation, meals, transport and housing insurance.

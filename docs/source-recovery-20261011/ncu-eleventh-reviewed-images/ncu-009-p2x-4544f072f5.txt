Certification labels
Triple crown
The EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA international accreditations are 
indisputable guarantees of excellence on the world stage. They 
recognise EM Normandie's excellence in terms of all the essential 
requirements: the high academic level of its teaching staff, its strong 
research dynamic, the relevance of its programmes in a constantly 
changing world, its strong internationalisation, its close links with 
companies, the strength of its student and alumni community and 
the added value of its personalised support system. Today, less than 
1% of Business Schools in the world hold the triple crown of 
EQUIS, AACSB and AMBA.
EM Normandie’s flagship Bachelor's and 
Master's degrees are quality-assessed by 
the CEFDG, the French Commission for 
the Evaluation of Management Training 
and Qualifications, which issues State 
recognition.
EM Normandie forms part of the 
Conférence des Grandes É coles. This 
select group consists of 38 French 
Business Schools that award the state-
recognised Master's degree out of some 
400 business and management schools 
in France.
The BSIS label, issued by the 
FNEGE (Fondation Nationale pour 
l'Enseignement de la Gestion des 
Entreprises) and the EFMD (European 
Foundation for Management 
Development), assesses the extent 
and nature of a business school's 
impact on its environment. This 
label covers seven dimensions and 
highlights the sustainable and 
responsible impact of a school on its 
local area.
EM Normandie also holds the 
Bienvenue en France label, with a 
maximum three-star rating, which 
guarantees the quality of the School’s 
hospitality towards international 
students.

programme in your acceptance letter.  
OUR ACADEMIC
YEAR
The European Credit 
Transfert System ECTS
  
The validation of one semester is equivalent to 30 ECTS credits, a year to 
60 ECTS
GRADE SCORE INTERPRETATION
A >18 Outstanding - Pass
B 16<18 Excellent - Pass
C 14<16 Good - Pass
D 12<14 Satisfactory - Pass
E 10<12 Average - Pass
FX 7<10 Insufficient - Fail
F <7 Poor - Fail
Grades in France are on a 20-point scale: 0 being the lowest grade and 
20 the highest. The passing grade is 10/20.
Grading system 
& Exams
Types of Exams
  
There are 3 types :
Continuous assessment: 
allows for the validation of acquired knowledge throughout 
the year with regular evaluations in each subject.
Final exams: (or partial exams) take place at the end of 
each semester.
 
Remedial exams:  
if you obtain a grade below 10/20 or are absent from a 
partial exam, you have a second chance to validate the 
concerned subject. Remediation is both a right and an 
obligation (except for students who have signed a ”Learning 
Agreement”).
To validate an academic semester: 
 - You must achieve a general average equal to or  
  greater than 10/20. 
 -  Each subject must be validated with a score of  
  10/20.  Generally, the calculation per subject is  
  done as follows: 40% continuous assessment /  
	 	 60%	final	exam.
Conversely, you will not validate your academic semester if:
 - Your overall average is less than 10/20.
 - You are absent from a remedial exam (no   
  second chance for remediation is possible).
At the end of each semester, a committee either approves 
the success of the semester or lists the subjects that need 
remediation. Students are then informed accordingly. A 
school board meets after the remedial exams to confirm 
semester validations or not.

The rule is simple: classes are mandatory.
 
A percentage of absences per subject is tolerated under certain 
conditions (refer to the academic regulations of your program).
Please note:
 • Be careful not to miss a continuous    
 assessment; it could result in a score of 0/20    
in case of absence.
 • Excessive absenteeism can significantly impact your 
academic success. 
  This may lead to:
  -  A prohibition from taking final exams (you will directly 
   move to makeup exams, and the result will replace all  
   grades obtained in the subject). 
  -  Referral to a Disciplinary Board.
 
For students in apprenticeship programs: you must attend all 
classes as they are paid for by your employer (refer to the Learning 
Booklet).
Attendance
No tolerance for acts of academic fraud, including plagiarism or 
cheating, will be accepted. EM Normandie considers such acts 
contrary to fairness and ethics, of which it is the guardian.
To combat fraud and promote academic integrity, the School is 
committed to three aspects:
 - Information and prevention;
 - Control and detection;
 - Heavy sanctions, after referral to the Disciplinary Board,  
  including possible expulsion.

The diploma (Bachelor, Master’s degree from the Grande 
Ecole Program, Master of Sciences/Specialized Master) 
is awarded to students who have validated:
 - All required ECTS credits,
 - Mandatory internships,
 - The thesis or final study project with a score of  
  10/20.
Attention! For the Grande Ecole Program Master’s 
degree, an additional requirement is:
 - An English test chosen by the school: for students  
  graduating in 2025-2026 and beyond, the   
  required official TOEIC score (or equivalent) is 785.
 - Regardless of the graduation conditions specified  
  in the academic regulations, you must, to obtain   
  your diploma, be in compliance administratively,   
  including settling all tuition fees.
Diploma juries are scheduled in December and July 
each year. Students who do not meet the diploma 
requirements are granted a one-year period, 
renewable once, for a maximum of 2 years after the 
end of classes to fulfill these conditions.
The diploma ceremony generally takes place in March 
of the year following the diploma attainment.
Obtaining a diploma

During the initial two weeks of the academic program, 
the add and drop process is exclusively available for 
exchange, double degree, and free mover students.
Within this timeframe, students have the flexibility to 
make changes to their courses. They can add or drop 
courses by submitting modifications on the second page 
of their learning agreement.
Process: 
Add and drop period
Step 1
Review the course offer to 
identify the course you would 
like to add
Step 2
Modify the second page of 
your Learning Agreement (LA) 
and submit it to your university 
for signature.
Step 4
Await comments/validation 
from your international affairs 
coordinator
Step 3
Implement the changes on the 
platform we provided and upload 
the new LA.
Some importante informaiton
 about add and drop period : 
- It is not possible to mix courses from different programmes. 
- You may take less than 30 credits (ECTS) if you wish to, the minimum to validate the semester for exchange students is 15 credits.
- We 	sugest	you	to	confirm	the	number	of	credits	that	you	are	taking	with	your	home	university	(except	for	free	mover	students).	
-	 In	the	course	offer	you	will	be	able	to	find	your	programme	and	the	courses	that	it	is	offering,	note	that	some programmes 
have electives, and read carefully the availability of the number of electives. 
- After the two weeks any change is available

Campuses
LAB University of Applied Sciences has campuses in Lahti and Lappeenranta. The common areas on our campuses, including the academic library, restaurants and exhibition spaces, are open to everyone on weekdays from 8 AM to 4 PM.

Campus park
Located along the Lahti campus promenade, the campus park is an open meeting and recreational area for everyone. It can also be used for small events or outdoor lectures.
LUT Academic Library
You can find LUT Academic Library on both campuses and online. The academic library has a vast collection of both print and electronic materials to help with your studies.  LUT Academic Library is the joint library of LUT University and LAB University of Applied Sciences.

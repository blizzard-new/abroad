Lahti campus
The Lahti campus consists of two buildings – Mukkulankatu 19 (M19) and Niemenkatu 73 (N73 or Nie73).
Mukkulankatu 19 Opening Hours
Main entrance: Mon-Fri 7:30 am to 16.00 pm. Closed on weekends and public holidays!
While the main entrance is closed, access to the building is available only with an access card. Every person entering the campus is required to use their own access card. Please note that letting other people into the building is strictly prohibited.
EXAM visitors:
Please see the instructions for ordering an Exam visitor access on the
Lahti campus – Exam facilities page.
The M19 building is open to students with an access card from 6:00 AM to 12:00 AM.
With a separate night work notification, access can be granted for the hours between 12:00 AM and 6:00 AM. The notification must be sent by email to the teacher responsible for studies and to Facilities Services at tilapalvelut.lahti@lut.fi. The notification must include information about which spaces access is needed for. Access is granted for one night at a time for a specific study-related reason. Once the responsible teacher has confirmed the notification, access rights will be updated to the access card.
Allowing other individuals to enter is prohibited!
Lobby Services Opening Hours
Service desk open:
Monday: 8am-8.45 and 10.15-12.00 and 1pm-2pm
Tuesday-Friday: 8am-12.00 and 1pm-3pm
Porters room A103 - room next to the lobby. Entry to the room from the back door.
Facility Services’ electronic services via the Jelppi portal or by email:
tilapalvelut.lahti@lut.fi
Contact us by phone: +358 29 446 2513
Niemenkatu 73 Opening Hours
B2 entrance (access from Kampusraitti or Laatikkotehtaankatu):
Mon–Fri: 7:45 AM – 4:00 PM.
Accessible access via Main Doors (access from Niemenkatu):
Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 4:00 PM.
MOVEOn members can access daily from 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM with HID mobile access obtained through MOVEO membership purchase. More information:

Parking
Parking for staff and students
Parking at Mukkulankatu 19 / Isku Center
Parking on campus is managed by the property owner, ISKU Center, and is generally subject to a fee.
Short-Term Parking (P1)
The P1 parking area is located in front of the Isku Center building. Parking can be paid using the eParking, EasyPark, Moovy, or Parkman mobile applications. First hour free of charge and after that: €1/hour.
Download the app before arriving in the area.
Electric Vehicle Charging (P1)
Electric vehicle charging and the use of heating poles for slow charging are available through the
eParking
application. Prices: Charging: €0.25/kWh, Slow charging / heating pole: €0.18/kWh. First hour free of charge, after that €1/hour
Please note that vehicles parked in charging spaces must be actively charging. Once charging has ended, the vehicle must be moved to a regular parking space.
Long-Term Parking Permits
Long
‑
term area parking permits for staff and students are purchased through the
eParking
application with HAKA user.
Student Parking Permit (P3)
Area P3 parking permit is intended for students. Price: €10/month. Permits can be purchased through the eParking application with HAKA user.
Area Parking Permits (P2, P4 and P7)
Area parking permits can be purchased through the
eParking application with HAKA user
. More detailed prices available in the application.
If you have any questions regarding area parking permits, you can contact eParking customer support via the eParking application or by phone at +358 34 108 9272 (weekdays 7:00–18:00, local call/mobile charge).
Evening and Weekend Students
Parking in areas P2 and P3 is free: Weekdays after 4:00 PM and weekends.
Niemenkatu 73
Niemenkatu building on the Lahti campus, LUT/LAB staff and students have free parking available at the sand field at Laatikkotehtaankatu 2. The closest entrance is through the pedestrian crossing at door B2 (open 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m.). At door B2, there is a ParkkiPate device where you must enter your registration number. You can only park in the Laatikkotehtaankatu parking area on days when you are studying or teaching at Niemenkatu.
More information:

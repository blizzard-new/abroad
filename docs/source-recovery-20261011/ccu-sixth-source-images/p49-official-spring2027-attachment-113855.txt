5. Student Life at Sungshin
                       Private Health Insurance (Mandatory)
                       Ÿ     As required by the Korean Ministry of Education, you are required to be
                             covered by Korean private health insurance.
                       Ÿ     For a one-time payment of around KRW 80,000, coverage is provided for
                             six months (one semester).
                       Ÿ     The purpose of this private insurance is to cover medical gaps that may
                             arise from the time you enter the country until your National Health
                             Insurance coverage begins, which can take several months.
                       Ÿ     The Certificate of Admission will be issued only after the payment has
    Health Insurance         been confirmed. Further details will be provided to students who have
                             completed the application.

                           National Health Insurance (Mandatory)
                       Ÿ     National Health Insurance is mandated by the law.
                       Ÿ     National Health Insurance is applied upon the issuance of the Residence Card.
                       Ÿ     Details about registration and fees are TBA.

                       u Note
                           - After receiving the Certificate of Admission and the Business Registration
                            Certificate from us via email, you can apply for a D-2 student visa at the

      Student Visa

         (D-2)

                            obtain your ARC before departure during your exchange period.
                           - Please note that your D-2 visa will be used upon your first entry into
                            Korea and will be invalidated when you depart from Korea in the
                            absence of a valid Registration Card. Should you wish to go ahead with
                            the above planned itinerary, you will need to apply for D-2 visa again
                            for your exchange program.

Notice Regarding the Effective Date of the Revised Student Residence Fees 
（Effective April 1, 2027） 
 
On December 10, 2025, the University of Tsukuba announced a  revision of student residence 
fees in response to rising maintenance and management costs and the need to improve residence 
hall facilities. 
Subsequently, on March 26, 2026, the university issued a notice postponing the effective date, 
which had originally been set for April 1, 2026. 
The university hereby announces that the revised residence fees will take effect on April 1, 2027. 
 
(Background of the Revision) 
The University of Tsukuba’s student residence halls were established to provide students with 
a conducive environment for stud y and to foster independent living. Since the university's 
establishment, the residence halls have played an important role in supporting students' growth. 
However, due to the aging of the buildings and rising prices, maintenance and operational costs 
have continued to increase. As a result, it has become difficult to maintain the residence halls and 
improve the living environment under the current residence fee structure. 
Therefore, to ensure that students can continue living in the residence halls safely, securely, 
and comfortably, the university has decided to revise the student residence fees and has now 
determined the effective date for the implementation of the revised residence fees.  
 
(Dialogue on Student Residence Halls) 
Following three information sessions held for students between January and March 2026, we 
organized a “Dialogue on S tudent Residence Halls” from May to June. During this period, we 
provided explanatory videos, conducted individual listening sessions and surveys, and held a  
dialogue session regarding the purpose and necessity of the revision.

We received valuable feedback through this dialogue. We will continue to exchange views with 
students and work to improve the management and operation of the residence halls.

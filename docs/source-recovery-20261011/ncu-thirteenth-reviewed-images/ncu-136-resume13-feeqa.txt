Q&A Regarding the Revision of Residence Fees 
September 28th, 2026 
Department of Student Affairs , University of Tsukuba  
 
Q1  When will the revised residence fees take effect? 
A1  The revised residence fees will take effect on April 1, 2027. 
However, residents of the Short-Stay House (SSH) or Global Village who have been 
granted permission to stay for a period extending beyond Academic Year 2026 will 
continue to be charged the current rates until the end of their approved residence 
period. 
If you are unsure of your approved residence period, please contact the Residence 
Administration Office. 
Example: If your approved residence period is from April 1, 2026, through March 31, 
2028, the current residence fees will continue to apply until March 31, 2028. 
 
Q2  What procedures are required if I wish to continue living in a residence hall next 
academic year? 
A2  An application process for current students will be conducted. 
    Details will be announced around late October. 
 
Q3  M y permitted period of residence expires at the end of AY 2026. What residence fees 
will apply after 1st April 2027 if I am permitted to continue living in the residence hall? 
A3  The revised residence fees will apply. This is because continued residence is treated 
as a new permission for residence, even if you continue living in the same room. 
 
Q4  Can I move to the lower-cost room next academic year? 
A4  Information regarding the application process for current students  will be announced 
around late October. At that time, please contact the office listed at the end of this 
document. 
 We will make every effort to accommodate your request. 
 
Q5  I will graduate from/finish the university in the middle of AY 2027, but my current 
permitted period of residence ends in AY 2026. Can I continue living in the residence 
hall until my graduation/finishing? 
A5  Information regarding the application process for current students will be announced 
around late October.

Q6  When will an air conditioner be installed?  
A6  The University began to procure air conditioners during the current academic year and 
will continue installing them in phases. We are working to ensure that rooms requiring 
air conditioning are equipped by the summer of 2027. 
Regarding rooms where an air conditioner has not yet been installed, 2,500 yen will 
be deducted from the monthly residence fee from April 2027 until the University 
installs an air conditioner in the room. 
 
Q7  I currently use a rental air conditioner that I arranged by myself. If I am allowed to live 
there next academic year, can I continue to use it? 
A7  Yes. If you are permitted to live continuously and wish to continue using a rental air 
conditioner that you arranged by yourself, the University will not install an air 
conditioner in your room during your period of residence.  
In this case, 2,500 yen will be deducted from the monthly residence fee. 
We plan to ask residents about their preferences regarding air conditioner 
arrangements during the application process for current students, which is scheduled 
for around late October. 
 
Q8  I’ d like to continue living in the residence hall next academic year but I do not wish to 
have an air conditioner installed by the University. What will happen in this case? 
A8  The same arrangement described in A7 will apply. 
 
Q9  I currently use a rental air conditioner that I arranged by myself, and plan to move out 
this academic year. In this case, what should I do with the rental air conditioner? 
A9  The case is currently under consideration, and we will provide further information once 
a decision has been made. For the purposes of this FAQ, a "rental air conditioner" 
refers to a standard room air conditioner. If you have a rental window air conditioner 
or other similar equipment, you must remove it before moving out in accordance with 
the terms of your rental agreement.

Q10  I currently use an air conditioner that I purchased by myself, and I plan to move out 
this academic year. In this case, what should I do with it? 
A10  You must remove it by yourself by the date of moving out.   
 
Q11  I applied for an air conditioner installation by the U niversity this summer and had it 
installed.  How about the charges for use if I am permitted to live continuously next 
academic year? 
A11  Currently , residents pay 2,500 yen per month as the charge for use. However, 
beginning in April 2027, this cost will be included in the revised residence fees, and 
no additional payment will be required. 
 
Q12  Is there financial support for residents?  
A12  Yes. Regardless of whether a student lives in University housing, various forms of 
financial assistance are available, including tuition and admission fee exemptions, 
tuition fees deferment, and scholarships. 
Regarding scholarships, there are approximately 200 scholarships provided by 
private organizations and local governments in addition to scholarships of Japan 
Student Services Organization (JASSO).  The University also offers its own 
scholarship program “Tsukuba Scholarship”. Students seeking advice based on their 
individual circumstances are encouraged to consult the Division of Student Welfare or 
other relevant offices. 
 (Official website for University of Tsukuba: support-scholarship) 
     https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/campuslife/support-scholarship/ 
 
Q13  What are the residence fees for barrier-free rooms? 
A13  The residence fee s of Ichinoya Bldg. 32 and 33 that have barrier-free rooms will be  
92,750 yen per month. If a student with a disability is determined to require a barrier-
free room, they should pay 51,980 yen per month and the University will support the 
difference. 
 
Q14  What procedures are required to receive support for the residence fees for a barrier-
free room?  
A14  First, please consult the Bureau of Human Empowerment (BHE) regarding academic 
accommodation support. After that, please submit an application using the prescribed 
form.  
Support will be provided if, based on BHE's professional assessment, the Vice

Photo Requirements 
 - The image should be clear and in color. 
 - Only the applicant should be in the photo. 
 - Must be taken within the past 3 months, showing current appearance. 
 - Do not wear a hat or headgear that obscures the hair or hair line. 
- There should be no reflection or glare on your glasses if you wear them. 
 - There should be spaces above and beside the face. 
 - Full face with front view 
 - Aspect ratio: 4:3 (Height: 4, Width: 3) 
 - Background: white or off-white without shadows, texture, objects, or lines. 
 - File format: JPG

For students who apply for the NUFS scholarship 
The minimum required balance is 40,000 yen per month. 
■ One semester: 40,000 yen x 5 months = 200,000 yen 
■ One academic year: 40,000 yen x 10 months = 400,000 yen 
 
For students who do NOT apply for the NUFS scholarship 
The minimum required balance is 100,000 yen per month. 
■ One semester: 100,000 yen x 5 months = 500,000 yen 
■ One academic year: 100,000 yen x 10 months = 1,000,000 yen

To those who have visited Japan before, 
 
Please be sure to upload copies of ALL Japanese ENTRY and EXIT stamps in 
your passport(s). If the stamps are in your old passport, please also upload 
the photo page of your old passport. 
If you went through an automated gate and did not get an exit stamp when 
you left Japan, please upload a copy of your e-ticket/boarding pass or flight 
itinerary that proves the departure date from Japan. 
 
 
 
➢ Nationals of the Philippines, Vietnam and Nepal who live in these countries 
are required to get a chest X-ray at medical facilities designated by the 
Japanese government and obtain the TB Clearance Certificate. For more 
details, please refer to the website of the Ministry of Health, Labour and 
Welfare below. 
https://jpets.mhlw.go.jp/index.html

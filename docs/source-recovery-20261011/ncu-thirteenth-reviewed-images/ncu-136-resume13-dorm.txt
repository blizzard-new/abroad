Residence Halls Outline

What are the University of Tsukuba’s residence halls?
The residence halls were built with the intention of providing students with a superb learning environment while enabling 
them to experience living as independent members of the public. 
Furthermore, 
since April 2016 the University started to run the Short Stay House, and from April 2017 the 
house-share-type Global Village, which focuses on international interaction and was built to encourage the 
internationalization of the campus. In May 2026, the Future Society Design Complex opened as a landmark in the 
residence area.
This is what make the residence halls so attractive!
- The rental costs are slightly lower than private apartments located in the vicinity (as the University pays the basic tariff
for your utility costs you only have to pay the amount you actually use!)
- As they are located on-campus you are never far from where your next lecture will be held.
- You’ll be living with other University of Tsukuba students so it’s easy to make friends! You can make groups of friends
regardless of your school or college!
- There’s a residence halls festival (the Yadokari Festival) held each May, which provides a lively time for those entering
the halls!
How big are the residence halls?
The residence halls are the largest of any national university in Japan, with 3,  rooms spread across four areas.
In recent years, the older buildings were closed and Global Village was established to encourage the internationalization 
of the campus.
  ʲResidence hall data for the academic year 2026ʳ
Reliable administration offices
The day-to-day management of the residence
halls is conducted by administration offices 
that are located in each of the areas, and those 
living in the halls are welcome to speak with 
staff at any of the offices about any troubles 
they may be experiencing.
Who can stay in the residence halls and for how long?
- Basically only students of the University of Tsukuba can move into the residence halls (students on leave of absence
cannot in principle be accommodated)
- Consideration is paid towards accommodating in regular single rooms for students newly entering a school and
overseas students on a prioritized basis. (A little over 40% of newly enrolling students move into the residence halls
every year.)
- The type of room provided to students may vary according to student category, years of study at the University, room
availability, time of moving in and so on. For further details, please consult the persons-in-charge of student residence
halls in the Division of Student Welfare.

○Communal facilities at residence halls
 ʲSecurityʳ
- Entrance security system
To protect residents and prevent crime, the security system is installed at the entrance of each residential building,
PIN number security system in the OIKOSHI and HIRASUNA areas, IC tag system in the ICHINOYA and KASUGA
areas. Only residents can enter the building.
- Security camera
In order to prevent the intrusion of suspicious persons, security cameras are installed outside the buildings.
 ʲFacilities in the residence hallsʳ
- Facilities in rooms
Facilities vary according to the type of room. Please refer to the plans for more details.
- Residence hall facilities
The facilities in the Standard single room that account for the majority of residence halls are as follows.
⾣Six-item set comprising a quilt, mattress pad, sheets, pillow, pillow cover and quilt cover
⾣It is possible to exchange the items for clean ones around once every 10 days at the linen
room
⾣The costs are included in the residence fees except in the cases of certain rooms (such as family 
rooms)
⾣Panel heater
⾣Heaters can be used between 06:00 and 08:00 and 17:00 to 23:00 from late
November to early March (06:00 to 10:00 and 15:00 to 22:00 on holidays)
⾣Air conditioners are not installed, but it is possible to enter into a leasing agreement for
one at the electronic goods stores in Ichinoya, Oikoshi and Hirasuna Community
Centers.
⾣In certain rooms (such as those in the Global Village) air conditioners for both cooling
and heating are installed instead of panel heaters.
⾣All rooms are equipped with an optical line and wireless LAN
⾣There is no need for residents to enter an agreement with a provider
⾣Round-the-clock support available from specialists

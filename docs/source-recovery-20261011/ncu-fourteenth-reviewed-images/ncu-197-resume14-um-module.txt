Module Restrictions:
The university does not offer modules from the Bachelors of Dentistry & Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery programmes for mobility students.
Undergraduate students are not eligible to take postgraduate modules, but Postgraduate students are allowed to take both undergraduate and postgraduate modules.
*The university reserves the right to make changes to the list without any prior notice.
Helpful Tips:
Academic workload per semester: No credits and up to a maximum of 20 credits per semester.
Module selection is based on the fundamentals of multidisciplinary whereby students can study modules from different faculties which covers Sciences, Arts & Social Science, Business, Language, Engineering, Computer Science, etc.
There will be a module registration week before the semester begins for you to register for the final module based on the available capacity and timetable. Also, students can not enroll in classes that are clashing in timetable, hence students are advised to be prepared with alternative options before the module registration.

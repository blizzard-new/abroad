Alien Registration
Within 90 days of your arrival in Korea, you must register to get an Alien Registration Card regardless of your visa type unless your total period of stay in Korea is less than 90 days. The International Center provides exchange students with this service.
Upon your arrival, please submit the following documents:
Completed Application Form (application form is available at the International Center)
One color photos [3x4]
(The Background must be a white color.)
Passport
Certificate of Enrollment
A copy of Housing Contract
Tuberculosis(TB) Test Result
(※ TB Test result is required only for those who applied to stay at Inha dormitory during their academic semester(s).)
Photocopy of Insurance Policy
Application Fee: KRW 30,000
Student ID
The student ID card is both your student identification card and your library card. It is issued to all Inha students in order to access resources such as campus libraries and photocopying.
The student ID card would be issued prior to the beginning of a semester. Distribution date and pick-up location will be announced via Inha University website (Inha New>notice) or e-mail.

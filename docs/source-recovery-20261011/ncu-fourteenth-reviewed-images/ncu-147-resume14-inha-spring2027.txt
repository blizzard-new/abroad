Insurance 
Insurance ‘Private insurance & Korean National Health Insurance (NHIS)’ are mandatory for 
all students. It is government regulation with no exception. 
 
Private insurance: will be purchased first week of school 
NHIS: will automatically qualified  
※  Even if you already have health insurance at your home university, 
you must purchase a designated private health insurance at Inha university 
according to government regulations. 
If you have any further questions, please contact us at interservice@inha.ac.kr 
 
Accommodation and Housing Assistance  
(It can be subject to change for 2027 Spring) 
1. On-Campus Dormitory Dormitory Fees (as of Spring 2027): 
Approximately KRW 1,500,000 per semester (including one meal per day) 
Room Type: Quadruple rooms with shared bathroom facilities

※  Check-in will not be permitted without a copy of a valid Tuberculosis (TB) test 
result. 
 
For more information, please visit: https://dormeng.inha.ac.kr/dormeng/index.do

2. Off-Campus Housing 
Introduced by INHA 
Off-Campus Housing Fees (as of Spring 2027): 
Approximately KRW 700,000 per month 
※  A penalty of KRW 100,000 will be imposed for cancellations made within 30 
days prior to the check-in date. 
※  The International Center provides assistance for students who wish to stay in 
studio-type (one-room) accommodations near campus.

do so independently, preferably upon early arrival. 
※  Off-campus housing fees are subject to change each semester.

you by email in early January (for students studying at Inha University in the Spring 
semester) or in early July (for students studying at Inha University in the Fall 
semester).

Buddy Program The Inha Buddy Program is designed to promote cultural exchange and interaction

※  Students who wish to apply for the Buddy Program should select the appropriate

Personal illness and 
medicines 
Students are responsible for managing their own health and personal illnesses. 
If students plan to bring medications from their home country to Korea, they must 
check in advance whether the medications are permitted by the Korean Ministry of 
Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) 
(https://www.mfds.go.kr/eng/index.do). 
If required, students must complete the necessary procedures to obtain prior 
approval for carrying such medications in accordance with MFDS regulations. 
Estimated Living Expense KRW 1,300,000 per month 
(Including accommodation and meals; applicable if not staying in the University 
Dormitory) 
KRW 150,000 per month for miscellaneous expenses 
(Including transportation, textbooks, etc.)

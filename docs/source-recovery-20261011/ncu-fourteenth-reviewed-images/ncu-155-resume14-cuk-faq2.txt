Q
[Dormitory] WIFI
A.
Free WIFI is available on campus
including dormitory. Look for the “Catholic_Univ” signal. The password is
1234567890.
29
Q
[Dormitory] Dormitory Rules & Regulations or any other dormitory information
A.
It is for a student who use a room for
4 students(4-bed room). If you use the top of a double deck bed, you are
supposed use the desk close to the window. If you use the bottom of a double
deck bed, you are supposed to use the desk near the door.
※
If there are two foreign students in a
quad room (a room for 4 students), the two foreign students should use only one
side only (bed 1,3 or bed 2,4)
28
Q
[Dormitory] Curfew
A.
From 1.am to 5.am.
27
Q
[Dormitory] Prohibition
A.
It is not allowed to drink alcohol and
smoke. Anybody caught breaking this rule will be out of the dormitory right
away.

Q
[Dormitory] Can I send my luggage to the dormitory?
A.
You can send your luggage to the
address below. When you have a room number after entering our school, you must write
the room number and your name to reduce the risk of losing it.

Q
[Dormitory] Forbidden Stuff
A.
Possession or use of personal electric
heating appliances, boilers, irons, microwaves, any flammable and hazardous
materials are forbidden.
24
Q
[Dormitory] Personal belongings
A.
Desk lamp is mandatory for students
who are living in 3 beds room. You can bring hair dryers, laptops, clothes,
book etc.
23
Q
[Dormitory] Power Plug Adapters
A.
The standard voltage in Korea is 220
volts. If you do not have a multi-voltage travel adapter, please make sure to
purchase one before arrival.
22
Q
[Dormitory] Thick bed cover
A.
You must bring or purchase your own
ONE THICK BED COVER to prevent mattress contamination.
21
Q
[Dormitory] Linen & Bedding
A.
You can either purchase your linens in
Korea or bedding rental is provided by the Dormitory with a KRW 50,000 charge
in cash. (Includes pillow, pillowcase, bed sheet, blanket).

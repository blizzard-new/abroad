Q
[Academic Records] Grading
A.
Students will receive a letter grade
ranging from A+ to F. The grade of “A” is given for excellent performance; “B”
for above average; “C” for average; “D” for minimally passing performance; and
“F” for failure. In addition, “+” and “0“ are used for further differentiation.
A student’s performance with “+” indicates a superior performance in that
letter grade, and “0“ indicates a slightly weaker performance in that letter
grade. Other grades include “P” (pass) and “F” (fail). The grade will become a
part of permanent record at CUK.

Q
[Courses Registration] Is there any other information that I should know?
A.
Students
cannot drop any official grades including classes that received an ‘F.’
Therefore, make sure to attend all classes on the first week of school and if
there are any courses you would like to drop, you may add or drop courses
during the “C
lass Schedule Change Period”.
8
Q
[Courses Registration] Can I withdrawal the courses after the “Class Schedule Change Period”?
A.
Students
may withdrawal from enrolled courses during the given period, by the 4
th
of week of September without penalty. However, please keep in mind that course
addition is not allowed during this period.
The
students are required to have their withdrawn courses approved by the course
professor for the process to be completed. In addition, the “Class Schedule
Change Form” must be submitted to the department you belong to, during this
period. If not, students may face academic penalty such as receiving an “F”
grade for the course.
7
Q
[Courses Registration] Can I change the courses after registration on orientation?
A.
Students
may add or drop courses during the “C
lass Schedule Change Period”
(the first week of the semester), by
logging in to the school website called Trinity System.
6
Q
[Courses Registration] Is there a maximum of credits I can register for?
A.
Students
can take almost all courses offered at undergraduate level from 9 credits up to
a maximum of 19 credits per semester. If you register less than 9 credits you
must submit an statement of reasons to Office of International Affairs.

Q
[Courses Registration] When and how do I register?
A.
In
the third week of August (around Aug 10~14), there will be a course
registration date, in that period, most students will self-quarantine.

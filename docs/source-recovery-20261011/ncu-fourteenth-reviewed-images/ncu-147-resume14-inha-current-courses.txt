Class Load/Hours
Most classes at Inha comprise of 3 hours per week, which means that they are designed as three(3)-credit courses.
1 credit hour equals 15 class hours with the exception of experimental courses being 30 hours.
Course Search
The list of courses offered in English can be found online at the university’s course registration website.
(Fixed time table and course lists will be available approximately a month before the semester starts.)
Go to
http://sugang.inha.ac.kr/
and click
‘English’
on the upper right side menu.
(Our course registration website(i.e. Sugang site) is optimized at chrome browser).
Process
Click ‘Course Schedule’ on ‘Curriculum’ on the left side menu. Once you click it, a pop up screen shows up.
Select ‘foreign language’ on the ‘etc.’ bar on new pop-up window.
Please check the Note column on the course table to see the instruction language.
Exchange students can take courses across the majors upon successful course registration. Course lists are subject to change due to departmental circumstances.

Exchange Student Application
In order to be accepted as an exchange student at Inha University, your home university must have an exchange agreement with Inha. Please check whether your university has a partnership with Inha. See
partner institutions.
Online Application
Once you have been officially nominated as an exchange student by your home university, you can directly apply online.
Deadline for online application :
Please find our updated
Factsheet
for details as dates may differ each semester.
Submission of Documents :
Applicants do not need to mail the application documents to the Inha University International Office.

Nomination :
International coordinator at home university should send the nomination list to Inha University International Center.

Applicant should complete their online application here.

Application Review :
Inha University International Center reviews the application documents
Acceptance :
Once you are accepted as an exchange student, the International Center will send out the official letter of acceptance via e-mail.
Further information will be sent out via e-mail. Therefore, it is very important that the e-mail address you stated on the application form is valid and that you check your e-mail regularly.
Online Application Guide
Once you have been officially nominated as an exchange student by your home university, you can directly apply online. The online application system serves as a one stop service program which not only allows exchange students to apply online but also enables them to check their application status.
Inha University receives exchange nominations on a semester basis.
Application Process
Once you are accepted as an exchange student, the International Center will send out the official letter of acceptance via mail.
Further information will be sent out via e-mail. Therefore, it is very important that the e-mail address you stated on the application form is valid and that you check your e-mail box regularly.

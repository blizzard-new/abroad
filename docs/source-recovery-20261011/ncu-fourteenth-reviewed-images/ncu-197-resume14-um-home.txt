PROGRAM OVERVIEW
The program is conducted for a minimum duration of one semester up to a maximum of one academic year (12 months). It involves credit bearing courses with lectures and tutorials. Students can choose from a wide range of courses which are offered by 15 faculties in Universiti Malaya.
SEMESTER OFFERING
Semester 1 (October - February) | Fall Semester
Semester 2 (March - July) | Spring Semester

ELIGIBILITY
Enrolled as an active student (Undergraduate/Postgraduate Coursework) in home university
Have completed at least one semester of study in home university
Have good academic standing with a CGPA of not less than 3.0 or equivalent
Have good English proficiency in speaking and writing
Nominated by the home university through the nomination form provided by UM

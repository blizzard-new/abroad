Guiding Note: Confirmation Letter
Please submit a pdf copy of a confirmation letter from your home university. The template is provided in the 
invitation for application email. If you do not have it, you can get the template here.
Please ensure the student's name is written according tothe
Passport Machine-Readable Zone (MRZ) Format
Check out on
#5 Applicant’s Name as in Passport MRZ Guideline
Please ensure the home university’s coordinator or any
authorized person sign the letter .
Letter with no signature will be rejected by Immigration
Department
Important Note
Please ensure the confirmation/recommendation letter is with
letterhead of the home institution
Please ensure the confirmation/recommendation letter is
complete with the date of letter issuance.

3.0  Fill up the Application Form
Guiding Note: English Proficiency Certificate
Language Proficiency Certificate is required 
for students. Students must at least have 
acquired either one of the following English 
proficiency:
1.IELTS (Academic): Band 6
2.TOEFL iBT:80
3.TOEFL cBT: 214
4.ITOEFL pBT: 550
5.TOEFL ITP: 550
6.TOEIC: 690
7.CEFR: B2
8.Other: Equivalent to IELTS Band 6
A Support/Recommendation Letter issued by the Home 
University's Coordinator to confirm the student’s English 
proficiency will be sufficient for those who do not have the 
ceritifications. The template can be found in the link here.

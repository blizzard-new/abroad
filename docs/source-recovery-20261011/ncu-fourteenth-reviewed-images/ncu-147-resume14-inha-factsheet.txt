3. Academic & Language Qualifications 
Common Requirement Currently enrolled students at one of Inha University's partner institutions. 
Cumulative GPA Minimum 2.5 / 4.5 cumulative GPA (or 2.22 / 4.0, or 75 / 100 points equivalent) at 
home institution. 
Language Requirement 
(English or Korean) 
• Courses in English: Official score taken within 2 years: 
  - TOEFL: iBT 71 or above (4 or above according to new TOEFL scale) 
  - IELTS: 5.5 or above 
  - Duolingo English Test: 90 or above 
  - CAE (Cambridge Advanced English): 173 or above 
  *Score must remain valid until the application deadline (October 21st, 2026). 
 
• Courses in Korean (Department Requirements): 
  - Dept. of Korean Language & Literature: Undergraduate TOPIK 3+ | Master 
TOPIK 4+ | Doctoral TOPIK 5+ 
  - Dept. of Korean Language & Culture (KLC): TOPIK 3 or above 
  *TOPIK score must be valid throughout the exchange semester. 
Letter of 
Recommendation 
•Mandatory Submission: A Letter of Recommendation is required for all 
applicants. 
※ Must be issued by your international coordinator or advising professor. 
•For those who do not meet the minimum GPA or lack an official English test 
score, this letter can be used to satisfy (replace) those requirements. 
• Even if a recommendation letter has been submitted, the applicant may not be 
finally selected based on a comprehensive evaluation of academic performance 
and other assessment criteria.

7. Financial Requirements (Bank Certificate) 
Mandatory Bank Certificate Verification Standards 
 
• Submitting a bank certificate under your own account is strongly recommended. If using parents' account, submit 
Family Relations Certificate. (* Certificates from siblings are not accepted.) 
• Format & Authentication: Must be issued officially in English after 8th of September 2026 with a bank official's 
signature or bank stamp. Do NOT download or print at home — you must physically visit the bank. Transaction 
statements alone are strictly rejected. 
• Balance Requirement: 
   - 1 Semester applicants:  more than USD 6,000 
   - 2 Semesters applicants: more than USD 8,000 
• Mandatory Contents: Issuance date, account holder's full name, account balance, and bank official's stamp or 
signature. 
• HARDCOPY SUBMISSION: Non-OECD nationality students must send the original physical bank certificate to Inha 
University by postal mail before the application deadline. 
• SPECIAL STRICT NOTICE FOR UZBEKISTAN CITIZENS: 
- ONLY KDB Bank certificates are accepted. Absolutely no exceptions — applications with certificates from any other 
bank will be automatically rejected regardless of balance. 
- 1-Month Deposit Duration Rule: The required amount must have been held in the account for more than 1 month 
prior to the date of certificate issuance (e.g., if issued on 13th March, funds must be deposited on or before 13th 
February). The certificate or bank statement must explicitly reflect this duration. 
- Balance Requirement: 
   - 1 Semester applicants: more than USD 6,000 
   - 2 Semesters applicants: more than USD 8,000

Step-by-Step Course 
Search Instructions 
1. Visit http://sugang.inha.ac.kr/sugang/ and click 'English' on the top-right menu. 
2. Click 'Course Schedule' under 'Curriculum' on the left navigation bar (a pop-up 
window will appear). 
3. On the new pop-up window, select 'foreign language' on the 'etc.' dropdown 
menu. 
4. Check the 'Note' column in the schedule table to confirm the exact instructional 
language. 
5. Attention: Do NOT use the 'Department/Major' filter bar, as doing so will 
automatically reset the foreign language filter setting. 
6. Policy: Exchange students are permitted to take courses across different 
departments/majors upon successful registration.

Q
[Dormitory] Toiletries
A.
Toiletries can be purchased at the
nearest convenience store or market here.
19
Q
[Dormitory] What do I need when moving into the dorm?
A.
Students will need to bring their own
pillow, linens, blankets, towels, and toiletries.
18
Q
[Dormitory] When can I move in and move out?
A.
It is available from 9AM to 10PM at
the end of the August (the exact date will be notice). Please be sure to keep
your time, as it may not be possible to move into in other times.
Students who chose a 16-weeks plan,
have to move out by 14th December 2019. Students, chose a 24-weeks plan, have
to move out by the mid of February. If students chose a 16-week plan at first and
changed to a 24-week plan after arriving in Korea, students should leave the
dormitory for 7 to 10 days, dormitory cleaning period, right after the fall
semester. Please choose carefully.
17
Q
[Korean Language Class] How much is the tuition fee?
A.
Students from our partner universities
could receive a 30% discount. So, the tuition is KRW 910,000.
However, Korean language courses are
offered free of charge to every partner university which offers free language
courses to CUK students, following the reciprocity principle. In this case, Tuition
exemption applies only to Spring and Fall semesters. For summer and winter
semester, KRW 910,000 is required.
16
Q
[Korean Language Class] How does Korean language class work?
A.
Our Korean Language Education Center(KLEC)
offers Korean language classes at several different levels from beginner(level
1) to advance(level 6). Students will be assigned in accordance with their
placement test results. The placement test includes writing and an interview.
Beginners with no Korean background are also required to take the test. Korean
classes are offered 4 hours a day, from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, Monday through
Friday. A total of 10 weeks and 200 hours of classes are offered.
15
Q
[Attendance] Regarding Absence Waivers for a menstrual period
A.
Female
students may use an absence waiver once a month
, by logging in to the school website called Trinity
System.
14
Q
[Attendance] Regarding Attendance for the Korean Language Education Center
A.
Korean language classes are 200 hours
in total for 10 weeks. If you miss more than 20 hours, you will not be able to
pass.
13
Q
[Attendance] Attendance for Regular Courses
A.
It is
an automatic F, when students are absent more than 1/4 of the classes. If students
suddenly become sick and cannot attend classes, it is needed to tell the course
professor. In addition, students should bring medical records and other
documents to discuss with the course professor whether there is any way that it
will not be treated as absent.
12
Q
[Academic Records] How many credits can I transfer?
A.
Credits earned at CUK can be
transferred. Please consult with your study abroad advisor or academic advisor
at your home institution for regulations concerning credit transfer.
11
Q
[Academic Records] How can I get my transcript?
A.
Transcripts will be sent to the
International Affairs of your school after the end of the semester. Students
who are enrolled for one year will have transcripts sent at the end of the
second semester. If you request more copies, you should pay for the transcript
issuance fee and delivery fee.

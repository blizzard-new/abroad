PARTNER UNIVERSITY'S STUDENT
Stage 1: Application Steps
Step 1:
Students will be receiving an email from UM confirming the nomination by the Home University. Application procedure & guidelines will be given in the email for reference.
Step 2:
To begin the application process, you need to first create an account on
apply.um.edu.my
Step 3:
Plan your list of modules that you would like to study during your semester abroad at UM. You may also want to map out the modules' syllabus with your home university for credit transfer purposes. List of module is available
here
Step 4:
Program Selection: Start the application by selecting the correct program code in the application system. You may refer to the Application Guideline for the accurate programme code.
Step 5:
Read, understand and download Offer Letter & Appendixes. Accept offer through application portal to confirm your participation
Step 6:
Apply for Student Mobility Visa, Purchase of Flight Ticket

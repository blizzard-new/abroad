Insurance
Inha University has made it mandatory for all international students to obtain an insurance plan from their home country or in Korea, which would cover cases of illness or injuries while staying in Korea. Students who do not purchase insurance policy may experience disadvantages when attempting the issuance of student ID card, online class registration and alien registration.
Exchange students are required to purchase the health insurance in their home country.. Degree-seeking student would have university’s designated insurance if they do not have a private health insurance or National Health Insurance.
To see more about university’s designated insurance, please see
Insurance Guide download
University Health Center
University Health Center has some trained health professionals to provide counseling, health promotion and simple medical treatment to all students at Inha University. Any international student can use their services.
Location: 2nd Floor Student Center
Tel: 032-860-8311
International Health Care Center at Inha University Hospital
The International Health Care Center at Inha University Hospital offers a wide range of medical services for foreigners in Korea. The center, located on the 3rd floor of the University Hospital, is staffed by doctors and nurses who are fluent in several languages (e. g. English, Chinese, Russian, Japanese etc.). For more information, please visit its website
https://www.inha.com
Counseling Service for International Students
The International Center offers international students free counseling service in Chinese and English. This counseling service assists students facing the special challenge of adapting to a new culture or dealing with problems that arise from cultural differences between you and your friends or other members of the university.

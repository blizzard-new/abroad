airport pick-up service has been canceled.

use a designated taxi for overseas entrants at the airport
and more details can be found at the airport.
For students who wish to come to Inha on their own, please refer to the visitor’s guide for the campus location.
For all international students who are planning to stay at dormitory, it would be good to arrive in Korea on the opening day of the dormitory. All exchange students will be notified of the dormitory opening day via e-mail. Please refer to the a academic calendar to check your academic schedule.

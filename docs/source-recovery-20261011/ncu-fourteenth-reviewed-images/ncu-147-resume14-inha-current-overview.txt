Exchange Student Program
Every year Inha University welcomes hundreds of incoming exchange students from more 80 countries all over the world. Inha University is a comprehensive university situated in the third biggest city Incheon and we offer courses in English throughout numerous departments.
Exchange semester at Inha University a perfect opportunity to widen your perspectives in the field of your studies and build up international networks!
In order to best help students adapt to their new environment, we offer many options and activities for exchange students as below.
Exchange Student Orientation
International Student Service team at Inha university help students smoothly adapt to the new student life through orientation sessions in the beginning of each semester. The main issues discussed are: academic calendar and key dates, course information and registration, the accommodation policies, and visa-related matters.
ISN(International Students Network)
INHA University's Davison of International Affairs closely cooperates with International Students Network(ISN). It hosts a series of cultural activities and language exchange tutoring open for exchange students. It also runs ISN lounge, a cosy separate space where incoming students can stop by and to network with other students and use computers do reading in between their lecture breaks.
Extension of Exchange
The period of exchange can be extended as long as INHA and a student’s host university both agree on it. To apply, an international exchange coordinator at the host university must officially confirm an exchange student’s intention to extend the exchange period by sending an email to their counterpart at INHA University. The student applying for exchange student should report the intension for extension to international student service team by the given due date.

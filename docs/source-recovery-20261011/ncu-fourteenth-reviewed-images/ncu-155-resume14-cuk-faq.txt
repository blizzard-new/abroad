Q
[Dormitory] When can I make the payment for dormitory fee?
A.
The dormitory office will inform you the
specific payment schedule and method after you arrive. It is acceptable only by
domestic wire transfer. Please bring cash or withdraw KRW from ATM and visit
Woori Bank, located in the 1st floor of the International Hub Building, makes the
payment.
Please check the dormitory fee below;
General residents (16 weeks)
Long-term residents (24 weeks)
Double
KRW 1,248,000
KRW 1,835,000
3-Bed
KWR 1,040,000
KRW 1,529,000
4-Bed
KRW 790,000
KRW 1,161,000
Before
leaving your country, please be sure to check whether you can withdraw money
from Korea ATMs and withdrawal limit in a day.

Q
[Dormitory]  Cleaning Period
A.
The dorm cleaning period is for about 7-10
days right after the end of the fall semester and just before the start of the
spring semester. If you selected a 16-weeks plan at first and changed to a 24-weeks
plan after arrival at CUK, you must leave out the dorm for this period. The
dormitory office will offer temporary accommodation on campus during this
period at a reasonable price.
If you selected a 24-weeks plan from
the start, you don't have to leave at this time. However, you have to leave the
dormitory during the cleaning period just before the spring semester starts.
The dormitory will offer temporary accommodation at reasonable prices.

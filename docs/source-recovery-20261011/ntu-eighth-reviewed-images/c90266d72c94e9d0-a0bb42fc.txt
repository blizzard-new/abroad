Academic Load 
Kobe University does not have a full -time load. 124 credits or more are necessary to 
complete an undergraduate program (Bachelor degree) at Kobe University (approx. 30 
credits/year). Maximum load per semester depends on each faculty, and there is no 
minimum load. However, all international students including exchange students must take at 
least 10 lesson hours (equivalent to 6-7 classes) per week in accordance with immigration 
law.

Class Types  
and Schedules 
Classes include lectures, seminars, experiments, exercises, and practical training. We will 
offer classes according to patterns A) – C) described below. Patterns A) and B) are "quarter 
classes", and pattern C) takes the "semester class" format. Please see the syllabus for 
further information about each class. 
A) 1 class per week for 8 weeks (including examination days) 
B)  2 classes per week for 8 weeks (including examination days) 
C)  1 class per week for 16 weeks (including examination days)   
 
Credits 
Grades and credits are acquired at the end of each academic semester. According to Kobe 
University General Rules for Students, 1 credit is usually awarded for 15 hours of lectures. 
To receive 1 credit, a total of 45 hours of study are required (thi s includes self-study hours 
to prepare for or review classwork).

Requirements for  
Entry to Japan 
1. CoE (Certificate of Eligibility) 
CoE is a necessary document to apply for a student VISA. 
In order to support international students’ smooth entrance to Kobe University, we offer 
prospective students a proxy application service for it. 
< Necessary documents: The notification will be sent to the students later > 
- ID photo 
- A copy of Passport 
- A copy of proof of financial support 
2. STUDENT VISA 
The applicant must apply in person to the Embassy or Consulate-General in their country. 
Visa cannot be obtained after arriving in Japan. 
For detailed information about necessary documents and procedures, please refer to the 
URL of Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan.

- Medical Checkups 
Kobe University carries out annual medical  checkups in April and October. New students 
must undergo a checkup in either of these months depending on their enrollment month. 
Students who do not take the free medical examination at Kobe University must take an 
equivalent medical checkup at an external hospital, for which it will be necessary to pay a 
fee for both the checkup  and certificate. Then they must present the certificate to the 
Medical Center for Student Health. 
 
- Medical Support Consultation Form 
For students with chronic conditions or disabilities (physical, developmental, or mental), 
we will make preparations to provide support where possible if they let us know in advance. 
If they are taking medication and need help finding continuous treatment in Japan, please 
let us know using the “Medical Support Consultation Form” before coming to Japan. This 
form will be enclosed when we send them  Pre-Arrival Information. (Fall:  around July , 
Spring: around January) 
Tutors 
Each international student is supported by a tutor with the aim of helping with and improving 
the effectiveness of their study and research. The tutors are recommended by the academic 
adviser, and are chosen from students with background knowledge in the international 
student's field of study. Ask your F aculty/School, Graduate School or GEC for further 
information.

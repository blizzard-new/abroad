Overview
Under the Academic Course Access Program (ACAP), Science Tokyo waives the application, enrollment, and tuition fees of undergraduate (second-year or above) and graduate-level International Exchange Students from participating partner universities [
LIST of agreements
]. ACAP students are accepted for a period of one quarter to four quarters (a year) – for JASSO scholarship recipients, a period of two quarters (a semester) to four quarters (a year) – and are able to study alongside Science Tokyo students in regular courses offered on a quarter or semester basis.
*ACAP students are not permitted to take courses offered by the faculties/graduate schools of Medicine, Dentistry and Health Care Sciences.
Eligibility
Applicants must be enrolled at partner universities with which Science Tokyo has a tuition waiver agreement.
Those with student status at their home university, enrolled in the following programs at the time they start coursework at Science Tokyo:
・Doctoral program
・Master’s program
・Bachelor’s program (at least one year of study)*
*
Except for undergraduate students in their final year, an official Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT N1) result or equivalent document is required as students are supposed to take courses conducted in Japanese.
Applicants must carefully consider what they wish to study at Science Tokyo and prepare an ACAP Course-Oriented Program Sheet, which will be submitted to Science Tokyo through the partner university at the time of nomination.

Application Procedures
Once you have decided to apply
Step 1. Contact the Student Exchange Officer (or equivalent) at your home university
ACAP Course-Oriented Program Sheet
ACAP applicants must be officially nominated to Science Tokyo by their home university, and Science Tokyo must be informed of the nomination BEFORE applicants are invited to apply online. At the time of nomination, the "ACAP Course-Oriented Program Sheet" prepared by each applicant should be submitted to Science Tokyo by their home university.
After receiving the official nomination from your home university, Science Tokyo will directly send you the necessary information for logging in to the online application form via email to you.
Step 2. Be assigned an Academic Supervisor at Science Tokyo
Science Tokyo's Schools
For all applicants, the Global Education Division will assign an ACAP Specialized Faculty member, selected from the applicant's desired department based on the ACAP Course-Oriented Program Sheet, to serve as their academic supervisor, and will notify the applicants of the result by email before application deadline. Applicants are NOT required to contact any faculty members at Science Tokyo directly.
Applicants applying to the Department of Architecture and Building Engineering are required to submit their Curriculum Vitae (CV) and portfolio via the online application form.
Currently, no applicants are accepted to the Department of Innovation Sciences.
After the application deadline, applicants are required to contact the assigned ACAP Specialized faculty member to confirm their acceptance and for an initial greeting.
Note that some Departments may exceed their capacity due to a high number of applicants and will be unable to accept additional students. In such cases, Science Tokyo will request the applicant to change their desired Department.
Step 3. Submit the required application materials online.
Three months to one month before enrollment

Step 5. Apply for your visa on your own with the COE which you have received.
Visa application processing time is expedited when you submit a COE with your visa application.

Course Information for International Exchange Students
ACAP participants are mandated to take 6 classes (600 minutes) or more per week to maintain student visa status. All participants are assigned to one of Science Tokyo's Schools or Departments. In principle, they should plan their course of study mainly around courses offered in the Department to which they are assigned. However, not all courses offered at Science Tokyo are available for International Exchange Students. Final-year undergraduate students should refer to previously approved 400-level courses and carefully consider the courses they can take while studying abroad at Science Tokyo. Please refer to the document “
Science Tokyo Course Information to Help Develop Your Study Plan
.”
Housing
For students who have submitted a request to move into a Science Tokyo dormitory through the online application, the Global Education Division will request that the department in charge of university dormitories will allocate a room for them. (Students are not required to apply for dormitories individually.) Students will be notified by email 1 to 1.5 months prior to enrollment whether or not they will be able to move into the dormitory. In that case, please follow the instructions in the email. We do not accept changes of dormitories assigned to applicants. Note that not all incoming students will be assigned a dormitory room as room availability is limited.
Cost of Living in Tokyo/Japan
It depends on where you live, but you will need at least JPY 100,000 per month as of 2024.
References:
・Science Tokyo Dormitory fees: JPY 27,500-65,000 (plus a separate move-in fee)
・Bottle of water: JPY 100-150
・Lunch at cafeteria at Science Tokyo: JPY 400-600
・Lunch at café or restaurant in Tokyo: JPY 1,000-1,500

Tuition
Exchange students:
Exchange students do not pay tuition fee.
Independent  Student:
Independent  Students pay tuition. The academic tuition will be charged according to the  number of credits taken in each term.
Tuition Fee - Method of Payment:
The fees must be paid in Reais (R$) currency rate.
Example:
If the student registered online for 19-20 credits, he will pay for  19-20 credits. According to PUC's table of values, in
2026
International  regular students must pay the total tuition of
R$ 44.802,00
per term or
R$  7.467,00
per month over 6 months for 19-20 credits. In the case of an  international student who arrives ON TIME for classes in late February/early  March or August, the student will pay for 40 credits in the first and second  monthly installments and for 19-20 credits in the third and fourth installments.
In case the student wants to cancel any class, the  reimbursement will only appear in the next month's installment.
If the student adds more classes during the Drop & Add period the  tuition amount will change too.
Ex: Before the Drop & Add the  students had chosen 20 credits and during the Drop & Add added one  class of (2 credits). His/her tuition will change from 20 credits  to 22 credits; consequently, the difference of these 2 credits will appear in  the others installments
.
After  the Drop & Add day, if the student cancel (drop) any class, the total of  the tuition and the installments will  not change.
Method of Payment:
The fees must be paid in Reais (R$), according to the dollar rate of the day.

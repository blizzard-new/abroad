Exchange students
WHO:
Undergraduate and graduate students enrolled in institutions that have an exchange agreement with PUC-Rio. In addition to the Portuguese as a Second Language (required) course, these students may attend other regular courses according to her/his area of interest.
CREDITS:
Academic credits are obtained.
TRANSCRIPT:
The student will receive an official PUC-Rio transcript listing the courses taken with its corresponding total number of class-hours, number of credits obtained and the final course grade.
APPLICATION:
In addition to the specific exchange program requirements and application process of the student´s home institution and study abroad office, the student must also observe all the requirements and deadlines of the PUC-Rio application process.

TUITION/FEES: An  administrative cost is charged
. Independent   Students pay tuition.
The academic tuition will be charged according to the number of credits taken in each term. In Brazil, the yearly tuition is divided into 12 monthly payments. The semester (term) tuition is divided into 6 monthly installments for regular students. Thus, in our first term of the year (late February/early March to late June/early July), students make 6 payments: in January, February, March, April, May and June; The same happens in the second term, with payments in July, August, September, October, November and December.
For the international students it is divided differently, based on the date of arrival.
Because  international exchange and Independent  students arrive at the university only in the second month  of the semester, the semester tuition will be divided into 4 installments  (March, April, May, June), THE FIRST TWO INSTALLMENTS  EQUAL the sum of 2 monthly  tuition payments each. In  case of a late  registration, the total tuition  amount will be divided  into 3 monthly payments (April, May, June).
Please refer to
http://www.puc-rio.br/ensinopesq/ccci/incoming/fees_tuition.html
to get more information  about the tuitions and fees.

The double degree program is a special cooperative  program between  
  PUC-Rio and other institutions (see the list of  institution on the website).  
  The students who have concluded the first two years  at his/her home  
institution can spend two years studying at PUC-Rio  and obtain the two  degrees: one from PUC-Rio and one from his/her home institution.
The international students who participate in this  program are considered to be a part of the exchange program and are subject to  the same rules. Furthermore, as they will obtain a diploma from PUC-Rio, they  must provide the documents 
   listed below  and that they pursue a certain number of obligatory courses, in  particular, disciplines of religious culture (see the statement below).
1.
Documents. In order for the university to be able  to register the students and,  ultimately, give them a diploma, they must provide the following documents,  translated into Portuguese by an official translator.  
  The student should verify whether it is in their  best interest to do the translation  in his/her country or in Brazil.
Copy of official birth certificate
Certified copy of Bachelor's degree
Record of preparatory grades
Record of grades from the first two years at his/her  home university
Record of grades from the  Bachelor's degree
and from the last three years of the High School (Not necessary for French institutions)
2.
PUC is a  Catholic university. All of the students, independent of their religion, must  obtain a certain number of credits in qualified subjects such as religious  formation. It is impossible to receive a diploma from PUC without satisfying  this condition. It is important that the international students are aware of  this rule.
The following  is the list of mandatory religious culture subjects:
CRE1100 O Homem e o fenômeno religioso
CRE1160 Ética Profissional

IT IS IMPORTANT TO STRESS THAT THE STUDENT VISA MUST BE OBTAINED BEFORE THE ENTRANCE IN BRAZIL. A TOURIST VISA CAN NOT BE TRANSFERRED INTO A STUDENT VISA INSIDE THE COUNTRY.
International  students can study at PUC-RIO as Exchange Students, as Independent  Students or as  Extension Students. However, please  be aware that after your arrival in Brazil you are not able to change  your student status. For instance, if you are registered as an Independent  Student  you cannot change your  status to Extension Student or Exchange Student.
Remember that if you only come to  participate  in the Intensive Portuguese  Course, a student  visa is not required. However,  if you intend to study during the entire semester, the tourist visa is  not accepted, and   a student visa is therefore required.
Please read the Registration Procedures carefully.
IMPORTANT:
Please be aware that all payments (tuition, orientation fee, administrative cost, etc.) must be made in cash (Reais). Traveler's checks, credit cards, checks are not accepted.

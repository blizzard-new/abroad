Nomination of a Graduate Student 
A student who wants to enroll to one of AGU’s graduate schools (Master’s 
Program) must belong to a graduate school at the time of application. 
Graduate students may be accepted on a limited basis subject to approval 
from the graduate school based on the student’s academic objectives. 
 
Some Graduate Schools require a high level of Japanese language proficiency 
(JLPT N1 level). A few Graduate School s, such as International Politics, 
Economics & Communication, do not require Japanese language proficiency. 
Please contact the International Center before nominating a graduate 
student. 
NOTE: A graduate student of our partner institutions can belong to AGU’s undergraduate 
program. However, they are NOT allowed to take courses for graduate students  in this 
case. 
Nomination of a student who receive s 
reasonable accommodations or who 
takes medications regularly for pre -
existing conditions 
If the nominated  student is receiving reasonable accommodations at their 
home university, such as special support or extended exam time, please be 
sure to contact us, to the extent possible, before submitting the nomination . 
The International Center is working with the S upport Center for Students 
with Disabilities for their support during their study abroad period. Please 
note that we may be un able to provide the same reasonable 
accommodations as those provided at your institution. 
 
Please note that if a student is taking  regular medication, which is very 
important for maintaining his/her physical and mental health, and the same 
medication is unavailable in Japan, we may decline to accept the student.

Health Insurance and Travel Insurance 
1) Japanese National Health Insurance (NHI) is compulsory  (approx. 
JPY2,000/m. Varies according to the city/ward where the  student 
resides). Must apply on arrival, at the local city/ward office. 
2) Besides the above, students must arrange appropriate overseas travel 
insurance in their home country, which should cover their study period 
in Japan. 
International Commons 
(at Aoyama Campus) 
The “International Commons,” located at Aoyama Campus, has a Chat Room, 
an English counseling service, as well as a lounge space where international 
students and local students can get together to talk, play games, etc. Various 
social and cultural events are also held there. 
University Fitness Center 
The membership fee is JPY3,000/per academic year. Students must 
undertake a Fitness Center orientation and university health check-up to 
use the center.

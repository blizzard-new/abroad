Health Insurance and Other Insurances
Everyone registered in Germany has to have valid health insurance. If you are an international visiting scholar, doctoral candidate, or international researcher employed on a contract at TU Berlin, you need health insurance cover. As a student, you will need proof of valid health insurance or an exemption, for example when applying or enrolling.
As an international visiting scholar, you and any accompanying family members must be insured with a health insurance provider
for the entire duration of your stay in Germany
. You will also need proof of health insurance to obtain a residence permit.
Your health insurance must at least cover medical treatment in the event of acute illness and accidents
. If your health insurance from your home country does not provide for this, you can take out additional health insurance in Germany.
In Germany, you can choose between two types of health insurance: state/statutory health insurance and private health insurance.
State/statutory health insurance
With employment contract: For (international) TU Berlin employees subject to social security contributions, incl. family insurance
Since March 1, 2024: Voluntary insurance for mobile researchers (Section 18d of the Residence Act) on a scholarship or who are self-financing, incl. family insurance. Voluntary health insurance must be declared within three months of taking up residence in Germany. Voluntary membership in the statutory health insurance scheme is subject to compulsory insurance in the social long-term care insurance scheme in accordance with Section 20(3) of the 11th Book of the Social Security Code (Sozialgesetzbuch).

Private health insurance
If you have an employment contract, you can take out either statutory or private health insurance.
If you are financing your stay in Germany yourself or are receiving a scholarship and your stay is less than 12 months, your only option is private health insurance.
Please note:
To apply for a residence permit, you need proof of a secure livelihood, which also includes proof of sufficient health insurance cover. If you have statutory health insurance, you are sufficiently insured. If you have private health insurance, you need to check the type and scope of your health insurance. You can find more information on this in the
information sheet from the Berlin Foreigners' Registration Office.
When do you not need German insurance?
For 90-day stays, travel health insurance with cover of 30,000 euros and validity for all Schengen states is sufficient.
As an EU citizen: If you are not gainfully employed in Germany and continue to pay health insurance contributions in your home country. For shorter stays, it is advisable to ask your health insurance provider for the
European Health Insurance Card
. Please note that this only covers emergencies. For extended cover, please apply for the European S1 certificate of entitlement to healthcare from your health insurance provider in your home country. Further information on special conditions for EU citizens can be found on the competition-neutral information platform
www.eu-patienten.de
.
Persons from non-EU countries who are insured in their home country for insurance cases in Germany must prove that their health insurance provides the same cover as German health insurance. As this is difficult to prove, we always recommend taking out additional insurance with a German insurance company to be on the safe side.
In Germany, anyone can be held liable for unintentionally causing damage to third parties. Parents are liable for their children. It is therefore usual to take out private (family) liability insurance.
Please note that TU Berlin does not generally take out liability insurance for its members.
All members of TU Berlin are insured by the Unfallkasse Berlin in the event of accidents
during their work at TU Berlin
. In the event of accidents, insurance cover is also provided by the Unfallkasse Berlin for activities undertaken at TU Berlin by persons who are not members of the University, provided these persons can be considered part of the organizational operations of TU Berlin for the time they are at the University. This includes shorter periods of time, even just one day, for example, in the case of a lecture given by a visiting academic.

Insurance is a legal requirement in Germany and you need to provide proof of
German
statutory health insurance
or
proof of exemption
before enrolling at a university.
Proof in both cases must be provided to TU Berlin in electronic format by a statutory health insurer. You can find further information
here
.
For students from abroad, this means either taking out statutory German health insurance or providing proof of exemption from the requirement to do so, for instance if you are covered by insurance in your own country (e.g.
EHIC
,
German-Turkish social insurance agreement
, private insurance) or if you have private insurance in Germany.
In other words, if you already have insurance in your home country which is also valid in Germany, you can apply to one of the German statutory health insurers for
an
exemption
. The same applies for students who have private health insurance in Germany. You can use the confirmation of exemption when enrolling.
Please note, however, that you generally cannot then take out statutory insurance while studying.
Please carefully read the
information about health insurance provided by the Foreigners' Registration Office (Ausländerbehörde)
before applying for health insurance as a student at TU Berlin.

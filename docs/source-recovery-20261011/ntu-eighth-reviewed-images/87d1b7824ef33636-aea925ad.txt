Detailed and Program Specific Application Information
Erasmus+ Student Mobility and ENHANCE Thematic Paths
To enroll in Erasmus exchange studies at RWTH Aachen University, your home university must have an
Erasmus+ partnership agreement
with RWTH. Before applying through the
Incoming Students Online Portal,
all Erasmus+ Student Mobility applicants must first be nominated by their home university. These guidelines also apply to ENHANCE Thematic Paths.
Required documents:
Online Learning Agreement listing your chosen courses, signed by both you and your home university coordinator. For additional details on course selection, please visit our
Useful Information for Course Selection
page.
Official language certificate
at the B1.1 or B2.1 level in either German or English, depending on your course and faculty selection
Official transcript of academic records
If you plan to pursue an internship, research project, or thesis under the supervision of RWTH Aachen University, please submit a signed
confirmation from your academic supervisor
. Be sure to review our guidelines for
Thesis Registration for Incoming Students
.
Application deadlines:
Winter Semester (October 1 to March 31): Application are accepted from April 1 to May 31*
Summer Semester (April 1 to September 30): Application are accepted from November 1 to December 31*
* Please note:
For the Faculty of Medicine, the Application Deadline is May 31 for both semesters.
Due to extended Visa processing times, students from Turkish, Chinese, and Indian universities must complete their application one month prior to the standard deadline.
Erasmus+ Traineeship
Participants in the
ERASMUS+ Traineeship
need to find a supervisor on their own. For tips on how to do this, please see our leaflet
How to find a supervisor
. Once you have found a supervisor, please start the application process at least two months before planned start of your stay by applying via
Incoming Students Online Portal
. Please also read our guidelines on
Thesis Registration for Incoming Students
carefully.
Required documents:
Signed
Confirmation of supervision
Proof of enrollment at your home university (such as a certificate of enrollment)
For Erasmus+ trainees: a
Learning Agreement
, submitted either as a digital form provided by your home university and signed by your RWTH supervisor, or through the Erasmus Traineeship Online Learning Agreement. If you use the online version, please enter your supervisor’s name and email address in the Receiving Contact field.
* If you plan to carry out a research project, write your thesis, or complete an internship at Forschungszentrum Jülich or a Fraunhofer Institute and wish to be enrolled at RWTH during your stay, you must be supervised by an RWTH professor.
Important: If you require a visa, please apply for a student visa, not an internship or traineeship visa. Internship and traineeship visas are intended for placements in industry and are not appropriate for Erasmus+ trainees. Although you will be completing an internship at a university institute, the primary purpose of your stay at RWTH is your studies.

nominated

Learning Agreement
signed by you and also the coordinator at your home university. Please also see more information on

Language certificate

Transcript of Records
If you intend to complete an internship, project or thesis work under the supervision of RWTH Aachen, please include a signed
confirmation from your academic supervisor
. Please also read our guidelines on
Thesis Registration for Incoming Students
carefully.
Application deadlines:
Winter Semester (October 1 to March 31) Application open from April 1 to May 31*
Summer Semester (April 1 to September 30) Application open from November 1 to December 31*
* Please note:
For Faculty of Medicine the Application Deadline is May 31 for both semesters.
Due to long Visa procedures students from Turkish, Chinese, and Indian universities need to finish their application one month before the deadline.
Free Movers
From the Winter Semester 2023/24 onwards, we no longer accept Free Mover applications due to limited capacity. If you have chosen RWTH as your prospective host university, but your home university does not have a partnership agreement with RWTH, your home university’s outgoing coordinator may contact the
Incoming Student Services Team
to inquire whether admission is possible. In this case, it is not helpful for students to contact the Incoming Student Services Team directly.
Visiting Project Students
Visiting Project Students need to find a supervisor* independently. Please see tips in
How to find a supervisor
. Once you have found a supervisor, please start the application process at least 2 months before planned start of your stay by applying on
Incoming Students Online Portal
. Please also read our guidelines on
Thesis Registration for Incoming Students
carefully.
Required Documents:
Confirmation of supervision
Evidence of student status at home university, such as an enrollment certificate
Erasmus+ trainees:
Learning Agreement
for Traineeships, provided by your home university and signed by your RWTH supervisor
Important: If you need to apply for a visa, make sure to apply for student visa and not internship or traineeship visa. The latter one is intended for internship and traineeships in the industry and is not appropriate for Visiting Project Students. Even though you will be doing an internship at an institute, the primary reason for your stay at RWTH is your studies.
* Students, who are going to conduct a project, a thesis work or an internship at Forschungszentrum Jülich or at the Fraunhofer-Institute, must be supervised by a RWTH professor in case they want to be enrolled during their say at RWTH.
ENHANCE Virtual Mobility
Through the
ENHANCE Alliance
, students have the opportunity to attend one or more courses at RWTH entirely online. We welcome all students from ENHANCE universities interested in exploring this format.
To view our current ENHANCE online offerings, please visit the
ENHANCE Course Catalogue
. Important: Please select courses corresponding to your current level of study. Bachelor-level students are not eligible to take courses offered to Master-level students.
Registration Deadlines
Winter Semester (October 1 to March 31): Registration is open  August 1 through 31
Summer Semester (April 1 to September 30): Registration is open February 1 through 28
How to Register & Enroll
Create an account and register via the
Incoming Students Online Portal
within the deadlines listed above. Fill out the form “Application: Non-Exchange Incoming Students”  and choose the Program “ENHANCE Virtual Mobility.” Please consult our
Guidelines for Registration
for step-by-step instructions on how to complete the application based on your selected courses.
Upon successful registration, you will receive an official Admission Letter and a guide titled “How to enroll.”
Required Documents:
• Certificate of Enrollment at an ENHANCE Partner university
• Copy of passport
Enrollment is only possible with a valid Admission Letter.
Enrollment Deadlines:
•  Winter Semester: August 1 – September 20
• Summer Semester: February 1 – March 20
After successful enrollment, please register for your ENHANCE Online Course via RWTHonline.
Please note: If your home university is not a member of the
ENHANCE Alliance
or does not even have a formal partnership agreement with RWTH, please contact the
Incoming Student Services Team
to inquire whether admission for Virtual Mobility is possible.

Research Internships and Projects
RWTH Aachen University offers international students a variety of research internships and the opportunity to complete their final thesis at RWTH. Please note that students completing their final thesis at RWTH will not receive a degree from RWTH Aachen University. Students wishing to complete a research project or write their thesis at RWTH outside of a listed mobility program can do so through programs such as
Erasmus+ Traineeship
or a

T.I.M.E and Other Double Degree Programs
Students enrolled in an RWTH Aachen University double degree program will complete part of their Master’s studies in Aachen. Upon successful completion, they will earn a Master’s degree from RWTH and the degree from their home institution.To see if your institution has a double degree agreement with RWTH, please check the designated
website
.
The application process begins at your home university. Contact your outgoing coordinator for detailed information. Once your home university nominates you for the double degree program at RWTH, proceed with the following steps to register:
Register through the
Incoming Online Portal
.
Upload the required application documents to the online registration form.
Required documents include:
Transcript of Records (an overview of your academic achievements at your home university)
Letter of motivation (one to two pages)
CV (one to two pages)

Electrical Engineering and Information Technology

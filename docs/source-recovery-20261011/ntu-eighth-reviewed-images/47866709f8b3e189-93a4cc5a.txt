KAIST is South Korea's first research-oriented science and engineering university, located in Daedeok Innopolis, 
Daejeon — a national hub for science and technology research about 150 km south of Seoul. 
Campus Locations 
 
Main Campus (Daejeon) 
KAIST's principal campus. Exchange students are assigned dormitory housing 
here, and the vast majority of courses, orientation, and campus events take 
place on this campus. 
* Exchange students affiliated with the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI are assigned to dormitories on the Daejeon Campus, 
just like other exchange students. Most courses in this graduate school are conducted in a hybrid format.  
* Courses for the Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility are held at both the Munji Campus (approximately 15 –20 minutes 
away by shuttle) and the Main Campus; dormitory housing for these students is assigned at the Main Campus.  
Eligibility 
 
GPA requirement 3.0 or better on a 4.3 scale, or equivalent 
Minimum study completed Undergraduate (Bachelor): minimum 1 year of full study at a partner university 
Graduate (Master): minimum 1 semester of full study at a partner university 
Language of instruction English (90% or more of courses are conducted in English) 
English language requirement 
TOEFL iBT 80 (0-120 scale) or 4.0 (1-6 scale) / IELTS 6.5 / TOEIC 720 / B2 or 
equivalent, taken within 2 years of nomination date. Waived for students from 
institutions where English is the medium of instruction. 
Level of study As per bilateral exchange agreement (undergraduate or graduate)

Application Procedure 
 
① Nomination confirmed. Once the nomination has been accepted, home institutions should inform 
their students to proceed with online registration at https://io.kaist.ac.kr/exchange.do. 
② Online registration (for applicants). Applicants must receive a nomination code before signing up; 
codes are issued only to coordinators who have submitted the nomination form and should be shared with 
nominated students only. 
● Create an account using a valid personal e-mail address (institutional e-mail addresses are not 
accepted; Gmail is fine. If you use an institutional (school) e-mail address, authentication codes for 
KAIST portal login may sometimes fail to arrive later on; using a personal gmail address is 
recommended). 
● Complete the online application form and upload the required documents (see below). 
○ Original (Official) Transcripts; All in English 
○ English Language Proficiency Test Result 
○ Copy of Valid Passport (Front page, not cover). Expired passport is not accepted. 
○ 3cm x 4cm Official Photo (not an ID card photo)

Courses and Grading 
 
Course catalogue KAIST course information system 
Academic Bulletin KAIST Academic Bulletin 
Full course load Undergraduate: max. 8 courses (24 credits) — Graduate: max. 4 courses (12 
credits) 
Minimum course load Undergraduate: min. 4 courses (12 credits) — Graduate: min. 3 courses (9 
credits). Lower loads possible if permitted by home institution. 
Restricted courses/programs 
Graduate School of Medical Science and Engineering and College of Business 
(Seoul Campus) are not open to exchange students. 
Students may not mix undergraduate and graduate level courses, except 
courses marked "Y" (mutually recognized). 
Graduate students may take an undergraduate course only as an exception, 
with advisor and department chair approval (not guaranteed). 
Course list availability Uploaded 2 months before semester start; registration opens 1–2 months 
before semester start. 
Minimum credit policy 
KAIST does not penalize exchange students for not meeting the minimum 
credit load stated above. Course registration should simply follow the 
student's home institution's regulations. 
Course changes after 
application 
It is common and acceptable for the courses selected during the online 
application to differ from the courses actually taken after arrival at KAIST. 
* We encourage exchange students to take courses required for their graduation at their home university, in preparation for any 
changes in circumstances at KAIST. 
* The study plan may be based on the previous year's course list as a draft. Final course registration takes place approximately 
one month before the semester begins, once the finalized course offerings are available.

Housing - Daejeon Campus 
 
Accommodation 
KAIST applies for on-campus dormitory housing for all exchange students 
(double/triple occupancy), furnished with bed, closet, desk, chair, one pillow 
and one blanket. Shared restrooms, showers, laundry, and lobby per floor. A 
dormitory application survey will be conducted before the start of the 
semester. 
Cost range Approximately USD 550 – 900 per semester 
Website https://kds.kaist.ac.kr/main.do?lang=en 
 
 
Health Check and Insurance 
 
Health insurance requirement 
Students on a D-2 visa must subscribe to National Health Insurance (NHI). As 
enrollment takes over a month, students should arrange traveler's insurance 
covering at least their first month in Korea. 
Dormitory health check 
requirement 
A completed Health Check Form is required to move into KAIST dormitory 
housing. The form must show MMR vaccination status and Tuberculosis 
screening results (IGRA or chest X-ray). Results are only valid if dated within 3 
months prior to move-in. 
 
Please check your MMR vaccination status in advance. Since two weeks 
are required between the 1st and 2nd MMR doses, Please prepare your 
vaccination record as soon as you receive your acceptance, or even 
earlier if possible — dormitory assignment is only possible if this is 
submitted on time. 
 
 
 
Visa Requirements 
 
All non-Korean applicants need a visa to study in Korea. To obtain one, apply at the embassy or consulate 
of the Republic of Korea in the home country, using the Certificate of Admission issued by KAIST. 
Please note: entering Korea without a visa may result in cancellation of your study, and applying for a visa 
after arrival requires significant additional cost and documentation.

Academic Transcripts 
 
Grades are announced in early July for the Spring semester and early January for the Fall semester. 
Exchange students may download and print their own official English transcript free of charge within one 
month following the grade announcement, and submit it directly to their home university. A certificate-
verifiable electronic transcript is also available for download for a small fee.

HOW TO APPLY
Applying for admission
Aarhus University welcomes applications from nominated students from partner universities. Please contact the International Office or exchange coordinator at your home university to find out if there is an agreement with Aarhus University, if you are eligible to apply, and to obtain information regarding courses and selection procedure.
Once you have been nominated by your home university, you will receive an email from Aarhus University inviting you to activate your AU account and proceed with the exchange application.
Only nominated students will be able to access the application module.

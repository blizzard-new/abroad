About the Campus
Campus Santa Fe opened in August 2001. Today, it hosts over 3,600 students across High School, Undergraduate, and Graduate programs, offering a vibrant and diverse learning community.

About the City
Welcome to the most cosmopolitan city
Mexico City is a truly one of the most Cosmopolitan cities in the world!
Full of contrasts that range from the most traditional aspects of Mexican culture to the most modern of any capital.
The city offers its visitors a very rich intercultural environment, as well as museums for all types of interest, fun nightlife, variety of food, a very pleasant climate, and a great balance between traditional and modern life.
Thanks to the fact that Mexico City is in the center of the country, it is the best point to travel to other tourist centers in the country, either by bus or plane.

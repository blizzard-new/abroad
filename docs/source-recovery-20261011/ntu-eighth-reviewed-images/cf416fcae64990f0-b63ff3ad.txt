How to Apply
Application Requirements
Students will receive an email with a link to the UNC application once their home institution nominates them.
All applications are completed online and include several parts specific to each program. Each required item will appear on your application account page. Follow all instructions carefully, as some items may include follow-up steps.
Below is a list of items required for most applications. Some programs may require additional materials, which will be noted in the application.
Students Rights & Responsibilities and UNC Honor Code:
These items discuss important UNC rules, regulations, and policies you are required to follow as part of your program at UNC
English Language Proficiency:
If English is not your native language, you will need to provide copies of your scores on the TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge English First test B2 on the CEFR, or Duolingo tests.
Essay:
Respond to the prompt to tell us about your plans to study at UNC Chapel Hill.
Transcript:
Upload a copy of your official transcript from your home institution.
(GVSP only)
Letter of Reference:
Applicants to the GVSP must submit two letters of reference on official university letterhead from instructors or professors who are familiar with your academic work.
When you finish all required items, click
Submit.
Please complete all requirements before the deadline to be considered.
Please note: No items need to be physically mailed to the UNC Study Abroad Office. All required items are submitted in this system through Slate.

Other English proficiency guidelines
We will accept a letter from the student’s home institution that certifies that the student’s level of English proficiency meets the requirements based on prior studies in an English-taught curriculum (secondary school or university), or a good passing grade in an English language training course specifically for exchange applicants.

Application Review and Notification
All applications will be reviewed after the deadline closes. Students will receive an acceptance email within two weeks of the application deadline with next steps of action. This email is confirmation for acceptance and students will not need to do anything to confirm.
In the meantime, please review the Academics, Planning your Arrival, and Life on Campus and in Chapel Hill pages to find out more about your experience at UNC Chapel Hill!

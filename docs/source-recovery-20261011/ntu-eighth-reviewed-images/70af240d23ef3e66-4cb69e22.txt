Student Health Insurance Requirement
The UNC Board of Regents requires most students enrolled in UNC System Colleges and Universities to have health insurance. This requirement applies to students who meet the following criteria:
Enrolled in an on-campus degree seeking program
Undergraduate students enrolled in 6 or more credit hours in an on-campus degree seeking program
Graduate students enrolled in 1 or more credit hours in an on-campus degree seeking program
Eligible to pay the UNCG Student Health Fee
*PLEASE NOTE: Students enrolled in online programs are not eligible for student insurance and not required to meet the insurance requirement.
Students who meet that criteria must take one of the following steps by September 10, 2026
:
Enroll
in the Student Blue Health Insurance Plan
Obtain a
waiver
by demonstrating proof of existing health insurance coverage
*PLEASE NOTE: Students not completing one of the required steps by September 10 will be default enrolled in the Student Blue Health Plan.

Enrolling in student Blue
The UNC System offers a student health insurance plan that covers most health care expenses not included in the UNCG Health Fee. This plan is called Student Blue and is administered by Blue Cross / Blue Shield of North Carolina.
How to Enroll

Student Blue Portal

Waiving out of Student Blue
Students who have existing health insurance coverage may opt out of the Student Blue Insurance Plan by requesting a waiver. Waiver requests can be submitted through the
Student Blue Portal
. Students will receive an email notification informing them when their waiver has been approved or denied.
*PLEASE NOTE: If a waiver is not granted by September 10, students will automatically be enrolled in the Student Blue Health Plan.
Waiver Requirements
Domestic Students
:
To request a waiver, your health insurance plan must meeter the following requirements:
be effective (active) by September 1 for the fall semester or by February 1 for the spring semester
must cover you for the entire semester you are waiving and
must constitute creditable coverage
Creditable coverage is defined as health insurance coverage under any of the following:
a group health plan
individual health insurance
student health insurance
Medicare
Medicaid (Medicaid Family Planning Only is NOT considered Creditable Coverage for waiving Student Blue)
CHAMPUS and TRICARE
the Federal Employees Health Benefits Program;
Indian Health Service
the Peace Corps
Public Health Plan (any plan established or maintained by a State, the U.S. government, a foreign country)
Children’s Health Insurance Program (CHIP) or
a state health insurance high risk pool
Waiver Appeals
Students who miss the waiver deadline or receive a denied waiver have a two‑week appeal window. During this period, the Student Blue portal will remain open for both enrollments and waiver submissions. Students who do not submit an appeal by the deadline will remain default‑enrolled in the Student Blue plan. Once the appeal period ends, no additional enrollment or waiver appeals will be accepted for that semester.

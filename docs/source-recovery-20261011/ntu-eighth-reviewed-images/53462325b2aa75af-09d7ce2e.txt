Immunizations for Exchange Students
All exchange students must comply with the university and North Carolina immunization requirements.
Immunization Records
Prior to arrival on campus, all exchange students must submit their immunization records. Students must logon to Healthy Pack Portal to enter in their dates and upload supporting documents.
Read more about requirements
TB Testing
Students from a high-risk country must have Tuberculin PDD testing completed once they arrive on campus. Appointments will be scheduled for students during Orientation.

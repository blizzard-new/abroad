Courses Offered
MSU offers 250+ areas of study, most of 
which are open to exchange students without restriction. A list of
majors and minors, as well as the course catalog, can be found
under the Academics tab on the MSU website.
Restrictions
Architecture, graphic design, fine arts, film, and photography
classes are extremely limited for exchange students. Most of these
classes are only open to students who are majors in these subjects
at their home universities and require students to present a
portfolio of completed work. Classes offered by the College of
Nursing are not open to exchange students.

Application Information
How To Apply
Your host university coordinator must send a nomination
email to incoming@montana.edu. When your nomination is
received we will send application instructions and a link to our
online application.
Nomination Deadlines

March 10

Accommodation
Most exchange students live in campus housing, though exchange students from partner schools are
not required to live on campus and may find their own accommodations in the area. However, please
be aware that finding off-campus housing, especially for only one semester, can be challenging. We are
usually able to reserve campus housing for all exchange students.
Immigration
All international students will be required to obtain a J-1 visa through the U.S. government. Following
acceptance, our office will supply a DS-2019 before the student applies for the J-1 visa.

Housing
1. Basic Information
Types of Accommodation
Nagoya University offers the following types of accommodation. No application is necessary. Unless correspondence to the contrary has been exchanged, all NUPACE students will live in one of these university dormitories. The results of the room allocation will be announced in late August.
Please note that they do not have the option of choosing in which residence they will reside.
Mixed Residences for Japanese and International Students
International Ohmeikan
International Residence Daiko
Residences Exclusively for International Students
International Residence Yamate (North)
International Residence Yamate (South)
Payment of Dormitory Fees
Residents must pay
monthly rent in full
, even if residing in university accommodation for only one day of a particular month.
      Rent cannot be calculated on a per day basis.
      Please also note that monthly residential fees (including the bedding lease fee)
will NOT be waived or refunded
during a period of absence.
Access to Higashiyama Campus
International Residence Daiko is approximately 20 minutes from the Higashiyama Campus by subway and on foot.
International Ohmeikan and International Residence Yamate are both located about 10 minutes on foot from the Higashiyama Campus.
For more details, please refer to the accommodation overviews below.
Meals
Currently, there are no university-managed canteens in any of the residences and, as such, students should bear in mind that they will have to cater for themselves or eat out.
Common Rules
Entry to the dormitories by non-residents, including family and friends, is strictly prohibited.

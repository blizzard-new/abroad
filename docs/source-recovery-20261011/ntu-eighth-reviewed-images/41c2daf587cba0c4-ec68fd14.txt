Grading and Credit Approval
A transcript will be issued to special auditors in this educational program indicating creditable courses, grades and number of credits.
Credits will be awarded by the student's home university based on the transcript issued by Kanazawa University.
The decision as to whether or not to recognize them as the credits for degree acquisition (graduation or completion) is left to the university, etc., which sent the international student.
Transcripts will be sent by e-mail to the staff of your home university. The transcripts will be sent around May for those who complete in March and around November for those who complete in September.
Scholarship
KUEP students may receive a monthly scholarship of 80,000 yen from the Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO) for a few students only if this program is accepted by the JASSO. Students who wish to apply should select "Yes" in the "Do you wish to apply for the JASSO scholarship" section of the online application. The number of recipients is limited. However, the applications will not be accepted on the premise of receiving a JASSO scholarship.
In addition to the application qualifications of this program, scholarship applicants must meet all of the following requirements to be eligible for the grant.
Nationals of countries that have diplomatic relations with the Government of Japan. Students from Taiwan and Palestine are also eligible. Japanese nationals are not eligible.
Those who have excellent academic records at their home university and have good character.
Those who are in difficulties to study abroad solely at their own expense for financial reasons.
The total monthly amount of scholarships received from other organizations for study at Kanazawa University does not exceed 80,000 yen.
Dormitory
Dormitories for new international students coming to Japan will be arranged by Kanazawa University. However, if the number of applicants exceeds the number of available rooms in university dormitories, or if you wish to live in a private apartment instead of the dormitory arranged by Kanazawa University, you will have to find an apartment yourself through a real estate agency. In this case, the monthly rent for a one-room apartment will be 40,000-60,000 yen, and the deposit and key money will be 150,000-300,000 yen, as well as insurance and other fees. Japanese apartments are usually unfurnished. Our staff will contact you regarding dormitory application.

If you have any questions, the Newcomer Support Team  <ino@adm.kanazawa-u.jp> is here to help

Foreign nationals who wish to enter Japan for the purpose of studying abroad must obtain a College Student Visa from the Japanese Embassy or Consulate before entering Japan.
Kanazawa University The student's representative (the "College Student Visa") will apply for a "Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)" at the Japanese Immigration Bureau and send the issued "Certificate of Eligibility" to the student.
From October 2026, students admitted in October 2026 will be required to pay the "Service fee for Certificate of Eligibility (CoE)" (for international students only).
＊This fee is required for the procedures related to obtaining a Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) through Kanazawa University. International students who do not need a CoE are exempt from this fee.
＊This fee must be paid dutirng the pre-arrival procedures. Please check the information sent to you by the Newcomer Support Team on how to make the payment.
*After enrollment, the school may charge a prescribed fee as necessary to enhance living and study support for international students.
Students are required to apply for a College Student Visa by presenting a "Certificate of Eligibility" together with their passport at a Japanese diplomatic mission abroad.
This "Certificate of Eligibility" must also be presented at the time of entry into Japan, so please be sure to bring it with you.

National Health Insurance
National Health Insurance is Japan's medical insurance system, which all international students are required to join. You are required to pay a monthly premium of about 1,600 yen. The co-payment for medical expenses (treatment, hospitalization, etc.) in the event of illness or injury is only 30%.
For more information
Personal Accident Insurance for Students Pursuing Education and Research (Gakkensai)
All international students must join "Personal Accident Insurance for Students Pursuing Education and Research (Gakkensai)". The insurance fee is 1,000 yen (for one year). This is a compensation system for students who suffer physical disabilities as a result of accidents during educational and research activities at the Kanazawa University.
For more information
Comprehensive Insurance for Students Lives Coupled with Gakkensai (Inbound futai-gakuso)
All international students are required to join "Comprehensive Insurance for Students Lives Coupled with Gakkensai (Inbound futai-gfakuso)". The premium for one year is 1,860 yen (without medical treatment coverage) or 11,500 yen (with medical treatment coverage), and for six months is 1,310 yen (without medical treatment coverage) or 8,060 yen (with medical treatment coverage). This insurance covers injuries, illnesses, and liability to others that occur not only in regular university activities but also in daily life.

Hitotsubashi University is a national university, and is the only university in Japan to specialize exclusively 
in the humanities and social sciences. Since its foundation in 1875 as the oldest business school in modern 
Japan, Hitotsubashi has been at the forefront of Japan’s innovation. It has been a powerhouse for generations 
of Japan’s globally active business leaders as well as a research hub producing cutting- edge research in the 
social sciences, contributing to the global academic community. 
Hitotsubashi has a teaching staff of about 5 00, with approximately 4, 300 students enrolled on 
undergraduate courses and 1,800 on postgraduate courses. There are about 900 international students, and 
we also welcome about 170 researchers from around the world each year (as of 2023).

STUDENT EXCHANGE AT HITOTSUBASHI   
Students from our partner institutions who have been nominated as exchange students can apply to study 
at Hitotsubashi University for one of the following four periods: 
 
1. From the Autumn to the Winter quarter (September 2025 to January 2026) 
2. From the Autumn to the Summer quarter (September 2025 to July 2026) 
3. From the Spring to the Summer quarter (April 2026 to July 2026) 
4. From the Spring to the Winter quarter (April 2026 to January 2027) 
 
Exchange students must have completed two (2) years of undergraduate study (4 semesters/ 6 
trimesters/ 8 quarters)  or possess equivalent academic ability prior to commencing their exchange at 
Hitotsubashi University; however, this shall not apply to students from partner institutions if this is not specified 
in the relevant student exchange agreement: 
https://international.hit-u.ac.jp/en/pros/exchange/ 
 


 
 
REQUIREMENTS 
 
LANGUAGE REQUIREMENT 
Exchange Students are required to fulfill one of the following qualification criteria: 
 English: TOEFL (iBT) 79 / IELTS (academic module) 6.0 / CEFR B2 or equivalent 
 Japanese: JLPT Level 2, N2 or equivalent 
* Applicants from partner institutions in the U.S., Canada, U.K., Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Hong Kong are exempted 
from the English language requirements.   
** Applicants from partner institutions whose  programs are conducted entirely in English are exempt ed from the above language 
requirements with a letter of proof from the partner institution. 
GPA SCORE REQUIREMENT 
Exchange Students must have a minimum GPA of 2.7 or above on a 4.0 scale. 
* If applicants are from partner institutions in which the 4.0 scale does not apply to the grading system, their score must be converted 
to GPA on the 4.0 scale. 
 
 
 
 
STUDYING AT HITOTSUBASHI 
Hitotsubashi University emphasizes small-group class education, and one of the prominent characteristics 
of this is our seminar style approach. The seminar style approach keeps class sizes small and focuses on 
intense student-faculty interaction, providing rich course content. 
 
(1) Enroll in Specialized Courses/Seminars Conducted in Japanese or English 
 Courses/Seminars Conducted in Japanese 
Both postgraduate and undergraduate courses in Japanese are available. Students must possess 
a minimum language proficiency in Japanese of JLPT Level 2 or N2. 
 
 Courses Conducted in English 
Both postgraduate and undergraduate courses i n English are available; however, most courses 
conducted in English are undergraduate-level courses, and only a limited number of postgraduate-
level courses are available in English.  
 
(2) Enroll in Japanese Language Courses 
Hitotsubashi University offers a wide variety of Japanese Language Courses ranging from introductory to 
advanced levels. 
https://international.hit-u.ac.jp/en/courses/japanese/ 
 
(3) Research Under a Supervisor (*this option is only for postgraduate exchange students) 
Postgraduate exchange students can do their research under a supervisor who specializes in a field that 
matches their interest, if they apply during the application period and are accepted by the supervisor.

COURSE REGISTRATION 
At Hitotsubashi, each class session runs 105 minutes.  Each term, all exchange students , except 
postgraduate students who research under a supervisor,  shall register for courses with a total of at least six 
105-minute slots of class time per week. Typically, two- credit courses meet once a week for a 105- minute 
lecture over 14 weeks in the case of semester courses, or meet twice a week for a 105-minute lecture over 7 
weeks in the case of quarter courses. 
https://international.hit-u.ac.jp/en/pros/exchange/reg/ 
 
 
WORKLOAD HOURS 
In accordance with the Standards for Establishment of Universities, all Japanese universities have the 
same benchmark for workload hours. The calculation of workload hours for credit-bearing courses is as follows: 
1 credit = a workload of 45 hours; 2 credits = a workload of 90 hours.

HOUSING 
Hitotsubashi University has a residential hall for exchange students : Residence Hall Ikkyo- Ryo on our 
Kodaira campus. Exchange students are given priority for a single room in the residential hall, with use of a 
common area to be shared with residents on the same floor. However, not all exchange students may be 
guaranteed a room due to limited availability. We ar e unable to consider your personal preference about the 
residential hall in which you wish to live when assigning rooms. For more details of housing costs, please refer 
to the websites below: 
 
Residence Hall Ikkyo-Ryo: https://international.hit-u.ac.jp/en/curr/accom/kodaira/

 Japanese Government Scholarship (Monbukagakusho: MEXT)  
In order to apply for the MEXT Japanese Studies Students Scholarship, applicants must meet all of 
the following eligibility requirements: 
 1) Start his/her period of study at HU in September 
 2) Be a one academic year exchange student 
 3) Possess a high level of Japanese language proficiency (JLPT Level 2, N2 or higher). 
 Japan Student Services Organization Scholarship (JASSO) 
 
 
APPLICATIONS 
Applicants wishing to apply for student exchange must be nominated by their home institutions first. Direct 
applications from students with no nomination by their home institutions will not be accepted.  Once the 
nomination is completed, applicants and/or their home institutions should submit the application forms  by the 
designated deadline. If nomination is not received on time without prior consultation, it will not be accepted 
under any circumstances.

Language and Testing Requirements
Students are required to submit personal, family,
and/or financial loans to prove they have sufficient
funds for living expenses to support their stay. The
DS-2019  (J-1 visa document) and supporting details
are emailed and a hard copy of the DS-2019 is mailed
abroad (at the student’s own cost). Additional fees
related to the visa will also need to be paid, including
a DHS SEVIS fee and the cost of the DS-160. Most
countries will require a personal interview at the U.S.
consulate and wait times for these interviews will
vary, depending on the location and time of year.
Review visa wait times here and information on pre
and post arrival, see here. Finally, be sure you
understand what is required to maintain your J-1 visa
here.

Course Selection and Scheduling
Students must be able to demonstrate completion of
all necessary pre-requisites to be enrolled in a
course. This most often is evaluated based on
successful completion on an academic transcript.
Areas this is particularly important, but not limited to
– Business, Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering,
Computer Science, and all lab Sciences. 
 
To ensure best chances of enrollment, students must
have syllabi prepared with necessary prerequisites at
the time they apply to study at Rutgers. Exchange
students are enrolled at the end of the registration
period, thus courses offered may not be open or
available for registration once they are eligible. 
 
Students should anticipate enrolling in a variety of
courses during their time of study at Rutgers. A
typical course load would be 2 to 3 courses in a
student’s major or intended area of study, with 2 to 3
courses that support a student’s general studies or
serve as degree requirements. 
 
Students should NOT plan to take 4+ courses in their
major, primary field of study, or single discipline. No
course listed in the Schedule of Classes is guaranteed
to be offered each semester, nor do we guarantee
space/availability at the time of exchange student
enrollment.

Exchange Students must take a minimum of 12
credits per semester. The maximum number of
credits per semester is 18.   
**Internships and Independent Studies
Internships and Independent Study courses are
only for Academic Year exchange students
during the Spring semester. 
It is the student’s responsibility to find an
internship placement. Internship placements
are not guaranteed and the Rutgers Global
Office cannot assist with finding an internship.
Students who want to participate in an
internship are required to work closely with
Carissa McCarthy in the ISSS office for
immigration purposes.  

On-campus Housing is guaranteed and required.
Students coming to Rutgers must live on-campus. No
exceptions will be made.  
Students are usually housed in University Center at
Easton Avenue apartments. In the university
apartments, students will have access to shared
kitchen facilities and be able to remain on campus
during breaks and holidays.

Health Insurance
All incoming exchange students are required to carry
adequate health insurance. Rutgers University offers
health insurance and will automatically bill you for
this insurance, unless you show proof of other
coverage that is equal to Rutgers insurance coverage.  
A guide to minimum insurance requirements at
Rutgers can be found here, but students will not be
considered exempt until their policy has been
reviewed by Rutgers. Prior to arrival, students will
have the opportunity to complete an Insurance
Waiver form and to submit a copy of their current
coverage. 
Disability Accomodations and Special
Services
At Rutgers, students have access and are encouraged
to use the Office of Disability Services if needed. 
The ODS team will work with each student
individually in order to develop an effective and
comprehensive plan for appropriate services and
reasonable accommodations.

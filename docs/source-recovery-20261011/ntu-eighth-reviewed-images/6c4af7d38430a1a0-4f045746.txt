ENGLISH REQUIREMENTS
The language of instruction at UVic is English.  The ICS Exchange Program does not require incoming
exchange students to provide proof of satisfying UVic’s English Language requirement. Partners are
trusted to select and nominate students whose proficiency in English is suitable for a university in which
courses are taught in English. For reference, current UVic English requirements are TOEFL iBT 90 (No
section less than 20) OR IELTS 6.5 (No band less than 6.0).
Non-English majors planning to take an English course should take the Academic Writing Self Placement
Test. Select: 1) Academic Writing Requirement (AWR), 2) Selecting an AWR-designated course, 3) self-
placement questionnaire. For-credit English courses are available for incoming exchange students with
permission from the English Department.
A Business English and Communications course
(COM 206) may be open to English as an Additional
Language students if there is space available.
The UVic English Language Centre offers English
courses for an additional fee in the summer before
the September exchange term. The ELC programs
include tests that meet the UVic admission criteria.
Once students complete the ELC program
successfully, they are ready to take courses at UVic.
The UVic Library offers numerous resources to
support student success, the The Academic Skills
Centre (ASC) is here to help with  writing, reading and
speaking needs, as well as academic integrity.

Residence Services at UVic offers on-campus dormitory rooms or cluster units (private bedroom with

Applying for On-Campus Housing
Once students have been accepted into the ICS Exchange Program, they must:
1.Receive their UVic student number (which begins with V00), and
2.Create a NetLink ID
After completing these steps, students are eligible to apply for on-campus housing through the UVic
Housing Portal.
⚠  Important application dates and deadlines are available on the Residence Services website. UVic on-
campus residence spaces are limited and competitive. Placement is not guaranteed for all applicants.
Cancellation Fee: On-campus accommodation is limited, therefore cancellation fees apply.
See the Refund and Cancellation Policies in the Residence Contract for more information

Priority Consideration
Students with specialized housing requirements or who require priority admittance to housing outside
of current admissions practices or eligibility criteria, based on restrictions or limitations resulting from
disability, chronic health conditions, dietary restrictions, or other considerations, are asked to submit a
Priority Consideration Request (PCR) as part of their housing application. 
Minimum Course Registration Requirement for on-campus housing
Exchange students living in on-campus housing must be enrolled in a minimum of 4.5 UVic units per term.

Arriving to UVic
Students with confirmed room bookings will be emailed their scheduled arrival date. Do not book your
flights until your arrival date has been confirmed by Residence Services. For further information,
contact Residence Services. 
Early Arrivals are only approved for Term 1 (Sept-Dec)
and must be received in writing through the online
application and within the request deadlines. Early arrivals
are not permitted for Term 2 (Jan-April), and students are
able to arrive only on the date given to them by Residence
Services (via their room assignment email). Please check the
Residence Services website's Arriving Early section in July for
information on how to apply for an early arrival for Term 1
(Sept-Dec). 
Early Move-in
Only students who are registered for UVic’s International
Student Welcome event in late August will be eligible to
apply for early arrival to on-campus housing. Additional
fees apply. Only students who have been approved for an
Early Arrival are able to move-in prior to their scheduled
check-in date. 
Additional Information 
Students who apply for both term sessions
will be prioritized in the room assignment
process. Residence Services sends room
offers from May to late August via email.
Once you have your student number and
can log-in to your Online Tools, ensure that
your My Page account has your current
email address and that you are checking
your emails daily. 
Leaving UVic
You are required to move out of your
residence 24 hours after the end of the
final exam period. Do not book your
return flight home until you have
confirmed your residence departure
date. Otherwise you will be required to
find alternative housing while waiting
for your scheduled flight home.

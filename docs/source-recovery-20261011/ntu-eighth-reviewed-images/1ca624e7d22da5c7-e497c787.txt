 
 
 
English Language Proficiency Requirements  
 
Student exchange applicants applying to the CSU Systemwide Student Exchange Program  who have not attended an 
education institution at the secondary level or beyond where English is the principal language of instruction for at least 
three years  are required to take  an English proficiency test . This include s applicants studying at Francophone 
institutions in Canada and students from non-English speaking countries who are studying at institutions in English 
speaking countries as an international student. Note that some campuses may have more stringent requirements. 
 
The tests accepted for this program are: 
• ETS TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) . Note that Institutional TOEFL scores are not accepted at 
all campuses. 
• IELTS (International English Language Testing System) 
• Duolingo. The following campuses do not accept Duolingo scores:  Bakersfield, Fullerton, and Maritime Academy.  
 
For further information, click on the website links above. Test scores are usually valid for two years only. Scores must 
not be older than two years prior to March 30 for fall term applicants and August 31 for January term applicants.  
 
A test score report copy must be submitted with the CSU Systemwide Student Exchange Application. Campuses may 
request original test score reports in order to be admitted to the campus. Campuses which may require original reports 
include Bakersfield, Chico, Dominguez Hills, Monterey Bay, Pomona, Sacramento, San Bernardino, San Diego, San Jose 
and Sonoma. (List is subject to change.) Campuses will advise applicants if an original test report is required.  
 
CSU campuses  establish minimum scores base d on campus -specific factors.  Minimum scores for applicants are  
provided below (subject to change). Exchange partners reserve the right to enforce higher TOEFL/IELTS requirements. 
To view campus minimum scores, refer to the most recent document “CSU Campus Costs and Requirements”.   
  
The following campuses accept the TOEFL “MyBest” Score for undergraduate study:   
• Bakersfield (61) 
• Channel Islands (61) 
• Chico (61) 
• Fresno (61) 
• Monterey Bay (61) 
• Northridge (61) 
• San Francisco (61) 
• San Diego (80) 
• San Luis Obispo (80 – Home Edition allowed) 
• Stanislaus (61) 
 
Postgraduate students are normally required to have a minimum score of 80 (ETS TOEFL internet-based) or 7.0 (IELTS) 
although some programs may require higher scores. Some campuses will require higher Duolingo test scores for postgraduate 
students including Dominguez Hills and Los Angeles.    
 
Test score requirements are subject to change.

How can I become an exchange student at TU Berlin?
You can only apply to be an exchange student at TU Berlin if there is a cooperation between TU Berlin and your own university.
You then have to be nominated for an exchange by your university by a specific date and submit your application via Mobility Online before the deadline expires.
You can find more information about applying
here

Do I need to speak German to study at TU Berlin?
Yes, as nearly all courses are taught in German!
As such, it is important to take German courses before your exchange at TU Berlin. Please note: ERASMUS students must demonstrate A2-level German when applying!
Does TU Berlin offer preparatory courses in German?
Yes, the Center for Modern Languages (
ZEMS
) offers a
preparatory German course
for exchange students a month before the semester starts.
ERASMUS students can only take part in this course at level B1 or above.
Overseas students may attend at a lower level.
How do I find accommodation?
Student accommodation and flats are in short supply in Berlin.
We therefore strongly advise you to look for accommodation as early as possible. You can either apply for a
place in a student dorm via studierendenWERK Berlin
or look for
alternative accommodation
.
When should I arrive in Berlin?
If you want to take part in a pre-semester German course, we recommend you arrive shortly before the information event for participants (at the start of March for summer semesters, and the start of September for winter semesters).
Otherwise you should aim to arrive just before the orientation events for the semester (at the start of April for summer semesters, and at the start of October for winter semesters).
This will give you time to take care of various administrative issues etc. and settle in Berlin and at TU Berlin as well as find accommodation if you still need to.

How much money do I need as an exchange student in Berlin?
This depends on your personal lifestyle. A rough
monthly guide
might look something like this:
Accommodation: approx. € 350-650
(statutory)
health insurance
: approx. € 120-130
Public transport is included in the
semester contribution
of approx. €250 per semester (studierendenWERK Berlin, student body  and
Deutschlandsemesterticket
- exchange/dual degree students are exempted from paying the administrative fee) for enrollment
Food: approx. € 150
Clothing: approx. € 60
Books: € 40
Other costs: € 40
Total costs: approx. € 900 per month
A good rough guideline is the
funding you need to provide proof of when applying for a visa
.
From January 2023, this will be € 934 per month.
Please ensure that you have sufficient funds to finance your stay abroad before traveling to Berlin.
Can exchange students apply for scholarships at TU Berlin?
Usually exchange students apply for scholarships at their home universities or scholarship providers in their home countries. As a rule, we are unfortunately not able to provide scholarships. See
here
for scholarship options for Germany.
However, a very limited number of Kontakt or
Erasmus KA171 scholarships
is available for students from certain overseas partner universities. Information regarding the Erasmus KA171 scholarship is provided by the respective home university.
Are exchange students permitted to work?
In general, this is possible but there are some rules. International students are permitted to work 120 days or 240 half days a year. You can find further information about working as a student
here
.
Do I need a visa to come to Germany?
That depends on your nationality. Students from certain countries need a visa others do not, e.g. EU citizens. You can find detailed information about visa requirements
here
.

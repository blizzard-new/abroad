Overview of Charges and Fees
wirestock
General Tuition fee
International students coming from outside of the EU to study at KIT are obliged to pay tuition fees in the amount of EUR 1,500.- per semester when newly enrolling for a degree program. When enrolling for a second degree program, tuition fees of EUR 650.- are charged per semester as of that date. Students who have enrolled in two or more degree programs at the same university or at several universities of the state (parallel studies) are obliged to pay tuition fees as of the start of the semester after successful completion of the first degree program.
Detailed information is provided by the
International Students Office
.
Administrative Charge
For the students-related administrative services of the universities, students have to pay a charge of EUR 80.- per semester (previously EUR 70.- per semester) as of the 2025 summer semester. This payment is supposed to cover expenses for facilities and services that are offered to administrate and support the students, but are not directly associated with higher education activities.
In case of de-registration within one month upon the start of the lecturing period, the administrative charge will be reimbursed upon submission of the corresponding request on the
Students Portal (Studierendenportal
). For information on the reimbursement of charges paid until the 2023/24 winter semester, contact the
Students Office (Studierendenservice)
.
Charge for the Studierendenwerk
At the moment, students are charged EUR 92.- for the services of Studierendenwerk Karlsruhe. This amount also includes a liability insurance for the students. Having paid this charge, students have the right to use the Studierendenwerk’s psychological help services (Psychologische Beratungsstelle, PBS).
In case of de-registration, reimbursement of the charge for the Studierendenwerk must be
applied for in writing with the Studierendenwerk Karlsruhe
subject to the deadlines given.
Charge for the Students Union
The Students Union or Student Government of KIT (Verfasste Studierendenschaft) is a statutory body under private law of KIT. Notwithstanding the responsibilities of KIT and Studierendenwerk Karlsruhe, the Students Union fulfils the tasks specified in
Article 65, par. 2 of the Act of Baden-Wuerttemberg on Universities and Colleges (LHG)
. To be able to fulfill its statutory tasks, the Students Union charges membership fees in the amount of presently EUR 12.- according to Article 65 a, par. 5, clauses 2 through 5 LHG and taking into account social concerns.
All enrolled students, including doctoral researchers and students on leave, are obliged to pay the charge for the Students Union. The payment obligation does not apply to students enrolled for a limited term only.
In case of de-registration or withdrawal of the enrollment within one month upon the start of the lecturing period, the charge for the Students Union will be reimbursed upon submission of the request on the
Students Portal (Studierendenportal)
. For information on the reimbursement of charges paid until the 2023/24 winter semester, contact the
Students Office (Studierendenservice)
instead.
Fee for Attending Guest Student Programs
The fee for attending guest student programs presently is EUR 75.-.
Late Payment fine
Please note that KIT charges a late payment fine of EUR 18.- in case of delayed re-registration according to
Article 2 of the Statutes on the Charging of Fees for Attending Guest Student Programs and Other Studies-related Services (Satzung des Karlsruher Instituts für Technologie (KIT) ueber die Erhebung von Gasthoerer/-innengebuehren und sonstigen studienbezogenen Dienstleistungen)
.

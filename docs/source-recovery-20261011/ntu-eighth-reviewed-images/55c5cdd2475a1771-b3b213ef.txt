EXCHANGE REQUIREMENTS 
Language Requirements If applicant would like to join college of Japanese language and Japanese culture, 
JLPT N2 level is required 
If applicant would like to join Master’s Program in Service Engineering, JLPT N1 
level in required. 
For others; TOEFL iBT 79 or Cambridge First Certificate level (recommended) 
GPA 2.3/3.0 (recommended) 
 
 
 
ESTIMATED COSTS 
Visa Requirements  To obtain student visa, applicant needs to have bank balance for at least 100,000 JPY / per 
month. (including student loan, scholarship) 
Accommodation  Deposit at move-in：¥30,000 ( it will be applied to cleaning fee when move -out or 
overdue unpaid rent) 
Monthly rate：¥23,000~¥44,000  (depending on the room type) 
Medical insurance ・Japanese National Health insurance： around 23,000JPY per year (required) 
・in-campus accident insurance： around 1000 JPY per year (required)

SCHOLARSHIP 
 
JASSO Scholarship Eligible applicants can apply for JASSO scholarship. 
Information：(page3) https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/education/international-short-term-
students/application-guide.pdf 
*The JASSO scholarship is highly competitive. Only a handful applicants will be able to get 
the opportunity. Please plan on living in Japan without relying on receiving JASSO 
scholarship.  
Others There are no other scholarship we can offer to the short-term exchange students besides 
JASSO scholarship.

TU Dortmund University has a unique
profile
4-week
German Intensive Course
prior to each semester (several levels & free of charge for exchange students!)
Welcome Session & Welcome Week
Language courses
during the semester (several levels & free of charge for exchange students!)
Dortmund Doubles/Buddy Programm
(exchange students are brought together with a Dortmund student in advance to make their start in Dortmund easier)
Academic support
before, during and after the mobility (individual course guidance)
Exchange students can choose
courses
from all departments - the choice is huge!
Alternatively, the International Office can also arrange full-time
teaching internships
in the Ruhr area
Reservation of accommodation
possible,
assistance
with administrative formalities, simplified registration (the International Office offers a range of services for exchange students)
A
colorful campus life
due to numerous offers from
university sports
,
university music
and
campus gastronomy
(in German).
Special support
for students with disabilities or with child(ren)
With the included
"Germany Semester Ticket"
, students are mobile throughout whole Germany (to travel by public transport)
Special prices for TU Dortmund University students at the
Theater Dortmund
In exceptional cases, additional courses can be attended at the Ruhr-Universität Bochum and/or the University of Duisburg-Essen (
UA Ruhr
)

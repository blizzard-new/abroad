Students from partner universities all over the world can study at TU Dort­mund University within the framework of a one or two semester stay. In addition to the regular semester exchange, TU Dort­mund University also offers its overseas partners a summer program and a combined study/internship year program. Various internship opportunities are also available to students from European and non-European countries.

Incoming Exchange Studies
Tel Aviv University academic exchange program allows students from partner universities in a variety of different academic levels (B.A., M.A. or Ph.D.), to come to Israel for a semester or a year and embark on a non-stop adventure in Tel Aviv. Exchange students are eligible to take classes in English from a variety of different faculties: Humanities (communication, psychology, Middle Eastern studies, Israel studies, Business, etc..), Engineering, Environmental Studies, Security & Diplomacy, Archeology, and more.
During the semester or year in Israel, international exchange students can participate in trips and activities across the country with other international students.
Come study in Israel and enjoy the culture and our diverse campus life!
Incoming Exchange Research
This program allows students from partner universities in a variety of different academic levels (B.A., M.A. or Ph.D.), to come to Tel Aviv for a semester or a year as part of an exchange and complete a research internship. These students should find their own TAU supervisor and follow the application requirements under the tab "application requirements". Students in the incoming exchange Research program do not need to take any classes, but should still come in the same time frame (semester dates) as the regular incoming exchange students.

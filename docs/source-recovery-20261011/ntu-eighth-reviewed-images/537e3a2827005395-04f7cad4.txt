Nomination 
The nomination form must be submitted by the partner institution. The 
nomination periods are: 
• For Fall entry: from January 12th to March 2nd, 2026 
• For Winter entry: from June 1st to August 31st, 2026 
Online Application Application instructions will be sent directly to students following their 
nomination. 
Application Documents https://international.umontreal.ca/english/international-
students/student-exchange-program/application-documents/  
Application Deadlines 
Students must upload all the required documents by: 
• March 24th, 2026, for an exchange beginning in Fall 2026  
• September 14th, 2026, for an exchange beginning in Winter 2027  
Evaluation of the 
Application 
Acceptance is not guaranteed even if the student had been selected by 
their home institution. All applications must be reviewed by the chosen 
academic department and this may take several weeks. 
 
Official acceptance documents will be available online on the Student 
Center (Centre étudiant) once the application has been accepted by the 
academic department.

Academic Information 
Program of Study and 
Courses 
 
Students must choose courses from a single program of study (one elective 
course per term is permitted). Please see the list of programs with particular 
requirements before nominating students.  
 
A full-time course load is mandatory.

Credit System 
 
Bachelor: 1 UdeM credit = 2 ECTS 
Master and PhD: 3 UdeM credits = 8 to 10 ECTS 
Transcript of Records and 
Grading Scale  
Official transcripts can be ordered at the Registrar’s Office. Please note that 
fees apply.  
 
See here for the grading scale of UdeM > 
Faculty Fees  
Additional fees may be directly added to the student’s bill on the Student 
Center at the request of the academic department to cover course 
materials, equipment use or laboratory access. 
 
Additional fees for language courses are to be expected and can vary 
depending on the nature of the course and the responsible faculty. 
 
Language Requirement 
Université de Montréal is a French -taught institution and most courses are given in French. Proof of  a 
minimum of B2 level French proficiency  (from the Common European Framework of Reference for 
Languages (CEFR)) is therefore required. Some programs of study  may require a higher level and official 
tests; see the list of programs with particular requirements for further details. 
Students who are not from a French-speaking institution must provide proof of French proficiency delivered 
by the language centre of their home university, French Alliance, language schools, etc. 
French courses : Non-French-speaking students can take one 3-credit French language course per term 
offered by UdeM’s École de français (students will be charged around 75$ for course materials).  
A placement test will be required; information will be provided to students once admitted. Please see our 
website for further details.

Immigration 
It is the student’s responsibility to ensure that immigration requirements are met and that they carry out 
the necessary immigration procedures to study abroad at UdeM as soon as they are accepted.  
Procedures and timeframes may d iffer according to the student’s nationality, the length of the exchange 
and the activities to be undertaken during their stay. 
The Immigration team at UdeM international is the only office at UdeM that can assist international 
students with immigration-related matters: immigration@umontreal.ca. 
Medical Insurance and Student Services 
Medical Insurance 
Coverage 
All exchange students attending UdeM are automatically enrolled to a 
mandatory group medical insurance. Cost in 2025-2026 (for reference): 
300$/term. Certain students can request to be exempted.  
 
For further details > 
Housing  
On-campus housing is primarily allocated to regular UdeM students. Very 
few spots are available for exchange students during the fall term, and no 
spots are available for the winter term.

Disability Support 
Students can contact the Office for Students with Disabilities  for assessment 
and support prior to arrival to ensure that they qualify for services. Additional

Application documents will be checked on a first-come first-served basis. If we find any questions or problems, we will contact the coordinators or students accordingly.
Please be advised that any incomplete or missing documents will be corrected at a later date and will cause delay in screening procedure and the receiving of acceptance notice.
Acceptance notice will be sent out to students and their home institution by email once they are accepted. Students from the same university might not receive acceptance results at the same time as Sophia proceed applications to Faculty screening only after the student’s documents are confirmed to be completed.
Certificate of Eligibility (for non-Japanese citizens) and Housing Notice with payment instructions (only for those who arranged housing through Sophia) will be sent to students directly by e-mail. Other important pre-arrival information will be available through online application website.

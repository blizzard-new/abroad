HUSA Program
Eligibility
Eligibility
International students who meet the following conditions are eligible to apply for the HUSA program:
Application Requirement
Students must be enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree program at their university during their stay in Hiroshima.
They must be a student of a university with which Hiroshima University has established a formal short-term student exchange agreement.
See Partner List
Students must have a good academic record.
Students must have sufficient English
OR
Japanese language proficiency to be able to comprehend classes, give presentations, write reports and take examinations. Attending HUSA without sufficient language ability can cause difficulties in academic achievement.
Language Proficiency Requirement
Students must submit either ①English Language Proficiency Proof
OR
②Japanese Language Proficiency Proof upon application.
*Native English speakers without① ② are also required to submit this
document
as part of the HUSA application.
① English Language Proficiency Proof
A copy of a TOEFL score (iBT above 61 (0-120 scale)  or above 4 (1-6 scale) / CBT above 173 / PBT above 500) or IELTS score (Academic Module above 5.0)
*If a student has been enrolled in a program or course whose instruction language is English, you can provide a
document
by a professor or the program director.
OR
② Japanese Language Proficiency Proof
A copy of certificate of Japanese Language Proficiency Test 2nd Grade (Nihongo Noryoku Shiken 日本語能力試験 2級) or above.

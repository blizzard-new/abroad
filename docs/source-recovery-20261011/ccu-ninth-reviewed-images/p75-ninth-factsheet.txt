Application Process  Once nominations are processed, Chandigarh University emails the 
International Mobility Coordinators an application form and documents 
that are filled out and submitted by the Applicants. 
 Applications are processed on a first come, first served basis. Applicants 
are encouraged to apply as early as possible.  
 In approximately 2-4 weeks, we hear from the partners who email us the 
offer letter and fact sheet. 
 From the numerous subjects offered, students choose subjects related to 
the ones that will be taught at CU in the respective semester. This learning 
agreement is then approved by their HODs and sent back to the partner 
university.

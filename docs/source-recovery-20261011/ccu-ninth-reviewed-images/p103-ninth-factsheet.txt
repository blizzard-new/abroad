University Information 
Portrait The ZHAW School of Engineering, one of the leading Engineering Faculties in 
Switzerland, focuses on topics that will be relevant in the future. Our 14 insti-
tutes and centres guarantee high-quality education, research, and develop-
ment, with an emphasis on the areas of energy, mobility, information, and 
health.  
Courses for Bachelor’s / Undergraduate students are available in the following 
fields: 
• Aviation 
• Computer Science 
• Data Science 
• Electrical Engineering 
• Energy and Environmental Engineering 
• Engineering and Management 
• Mechanical Engineering 
• Medical Informatics 
• Mobility Science 
• Systems Engineering 
 
Courses for Master’s / Graduate students are available in the following fields: 
• Aviation 
• Business Engineering 
• Civil Engineering 
• Computer Science 
• Data Science 
• Electrical Engineering 
• Energy and Environment 
• Information and Cyber Security 
• Mechanical Engineering 
• Mechatronics and Automation 
• Medical Engineering 
• Photonics and Laser Engineering 
Location  The School of Engineering is located in Winterthur, 5 minutes from Winterthur 
main railway station and just a 20-minute train journey from Zürich. Excellent 
public transport links allow for easy and comfortable travel throughout the 
country. With Switzerland located in the “centre of Europe” and Zürich Airport 
nearby, most destinations in Europe can be reached within 2 hours.

(Mobility-Online), including all necessary documents and instructions.

above. 
Late nominations may be accepted for students who do not require a visa, but 
only after prior consultation with the International Office.

Scholarship and SEMP/Erasmus 
SEMP/Erasmus+ Funds Students from SEMP/Erasmus+ partner universities receive SEMP funding during 
their stay at the School of Engineering (SoE). The grant amounts to a maximum 
of CHF 1’900 or CHF 2’200 per semester, depending on the country of the home 
institution.

Visa and Health Insurance 
Visa / Residence Permit  Non-EU citizens must apply for a student visa as early as possible after receiving 
their letter of acceptance. 
Students from Australia, Japan, New Zealand, Malaysia, Singapore, and the 
United Kingdom do not require a visa but must apply for a residence permit 
(“Aufenthaltsbewilligung”) through the SoE International Office.  
All exchange students must apply for a residence permit (L-Permit) upon arrival.  
Proof of financial means (CHF 1’750 per month) is required for both visa and resi-
dence permit applications.  
Detailled information will be provided by the International Office after the applica-
tion process.

Health Insurance Basic health insurance is mandatory by law in Switzerland and can be obtained 
after the student’s arrival. Detailed information will be provided during Orientation 
Week.  
Non-EU residents must purchase Swiss health insurance (starting from CHF 61 
per month); international health insurance obtained in the student’s home country 
is usually not sufficient.  
EU residents with EHIC cards are exempt from this obligation. They must submit 
a copy of their card, which must be valid for their entire stay in Switzerland.  
Further Information please read here.

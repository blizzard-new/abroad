Application & Eligibility
Nomination
Applicants must be nominated by the home institution to be eligible for a place in the programme. First-year students are normally NOT eligible.
Grade Point Average
Applicants are expected to attain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 on a 4.0 scale or equivalent.
English Proficiency
Applicants who are not native English speakers will need to fulfil the following language requirements and provide documentary evidence when submitting their applications:
TOEFL
Internet-based: minimum
80
(For exams taken before January 21, 2026) or minimum
4
(For exams taken on or after January 21, 2026)
IELTS
Minimum overall band of
6
in the academic version
Home Institution
A letter/ signed declaration from the home institution’s International Office certifying proficiency
Application (For students from Chinese Mainland, Taiwan, China and Macau, China please refer to Chinese version)
Applicants are REQUIRED to complete the application online and submit the following documents to EdUHK via GEMS or mailing as appropriate before the deadline stipulated.
Documents Required – to be uploaded to GEMS:
A scanned copy of official transcript
A scanned copy of TOEFL/IELTS scoresheet or English Proficiency Endorsement Form
Documents Required – to be mailed to EdUHK:
Visa application

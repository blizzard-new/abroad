About
POSTECH Student Exchange Program is exclusively for undergraduate/graduate students from partner institutions, providing the students
with the opportunity to study and immerse themselves in POSTECH.
Program
Course Taking (C)
All exchange students will be enrolled in the Course Taking (C) Program by default. Students can take courses at POSTECH and earn credits which can be transferred to their Home Institutions with Official Transcript.
Research Combined (C+R)
Students who wish to participate in Research are also eligible for the Research Combined (C+R) Program. However, students need to contact their to-be professor and lab at POSTECH by themselves and need to prepare a Letter of Approval document additionally before applying.

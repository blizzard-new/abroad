Academic Calendar 2019-2020 
For Summer and Winter calendars, visit summerwinter.uconn.edu 
Fall Semester 2019 
Mon, Aug 26 Fall semester begins 
Mon, Sep 2 Labor Day – No classes 
Tue, Sep 3 Last day to file petitions for course credit by examination 
Mon, Sep 9 Courses dropped after this date will have a “W” for withdrawal recorded on the academic 
record. Last day to add or drop courses without additional signatures. 
Last day to place courses on Pass/Fail 
Mon, Sep 16 Last day for students to make up Incomplete or Absence grades 
Tue, Sep 17-Mon, Sep 23 Examinations for course credit by examination 
Fri, Sep 20 Deadline to apply for graduation and to submit Final Plan of Study for conferral of a Fall 
2019 degree 
Tue, Sep 24 Dean’s signature required to add courses 
Fri, Oct 4 Mid-semester progress reports due students from faculty 
Mon, Oct 21 Registration for the Winter 2020 and Spring 2020 semester via Student Administration 
System begins 
Mon, Oct 28 Last day to withdraw from a course 
Last day to convert courses on Pass/Fail option to letter grade 
Sun, Nov 24-Sat, Nov 30 Thanksgiving Recess 
Fri, Nov 29 Last day for degree candidates to submit thesis and dissertation final copies to Open 
Commons and related paperwork to Degree Audit in the Office of the Registrar for 
conferral of a Fall 2019 degree. It is recommended that students defend at least two 
weeks prior to this deadline to allow for revisions. Defense must be announced two 
weeks prior to defending. (Graduate students only) 
Fri, Dec 6 Last day of fall semester classes 
Sat, Dec 7-Sun, Dec 8 Reading Days 
Mon, Dec 9 Final examinations begin 
Thu, Dec 12 Reading Day 
Sun, Dec 15 Final examinations end 
Sun, Dec 15 Conferral date for Fall 2019 degrees 
Wed, Dec 18 Semester grades due at 4 pm 
Fri, Dec 27 Last day for Plan B master’s students to submit final exam paperwork to Degree Audit in 
the Office of the Registrar (Graduate students only)

Exchange students
Your journey starts here! We’re thrilled that you’re considering Umeå University in the heart of northern Sweden for your study abroad experience. Umeå offers not just top-quality academics, but also unforgettable adventures, a vibrant student life, and the magic of the Arctic seasons. These pages provide an overview of the application procedure to Umeå University as an exchange student, as well as information regarding the next steps, as you are preparing for your studies.

Continuing Studies at UVic international programs
Let's get global ready.
In Continuing Studies at the University of Victoria (UVic), we believe in the power of international education and training to build connections, enhance innovation and prepare you to become a successful global citizen.
Here's what getting "global ready" looks like:
An interconnected world
Working well together across borders, boundaries and differences is essential to solving the complex issues of our time. Our international programs focus on developing advanced communication skills and engaging with real-world issues through active, competency-based learning.
A unique experience
Every educational journey is different. Improve your language skills to live or work abroad and learn about different cultures to develop new collaboration skills. As you approach your goals from new angles, the new knowledge and skills you’ll gain will further your future success.
Building relationships
We are proud to work collaboratively with many international partners to provide the resources you need to build professional networks and lifelong connections around the world.

Get Global Ready: Improve your English
Pursue a degree or diploma
Studying English is often the first step if you want to further pursue academic or professional studies at UVic. The ELC offers many options to get you closer to your goals:
University Admission Preparation (UAP)
An advanced English program for those who meet UVic’s admission requirements (except for English proficiency). Many UAP learners qualify for conditional admission, with a passing UAP grade satisfying the English requirement, for entry into their UVic undergraduate degree.
Learn more about UAP.
UVic Pathway
This first-year program allows you to meet UVic's English language requirement while completing academic credit courses as part of an undergraduate degree.
Learn more about Pathway.
ELC Preliminary Admission
Do you want to enter undergraduate degree programs at the University of Victoria (UVic)? The ELC Preliminary Admission is designed to help you meet UVic’s English requirements within 24 months.Learn more about ELPI.
Learn more about Preliminary Admission

An incredible community of international learners choose to get global ready through our online, and face-to-face, business programs at Continuing Studies.

Get Global Ready: Gain Professional Skills
Enhance your teaching
The ELC's expertise in English language programming includes professional development training if you are an international educator who teaches English, or teaches their subject area(s) in English. Academic and curriculum specialists at the ELC develop a variety of options for pre-service and in-service international educators, covering fields from early childhood education to the post-secondary level.
In these programs, you’ll explore education-related topics from a Canadian perspective while building a professional network. Our interactive learning environment allows you to develop confident English communication skills and to enhance teaching methodologies so you can teach more effectively in your home country.
Programs for International Educators:
Customized English as a Medium of Instruction
Customized Teaching English as a Foreign Language

Customize team training
Continuing Studies has extensive experience offering customized, international training programs to institutional, corporate and government groups.
We use a collaborative three-step process to develop specialized training programs that equip you with fresh, relevant skills to prepare you for success in your studies and careers.
We work with you to better understand your current situation and your team’s learning needs or goals.
We identify your priority learning objectives, and program elements, and then collaborate with our team of advisers, educators and real-world experts to design a program uniquely suited to your group.
Our instructors deliver your customized program, ensuring your expectations are met, and provide opportunities for reflection and feedback.

Why choose Continuing Studies at UVic?
As a UVic international student, you’ll be studying at one of the best universities in Canada. UVic is consistently ranked as one of Canada’s
top-ranked comprehensive universities
. Since UVic started in 1963, Continuing Studies has been an integral part of campus, providing adult and continuing education programming to domestic and international students. We partner with all UVic faculties to provide stimulating, high-quality education opportunities to local and international learners.
If you choose to study with Continuing Studies at UVic, you’ll benefit from:
International experience
– We have a long history of offering customized and scheduled programming to learners from around the world. As an international student at UVic, you can expect world-class education with a global perspective, preparing you for wherever your studies or career lead you in the future.
Welcoming environments
– We aspire to create an intercultural community that promotes harmony and understanding between everyone. Our academic programs are complemented by social activities where you can meet new friends and share the unique aspects of your country or culture in a safe, respectful environment.
English-language development
– Our award-winning team of English language specialists (in the ELC programs) are here to help you develop your English skills and prepare you for further studies or a career in an international market.
Talented experts
– Learn from a team of experienced instructors and presenters who are real-world experts, delivering relevant and practical content in your field of study. To bring a truly global perspective to your studies, there will be many opportunities to network and make long-lasting connections.
Dedicated on-campus support
– We understand it can be scary and challenging to move to a new country. Our student services and specialized support staff are here to help you every step of the way—before, during and after your studies. Students have access to the Student Wellness Centre, Campus Security Services 24/7, Virtual Centre for Student Learning, Immigration advising, Career counselling and more.

If you're an international student where English is not your first language, we offer a variety of academic and professional programs through the English Language Centre (ELC).

Step 1 - Check Entry Requirements
To apply for the exchange programme at HKU, students must meet the following entry requirements.
Ensure you have been nominated by your home institution.
Complete at least one year of university-level study by the time you begin your exchange at HKU.
Provide valid and acceptable
English Proficiency Proof
(required for both native and non-native English speakers).
Be in good academic standing at your home institution. Higher entry requirements must be met if applying for the following faculties:

Application Processing Time
After the application deadline, students’ applications will be reviewed by their proposed faculty.
Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and may take up to 1.5 months. Students can refer to the Exchange Application Schedule for details.
You will be notified of the result by email.
Students are reminded to check their emails regularly during this period.

Step 3 - Housing Application
Please refer to the
CEDARS Housing
website.
You may apply for housing during the designated application period as soon as you have started your exchange application; there is no need to wait until you receive an offer.
Apply online
through CEDARS using your 10-digit exchange application reference number.
Housing is not guaranteed. Due to limited spaces, the allocation of housing is subject to availability.
After receiving your Notice of Admission, email a signed copy to
CEDARS Housing
for further processing.
Housing results will be sent by email. If offered a place, confirm with the hall manager and state your check-in date. Offers will be withdrawn if not accepted.
For housing enquiries, please refer to the
FAQ
or contact CEDARS Housing at
cedars.housing@hku.hk
.
Step 4 - Visa Application
Begin your visa application only after receiving your Notice of Admission.
Visit the
HKU Student Visa page
for detailed instructions.
Register for an account on the visa application portal.
Upload your signed Notice of Admission and other required documents.
Submit the online application and mail hard copies as instructed by the Admissions Office.
Visa processing takes
at least 10 weeks
; the application fee is non-refundable.
Visa Application Paths
Depending on the student’s nationality, the visa application will follow one of two different paths:
Mainland students
(using an Exit-Entry Permit as a travel document and entering Hong Kong from the Chinese Mainland)
Apply here
International, Macao or Taiwan students
(nationalities other than Chinese or, Chinese nationals with permanent residency overseas, entering Hong Kong from places other than the Chinese Mainland)
Apply here

Admissions Office

Step 5 - Acceptance Procedures
After receiving the Notice of Admission, students are required to complete the following steps to accept the offer.
Accept the Offer
Check all information for accuracy (e.g., name, registration period).
Sign the Notice of Admission.
Log in to the online application account to accept or decline the offer and upload the signed Notice.
Email a copy of the signed Notice to your host faculty.
Secure Housing Application
Email the signed Notice of Admission to
CEDARS Housing
for further processing.
If you receive a housing offer, confirm with the hall manager by email and indicate your check-in date. Offers will be withdrawn if not accepted.

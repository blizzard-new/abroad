Temporary Housing
Expect to spend several days in temporary housing before locating and settling into a more permanent arrangement. For visitors staying less than one-year, finding a month-to-month apartment can be difficult and short-term contracts are usually more expensive than a regular lease. You may contact the property managers to see if they would consider a short-term lease or month-to-month contract.
Visit Corvallis
or
Oregon Welcome Centers
list temporary places to stay.

Corvallis iHouse
(short-term housing contracts available) is a home for international and U.S. undergrad and graduate students, postdocs, and interns at OSU. iHouse provides furnished bedrooms, large study rooms, inviting living rooms, movie room, game room with ping pong and foosball, yoga room, courtyard with barbecues, laundry facility, large dining room, large kitchen with many appliances and utensils, catered food, communal as well as private bathrooms and a large park across the street with volleyball courts and baseball fields.
International House
(short-term housing contracts available) offers a relaxed and quiet atmosphere and furnished bedrooms.
UHDS Family Housing
University Housing & Dining Services (UHDS) has an
on-campus Family Housing option called Orchard Court
for currently-admitted students who meet eligibility requirements. You can apply to be on a waitlist that can take 16-24 months.

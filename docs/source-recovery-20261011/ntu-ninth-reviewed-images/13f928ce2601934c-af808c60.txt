Additionally, your Hitotsubashi Network ID (Gmail service and 1284Wireless) will expire in 60 days after your official enrollment period is over (for those who leave after the Spring-Summer semester: end/October, for those who leave after the Autumn-Winter semester: end/May).
Procedures before Returning Home
Before the semester is over, check the email from the Study Abroad Section (Inbound), Educational Affairs Division  or your graduate school office and follow their instructions regarding necessary procedures before leaving.
Inform the Study Abroad Section (Inbound), Educational Affairs Division or your graudate school office of any temporary absences from Japan as well.

About
POSTECH Academic Exchange Program is exclusively for both undergraduate & graduate students from worldwide, providing the students with the opportunity to study and immerse themselves in POSTECH. Students should select their Path & Track before applying based on their decision or circumstances.
Duration
One
or
Two Semesters (One Full Academic Year)
Track
Exchange Track
Concurrent Track
Exchange Track is for
students in POSTECH partner institutions
who wish to study at POSTECH for one or two semesters. Exchange Track Students should be officially nominated by their home institution and pay the tuition to their home institution.
Concurrent Track is for
students in non-partner institutions
who wish to studay at POSTECH for one or two semesters. Concurrent Track Students should apply individually and pay the tuition to POSTECH.

Eligibility
Students must:
○ Have completed at least one semester before they apply
○
Currently be enrolled in their Home Institution
○
Overall GPA 3.0 out of 4.3 equivalent to:
-
Letter Grade B, 3.0 out of 4.0, 4.0 out of 5.0, 80%
○
English Proficiency of either
-
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS Academic 6.0, Home Institution Language Assessment C1
Process
Exchange Track
Concurrent Track
1. Nomination (Home Institution)
Partner Institutions' coordinators will submit the Nomination Online.
Please let the Nominated Students prepare for Required Documents from this step. Some students have trouble obtaining necessary documents, and this leads to delay on our Application Process.
2. Application (Student)
After finalizing nomination list, those students nominated will receive the Call for Application email.
Students should submit application through our IR Portal with Required Documents. During Application period, student may receive system emails to supplement the documents. If you do, please ensure to submit the updated or revised one as soon as possible.
3. Admission (POSTECH)
Admission Results will be announced via email with Admission Package including Exchange Student Guide.
1. Application (Student)
Students should submit application through our IR Portal with Required Documents. During Application period, student may receive system emails to supplement the documents. If you do, please ensure to submit the updated or revised one as soon as possible.
2. Admission (POSTECH)
Admission Results will be announced via email with Admission Package including Exchange Student Guide.
Required Documents
○
Certificate of Enrollment
- Should be issued within six months
○
Official Academic Transcript
-
Should be in English and include overall GPA
-
If your Official Transcript does not include overall GPA, you should submit additional paper issued from Home Institution to prove your overall GPA
○
English Language Certificate
-
Certificate should be issued within two years and be either:
; TOEFL iBT 79 (within two years)
; IELTS Academic 6.0 (within two years)
; Home Institution Language Assessment (Indication of Level should be "C1" or "Enough to Study in English")
○
Recommendation Letter
-
Should be issued from Home Institution Faculty or Staff
○
Copy of Passport
-
Should be valid for six months from the end of the program
Additional Required Documents for NON-OECD Nationals
○
Apostilled Certificate of Enrollment
-
Should be apostilled from Korean Embassy or Official Apostille Center, not Home Institution
-
It usually take at least 2 weeks to obtain apostille. If you are non-OECD nationals, please apply for the apostille at the beginning
○
Financial Proof
-
Bank Statement or Scholarship Certificate under student name
-
Amount should be at least 3,000 USD (for a semester), 7,500 USD (for two semesters)

GKS (Global Korean Scholarship)
○
Qualification
- Students of Foreign Nationality who have never studied in Korea before
- Overall GPA above 80%
○
Selection Procedure
- POSTECH will evaluate student's application including academic background, diversity of nationality, institution, and else and nominate selected students.
- After nomination, NIIED will review the nomination from POSTECH and share final result with POSTECH.
○
Scholarship Details
- 630,000 KRW per month & Economy Class Airfare
GPES (Global Postechian Exchange Scholarship)
○
Qualification
- Students who are willing to be POSTECH Global Ambassador with Social Network and other in-person networking
○
Selection Procedure
- POSTECH will evaluate student's application without any academic background or nationality, but only the quality of application and willingness of the students forward GPES Scholarship.
○
Scholarship Details
- 500,000 KRW per month

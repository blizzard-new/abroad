Campus Hostel Accommodation Notice
Dear Students,
Thank you for the overwhelming response to admissions for Academic Year 2026–27.
Please note that all
on-campus hostel accommodation (for both boys and girls) is currently full.
As a result, the university
will not be able to offer hostel accommodation to students who have taken, or will take, admission after 25 July 2026.
Such students are welcome to arrange safe, comfortable, and student-friendly accommodation near the campus and attend as day scholars. For assistance with off-campus accommodation, students may contact their respective admission officer.
Thank you for your understanding—we look forward to welcoming you to Bennett University.

Hostel Life
at Bennett University
Bennett University offers thoughtfully designed on-campus hostels for boys and girls, located
within the campus
, to ensure a safe, comfortable, and modern living experience for students. Caring wardens and vigilant security personnel create a supportive environment, allowing students to focus on their academics.
>The hostels can accommodate up to 10,000 students
and feature well-furnished triple-sharing rooms equipped with air conditioning, comfortable beds, and ample storage. Hygiene is a key priority, with regularly cleaned common washrooms and well-maintained facilities. Recreational common rooms with indoor games and entertainment options encourage relaxation and community bonding. With round-the-clock operations, power backup through DG sets, centralised temperature control, routine maintenance, and a dedicated safety team conducting daily checks, the campus supports seamless living. Students also have access to sports,
wellness maintained by MAX Healthcare
, and fitness facilities, promoting a holistic and enriching campus life. Hostel accommodation is available on a first-come-first-served basis.

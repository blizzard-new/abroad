Incoming Exchange Students
We are pleased to welcome international exchange and sponsored students to the University of Georgia. International students directly support the teaching and research missions of UGA in fundamental and comprehensive ways. Likewise, our office seeks to support this population and the departments that host them, with information and advice regarding visa options, benefits and obligations, and legal status in the U.S. We hope that you will not hesitate to contact us with any questions pertaining to international students at UGA.
For Incoming International Exchange Students, please contact us at
exchange@uga.edu
.
The University of Georgia's bilateral and research exchanges are made possible by BridgeUSA. Learn more at
www.bridgeusa.state.gov
. While connecting global leaders and students, BridgeUSA programs at UGA create lasting impacts on our campus community, providing academic excellence, inclusive excellence, and cultural exchange.

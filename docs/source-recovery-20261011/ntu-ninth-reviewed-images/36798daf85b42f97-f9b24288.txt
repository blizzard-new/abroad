Mobility application procedure
Nomination and application process is through the 
Administration System of Incoming Student’s Mobility 
(by its initials in Spanish “SGMEE”). Access must be 
guaranteed to log in the System and nominate students 
(verify the corresponding call).
1. The International Office from the HEI begins with 
the nomination procedure through SGMEE.
2. The student will fill in with the required information 
and will upload the required documentation 
including:
a. An institutional application letter issued by 
the student´s university supporting the 
request.
b. An official certificate of Spanish language 
proficiency, level B2 (SIELE or DELE), when 
it applies.
The general requirements and those requested by 
the schools and faculties (Specific requirements) 
are established into the call. The registration 
instructions will be available at DGECI website 
together with the publication of the call.
With the objective of supporting and guiding the 
mobility coordinators of each HEI and interested 
students, the DGECI offers informative sessions 
that will help to solve questions regarding the 
procedure for nominations, applications, among 
other topics. The dates of each session will be 
informed in the call.
Official Language
Courses are taught in Spanish. However, there are 
some courses given in English. For further informa-
tion about courses in English, please click here: 
www.unaminternacional.unam.mx/es/movilidad/entrante
• For students whose native or instruction language 
is different from Spanish, an international official 
Spanish Certificate in a B2 level CEFR (as minimum) 
is required.
• DELE Certificate (Instituto Cervantes) or SIELE 
Certificate (Servicio Internacional de Evaluación 
de la Lengua Española) are the only certificates 
accepted.
• SIELE tests can be found all over the world. The 
required certificate from SIELE are the 4 tests on 
“SIELE GLOBAL” Certification or the S5 SIELE 
modality (includes Written Expression and Interac-
tion & Oral Expression and Interaction). For more 
information:
   https://certificacion.cepe.unam.mx/siele/
• Through the Teaching Center for Foreigners 
(CEPE), interested students will be able to take 
an online preparation course and take the SIELE 
exam in the S5 modality. These courses are 
exclusive for mobility candidates, offered at a 
preferential cost. Check the links to the courses 
in the call published on the DGECI website or 
request information from the Incoming Student 
Mobility Coordination.
• For courses in English or other language, you must 
comply with the requirement determined by the 
corresponding academic entity.
Visiting Student
For visiting students with no university agreement, 
enrollment fees are:
• Tuition fee: $250 USD.
• Semestral fee per assignment: $275 USD.


Major Medical Health Insurance
Once students are admitted, they must acquire an 
International Major Medical Health Insurance. The insur-
ance is compulsory to undertake their mobility stay and 
must be acquired in their home country before traveling 
to Mexico and must be registered in the SGMEE system.
The insurance must be valid during the complete 
mobility stay in Mexico. The insurance coverage must 
include accidents, medical attention for any type of 
illness, diverse medical support services, major medical 
health expenses, hospitalization, COVID-19 sickness and 
hospitalization, and repatriation of mortal remains. The 
insurance is not managed nor acquired through UNAM.
Likewise, upon arrival, the students must pay a fee for an 
accident insurance (Field Practices) managed by UNAM 
(the cost is approximately $220 Mexican pesos).
Lodging
There is no residence available for international 
students. Admitted students can consult approved 
lodging options by CEPE near the university campus. 
Students must register on:
https://alojamiento.cepe.unam.mx/login.php
Immigration Checkpoint in Mexico
Students must have a residence permit or a visa to 
study in Mexico. They must consult the corresponding 
Embassy or Consulate for further information about 
the procedure to obtain the adequate migratory 
status.

UNAMigo program
Approximate cost for monthly expenses (USD)
• Lodging: 200 to 500
• Nourishment: 350 to 450
• Transportation: 40 to 60
• Studying materials: the materials vary depending 
on the degree
• Leisure: 100 to 200
UNAMigo Program offers support to our international 
and national students to get along with our volunteer 
UNAM students. Our local students will be their hosts 
during their stay. They will guide them along the 
campus, assist them in the school or faculty 
enrollment or other services, guide them about local 
transportation or food and accompany them in 
recreational and cultural activities offered by UNAM, 
DGECI or the schools or faculties in which they will 
carry out their mobility. To join the program they 
will be contacted prior arrival at UNAM.

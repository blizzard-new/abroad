Information about the academic year
Exchange students usually arrive in Vienna shortly before the semester and classes begin. Please make sure you arrive no later than at the beginning of the course period. In general, exchange students are expected to stay in Vienna until the end of the course period. The exact dates of your stay abroad may vary depending on courses and exams.
Please note that you need to be proficient in
German
to study at the University of Vienna. The majority of courses is held in German.
Exchange students usually do not have to provide
official proof of language proficiency
for a semester abroad at the University of Vienna.
However, your home university must confirm in the online nomination that you have the necessary language skills at the start of your studies at the University of Vienna.
Every Erasmus+ student exchange agreement between the University of Vienna and your home university contains information about the recommended level of language proficiency.
As a general rule, Erasmus+ exchange students need the following language proficiency levels to study at the University of Vienna:
Courses taught in German: German level B2
Exception – courses offered by the Department of German Studies: German level C1
Courses taught in English: English level B2

Discounts for incoming students
Incoming students
receive a
20 % discount
on the
intensive courses in February and September, on summer courses and on trimester courses
upon presentation of the ‘Certificate of Arrival’ issued by the International Office. There is no discount on semester courses.
For the intensive courses for incoming students (in September or February), incoming students receive a
refund of EUR 150 (academic year 2026/2027)
from the International Office if they attend regularly and pass the final exam. As this is a course to prepare you for your semester abroad, you can only receive this refund if you complete the course
before starting your studies
at the University of Vienna. Students who study here for a full academic year can therefore only claim a refund for the intensive course in September.
Please note that you must pay the
full course fee
when you register for the course. You will then receive the discount at a later date if you present your Certificate of Arrival to the Language Centre.
Please note that the
application for a refund
must be submitted directly to the Language Centre of the University of Vienna by
15 October
(course in September) or by
15 March

Textbooks
are
not included

Incoming students receive a 25 % discount on all courses in languages other than German.

Completing courses outside the field of study in which you are nominated
Please note that as an
exchange student, you must, in principle, choose courses from the field of study in which you were nominated
. If you are also interested in completing
courses in other fields of study
, you need to register for these courses as ‘courses outside your nominated field of study’.
The registration can only be discussed
directly with the mobility coordinator(s) of the other field(s) of study only AFTER you have arrived in Vienna
. However, there is no guarantee that a place will be available.
Registration for courses in other fields of study is handled differently in every field of study. You can find information provided by individual fields of study on the website
Information concerning Learning Agreements and courses outside your nominated field of study
.
Exceptions:
Lectures (VO),
courses relating to Austria (PDF, German only),
which are open to all exchange students in principle, and language courses provided by the Language Centre of the University of Vienna.
You have to
register in any case
, even if you are only interested in attending a course. You cannot attend a course if you have not registered within the registration period via the online registration system.
Information on how
to get courses from other fields of study confirmed on your Learning Agreement
is available in the ‘Learning Agreement’ section.
Stays at the University of Vienna for the purpose of research for an academic thesis
A nomination for an Erasmus+ stay for research purposes means that you are
independently
carrying out
research
at the University of Vienna.
If you are planning a stay at the University of Vienna for research purposes and require additional support, please discuss this with
your mobility coordinator
at the University of Vienna.
However, additional support cannot be guaranteed.
At the University of Vienna, you
cannot obtain
any ECTS credits
for research activities for bachelor's papers, master's theses, doctoral theses, or other academic papers. In accordance with the curricula of the University of Vienna, no ECTS credits are awarded for these activities. When studying abroad at the University of Vienna, you can only obtain ECTS credits for completed courses and exams. Therefore, ECTS credits for research activities cannot be approved in the Learning Agreement.

How do I prepare a digital Learning Agreement (Erasmus Without Paper, EWP)?
Your home university will provide you with information about preparing your Learning Agreement.
As soon as we have received your Learning Agreement at the University of Vienna, you will receive an e-mail.
In Mobility Online, you must then specify whether you would like to attend courses outside the field of study in which you are nominated.
Your Learning Agreement will then be forwarded to your mobility coordinator.
Confirmation of courses outside your nominated field of studies for your Learning Agreement
Please note that mobility coordinators can usually only confirm courses in the field of study for which you have been nominated in the Learning Agreement. Therefore, please only list courses offered in your nominated field of study in the Learning Agreement
before
arriving in Vienna.
You can add courses from other fields of study to your Learning Agreement
after
your arrival in Vienna.
As soon as your updated Learning Agreement has been sent to the University of Vienna, you will receive further information by e-mail on how to confirm courses from other fields of study on a separate form (‘Approval form for courses outside your nominated field of study’) and have them signed in the Learning Agreement.
Please note that you
must register for courses within the registration period in any case
, even if you are only interested in attending the course or if the course has not yet been listed in your Learning Agreement. You cannot attend a course if you have not registered within the registration period via the online registration system.
Changes to the Learning Agreement
If you want to make changes to your courses, you must make these changes in your home university's system. Contact your home university if you have any questions about changes to the Learning Agreement.
What do I need to consider regarding entry to Austria and visa requirements?
Exchange students and Erasmus+ trainees are subject to the same regulations regarding entry and residence as other persons who wish to enter Austria or stay here for an extended period of time. Which provisions apply to you depends on your
citizenship
.
Everyone, regardless of their nationality, is obliged by law to
register their residence within 3 days
of moving into their accommodation.
The Austrian Agency for Education and Internationalisation (OeAD) provides
further information about entry and residence for students
.

Entry of third-country nationals
Students from
non-EU/EEA countries
(third countries) may need a visa for Austria, even if they have a residence permit from another EU country.
Please note that incoming students and trainees
are responsible for applying for the visa themselves
. The International Office is not involved in the visa application process. As the processing of visa applications can take several weeks or months,
please take care of organising your visa as early as possible
.
Different regulations apply depending on the length of stay:
Entry, stay of less than 6 months:
If you plan to study in Austria for no more than 6 months, you need either a
visa C
(Schengen visa) or a
visa D
(for stays of at least 91 days up to a maximum of 6 months) to enter and stay in Austria.
Stays of more than 6 months:
Third-country nationals who do not have a residence permit from another EU country require a
residence permit – student
for stays of more than 6 months.
For your visa application, you may need
your letter of admission
from the University of Vienna. You will receive your letter of admission by 15 June (start of stay in the winter semester) or 15 December (start of stay in the summer semester) at the latest. If you need your letter of admission before then, please send us an e-mail.
In accordance with the Austrian Reporting Act (
Meldegesetz

taking up accommodation

Before leaving Austria, you must inform the authorities that you are leaving the country by de-registering at the municipial district office.

Main place of residence/secondary residence
You must register your place of residence as either your main or secondary place of residence. The term ‘main place of residence’ (in the German form: ‘
Hauptwohnsitz
’) refers to the accommodation that you intend to make the
centre of your life
. If this applies to several residences, you must specify the one at which you spend most of your time as your main place of residence. The other residences are additional residences (additional residence –
Nebenwohnsitz
, or secondary residence –
Zweitwohnsitz
). Criteria such as length of stay and location of the education/training institution are decisive for determining where your ‘centre of life’ is.

European Health Insurance Card/e-card replacement document
With the
EHIC card
(European Health Insurance Card), all EU/EEA citizens have the same entitlement with regard to insurance as Austrians. With the EHIC card, you will receive free medical care from
doctors who have a contract with a social health insurance fund (‘
Kassenarzt
’)
in Austria for all
treatments that are medically necessary during your stay
. This insurance coverage does not include check-ups and preventive examinations.
Students from
Turkey, Serbia, Bosnia-Herzegovina, North Macedonia, and Montenegro
receive the form TR/A3, SRB/A3, BIH/A3, MK/A3, or MNE/A3 in their home countries. Upon presentation of this form, they can obtain an e-card replacement document (‘
e-card Ersatzbeleg
’) from the Austrian health insurance fund (
Österreichische Gesundheitskasse
). Using this document, they can obtain free medical care from
doctors who have a contract with a social health insurance fund (‘
Kassenarzt
’)
in Austria for all treatments that are medically necessary during their stay. This insurance coverage does not include check-ups and preventive examinations.

Paying the Students’ Union (ÖH) fee
You will receive your student ID number once you have been admitted to your field of study (stays in the winter semester: no later than at the beginning of August; stays in the summer semester: no later than at the beginning of February).
As soon as you have received your student ID number, please pay the Students’ Union (ÖH) fee (at the latest by 31 October for the winter semester/31 March for the summer semester).
Exchange students are exempt from paying tuition fees at the University of Vienna,
but
they must pay the
Students’ Union (ÖH) fee
of
EUR 26.20
per semester (in the academic year of 2026/2027). Payment is mandatory to complete your
admission
process and to receive a
valid student ID card
. You can pay the fee in
u:space
, where you can also find more information on the different payment methods.
Please note:
If the payment is not received by the University of Vienna system within the payment period (winter semester: 31 October, summer semester: 31 March), your admission cannot be successfully completed.
The
Austrian National Union of Students (ÖH)
represents all students studying at Austrian universities. The ÖH fee also provides students with insurance coverage for accidents that happen in the context of their studies.

Orientation sessions for incoming students
The International Office offers on-site information events (orientation sessions) for exchange students at the beginning of each semester.
Exchange students must register for an orientation session in order to be registered at the University of Vienna.
To confirm your registration at the University of Vienna and receive your student ID card,
you must participate in person on site. You will receive information about online registration by e-mail in due time.
Orientation sessions for the winter semester take place between
the beginning of September and the beginning of October.
Orientation sessions for the summer semester take place between
the beginning of February and the beginning of March
.
During the orientation session, you will receive your student ID and complete your registration at the University.
You will receive the
Certificate of Arrival
as confirmation of your registration. If necessary, we can also
confirm
the date of your arrival in Vienna at the orientation session
if you arrived
no more than 5 days
before the event.
We therefore recommend
attending an orientation session as soon as possible after arriving in Vienna
.
During the orientation session you will also receive the most important information about living and studying in Vienna (e.g., first steps after arrival, public transportation, contact persons, etc.).
Please note that orientation sessions are held in English. The documents provided will also be available in German, and you can also ask questions in German. Orientation sessions take about 60 to 90 minutes.
The venue will be announced when you register for the orientation session
. The orientation sessions take place either in or near the Main Building of the University of Vienna (Universitätsring 1, 1010 Vienna).
Please bring the following documents with you to the orientation session:
Your photo ID (passport or identity card)
Is the start date of your exchange the date on which the preparatory German language course starts? In this case, please bring a confirmation of registration for the German language course/a certificate for the completed course if you need the start date confirmed on your Certificate of Arrival.
Does your home university require a separate signed form instead of our Certificate of Arrival? Then please bring the form with you to the orientation session.

Do I need a confirmation of my stay abroad?
As a rule, the home universities require Erasmus+ students to submit a confirmation of stay.
Please clarify with your home university in good time before you leave Vienna
whether you need a confirmation of stay. If yes, clarify whether your home university has a
specific form
for this purpose.
If you require a confirmation of stay, you must deregister at the International Office in person.
If you fail to do so, we cannot confirm the details of your stay.
If your home university does not accept the Certificate of Departure issued by the University of Vienna and insists on its own form, please bring the respective form with you.
We can issue the confirmation of stay
no sooner than five days before the end of your study-related activities
in Vienna.
If you still have something to do for your studies at the University of Vienna after the end of classes (e.g., exams, writing papers, researching in the library, etc.) and you therefore need to stay longer in Vienna, we can confirm your stay even after the end of classes if you come to our office in person. The International Office cannot confirm stays that were not conducted for study-related purposes.
Please note: Certificates of Departure are only issued for stays during which the student was physically present in Vienna.
Transcript of Records
The Transcript of Records contains all your academic achievements and documents which courses you have completed during your stay at the University of Vienna.
Please note that
The International Office
does not automatically transfer your Transcript of Records to your home university
.
The Transcript of Records only lists courses for which you have completed an
achievement
(e.g., written exam at the end of the semester, final paper, etc.).
It does
not
list courses completed at the
Language Centre
of the University of Vienna.
Grades for the winter semester can be entered until 31 March at the latest, grades for the summer semester until 31 October at the latest.
It usually takes no longer than one month after the exam to enter your grades. Therefore, not all of your grades may be listed in the system by the time you leave Vienna.
If you need a grade earlier, please contact the respective lecturer in good time before your departure to discuss this issue.
If there are
problems with your grades
, please contact the respective lecturers or the department.

How do I get my Transcript of Records?
Download the Transcript via u:space
You can download your Transcript of Records at any time (even after returning home) via your u:space account. You can find the function under ‘Personal matters’ > ‘My documents’.
The Transcript of Records downloaded via u:space contains an official digital signature and is therefore legally valid.
You can download your Transcript of Records at least until the end of your admission: until 31 March if you are staying in the winter semester, or until 31 October if you are staying in the summer semester.
If you have any questions or problems with downloading your Transcript of Records via u:space, please contact
the Vienna University Computer Center (ZID) Servicedesk.
Receiving your Transcript by e-mail or by post
If all your grades appear in your u:space account, but the official digital signature is not sufficient for your home university, we can also send your Transcript of Records
by e-mail or
by post
to you or your home university on request.
We are not automatically notified once your Transcript of Records is complete. Therefore, if you ask us to send your Transcript to you by e-mail or by post,
please check yourself in u:space (under ‘Personal matters’ > ‘My documents’) whether your Transcript contains all grades before submitting your request.
How do I de-register my residence?
At the end of your stay in Vienna, you must let the authorities know that you are leaving the country by de-registering at the
municipal district office
(
Magistratisches Bezirksamt
). You can do this anytime
between three days before and three days after you move out
.
You need the following documents to de-register:
the
travel document
with which you travelled to Vienna (e.g., your passport)
a fully completed
registration form
(‘
Meldezettel
’) WITHOUT the signature of the accommodation provider
.
You do not need any old registration forms and registration confirmations.
You may have to pay a processing fee when you present your travel document for the first time. The costs vary depending on the type of travel document. If you have already paid for the processing of your travel document, please bring the confirmation of payment with you when you de-register. Further information: see link below.

Can I extend my stay abroad?
If you have only been nominated for the winter semester, you can apply for an extension for the summer semester. An extension from the summer semester to the following winter semester is not possible.

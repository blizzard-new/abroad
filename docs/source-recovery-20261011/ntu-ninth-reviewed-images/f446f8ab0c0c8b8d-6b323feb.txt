Our Mission
The Off-Campus Life office, or OCL, serves as the first point of contact for students moving, and living, off-campus.
Living off-campus comes with many benefits, challenges and anxieties. A guide can help chart the path through the challenges and anxieties, to reach the good outcomes you seek from living off-campus.
At OCL, we want to be yours.
Browse all our resources in the top menu above. Below, we’ve included key questions and concerns that lots of students have, to get you off on a fast start to a great life off-campus. And when those resources don’t offer the help you need, reach out via social media, email, or phone. We’re here for you.

UC CHILE IN NUMBERS
UC Chile (Pontificia 
Universidad Católica 
de Chile) seeks 
excellence in the 
creation and transfer 
of knowledge and 
in the provision of 
a Catholic- based 
education to form 
people at the service 
of society.
Founded in 1888, the 
university currently has nearly 
33,000 students and 3,500 
professors. Nearly 1,000 
international come to  
UC  Chile experience a 
mobility program. 
We maintain rigorous 
academic standards. As a 
result, we are ranked the best 
university in Latin America (QS 
University Rankings 2023) and 
93 rd world 2024). 
Our university has four 
campuses in Santiago and 
one in Villarrica, located in 
the south of Chile.
It has 18 faculties,  
6 interdisciplinary academic 
units and the UC Chile College 
Program.

MIGRATORY PROCESS
Exchange students who come to Chile 
for less than 1 80 days may enter the 
country with a Temporary Stay permit. 
While those who come for a year must 
compulsorily apply for Temporary 
Residence (student subcategory).
National Immigration Service website
INSURANCE AND HEALTH
Exchange students must have insurance 
that includes medical expenses for at 
least USD 30,000; health and funeral 
repatriation services, and must cover the 
entire period of stay in Chile. 
You can check some options here.
More information about emergencies on 
our campuses can be found here.

“A comfortable and free living space like home, a luxury dormitory where dreams and youth come alive”                        Sunchon National University's Students' Living Hall is equipped with various welfare and convenience facilities such as accommodations that can accommodate a total of 1,896 people, restaurants, reading rooms, convenience stores, and computer rooms. The student acceptance rate of our living hall is the highest among universities in the country, and we have established and implemented rules for a 24-hour community life. Our university has dormitory facilities that can accommodate all international students, so you can enter as you wish regardless of marks.

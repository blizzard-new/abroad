Virtual Tours
Introduction
Campus Housing offers residence hall locations on the east, south, and west sides of campus.  With ten residence halls and a variety of room types unique to each of those halls, there are many different styles for you to explore and find your best fit.
Virtual tours provide an overview of room layouts. Please note that furnishings and included items may vary slightly between rooms. For more information, visit
housing.uic.edu/halls

Exchange Students
Based on the exchange agreements, we accept
                short-term exchange students from our partner institutions all
                over the world.
Requirement
・Applicants must be enrolled as a regular student at a
                        partner university and the graduation must be after the
                        end of this program at University of Tsukuba.
・The college, school, or program applicants wish to
                        pursue at University of Tsukuba must be within the scope
                        of the student exchange agreement.
・Applicants must be nominated by their home
                        university.

Student Status
Exchange Students(Tokubetsu
                    Chokogakusei)
Exchange students (Tokubetsu
                            Chokogakusei) are undergraduate students or graduate
                            students from other universities who wish to attend
                            courses at the University of Tsukuba. Their
                            admission is granted upon mutual consultation
                            between the two universities. Exchange students can
                            earn credits if they pass the required examinations,
                            and credit transfer is also available depending on
                            the system at their home universities. Exchange
                            students should confirm the credit transfer
                            arrangements with the International Office at their
                            home universities before applying for the exchange
                            programs at the University of Tsukuba.
Exchange Research Students
                    (Tokubetsu
                    Kenkyugakusei)
Exchange research students (Tokubetsu
                    Kenkyugakusei) are graduate students of other universities, with
                    which the University of Tsukuba has agreements, who are
                    receiving guidance from supervisors at the graduate level at
                    the University of Tsukuba.

COLABS – Application Procedures
Important:
From the Fall 2026 semester, prior consent request applications will be changed to be based on the application schedule. (Please refer to Step.5)
Unlike the other exchange programs, the COLABS program requires prior consent from the applicant’s prospective academic advisor at Tohoku University.
The Prior Consent of Acceptance Request Form opens on July 1, 2026 and close on October 15, 2026 at 15:00 (JST) for the Spring 2027 Semester.
Please be sure to submit an application within the deadline.
It is often the case that even if you ask your first-choice professor, you may not be accepted, so we strongly encourage you to prepare in advance so that you have enough time to apply to a second-choice or third-choice professor.
If the application is not accepted by
prospective academic advisor
,
the applicant may apply up to three times for the consent of acceptance request in total.
Please note, however, that once the application period has passed, a second or third application will not be accepted.
It will take longer than you think to get the consent, so please submit your application as soon as possible.
It is
NOT allowed
that COLABS applicants contact their prospective Tohoku University's academic advisor directly without going through the Student Exchange Division to avoid any possible confusion.

Q&A
Q1. Is there any specific period when I should contact professors to obtain consent?
A
: Yes. Please refer to the Consent Request Period in the table above.
Q2. I have not heard anything from the professor, who I contacted a couple of days ago. What should I do?
A
: First, please confirm when you will be notified of the result of your application when you submit the Prior Consent of Acceptance Request Form. The notification time of the result will vary depending on the date of submission. If you do not receive the result after a long period of time,  please contact the Student Exchange Division by email. We strongly recommend that COLABS applicants give him/her as much advance notice as possible so that there is enough time to complete the online application (TAO) by the deadline. No immediate responses can be expected especially for last-minute requests. Also, it is often the case that even if you ask your first-choice professor, you will be refused and you will have to find a second-choice or third-choice professor. It will take longer than you think to get the consent.
Q3. I was unable to complete the TAO application by the deadline, but I have already obtained consent.
What should I do?
A
: Unfortunately, your application will be disqualified, regardless of your advisor's consent.
Please note that we do not accept incomplete or late applications.
Q4. Can I re-upload my documents and/or make modifications to my application on TAO after the deadline? I have obtained consent but have found some mistakes on my application.
A
: No. If your application is deemed incomplete it will be disqualified, regardless of your advisor's consent.
Q5. I was unable to obtain consent within the consent of acceptance request application period, can I still apply for COLABS? Is there any way I can still apply for COLABS?
A
:No. Your advisor's consent is required to complete during the consent of acceptance request application period and then your online application on TAO by the deadline. We do not accept incomplete or late applications.
Q6. Can I send several consent request forms to the COLABS email account at once?
A: No. COLABS applicants are only allowed to contact one prospective academic advisor at a time.
Q7. When should I discuss my research plans with my prospective advisor?
A
: You will have a meeting with them before the semester starts in March/September. For details, please visit

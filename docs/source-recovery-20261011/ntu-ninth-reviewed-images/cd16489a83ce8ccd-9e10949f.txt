Finding a place to stay while studying abroad doesn't have to be a difficult task. Tec de Monterrey offers different housing alternatives to meet the needs, desires, budget, and lifestyle of each student.
From student residences to independent housing, each campus has different options to offer.
Tec Residence Halls
More than a room, it's like being at home.
Residences Halls managed by Tec de Monterrey providing a suitable environment for national and international students. With different types of buildings and amenities in each, all the options are equipped to satisfy the needs of everyday student life.

Host Family Program
Discover the hospitality of a Mexican family, experience their customs and authentic Mexican cuisine, in addition to practicing your Spanish every day.
This option gives you the opportunity to live in a Mexican home where you will learn how family members interact, being exposed to different aspects of Mexican culture while developing in a linguistically rich environment.
Different families are ready to open the door of their house and their hearts to you.

Compulsory health insurance in Switzerland 
Information for persons living in Switzerland for the 
purpose of basic/continuing education and training 
Version dated August 2022 
In principle, any person who lives or works in Switzerland is required to have health insurance in Switzer-
land. A person is required to take out basic compulsory insurance under the Health Insurance Act (HIA) 
with a Swiss health insurance company within three months of arriving in Switzerland. However, special 
provisions apply to persons who are staying in Switzerland for the purpose of basic/continuing ed-
ucation and training. You will find further information on this under “Ausnahmen von der Versicher-
ungspflicht” at www.be.ch/asv. 
In the Canton of Bern, the Social Insurance Office (ASV) is responsible for en-
suring compliance with the obligation to have health insurance. 
What do you have to do? 
After you have registered with your commune of residence in the Canton of Bern, you must complete the 
form in our online tool (www.be.ch/onlinetool) in order to confirm compliance with your obligation to have 
health insurance. To do this you need your Swiss social insurance number (756.xxxx.xxxx.xx). If you do 
not know what this is, please contact your commune of residence. 
Do you have any further questions or do you need to contact us? 
If you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact us. If you do, please remember to 
have your social insurance number (756.xxxx.xxxx.xx) to hand.

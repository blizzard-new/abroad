What is Campus Immersion?
Join a dynamic, immersive English-learning experience at one of the top universities in the U.S.! The Global Launch English Language Program is hosted on ASU’s vibrant Tempe campus, located in Phoenix, Arizona—the fifth-largest city in the U.S.
Program details
Whether you are looking for short-term English study or a longer academic experience, our flexible program offers options to meet your needs. Choose between:
Full-time study¹
Part-time study
Attend one 4- or 8-week session; or continue through the program to earn your certificate

visa or immigration status
. F-1 students must study full time. Students with a B-2 tourist visa or ESTA can study part time. Contact us to learn which study options are available based on your visa or immigration status.

How you will learn
In this program, you will attend weekly on-campus classes, join social activities and complete self-study. These help you build and improve your English skills. You will also learn with classmates from many countries.
Homework and practice:
Complete weekly assignments. Reinforce classroom learning. Build steady progress over time.
Ongoing support:
Get help from full-time support staff. Access advising, tutoring and student services. Prepare for university and career success.
Campus experience:
Use ASU facilities and resources. Join clubs and attend events. Practice English outside the classroom.
Social activities and field trips:
Join cultural and social activities. Attend events, conversation groups and trips. Practice English in real-world settings.
Admission requirements
Eligibility
The Global Launch Campus Immersion English Language Program is designed for English language learners at any skill level.
Campus Immersion Requirements:
Applicants must at least be 18 years old OR 17 years old with proof of high school graduation and parental consent.
Applicants are
not required
to take the TOEFL, IELTS, PTE or any other English proficiency exam for admission into the Global Launch Campus Immersion English Language Program.
For more information about the admission requirements, see
How to Apply
.
English skills you will improve
You will build confidence in academic and social communication and collaborate on team projects. You will also develop strong academic writing skills and deliver clear and professional presentations.
Throughout the program, you will strengthen key English skills, including:
Speaking:
Share ideas in discussions. Present information clearly.
Listening:
Understand lectures, conversations and multimedia content.
Reading:
Interpret academic and everyday texts with greater accuracy.
Writing:
Write organized essays, reports and professional emails.
Grammar and vocabulary:
Use clear and accurate language with confidence.

Our instructors have supported over 2.5 million learners from more than 160 countries. They teach through classroom learning, online programs and cultural experiences.

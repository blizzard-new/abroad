Incoming Exchange Student Application Materials 
1. Online OGE StudyAway Exchange Program Application
• Undergraduate, Graduate, and Intensive English Language Exchange Options
2. Undergraduate/ Graduate Application for Admission to UGA (no application fee)
• Student must be in good standing and recommended by home university
3. Official transcript or grade report in English from home university
4. Proof of English language proficiency (minimum scores)
• TOEFL iBT: 80 (for graduate-min. sub-score of 20 in speaking and writing)
• IELTS: 6.5 (for graduate-no sub-score below 6.0)
• Cambridge International Examinations: "D" (undergrad only)
5. Medical History and Immunization Forms
6. Copy of Passport
7. Course Request Form
8. On-Campus Housing Application, if desired (online after acceptance)
9. DS-2019 request for J-1 visa (submit upon request after acceptance)

 Additional Information
Student Visa & UGA Immigration Services 
• After acceptance, students will request the DS-201 9 form which is needed to apply for a J-1 Student
Exchange Visa
• Plan to demonstrate required funding cost per semester for academic and personal expenses
• https://globalengagement.uga.edu/international-students
Student Housing 
• UGA On-Campus Housing: Assignments made based on availability in UGA East Campus Village (ECV)
or Brown Hall: https://housing.uga.edu/
• Off-Campus Housing: Arrangements made independently by students; https://isl.uga.edu/housing-tips/
Insurance 
• UGA Insurance Policy for International Students is required and automatically assigned
• https://hr.uga.edu/students/Mandatory_Plan/student_mandatory_plan_home/

What to expect when coming to Bonn
 ▶ Wide variety of study programs at bachelor’s 
and master’s level (40 international master’s 
degree programs offered in English)
 ▶ International Classroom Bonn: additional 
interdisciplinary bachelor’s courses taught 
in English and open to exchange students
 ▶ Orientation weeks before the semester 
starts: Pre-arrival online info sessions, 
Free four-week intensive German language 
classes (levels: A1–B2), Free-time 
activities, and trips in the region
 ▶ Study Buddy Program with fellow 
students to help you get started in Bonn
 ▶ Guaranteed accommodation in 
single rooms or apartments
 ▶ Free in-semester German language 
classes (levels: A1–C1)
 ▶ Free-time activities such as game or cooking 
nights, the international choir and much 
more offered by the International Club

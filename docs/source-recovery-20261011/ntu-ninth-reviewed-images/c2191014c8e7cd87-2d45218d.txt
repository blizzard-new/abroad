Academic Calendars
The University of South Carolina's academic year includes a fall semester beginning
                        in August, a spring semester beginning in January and multiple summer sessions beginning
                        in May. The University Registrar sets the academic calendar for each semester at least
                        five years in advance.
Current and Future Calendars
The University Registrar establishes the official calendar that is followed by the University
                                    of South Carolina system. Access the full current academic calendar as well as future
                                    academic calendars to find key dates
and information including holidays, registration dates, payment deadlines, drop or
                                    add dates, exams and commencement for each term.

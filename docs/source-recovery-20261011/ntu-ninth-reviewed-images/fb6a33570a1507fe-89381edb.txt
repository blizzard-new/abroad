Accessible Housing Accommodation (AHA)
If you need an accessible housing accommodation, here’s what you can find in the residence halls. In general, these rooms fall in two categories: Fully-Loaded Mobility Rooms and Rooms that offer services for deaf students. See more information below.
Note:
We don’t have as many AHA needs as we have rooms with AHA features. If you find that you are assigned to an AHA room, but you don’t need these services, certain features will be there, but will not be active
(i.e. strobes will never flash.)
How to Request Accessible Housing Accommodations
UNCG will consider reasonable housing accommodations for disabled students. A student with a disability-related need for a modified housing assignment must follow this process:
Submit the
Request for Accessible Housing Accommodation Form
, available during the housing application period.
Provide sufficient documentation from a qualified professional addressing any physical or mental impairment that substantially limits one or more major life activities, as well as your unique housing needs to the
Office of Accessibility Resources & Services
. This can be uploaded in the Welcome Form section of the
OARS Portal
, or the
Student Files
section, if the Welcome Form has already been submitted.
Students requesting an
Emotional Support Animal
must follow this same process.
Contact OARS at
OARS@uncg.edu
or 336.334.5440, to schedule a virtual or in-person consultation to discuss your needs.
The deadline for requesting modified housing accommodations is the same as the deadline for guaranteed housing.
Modified housing needs are accommodated on a case-by-case basis, according to room availability, and
must be requested to housing annually
. Students requesting modified housing accommodations will be notified of the decision in writing.
AHA Accommodation Details
1) Fully-loaded mobility rooms,
including combinations of the following services:
Push Pads
Door Bell buttons
Door strobes
Automatic doors
Card readers instead of keys
Fire strobes
Roll-in showers
Lowered counters
Mass notification strobes
Elevator access
Laundry and kitchen accessibility
2) Rooms that offer services for deaf students,
including combinations of the following services:
Door Bell buttons
Fire alarm strobes
Mass notification strobes
Door strobes
Emotional Support Animals (ESA)
To get an accessible housing accommodation for an Emotional Support Animal, you must follow the Accessible Housing Accommodation process as outlined above.
If you are approved for an ESA
. You will be invited to a meeting to review and sign the ESA agreement. After that meeting, you will be allowed to bring the ESA to your room. You may be asked to provide updated documentation to remain in accordance with policy.

Housing for Transgender students
HRL is committed to working to meet the housing needs of all residential students by providing a nurturing community that promotes the dignity of all community members.
If you have housing needs based on gender identity or gender expression, please contact Guy Sanders or Mary Steele (
gmsander@uncg.edu
/
mesteeele@uncg.edu
or 336.334.5636) when applying for campus housing to discuss your specific needs.

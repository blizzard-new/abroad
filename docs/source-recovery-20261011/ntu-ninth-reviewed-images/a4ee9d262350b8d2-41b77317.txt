Maple [program]
Maple -- Outline
The
Maple Program
was designed by the
Center for Japanese Language and Culture
(CJLC) to help students enrolled in an
institution with an exchange agreement with OU
improve their language skills, enhance their knowledge of Japanese culture and society, and assist them in advancing towards their respective academic goals — in short, to develop individuals with an in-depth understanding of Japan, able to compare and contrast, from a variety of perspectives, aspects of Japanese society, culture, and language with their own mother tongue and cultural background.
The enrollment status of students at Osaka University
Special auditors: students take classes and can earn credits
Required language ability
Japanese Language Aptitude Test Grade N4 or equivalent
Duration
From September to August (12 months). Orientation held in September. Classes commerce in October.

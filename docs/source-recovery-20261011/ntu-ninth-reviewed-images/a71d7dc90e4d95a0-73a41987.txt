Tokyo International Exchange Center 
 Housing Information for International Students 
 
For international students, application is now open for Tokyo International Exchange Center (TIEC) 
provided by Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO) as follows. After nomination by Meiji 
University, residents will be officially decided through the paper screening by JASSO. 
 
1. Location: Tokyo Academic Park, 2-2-1 Aomi, Koto-ku, Tokyo 135-8630 Japan 
 
2. Access: 3 minutes walk from "Tokyo International Cruise Terminal"station on "Yurikamome" line 
             15 minutes walk from "Tokyo Teleport" station on "Rinkai" line 
            
3. Qualifications: 
Students who are able to commute to University  from TIEC and those students who meet the 
following criteria; 
(1) Students who have the visa status of “College Student” 
(2) Capable international students who are in Graduate School (including research students who 
have already accepted unconditional offer of study from University), aren’t attending beyond  
the term of study (in this case, military service is excluded).  
*In each case,  however, only those who are expected to meet the criteria and have evidence 
documents from expected university can apply for TIEC. The period of residence will be permitted 
from 30 days before becoming students of Meiji University. 
 
4. Available Rooms: 
(1) Single Room in Hall A and B: For applicant only 
(2) Couple room / Family room in Hall C and D: For applicant, his/  her spouse and their 
children.*Other relatives are not permitted to move in. (Only one pre-school child is allowed to 
move into Hall C.) 
(3) Couple room in Hall C and Family room in Hall D are for the applicant and his/her spouse, or 
applicant, his/her spouse and their children. Also they are required to live at all times. 
(4) No one is allowed to stay in the accommodation without permission (even relatives or friends).  
 
5. Application process and Required Documents:  
     One application is to be completed per person. Applying with inmate’s name or applying for plural 
rooms is strictly prohibited. 
(1) Tokyo International Exchange Center Application (University Form, for international students) 
(2) Application for Tokyo International Exchange Center (JASSO Form 1-1) 
(3) The proposal for implementation / participation of exchange activities at TIEC (JASSO Form 3) 
(4) Application for Tokyo International Exchange Center (JASSO Form 14-1) 
(5) Document which can proof your reside period (Photocopy of your student ID card or Admission 
Letter) 
(6) Certificate of Enrollment (you can issue it with a certificate issuing machine at each campuses)  
(7) Photocopy of passport (including photo and landing permit pages, both applicant and his/  her 
family) 
(8) [Exclude first coming] Photocopy of Resident Card (both sides, both applicant and his/  her 
family) 
(9) Documents proving your date of entry permission ( Passport or Resident Card with “Entrance 
Permission”, applicant only) 
(10) [Inmate] Official document certifying the family relationship with applicant 
Required document: a copy of one’s family register or a resident’s card (issued within 3 
months)

7. Application process and Result announcement: 
After nomination by the university, the candidates will be screened based on the following criteria by 
JASSO. The final result will be announced from the university. 
(1) Those who can proactively participate in various international exchange events organized by 
TIEC 
(2) Those who came to Japan within one year will be prioritized.  
(3) Candidates’ nationality may be considered in order to welcome as various nationalities as 
possible. 
(4) Candidates’ university may be considered in order to welcome as various universities as 
possible. 
(5) Expected long-term residents may be prioritized. 
(6) Number of Japanese resident assistants at home university may be considered 
(7) Students receiving education by the government policies, etc. may be prioritized.  
 
8. Move in Requirements: 
(1) The period will be within the term of study, or less than three years. Also, if the candidate had 
moved in TIEC before, previous years will be included in three years . For Couple room and 
Family room in Hall C and D, their inmates’ previous moving in years is included, too. (the 
longer one will be adopted) 
(2) Permission for moving in and extending the term: 
Permission for moving in and extending the term limit will be one year, renewing per one year.  
However, students whose period of residence is end of March, August, September, students  
need to leave TIEC by 25th of each month. 
To extending the term, the participating record for events and living attitude in TIEC will be 
considered.

(4) Time of moving in for inmates [For Couple room and Family room in Hall C and D] 
Residents are required to move in with their inmates at the same time. However, if there are 
some inevitable reasons, inmates may be allowed to move in later (about less than one 
month) only if they propose beforehand. (In this case the term won’t be changed from the 
residence permission.) If the inmate couldn’t move in until the date they proposed, both 
applicant and inmate would lose their qualification for moving in. 
(5) Submission of the pledge and enrollment confirmation 
Students who have been accepted to move into TIEC must submit a written pledge in the form 
prescribed by the office. If you do not comply with the terms of the pledge, your eligibility will 
be revoked. Students residing in TIEC are required to sign the enrollment confirmation book at 
International Student Office once a month. When signing, you need to show a document 
which can prove that you are paying the rent.

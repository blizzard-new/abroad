Hall Administration and Residential Education
The Hall Administration and Residential Education Team of the Student Resources and Support Section (SRSS) of the Student Affairs Office (SAO) is responsible for all matters related to hall admission, administration and residential education in the Student Halls of Residence. Working together with wardens and tutors, we aim at providing a comfortable and enriching living and learning environment for our students. Apart from ensuring adequate support in areas of housekeeping and security, a wide variety of programmes are organized to broaden residents’ horizons and to facilitate their all-rounded development
.

Student Halls of Residence (Homantin)
Comprising of two residential colleges and four individual halls, Homantin Halls accommodates over 1600 students in total. Each residential college and individual hall features a distinctive theme. Together, they form a living-learning community for our students to grow and develop.
Residential college is overseen by a College Master, a number of Resident Fellows and Tutors while there are one Warden and 5 Tutors in each individual hall.
College Master or Hall Warden is a University staff member appointed to provide pastoral care to student residents, ensuring their general well-being and compliance with hall-related regulations and policies. He/She is also responsible for maintaining a safe and healthy environment conducive to learning and development, as well as providing guidance to hall activities in the respective hall community.
Resident Fellows are academic staff appointed to provide training, coaching and mentoring for residents according to the themes of their respective residential college.
Hall Tutors are mostly postgraduate students appointed to assist the Warden in building up a harmonious and yet vibrant hall community. Living on the student floors and serving as the first contact point with the student residents, the hall tutors play an active part in the social life of the hall community and help enforce hall discipline.
Please feel free to talk to your Master/Warden and Resident Fellows/Tutors whenever you need them for advice and assistance. They are always ready to listen to you.

Introduction
The establishment of the Homantin Halls of Residence marks a new page of the development of student residence of the University. Comprised of 6 individual halls, the 25-storey Hall accommodates about 1,600 students.
It provides single room, double and triple rooms to suit the needs of different students. The design of 9-person-suite facilitates the formation of small communities among residents and thus encourages interaction and integration, while offer adequate privacy to students. A small communal space is included in each unit to offer students a platform for mingling and interaction.
There are 3 Sky Gardens, each shared by and linking 2 individual halls. The Sky Garden is a semi outdoor space which encourages interflow among different halls and offers a pleasant environment for leisure. Apart from the Sky Gardens, other outdoor space includes Podium Garden and Entrance Plaza, all of which are tastefully landscaped to create a pleasant living environment. Besides, the Central Student Lounges in each hall, communal areas at corridor ends and living rooms in each cluster are also great platforms for students’ interflow.
A. Location:
The Homantin Halls is conveniently located in the residential area of Homantin, within 15-minute walking distance to the Lee Shau Kee Building on campus. It is next to the Homantin MTR Station of the Shatin to Central Link which is currently under construction.

Transportation Guide for Non-local Students Just Arriving Hong Kong
1. From HK International Airport to Homantin Halls
By Bus:
Take Bus Route A22 at the Airport, get off at the 8th stop Valley Road and walk for 260m, about 4 minutes to reach the Homantin Halls (see map route
here
). The bus journey is about 45-60 minutes.
By Taxi:
Take a taxi from the airport and ask the driver to take you to the Homantin Halls (address: 15 Fat Kwong Street, Homantin).  The journey will take around 45-60 minutes and cost about HK$250 – 300, including toll fees for tunnels.  Surcharges of HK$5 will be charged per piece of luggage.
By Airport Express:
Take the Airport Express from the airport to the MTR Kowloon Station.  The journey will take around 21 minutes and cost HK$90.  Then, take a taxi from the Kowloon Station to the Homantin Halls.  It will take around 15-20 minutes and cost around HK$30 – 50.  Surcharges of HK$5 will be charged per piece of luggage.
2. From Railway Stations to Homantin Halls
If you come to Hong Kong by railway, you may get off at the MTR Homantin Station of the Kwun Tong Line(Green Line). Homantin Halls is situated opposite to Exit A1 (Crossing Yan Fung Street).

Residence Tours
Welcome to SFU Residence and Housings Tours page. There's a few ways for you to experience a tour at SFU. If you are looking for an in-person, on-campus tour experience, please visit the SFU Tours and Events page to book an all-campus (including residences tours). Please note that ALL residence tours are booked through the SFU Tours page. Residence and Housing does not provide additional tours. If you would prefer a digital tour, you can either visit the SFU Tours page, or, visit our Housing Options page, select the building you are most interested in, and watch videos of the building and what a typical room in residences looks like

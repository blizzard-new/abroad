ESTIMATED COSTS OF ATTENDANCE FOR AN ACADEMIC YEAR
The estimated Cost of Attendance Budget (COA) represents the estimated cost of attending Hawaii Pacific University based on full-time enrollment. The purpose of this budget is to provide you, the student, with an idea of what your expenses might be to attend.
For actual (direct) costs visit HPU’s
Tuition and Fees
and
On-Campus Housing
.
A variety of expenses are included in the Cost of Attendance, such as:
tuition
registration and other required fees
books
living expenses
transportation
an estimate of personal expenses related to attending school
The COA is only an estimate based on the average expenses of all students. These costs, therefore, do not necessarily represent every expense a student might incur throughout a given year. Your actual experience may be different from these averages based on your own personal spending choices and on how many courses you take each semester.
Note:
The institution will adjust the estimates based on a student's enrollment status throughout the year. The Federal Government allows a variety of expenses to be included, but not all.
The estimated budget is based on a student being enrolled full-time for
two
(2) consecutive semesters for our regular undergraduate and graduate programs.
The Physician Assistant, Doctorate of Physical Therapy, and Doctorate of Occupational Therapy programs are budgeted for
three
(3) consecutive semesters for their 2-year program.

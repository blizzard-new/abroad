Banking
Although most establishments in Pittsburgh accept major credit cards as a form of payment, select restaurants or smaller businesses may only accept cash.  Additionally, cash is often the preferred mode of tender when paying gratuity or "tips." As such, incoming students may want to open a bank account at one of the local banks here in Pittsburgh.  For convenience, students should note that the William Pitt Union located on Pitt's Pittsburgh campus has ATM machines with
Citizens Bank
and
PNC Bank
.  Students who choose to bank with PNC Bank will have the ability to
link the Panther Card to the PNC Bank Account
and use it as an ATM card.
PittPay
Pitt only issues electronic bills, so therefore all payments to Pitt are made through an online platform known as PittPay.  Through PittPay, incoming students may assign "Authorized Payers," such as a parent or guardian.  See
PittPay's website
for more details as well as a user guide and instructions on how to make international payments.
Financial Resources
Offered through the Student Payment Center, there are financial literacy resources provided to all Pitt students.  These include tools on creating a budget, understanding credit reports and protecting your information.  For details, go to the
Finanicial Literarcy Resources
page.

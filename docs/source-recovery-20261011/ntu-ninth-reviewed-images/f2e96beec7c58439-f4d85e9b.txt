For international students entering Japan
Updated on May 9, 2023
To ensure your smooth entry to Japan under the current border control measures, we provide information on the procedures required for your entry on this page.
The border control measures may change depending on the COVID-19 situation. Please also keep yourself updated with the latest information from reliable sources.

1. Current Border Control Measures (Updated on April 28, 2023)
Those arriving in Japan on or after April 29, 2023 are not required to bring a valid vaccination certificate or pre-departure COVID-19 test certificate. For more details, please refer to the link below.
Japanese Border Measures | MHLW
2. Visit Japan Web (Updated on May 9, 2023)
The Visit Japan Web service allows you to complete immigration and customs declaration procedures online. In accordance with the Japanese government’s policy,
Tohoku University ask all international students to use this service
.
3. Support Services
To help students start their life in Japan smoothly, the International Support Center provides various support services including assistance with ward office procedures and opening a bank account.

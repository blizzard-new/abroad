Did you know if your home university has a formal
exchange/study abroad agreement with SFU you can access
innovation and entrepreneurship (I&E) courses and unique co-
curricular opportunities through SFU Beedie’s Chang Institute? 
The Charles Chang Institute for Entrepreneurship (Chang
Institute) is the interdisciplinary home and academic hub for
unleashing the entrepreneurial mindset and community at Simon
Fraser University.  
Combine I&E courses with other disciplines at SFU, or dive into a
fully immersive semester in entrepreneurship, it’s up to you!

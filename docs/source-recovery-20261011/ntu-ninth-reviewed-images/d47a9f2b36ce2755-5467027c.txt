Medical Coverage
we will pay 70% of patient costs for medical clinics up to a maximum of one million KRW per year. In case of accidents that occur on campus or during university-organized events, we pay 100% of patient costs up to a maximum of 3 million KRW per year.
Disability benefit
In case of accidents that occur on campus or during university-organized events which result in disability, we offer graded payment of up toa maximum of 10 million KRW per year based on disability rating(Under Public Officials Pension Act, section 45).
Fatalities
We provide payment of up to 2 million KRW to the bereaved family. In case of accidents that occur on campus or during university-organized events which result in death, we offer payment of up to 20 million KRW.

Due to the University Festival, classes on Friday, October 30 and Monday, November 2 will be recessed. (Tsukuba campus)
To meet the required number of school days, some classes will be irregularly held on the days stated below. (Tsukuba campus)
April 30: Wednesday classes
May 8: Monday classes
July 23: Monday classes
October 15: Monday classes
November 24: Wednesday classes
January 13: Monday classes
Classes on Wednesday, November 25 - Friday, November 27 will be recessed due to the Examination for University Admissions upon Recommendation (Suisen nyūshi shiken) and its related events. (Tsukuba campus)
Classes on Friday, January 15 and Monday, January 18 will be recessed due to the Common Test for University Admissions (Daigaku nyūgaku kyoutsū test) and its related events. (Tsukuba campus)
(Tsukuba campus)
For the College of Medicine, the University will schedule March 10, 2027 as the date of graduation, but the commencement ceremony will be held on the date scheduled above.
For the Law School Program, the University will schedule March 15, 2027 as the date of completion, but the commencement ceremony will be held on the date scheduled above.
One academic year contains two semesters (spring semester and fall semester) which includes six modules, Spring A, Spring B, Spring C, Fall A, Fall B and Fall C. Each module
has five weeks.

Applying as a student
Would you like to live in one of our dormitory? Then you can apply online. The application for a room in a dormitory is non-binding and can be sent without a certificate of enrolment or letter of admission. However, you will only receive the actual authorisation to live in a room at the Studierendenwerk Bonn when you enrol.
An application for our dormitories is only possible via the application form on our homepage. Please follow the button ‘Apply online’.
Applications will be processed in the order in which they are received and according to the availability of places in the dormitories. Please understand that, due to the allocation procedure, we are unable to give an exact waiting time or provide interim information on the status of applications. Please note that an application does not constitute a legal claim to the conclusion of a tenancy agreement.
We start the monthly rental process approx. 4-6 weeks before the respective move-in date until all vacant units have been rented. If we are able to offer you a room on the desired date, you will receive a notification by e-mail. As the demand for student accommodation is very high, we are often unable to offer all applicants a room at the desired time.
If we are unable to offer you a room or flat on your desired move-in date, we will keep you on our waiting list. You will be asked by e-mail every month whether you are still interested in a place in a hall of residence. To do this, you must activate a link in the e-mail sent to you in order to continue to take part in the allocation process. Please also check your spam folder regularly in case the e-mail ends up there.
Tip: Experience has shown that the chances of getting a place before the start of the semester are better. If this option is suitable for you, for example to get to know your new university town, you can apply for the move-in date 1st of August. It is generally possible to apply for a place in a dormitory without being admitted by the university yet.

The Studierendenwerk Bonn operates a total of 29 residential units with around 3,781 places at the locations Bonn, Sankt Augustin and Rheinbach (as of 2024). German and foreign students enrolled at the University of Bonn or the University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg as regular students or doctoral students are eligible for accommodation.
Our offer includes single rooms in shared flats, apartments, double apartments and two- to four-room apartments for studying parents and single parents. The rents include all incidental costs such as electricity and heating as well as broadband Internet access - with the exception of family flats, which receive a statement of operating costs and have to register electricity with an electricity supplier.

Apply as an intern
Are you studying and completing an internship at a company in Bonn or the Rhein-Sieg district? Or are you expecting interns from abroad in your company? The Studierendenwerk Bonn offers foreign students a furnished room in a residential complex for the duration of their internship. The prerequisite is that the company in which the internship is being completed is located in Bonn.
Leases are agreed upon for full months. The start of the rental period is the first working day of the month and the end of the rental period is the last day of the week.
How to apply:
Please send your application including a certificate of enrollment from the university or technical college, as well as a copy of the contract with the internship company by e-mail to
wohnen@studierendenwerk-bonn.de
. Please do not forget to include the details of your desired period of residence and your exact postal address. There will be no acknowledgement of receipt. If it is not possible to offer you a dorm, your application will automatically be considered for the following month.
Do you have any questions? Visit our office or give us a call.

Life in the dormitory
Living in the dormitories of the Studierendenwerk means being part of a community – no matter if you are in a single room, an apartment or a flat. The student self-administration ensures that joint activities are undertaken and that residents can get involved in them. Common rooms are available for this purpose. Additionally, there are washing machines and dryers, the use of which is included in the rent.
Tip: Experience has shown that free accommodation units are already available before the beginning of winter term. If this option is suitable for you, for example to get to know your new university town, you can apply for the for the move-in date 1st of August. It is generally possible to apply for a place in a dormitory without being admitted by the university yet.
Tip: Another highlight is the high-speed Internet connection (1000 Mbit/s), which is available in all residential complexes. The costs for this as well as all other additional costs (e.g. energy, water, dispose) are already included in the rent. The prerequisite is that residents are cost-conscious and environmentally friendly regarding energy, water and waste. Rent depends on the type of housing, room size, furnishing and location.

What is a single room?
You have your own room, but share the sanitary facilities as well as the kitchen with other students.
What is an apartment?
You have your own room with a private bathroom and small kitchenette.
What's a semi-apartment?
You have your own room and bathroom, but share the kitchen with other students.
What is a double room?
You have your own room and share a bathroom with one student. The kitchen is shared by several students.
What is a double apartment?
You have your own room and share both the bathroom and kitchen with one other student.
Are the genders in the shared flats or corridor communities mixed?
Yes, there is no separation by gender in the shared flats or corridors.
How are the individual rooms furnished?
The furnished rooms are usually equipped with a bed, wardrobe, desk, chair and a shelf.
How to apply?
An application for our dormitories is only possible via the application form. Applications sent by email cannot be considered.
The more dormitories and room types you select in your application, the better your chances of getting a place in a dormitory.
Interns: Please note the separate information regarding the application process.
How are offers allocated and the length of stay determined?
In accordance with Section 2 (1) No. 1 of the Act on Student Services Organisations in the State of North Rhine-Westphalia (Studierendenwerksgesetz – StWG) in the version published on 16 September 2014 in conjunction with Section 2 (2) No. 2 of the currently valid statutes of the Studierendenwerk Bonn – Anstalt des öffentlichen Rechts (public institution) – one of the statutory tasks of the Studierendenwerk Bonn is to create, rent and arrange accommodation for students. In order to take into account the fact that not all students looking for accommodation can be provided with a place in a hall of residence by the Bonn Student Services Organisation, the principle of rotation pursued in these allocation guidelines is intended to ensure that as many students as possible are provided with affordable accommodation.

Will I receive an acknowledgement of receipt or an interim reply?
If you have applied for a place in one of our dormitories, you will receive an automated e-mail with the details of your application. The application must be confirmed by you via the confirmation link contained in this e-mail. Unfortunately, this e-mail often ends up in the spam folder. Please move it to the normal inbox to access the link. Please note that you will not receive any intermediate information on the status of your application. However, once a month the system sends an automated e-mail with a link, which you must confirm within 5 days if you are still interested in the application.
How many offers will I receive?
Generally, only one offer will be sent per application. Please note that the application is automatically cancelled when an offer is made. If you do not wish to accept the offer and are still looking for an accommodation unit, you can send us a new application.
How long is the application valid?
Provided that you confirm our automated e-mail on a monthly basis, an application for a place in a hall of residence is valid for a maximum of 12 months and is automatically cancelled when the deadline expires. If you only apply for the apartment type, and perhaps only in very popular residential buildings, your application may fail due to the maximum validity period of 12 months.
I do not have a matriculation number yet. Can I still apply?
The application for a room in a dormitory is non-binding and can be sent without a certificate of enrolment or letter of admission. If you are a new student, please note this in your application in the corresponding field.
Are students from another university or technical college also allowed to live with us?
Students from other universities are only allowed to live with us during internships that take place as part of their studies.
This is what we need:
The exact dates, when and for how long you need a room
A copy of the matriculation certificate and a copy of the internship certificate
Your address
You will not receive an acknowledgement of receipt. If we are able to offer you a room for the desired period, we will send you a rental offer about four weeks before the desired start of the rental period.
How can I apply for a preferential readmission after a stay abroad?
Studierendenwerk Bonn gives residents the opportunity to return to a dormitory in the event of a study-related interruption of the rental period after completion of the stay abroad or an internship of at least six and no more than twelve months.
Tenants who wish to take advantage of this rule must cancel their accommodation and apply for resumption in due time. Please apply online and make a note in the information field that this is a preferential resumption. Proof of internship or study abroad for study-related reasons must be attached to the application.
Applicants have the option of specifying alternative housing requests. However, the previously occupied dormitory - as far as this is possible due to available rooms - will be offered as a matter of priority! If a rental offer is not accepted, the entitlement to preferential admission lapses.
Extensions of residence
In order to give as many students as possible the opportunity to live inexpensively, the period of residence in the dormitories of the Studierendenwerk is limited to six semesters. Applicants who have previously stayed in a dormitory will be credited with the new tenancy. An extension of the rental period is generally not possible. However, the tenancy can be extended in exceptional cases. The reasons must be given in writing.
Is there an age limit?
No.

Do I have to fill out a direct debit authorisation?
When concluding a contract, you must always issue a direct debit authorisation. This is contractually regulated by us. Exceptions are holiday tenants as well as guest tenants.
How are the rents calculated?
Rents in the dormitories of Studierendenwerk Bonn (StwB) are generally calculated in accordance with the so-called
II. Berechnungsverordnung
(Ordinance on Housing Economic Calculations according to the Second Housing Act).
The StwB is an institution under public law, i.e. a social service enterprise in the public sector. Additionally, the StwB is classified as a non-profit organisation.
The legal basis for its activities is the
Studierendenwerksgesetz NRW
. Accordingly, the dormitories are to be managed in such a way that the income covers the costs (
efficiency principle
). In January 2018, the StwB provided detailed information on the rent calculation. Click here for a summary
documentation
.

When do move-ins and move-outs take place?
Moving in and moving out takes place from Monday to Friday. It is best to arrange an appointment with your property manager.
Where can I get the keys?
After concluding the contract, you should make an appointment with your property manager. You will receive your keys there.
How much ist the security deposit?
Tenants have to pay a deposit before finalising the contract. This is approximately 1.5 to 2 times the rental price. The deposit does not bear interest (according to § 551, Abs. 3 BGB).
Are there washing and drying facilities in the dormitory?
In every dormitory there are available washing machines and dryers. The use is covered by the flat rate amount of 4.00 euros per month.
Are utilities included in the rent?
With us, you pay
all-inclusive rents
: Thus additional costs, e.g. for electricity, gas, water, waste, Internet, etc. are already included in the rent. There are no additional charges at the end of the year. Due to public subsidies, the rents are usually below the market level.
Is internet access included?
Our dormitories are all equipped with a high-speed Internet connection. The costs are included in the rent.
I'm from abroad. What do I need to bring with me?
Basic equipment such as blankets, pillows, bed linen, towels, pots, pans, cutlery, plates and glasses do not need to be brought from afar. For 60 Euro you can buy the
Dorm Basic Set
, which contains these things.
May I sublet my room?
Subletting is not permitted. Please note that this is a reason for the cancellation of your lease.
Are there washing and drying facilities in the dormitory?
In every dormitory there are available washing machines and dryers. The use is covered by the flat rate amount of 4.00 euros per month.
How long can I live in a student dormitory?
In order to give as many students as possible the opportunity to live inexpensively, the period of residence in the dormitories of the Studierendenwerk is limited to six to eight semesters (family flats). An extension is only possible in exceptional cases.
What are the notice periods?
As an exception, the tenancy can be terminated by the tenant before the end of the contractual period on 28.02., 31.03., 31.08. and 30.09. of a year. In addition, termination is only possible if special circumstances are explained and verified.
In both cases, termination must be declared in writing no later than the third working day of a calendar month at the end of the month after next. The date of receipt by the landlord is crucial for meeting the deadline.
Example: If you want to terminate your rental agreement on 31.03.2023, you must submit your notice of termination to the Accommodation Department by 04.01.2023 at the latest. In this example, 04.01.2023 is the third working day of the month decisive for maintaining the deadline. To ensure that the deadline is met, we recommend sending the notice of termination as early as possible.
Is moving allowed?
It is not possible to move from one dormitory to another without a transitional period. You must cancel your current room, are not allowed to be a tenant of the Studierendenwerk for at least one month and can then apply again. Please note that a new application is not binding and does not entitle you to accommodation.
May I sublet my room?
Subletting is not permitted. Please note that this is a reason for the cancellation of your lease.
When do I get my deposit back?
The deposit or the unclaimed parts of the deposit will be transferred to the tenant by the landlord after departure and the return of rental objects to an account to be designated by the tenant. Any costs incurred, e.g. damage to the living space, bank charges, etc. will be deducted from the deposit. The deposit will be paid / transferred three months after the end of the rental period. The deposit does not bear interest (according to § 551, Abs. 3 BGB).
When do move-ins and move-outs take place?
Moving in and moving out takes place from Monday to Friday. It is best to arrange an appointment with your property manager.

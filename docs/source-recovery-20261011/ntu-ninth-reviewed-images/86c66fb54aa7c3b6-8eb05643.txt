Application Procedures for Exchange Programs
1. Program Information
Tokyo University of Foreign Studies has International Student Exchange Program of Tokyo University of Foreign Studies: ISEPTUFS.
The ISEPTUFS program is a semester or a one-year short-term exchange program designed for international students. The program provides students with the opportunity to study in Japan and helps them to acquire a firm understanding of Japan and the international community. Meanwhile, the program aims to promote meaningful cultural exchange and to contribute to the cultivation of outstanding professionals with international backgrounds.
Please visit the web link below for more detailed information about the programs:
https://www.tufs.ac.jp/english/admission/non-degree/exchange/
2. Application Procedure
The online applications for the exchange program and COE (Certificate of Eligibility) should be completed by yourself. The application documents should be sent by your home university via designated online form.(We do not accept the documents submitted by the students.)
Deadline for Spring Entry ： November 15th (previous year)
Deadline for Fall Entry ： March 15th
*Late, ineligible or incomplete application will not be processed.

2.0/4.0 or higher
*GPA should be calculated using the grades of the previous semester at the time of application.For the details, refer to the fact sheet.
Coordinators who have any questions about conversion to a 4.0 scale GPA, please contact TUFS Student Exchange Division (it is preferable to contact us before applying). TUFS will not respond to GPA inquiries from applicants.
6. About scholarships
The Ministry of Education of Japan supports the Japan Students Service Organization (JASSO) Scholarship, which international exchange students coming to TUFS receive each year. Basic information regarding the scholarship program is provided below. Please note that the chances of being chosen as the recipient of JASSO scholarship are very low.
Student Eligibility
Students from all countries that have diplomatic relations with Japan.（Students from Taiwan and Palestine are acceptable. Students with Japanese nationality (including dual nationality) are not eligible.）
Students accepted by Japanese school under a student exchange agreement or other student exchange arrangement with their home school.
Students not having the financial means to study in Japan.
Students eligible for "Student Visa". （For study in Japan for 90 days or less, Japanese school will arrange the status of residence.）
Students, upon termination of the period of study in Japan, will complete their studies at their home school.
Students demonstrating excellent academic and personal records at their home school.
Students receiving more than 80,000 JPY per month in total from other scholarships for study in Japan are ineligible for this scholarship.
When students are selected as a scholarship recipient, they must submit all required documents to TUFS during their exchange program and when they finish their program at TUFS.
Students must send their transcript to TUFS when they transfer the credits obtained at TUFS to their home university after they finish the exchange program. If there are incomplete or missing procedures or documents, students may be required to return the money to JASSO.
7. Dormitory
Presently we have three dormitories on campus of the university. However, the possibility exists that students cannot receive accommodation at these dormitories because of an increasing number of international students. Those who cannot be accommodated on campus will need to find and live in alternative apartments off campus. The average rent off campus per month is around 50,000~80,000 JPY. Please understand this fact beforehand.
For more information about on-campus dormitory, please refer to the following:
https://www.tufs.ac.jp/english/student/international_student/housing.html
Note: Check-in is only available on the designated dates and times.
- For spring entry: two days in early April
- For fall entry: two days at the end of September
Those who live in the dormitory must arrive in Japan by the check-in dates. The check-in schedule will be announced at the time of acceptance (for spring entry: December in the previous year, for fall all entry: June).

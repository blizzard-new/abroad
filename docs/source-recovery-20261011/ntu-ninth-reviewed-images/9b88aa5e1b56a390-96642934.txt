Special Interest Housing
Special Interest Housing
Special interest housing is an opportunity for undergraduate students who share a common interest to
live, learn, and grow
together. There are signature residential learning experiences based on academics and thematic interests that help establish one-on-one relationships between faculty, staff, and students in a close-knit residence hall environment. More residence hall options are available to students who apply to and are accepted by special interest housing communities. In addition to any application required for special interest housing, students must also
apply for housing.

: CONNECT Transfer Student Living Learning Community (LLC) is designed to help transfer students seamlessly transition to the campus at UNC Greensboro. By fostering a supportive environment, this community provides opportunities to build meaningful relationships with other transfer students, connect with campus resources, and get acquainted with the university culture. Through academic support, programs, social activities, and mentoring, the LLC aims to equip transfer students with the tools and confidence they need to thrive both academically and personally.

ESports and Gaming
First Year Students,
Limited spaces for selected upperclass students (SO, JR, SR)
Located in
Grogan
:
The Esports and Gaming Living Learning Community (LLC) is perfect for students who have a passion for esports or tabletop gaming. By choosing this LLC, students will have the opportunity to live and learn alongside like-minded peers, engaging in specialized events, programs, and activities designed to foster connections with fellow students, faculty, and staff. The community will also feature a faculty led programs and students will have the unique chance to game alongside faculty and staff, building meaningful relationships outside the classroom. Participants will also have exclusive access to a dedicated space equipped with tabletop games, gaming PCs, Xbox consoles, and other gaming equipment.

Health and Human Sciences
First Year Students,
Limited spaces for selected upperclass students (SO, JR, SR)
Located in
Reynolds
:
Students who participate in the Health and Human Sciences Living Learning Community should have an interest in a career in the health professions and majoring in Human Health Sciences (HHSci). Students who select into the HHS LLC will have an opportunity to live and learn together. Through our specialized activities, our students will be able to
build connections with peers, faculty, staff, advisors, alumni, and members of the community.
Our students will easily be able to
create study groups, have access to tutoring in the residence halls, enhance their academic skills, and will be able to explore topics and careers in the health and helping professions
within their own residence hall.

Studio 91
First Year Students,
Limited spaces for selected upperclass students (SO, JR, SR)
Located in
Cone
:
The arts-based living community,
“Studio 91”
is conveniently located near the Music Building and offers a wing of practice rooms for music and theater, a music composition computer lab, a drawing lounge, and a small dance practice room with a sprung floor and mirrors for all residents to access. Cone Hall is home to students interested in visual and performing arts.
Studio 91 is open to all majors and will be filled on a first come, first serve basis.

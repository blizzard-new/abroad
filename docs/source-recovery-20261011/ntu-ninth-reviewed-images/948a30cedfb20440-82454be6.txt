Exchange Specials
Exchange Specials offers international students the opportunity to gain academic insights at KIT, discover new perspectives and develop valuable intercultural skills. These programmes combine academic exchange with diverse academic and campus life, enabling students to experience KIT and Karlsruhe first-hand.
We are currently offering the following programmes:
INTL
IMPULSE
An exclusive research program in science and engineering for students from UNCC (USA), the University of Waterloo (Canada), and Queensland University of Technology (Australia).
More
INTL
GermanTECH
A semester-long programme designed for students in engineering who want to learn German and gain insight into German perspectives on technology, politics, history, and the social sciences.

The list below shows an estimate of the monthly living expenses for students in Austria. The
numbers given are only a guideline
and are
by no means binding
.
Accommodation costs are calculated on the basis of a room in a hall of residence or a shared flat.
Average cost of living per month:
Accommodation (incl. heating, electricity):
€ 450 - 700
Food:
€ 350
Study and personal requirements (book, spare time, etc.):
€ 400 - 500
Monthly expenses in total: approx. € 1400
Vienna has an
excellent public transport system
consisting of underground lines, bus services, trams and a city train network. For longer stays and frequent use of the Viennese public transport system, it is advisable to purchase one of the
Wiener Linien zone cards
.

Wiener Linien
What to do if you lose your ticket
If you bought the ticket at a
Wiener Linien service office
, first of all you need to make an official
"notice of loss"
("Verlustanzeige") or
"notice of theft"
("Diebstahlanzeige"). Then take the "notice of loss/or theft" with you to the
Wiener Linien service centre
("Kundenzentrum der Wiener Linien", Erdbergstraße 202) and get a new ticket there (administration fee: ~€ 10).
If you bought your personalised ticket via the
Wiener Linien’s online ticket shop
, you can just
print it out again yourself for free
.

list of national health doctors
(

Dentists can be found in the
list of dentists
(
Zahnärzteverzeichnis
, german only)
. In the event of an emergency, there is an
emergency service
(
Zahnarztnotdienst,
german only).

The Erasmus+ program gives special importance to the topic of the environment and combating climate change which is one of the horizontal priorities of Erasmus+. As such, students who travel by environmentally friendly means of transport receive special financial funding from their home universities through the Erasmus+ program, to name just one example for this.
Drinkable tap water from the Alps
Vienna's tap water
is of excellent quality and drinkable. The fresh spring water comes directly from the Lower Austrian-Styrian Alps to Viennese households and university buildings via spring water pipelines.
Drinking fountains in public spaces
The city of Vienna has
public drinking fountains
, which provide free drinking water in the city during the warmer months of the year.
Waste separation and waste avoidance
Residential buildings usually have bins for residual waste and waste paper (including cardboard), and some also have organic waste bins.A deposit is charged on disposable plastic and metal beverage containers (e.g. PET bottles, cans), and on some reusable glass containers (e.g. milk bottles). This deposit is refunded when the containers are returned to the retail outlet (e.g. supermarkets).All other plastic and metal packaging in Vienna is collected in the yellow bin. Clear and colored glass are collected separately in their own containers.
The Website of the City of Vienna provides information on
waste avoidance and recycling
.

Public transportation inside and outside Vienna
Vienna has a good
public transport network
.
Many surrounding countries can be easily reached from Vienna by train or bus. The
Austrian Federal Railways (ÖBB)
offer national and international train connections, including
night trains
. If you travel regularly by train, you may be able to save money with a
Vorteilscard or a "Klima Ticket".
If you have visitors in Vienna during your stay abroad, an environmentally friendly arrival and departure may also be an option for them.

Sustainability at the University of Vienna
The University of Vienna makes a sustainable contribution in the areas of research, education, outreach and in its own university management. With its wide range of disciplines and over 180 degree programs, the University of Vienna is broadly positioned and has a strong basis for dealing with these challenges in a targeted manner and promoting interdisciplinary thinking.
On the
website
Sustainability at the University of Vienna
you will find information on a wide range of topics related to sustainability at the University of Vienna (in German).
At the same time, everyone at the University can contribute to sustainability, e.g. in the areas of printing and online storage space or energy saving.

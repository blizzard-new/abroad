Impact & Prosperity Epicenter
End-Cap Communities:
End-cap Communities are the most coveted spaces on campus. They feature private sleeping quarters, exceptional views, and roomy shared spaces where the community of 20 students can gather and connect. Single-bedroom units open to expansive floor-to-ceiling windowed lounges that showcase dazzling mountain views.
Apartments (4 or 8 person):
The Epicenter brings apartment-style living to a premier on-campus location, allowing students to live together as teams or as groups of friends without sacrificing convenience. Both 4- and 8-bedroom units are available. Units feature private bedrooms with bathrooms, and a shared full kitchen and workspace, providing a more intimate setting for residents.
Classic Singles and Doubles:
Classic single and double rooms house one or two people. Residents share bathrooms, kitchens, and lounge space with adjacent residents on the floor. This housing style is a traditional option that will serve the needs of any student.

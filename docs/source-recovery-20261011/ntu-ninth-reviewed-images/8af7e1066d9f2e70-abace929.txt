Finding suitable courses for your exchange stay at RWTH can be challenging.
To help you prepare your Learning Agreement, please keep the following guidelines in mind:
At least 60% of your selected courses must be offered by your designated faculty at RWTH.
Make sure you meet the language requirements for each course. Please check the language of instruction and any specific requirements for exchange students in advance.
Choose courses at your level of study. Bachelor’s students may not enroll in Master’s-level courses. Exceptions may be granted to bachelor’s students who have completed at least 120 ECTS credits (or the equivalent) before arriving at RWTH. Please consult the faculty-specific information below for details.
Your Learning Agreement does not automatically register you for courses. Course registration takes place at the beginning of the semester via the RWTHonline platform.
If you have questions about course content, credits, language requirements, exams, transcripts, or other subject-specific matters, please contact the Departmental Coordinator at your RWTH host faculty.
How to Find Courses and Modules - and Filter According to Different Teaching Languages
1. Access the Course Catalog
Go to
RWTHonline
and select Continue without logging in. Then click on Courses to open the course catalog.
2. Choosing the Right Semester
Choose the semester in which you plan to study at RWTH. If the upcoming semester is not yet available, select the previous semester (winter or summer), depending on when you will be at University.
3. Finding the Right Course Catalogue for Your Degree Program
Under Curriculum, choose your degree program. Several options may appear. Please select either:
Bachelor 1 subject, or
Master 1 subject,
depending on your level of study.
Exception – Medicine: Select “Medicine Staatsexamen, Model Course of Study” and choose the most recent year shown in brackets.
4. Filtering Courses by Language and Course Type
You can filter courses by language of instruction using the filter function. Some courses have the same title but different course numbers. These belong to the same module but represent different components (e.g., lecture or tutorial).
The second line shows the course type. The most common abbreviations are:
VL – Lecture
UE – Tutorial / Recitation
SE – Seminar
You can also filter by course type if needed.
5. Information on Individual Courses and Modules
Click on a course title to see detailed information, including the course description and ECTS credits.
Scroll down to “Status within Curriculum.” Search for your degree program. Next to it, you will see a year displayed in blue—click on it.
A list will open with your selected course highlighted. The heading marked with an orange cross indicates the name of the module to which the course belongs. In the same row, you will find an icon that looks like an open book. Click this icon to access the full module description.

Online Learning Agreement (OLA) for Incoming Erasmus Exchange Students
Steps to follow when filling out your OLA
Please submit your application to RWTH and then submit your OLA. Please note that the departmental coordinators will evaluate both documents together, so they need to receive all documents around the same time.
Due to technical problems with the Erasmus Dashboard platform, please inform your
departmental coordinator
by email that your OLA is ready for signing once you have submitted it (signed by you and your home university).
I need help with my OLA, who should I contact?
Your home universit should assist you with completing the OLA. If you have any questions about RWTH courses, please contact your
departmental coordinator
. Please remember that at least 60 percent of selected courses need to be offered by the faculty or subject area to which you were nominated by your home university.
Information on the Receiving Institution
It is of the utmost importance that you fill in the information under “Receiving Institution” correctly in your OLA. Otherwise, it will not be received and not signed by RWTH.

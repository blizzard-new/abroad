APPLICATION PROCESS
1. Home institution submits nomination (Students may be nominated 
for in-person or virtual exchange)
2. Student completes UT Exchange Admission Application
Some Academic departments require additional review or application 
materials. Please visit the Before You Apply page for details.
APPLICATION DEADLINE
FALL SEMESTER (AUGUST–DECEMBER)
March 1
SPRING SEMESTER (JANUARY–MAY)
September 1
POST-ADMISSION STEPS AND DEADLINE
1. Login to myIO portal
2. Provide the following information in myIO
a. Passport
b. Current Academic Transcript
c. English Proficiency
d. Proof of Funding (required in order to obtain J-1 visa)
e. Course Preferences
DEADLINE
Rolling deadline. Students will not receive DS-2019 until  
they have successfully completed all steps in myIO.
IMMIGRATION AND VISA REQUIREMENTS
EXCHANGE STUDENTS
Students participating in the reciprocal exchange program are 
issued Form DS-2019 to apply for a J-1 visa to cover the duration 
of their academic study period, usually one or two semesters. 
Reciprocal exchange students enroll in a non-degree academic 
program, according to the terms of exchange agreements between 
UT Austin and their home universities. Students who have U.S. Citi-
zenship are required to enter the country with their U.S. passport.

GENERAL INFORMATION
HOUSING
Exchange students attending UT Austin usually choose from three types 
of housing: co-operative houses (co-ops), private dormitories, and apart -
ments. Space in UT-owned dorms is available, but limited.
HOUSING INFORMATION
offcampus.utexas.edu
LIVING EXPENSES
Living expenses can vary from $1,600 to $2,000 per month depending 
on individual lifestyle.
HEALTH INSURANCE
All reciprocal exchange students and any accompanying dependents 
(spouse/children) are required to have health insurance that meets 
both the U.S. Department of State and The University of Texas at Austin 
requirements. Enrollment in the UT Austin insurance plan is mandatory. 
Cost is approximately $1,500 USD per semester (subject to change). 
utx.global/student-insurance-plan
ENGLISH LANGUAGE COURSES
UT Austin offers English language courses through our English Lan -
guage Center (ELC) for UT Austin students and their family members. 
Tuition for courses through ELC is not covered by exchange agreements.

INFORMATION ABOUT UT
The University of Texas at Austin is one of the largest public universities 
in the United States. Founded in 1883, the university is a 350-acre main 
campus with 17 colleges and schools, about 24,000 faculty and staff 
and more than 50,000 students. 
utexas.edu/about/overview
INFORMATION ABOUT AUSTIN, TX
Austin is the capital of the U.S. state of Texas and the seat of Travis 
County. Located in Central Texas, Austin is the 11th-most populous city 
in the United States and the fourth-most populous city in Texas. Austin 
is the fastest growing city in the U.S. 
austintexas.org
CULTURAL PROGRAMS
ISSS offers 3-4 cultural excursions per semester to areas around 
the great state of Texas, such as the NASA Space Center in Houston. 
Friendship programs and language circles are also offered through 
Texas Global. Exchange students have a monthly meet-up and volunteer 
service project each semester.
utx.global/ISSSGetInvolved
EDUCATION ABROAD FAIR
Exchange students are encouraged to participate in the education 
abroad fair each semester to help promote their home university. Fairs 
are held in September and January. Students will receive an email 
invitation to participate.

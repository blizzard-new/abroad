1
Claim Your DuckID
Your DuckID is your primary network login for everything on campus. You must "claim" your DuckID to complete many pre-arrival tasks. Refer to
How to Claim your DuckID Instructions
.
You will need to know your UO ID number (Nine-digit number that starts with 95x-xxx-xxx)
and Personal Access Code (PAC is a six-digit number/letter security code that's used with your UO ID). Students receive their PAC in their admissions email. If you did not receive a temporary PAC or have misplaced your temporary PAC, please email
admissions@uoregon.edu
.
​​​​​
After you claim your DuckID, you will need to wait a couple of days
before you can apply for on-campus housing or
access other online portals.

5
Submit Proof of Immunizations/Vaccines
Registration Block:
You will be blocked from registering for UO classes until you submit evidence of having received the
Measles, Mumps and Rubella (MMR) vaccine
. Do not wait to submit the MMR vaccine proof so that the registration hold on your account can be removed. All other vaccines can be submitted up until the start of the term and do not have a registration hold.
How to Submit MMR and Other Required Vaccines
Contact your doctor and request documentation that you have received the
required vaccines
to study in the U.S. If your doctor cannot provide you with documentation, please ask your doctor to complete the
UO Certificate of Immunization Status
form.
Once you have the documentation or
UO Certificate of Immunization Status
from your doctor, log into
myUOHealth
with your
DuckID
and enter the dates of your completed vaccines and upload the documentation from your doctor.
Log into
myUOHealth
and complete the “Tuberculosis (TB) Screening Questionnaire” in the "Forms" section. If you are at a higher risk of TB, you will be notified by the UO Health Center with further instructions.
View instructions on how to use the myUOHealth portal
If you cannot complete all of the required vaccines in your home country prior to arrival, you will be required to get the vaccines at the UO Health Center and will be charged accordingly. You cannot register for classes until this requirement is met. We strongly recommend you complete all vaccines prior to arrival.
If you have any questions, please contact the UO Health Center at
immunization@uoregon.edu
.

6
Apply For On-Campus Housing
Winter 2026, Spring 2026 or Summer 2026:
Register for on-campus housing as soon as possible. Space may be limited so get your application in early.
Fall 2026
: Registration begins in mid-December and the deadline is June 30, 2026. Freshman are required to live on-campus and should register for on-campus housing as soon as possible. Non-freshman students (which would include transfer, exchange and visiting students) are invited to register for on-campus housing.
We anticipate spaces for non-first-time, full-time students will be limited. There will be an interest wait list if capacity is reached. The interest wait list will be used as spaces become available, with priority based on earliest registration date. Those who have not been offered a residence hall assignment by August 15, 2026, will be asked if they would like to remain on the list.

8
Academic English for International Students
AEIS courses help international students develop advanced expertise in English and acculturate to the UO academic community. AEIS courses are credit-bearing and count as elective credits toward UO graduation requirements. Students may enroll in other UO courses while taking AEIS courses.
Exchange students are exempt from the AEIS courses. However, they may take AEIS courses if they choose. Exchange students with an English a proficiency score below iBT 80, IELTS 6.5 or DET 110 should consider taking AEIS courses. To receive permission to take any AEIS courses please
contact AEIS Advising
. Please provide your student ID number and the course you would like to take in your email.

Course Load
Students are recommended to take 24-30 credits per semester (generally 6 credits per course) unless otherwise permitted or required by their home institution and host faculty.
At least 50% of the course load must be selected from a single faculty (your intended host faculty). Students have the option to take the remaining credits from other faculties. Common Core courses will not be included in the 50% course enrolment requirement for incoming students.
Students admitted to the Faculty of Law can only select up to 12 credits of non-law courses (i.e. two courses) per semester.
Students may take up to two
Common Core courses
from a selective menu, with each course chosen from a different Area of Inquiry: 'Scientific and Technological Literacy', 'Humanities', 'Global Issues' and 'China: Culture, State and Society'. Approval of courses is determined on a case-by-case basis.
Approval for taking Common Core courses is granted on a first-come-first-served basis during the Course Selection Period and by auto-ballot during the Add/Drop Period. More details are available
here
.
Course Information
All courses are taught in English, except for those offered by the School of Chinese.
The HKU credit system follows the ECTS (European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System) model. Under normal circumstances, a semester of study at HKU is considered equivalent in credit value to a semester at an ECTS-compliant institution. However, the actual transfer of credits will be subject to the policies and procedures of your home institution.
Students are advised to check the course pre-requisites before selecting their courses.
Taught postgraduate-level programmes are generally not available to incoming students. If you wish to conduct research under a supervisor, please contact the relevant
faculties
to make appropriate arrangements.
Course approval is determined on a case-by-case basis and is subject to faculty approval. Actual enrolment depends on class availability, courses offered in the first semester, quotas and class schedule.

Faculty of Architecture
Faculty of Architecture has
extremely limited spots
for accepting incoming students who have high academic grades.
Students must meet any prerequisites to be eligible for studio courses, and the quota for studio courses for incoming students is highly limited.
Application to Faculty of Architecture would be considered on a case-by-case basis and subject to whether the Faculty is included in the student exchange programme agreement between HKU and the partner institution.
Students should check with their home institution before the application period to determine whether they are eligible to apply for the Faculty.

Faculty of Business and Economics
(HKU Business School)
Courses offered by the Faculty of Business and Economics (HKU Business School) are
highly competitive
.
Final-year students, in particular, should note that course enrolment is NOT guaranteed.
Applicants to the HKU Business School should have a grade level of B average / CGPA of 3.0 / Satisfactory level.
Students must have completed the course pre-requisites before requesting to enrol in courses with pre-requisites (e.g. co-registering for Microeconomics I and II in the same semester is not allowed).
Economics and Finance courses place a strong emphasis on mathematics.

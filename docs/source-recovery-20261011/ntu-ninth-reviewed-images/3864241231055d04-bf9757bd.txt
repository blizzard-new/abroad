II. Application  
Application procedures/ 
Required documents https://nupace.iee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/apply/procedures.html 
Nomination procedure 
Partner institutions will receive an e-mail approximately one month 
prior to the nomination submission date, requesting that they enter 
their nominees’ information into an online system.  
Nomination submission dates Autumn Semester: Mar 1 Spring Semester: Oct 1 
Online application site https://nupace.iee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/apply/  
Application submission dates Autumn Semester: Mar 15 Spring Semester: Oct 15 
Length of  exchange Either one or two semesters 
Number of  exchange students 
Determined by mutual agreement between both institutions, and 
contingent upon the number stipulated in the Student Exchange 
Memorandum (SEM) 
Exchange participants (UG/PG) Undergraduate and graduate students, including graduate 
research students. 
GPA Minimum 3.0 on a 4.0 scale, or the equivalent 

                                                
Language requirements  
(Proficiency in either English OR 
Japanese) 
Non-native English speakers wishing to participate in the English-
language curriculum should possess one of the following scores: 
TOEFL iBT 79, IELTS 6.0, TOEIC 780, CEFR B2, or CET-6 485.  
(Students from Scandinavia, Germany, Austria, The Netherlands, Hong Kong 
and Singapore are exempt from this requirement, as are students who have 
an English language education background.) 
Students intending to take university courses instructed in 
Japanese must possess N1/level 1 of the Japanese Language 
Proficiency Test (JLPT), or the equivalent. 
Notification of  acceptance  
(e-mail) 
Vis-à-vis April admission: Mid-late January;  
Vis-à-vis September admission: Mid-June. 
Transcript issuance Digital copy sent both to home university and student 
approximately six weeks after the completion of the exchange. 
III. Course Information  
Semester dates Autumn Semester: Oct 2 – Feb 6 
Spring Semester: Apr 10 – Aug 7 (tentative) 
Credit load Minimum 15 (30 ECTS) credits per semester, or 30 (60 ECTS) per 
academic year. No maximum ceiling is set. 
Lists/syllabi of  courses 
English-taught courses:  
 https://nupace.iee.nagoya-u.ac.jp/en/academic/overview.html 
Regular Japanese-taught courses for students with JLPT N1: 
 https://syllabus.adm.nagoya-u.ac.jp/ 
Limitations regarding course 
selection 
1) NUPACE Special Undergraduate and Special Graduate 
Students majoring in Chemistry or Physics are generally not 
permitted to enrol in G30 courses with laboratory content. 
2) Students wishing to apply for clerkships at the School of 
Medicine should follow application instructions on the NUPACE 
website carefully. 
Provision of  language training 
courses 
Japanese and other foreign language classes are held during the 
semester for credit. 
IV . Scholarship s 
Scholarships 
Where allocated, Nagoya University will nominate highly-qualified 
students for a Student Exchange Support Program (JASSO) 
scholarship. Allowance: 80,000 JPY per month.  
V . Accommodation  
Type of  accommodation offered 
In principle, single rooms in student dormitories located within a 
short commute to the university, either on foot or by subway. 
University housing fees 25,000-45,000 JPY per month, including utilities. 
Application method No procedures necessary; NUPACE will apply for housing on the 
student’s behalf. 
VI. Expense s (in local currency ) 
Insurance Health (1,700 JPY per month) and personal liability insurance 
(1,800 JPY per annum) 
Food Approximately 20,000-30,000 JPY per month 
Public transport Students living in International Residence – Daiko will pay 5,400 
JPY per month in commuting expenses. 
VII. Special Needs  
Special Needs 
Students who have special health (mental/physical) or learning 
needs are encouraged share the relevant information with this 
Office prior to their arrival in Japan.

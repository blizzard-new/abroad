Living Off Campus
Cornell Housing supports students, staff, and faculty living off campus by providing guidance, education, and resources to help you make informed housing decisions. Whether you are exploring your housing options, searching for an apartment, reviewing a lease, understanding tenant rights and responsibilities, or preparing to move, our team is here to help you identify your needs and navigate each stage of the housing process.

Navigating the calendar
The OSU Academic Calendar displays important term dates in ascending order by month and year.
The filters can be used to view a specific
Category
of dates, a
Term
, a
Term Session,
and an
Academic Year
.
Academic Years begin with the summer term.
When selecting an Academic Year in the filter, the dates will run from June to June (the Monday after commencement to the Wednesday after the following commencement).
The majority of OSU courses are held in the Full Term Session,
but the nonstandard term and the individual summer session dates are also displayed.
View a quick tutorial video
A submission time of 11:55 p.m. (Pacific Time) applies to all registration deadlines unless stated otherwise.

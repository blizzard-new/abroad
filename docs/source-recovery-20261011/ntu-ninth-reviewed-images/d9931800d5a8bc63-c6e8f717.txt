English language requirements
English is the language of instruction at the University of Waterloo. Success in our programs requires a high level of English language proficiency.
Do you need to take a language test?
You need to provide an English language test score if your four most recent years of full-time education have
NOT
been in an English-language school system.
You'll know whether you need to provide an English-language test score by completing the Declaration of Academic Experience in the
applicant portal
once you've applied.

You
may
be exempt from submitting English language test scores if you meet one or more of the following exceptions.

Deadlines
Test results must be received by the
document deadline
for the program you've applied to.
If you suspect that you do not meet the English proficiency requirements, you should take a test as soon as possible to meet the document deadline.
Exemptions will not be granted if you’re unable to take a test by our deadline.
Required academic English courses cannot be used in place of taking an English language test.
Ensure your information is correct
Please ensure your name, email address, and date of birth on your English language score matches what you provided on your Ontario Universities' Application Centre application. If the information is different, your test scores will not be matched automatically to your application and you may need to contact us.
How do you know if we've received your test scores?
Please request that the test centre send your test scores directly to the University of Waterloo. When we receive your official language test score from the testing centre, the application checklist in
your applicant portal account
will be updated.
Unofficial results (which we can use to make an admission decision)
will not appear
in the applicant portal (only official results will).

Notes
If you completed your four most recent years of full-time education in an English-language school system and you
also provide a failing English language test score
, we reserve the right to withdraw your Offer of Admission or to place you in the
BASE program
.
We
reserve the right to withdraw an offer of admission
if you fail to meet the minimum English language requirements for the program to which you have applied.
All English language tests will be verified for authenticity
. If evidence of fraudulent documentation is found, we reserve the right to cancel your application or revoke any offer of admission.
A minimum overall
IELTS score of 7.0
with a minimum band score of 6.0 or better in all components, is also acceptable for admission.
If you're studying or have studied in a Toronto District School Board (TDSB) high school, you are
not
required to provide an English language test score if
your three most recent years of full-time education have been at a TDSB high school AND
you have a grade of 80% or higher in Grade 12 U English (ENG4U, EWC4U, or ETS4U).
Note: most programs specify ENG4U as an
admission requirement
.
Start earning a Waterloo degree while improving your English
Not sure you’ll meet Waterloo's English language test scores? Or you already know that you don't? You could still study at Waterloo.
Our Bridge to Academic Success in English (BASE) program lets you start working towards a Waterloo degree, improve your English skills, and develop valuable academic skills to set you up for success at university – and at a fraction of the normal tuition for international students.
How do you join BASE? Apply to your preferred Waterloo program and if you qualify, you'll receive an offer of admission to BASE.

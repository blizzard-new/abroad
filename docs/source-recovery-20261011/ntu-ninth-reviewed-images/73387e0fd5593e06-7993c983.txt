Notations:
(a) If a student cannot furnish proof of health insurance that meets campus requirements, the student must purchase health insurance from the campus unless instructed otherwise. 
(b) Campus to advise upon request.
(c) There are no campus fees charged or published at this time although this is subject to change. 
(d) San José campus- Engineering students are required to have a minimum 4.0 (80) TOEFL iBT or 7.0 IELTS test scores. 
(e) Students who study for one calendar year from January to December should demonstrate financial amounts in excess of those listed in the table above (due to inflation and to cover the summer period).
(f) Students who study for one calendar year from January to December who live on-campus may be required to leave on-campus housing during the summer months and find other housing. 
(g) Test score are different for postgraduate students.
Additional Comments:
(1) Campus requirements, costs, health insurance costs, and accepted insurance providers are subject to change. Actual costs may be higher than listed.
(2) As part of the application process, exchange students are required to furnish verification of finances for the amount listed for the campus (or campuses) that they have listed on their application. 
(3) All students are required to purchase health insurance that covers the exchange period. Campuses require that students purchase their health insurance regardless of whether the student has health insurance from 
      their home country.
(4) Campus-based fees are not covered by the exchange agreement and are subject to change (or added where not indicated).  Fees are due upon invoice. Nonpayment of fees by the deadline date can result in
      disenrollment in classes, registration holds, non-release of the final transcript and/or dismissal from exchange.
(5) In addition to campus-based fees (where listed), students may incur course fees that are not covered by exchange (for course supplies, lab fees or field trips).
(6) Fresno campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $752.50 and Spring semester is $1036.50 (estimated).
(7) Los Angeles campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $777 and Spring semester is $1058. Annual (2 semesters) total is $1834.
(8) Sacramento campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $735 and spring semester is $1015.
(9) San Bernardino campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $804, spring semester is $724, and spring + summer term is $1,152. 
(10) Postgraduate students are normally required to have a minimum score of 4.0 (80) TOEFL iBT or 7.0 IELTS although some programs may require higher scores. 
(11) San José  campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $832 and Spring semester is $1,140.
(12) San Luis Obispo campus- The cost of health insurance is different for each term. Fall semester is $754 and spring semester is $819.
(13) Beginning January 21, 2026, the TOEFL iBT uses a 1.0–6.0 scoring scale, with the overall score. Campus requirement scores are subject to change. 
(14) Campus information displayed in red is pending and will be updated when available.

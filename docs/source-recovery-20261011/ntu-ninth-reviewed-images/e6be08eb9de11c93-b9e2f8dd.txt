Student Exchange Program (Students of Partner Universities)
Doshisha University accepts exchange students from partner universities all over the world.
Exchange students from our foreign partner institutions are enrolled at Doshisha as studnets of the "Center for Global Education and Japanese Studies".
Students of the Center for Global Education and Japanese Studies are required to select either the Global Education Module (GEM) or the Japanese Language and Culture Module (Nichibun).
They may take subjects offered at each module
as well as access the same campus facilities as regular Doshisha students.

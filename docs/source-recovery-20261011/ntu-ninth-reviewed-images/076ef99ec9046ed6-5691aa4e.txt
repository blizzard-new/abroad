Requirements for Exchange Students
<YSEP  (Research-oriented program)>
Those with student status at their home university, enrolled in the following programs at the
time they start research at Science Tokyo:
- Doctoral program
- Master’s program
- Bachelor’s program (at least two and a half years of study)
<ACAP (Course-oriented program)>
Those with student status at their home university, enrolled in the following programs at the
time they start coursework at Science Tokyo:
- Doctoral program
- Master’s program
- Bachelor’s program (at least one year of study)
*For 2nd- or 3rd-year undergraduate students, an official Japanese Language Proficiency Test
(JLPT) result or equivalent document is required as they are supposed to take courses conducted
in Japanese.
*Both YSEP and ACAP students must have student status at their home universities during the
period of participation.
*If an undergraduate student has the academic ability equivalent to that of one in the final year
of a bachelor’s degree, he/she may take graduate courses offered in English. Some courses may
have prerequisites.
*YSEP and ACAP are eligible for the former Tokyo Tech partner universities’ students and they
are not permitted to take courses offered by the faculties/graduate schools of Medicine,
Dentistry and Health Care Sciences. Indeed, they cannot be accepted by the faculty members of
the faculties/graduate schools of Medicine, Dentistry and Health Care Sciences.

General Information about
Science Tokyo (Tokyo Tech)
On October 1, 2024 Tokyo Institute of Technology and Tokyo Medical and Dental
University are merged to Institute of Science Tokyo . Science Tokyo is Japan's
leading  national university in science, technology, medicine and dentistry. The
Institute's five campuses in Ookayama, Suzukakedai,Tamachi, Yushima, Surugadai
and Kounodai are home to 2,000 faculty members and approximately 13,000
students (around 6,000 undergraduates, 7,000 postgraduates) in 6 Schools, 2
Faculties and 2 Graduate schools.  International students comprise nearly 21% of
the student body.

Visa Status/
Procedures to obtain a student visa
International students at Japanese universities are required to obtain a "student"
visa. Obtaining "student" visa status is essential in order to apply for various kinds
of assistance and scholarships for international students.
<Procedures to obtain a student visa>
1) A
fter the application, the staff of the Global Education Division apply for the
student's COE (Certificate of Eligibility), which is documentation used for obtaining
a visa to study in Japan.
2) T
he Immigration Bureau in Tokyo issues the COE and sends it to Science Tokyo.
3) T
he staff of Science Tokyo send the scanned copy of COE to a student.
4) S
tudent prints out both sides of the COE and bring it to the Japanese
Embassy/Consulate-General in his/her country to apply for your student visa.
Dormitory for Exchange Students
<Both YSEP and ACAP>
Exchange students take priority when Science Tokyo dormitories are allocated. However, we
cannot guarantee each student a room, since the number of dormitory rooms is limited.
After the applicant's enrollment has been formally accepted, about 1.5 to 1 month before
enrollment, the staff of the Student Support Division contact the student by email to provide the
information about our dormitory.

The following insurance is compulsory for international exchange students:
1) National Health Insurance: Their payment will be 30 % of the medical expense
wh
en they receive treatment at hospitals due to sickness/injuries.
*It costs about 30,000 JPY per year. The exact cost varies by the municipal office
where the student is registered.
2-1) Disaster and Accident Insurance for Students (GAKKENSAI): Benefits will be
paid in case of unexpected disasters or accidents during regular
curricular/extracurricular activities and while commuting.
2-2) Personal Liability Insurance for Students (GAKKENBAI): To indemnify
students for personal liability provided by law for injuries caused to a third party or
properties damaged during regular curricular activities, school events and while
commuting.
*Disaster and Accident Insurance for Students (GAKKENSAI) & Personal Liability
Insurance for Students (GAKKENBAI) cost 1,340 JPY in total per year.
3) Additional “Comprehensive Insurance for Students’ Lives”  coupled with
G
AKKENSAI (students who enroll in GAKKENSAI is eligible to join)
covers indemnity  liability,  injury and illness on and off campus and coverage for

Information for International Students
International Center has opened the following web pages to enable international students to obtain necessary information and ask questions or consultation. Important information will also be communicated via RITSUMEIKAN STUDENT PORTAL and e-mail.
International Center Online Support Desk (FAQ, Inquiry Form)
Frequently asked questions (FAQ) from international students and a form for submitting questions and concerns are available.

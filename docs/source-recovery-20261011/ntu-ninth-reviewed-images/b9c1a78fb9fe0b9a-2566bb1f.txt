Suggested level of language 
proficiency for exchange program 
Duolingo English Test: Minimum total of 130; TOEFL IBT: 
Minimum total of 100; TOEFL iBT Home Edition: 
Minimum total of 5 with 4.5 subscores. IELT: Minimum 
total of 7.5 with 6.5 subscores on modules. Test scores 
will be submitted directly in the online application 
following nomination by the home institution. Students 
enrolled in a degree program taught entirely in English at 
their home institution may submit a verification of 
English proficiency from their exchange coordinator in 
lieu of test scores. English proficiency may also be 
verified by students’ home university via a letter on 
university letterhead. 
Students whose TOEFL is less than 100 or IELTS is less 
than 7.5 will need to take the English Placement Test 
upon arrival. Based on the outcome of the test, certain 
English language courses may be recommended for the 
student. (Intensive English Institute information at 
http://www.iei.uiuc.edu/). Proposed program may have 
additional admission criteria.

receive acceptance letter Approximately 6 weeks from application submission

III. Course Information
Course list 
Students may choose courses from over 70 fields of 
study. Visit https://courses.illinois.edu/ to view the 
course schedule. 
Language of Instruction in 
courses 
All courses are taught in English (except foreign language 
courses). 
Course limitations and selection 
Please remind your students that Illinois cannot guarantee 
enrollment in any 
specific course, so it is important for 
exchange students to be as flexible as possible in their course 
selection.  
It is not possible to take courses offered by the College of 
Business and College of Law.  
Exchange students may take some courses in the College of 
Engineering, but courses in certain departments such as 
Computer Science are restricted. Students will only be allowed 
to take CS courses if specifically stated in exchange agreement.

V. Accommodation
Type of accommodation offered 
Upon acceptance, students may apply for a room in a 
university residence hall www.housing.illinois.edu 
(some residence halls require students to purchase a 
meal plan), or they may choose to rent a private 
apartment. For sublease postings by Illinois students, 
see the Illinois Abroad Housing Board. 
Fees of on-campus / off-campus 
housing 
On-Campus: https://www.housing.illinois.edu/Cost 
Off-Campus: Numerous private rental management 
companies

Any limitation or suggestion on 
accommodation? 
Housing is not guaranteed in university dormitories due to limited 
spaces. Students should be aware that there is no guarantee that they 
will get the residence hall of their choice. Students should not sign a 1-
year lease (contract) with private management companies if they're 
exchange is only 1 term.

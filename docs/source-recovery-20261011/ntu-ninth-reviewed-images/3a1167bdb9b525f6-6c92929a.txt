Stay With Us
Stay at UPEI Residence Hotel - perfectly located, comfortably equipped
Whether you’re attending an on-campus conference or visiting Prince Edward Island’s scenic beaches, we welcome you to stay with us. Conveniently located in Charlottetown—just minutes from the airport and historic downtown—UPEI offers year-round accommodations for visitors, with dedicated guest floors separate from student housing.
Choose from one-, two-, or three-bedroom suites featuring full kitchens or kitchenettes, air conditioning, complimentary Wi-Fi, security services, and free on-site laundry facilities.
During the summer season (May–August), guests also enjoy 24-hour front desk service and free parking. Additional on-campus amenities include dining options, a fitness centre, swimming pool, and the UPEI Bookstore. The campus is within walking distance of shopping centres and is easily accessible by the city’s transit system.
Experience the comfort of home with the convenience of campus living at UPEI.

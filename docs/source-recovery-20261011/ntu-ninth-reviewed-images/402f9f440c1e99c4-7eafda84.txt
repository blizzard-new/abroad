Forms of Financial Documentation
Accepted Forms of Financial Documentation
A statement from a bank showing available funds in the exchange visitor's name (an
additional affidavit form
is needed if the bank statement is not in the exchange visitor's name)
A contract or an award letter from a sponsoring institution/government indicating the amount and dates that the exchange visitor’s expenses will be covered.
A continued salary letter from the exchange visitor’s employer indicating the amount of salary and that the salary will be continued during the dates of the program or while abroad.
The financial document(s) must be issued within the last 12-months and be written in English or include an English translation which includes the exchange visitor’s full name, currency type and amount.
Not Accepted Forms of Financial Documentation
Retirement fund accounts
Stock certificates
Real estate holdings
Letter of good financial standing from bank
Cryptocurrency or other non-liquid funds

We offer rooms for singles, couples and families across three different buildings for researchers from between one month and one year duration. All residences are located within walking distance from Sapporo Campus and offer lower rent than other private apartments. Each room comes equipped with  high speed internet access.
The management of the researcher’s accommodation is entrusted to by BeGoodJapan Inc.
For inquiries, please send email to hk@bgj.co.jp.
Residents staying in the researcher’s accommodation for more than one month are required to pay a deposit of ¥50,000 along with their first rent payment. This will be used for any unpaid fees (rent, lightning and heat expenses, etc.) and room damages. If you make all of your payments and no repairs are needed when you leave the accommodation , you will receive a full refund.

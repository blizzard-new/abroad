International Student Orientation
Orientation
At the beginning of each term, all new international students will be guided through a mandatory new student orientation designed to provide you with valuable information and facilitate your transition to OSU.
International Student Orientation includes information about:
immigration rules and regulations that affect your visa status
student health services and health insurance requirements at OSU
academic life at OSU
campus and community resources
opportunities to meet other new international and domestic students at OSU
advising and registration
All new students will receive an email with instructions specific to their program. Check your personal and OSU email frequently to learn more about your specific program or student type orientation.
Students billed directly through OSU will have an orientation fee added to their student account to cover the cost of orientation. Students billed through INTO OSU, including first-year undergraduates with
International Direct services
, have this fee included in their tuition.

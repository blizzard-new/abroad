Study Offerings of the Faculty of Mathematics
Course Descriptions / ECTS
At TU Dresden, you have the opportunity to attend courses from our
two Bachelor's
and
four Master's
degree programs in Mathematics. You can find an overview of the programs here:
Mathematics (Bachelor)
Business Mathematics (Bachelor)
Mathematics (Master)
Technomathematics (Master)
Mathematics in Business and Economics (Master)
Computational Modeling and Simulation (Master)
Course Catalogue of the Faculty of Mathematics
The
course catalogue
for the respective semester is usually published a few weeks before the start of the semester. You will prepare a preliminary Learning Agreement, which can be modified at the beginning of your stay at TU Dresden.
Please note that some courses extend over two semesters. If you attend only the second part of such a course during your semester at TU Dresden, you might lack necessary background knowledge. In this case, please consult the study documents for the
Bachelor’s
or
Master’s
program and contact the respective module coordinator to find out whether your knowledge is appropriate to participate in the course.
Course Registration via selma and OPAL
If you would like to attend a course at the Faculty of Mathematics, you must be enrolled in the respective course both on selma and on OPAL:
To enroll in mathematics courses on selma, please contact Ms. Claudia Hess
with your Learning Agreement.
If you would like to attend courses from other faculties, please get in touch with the responsible persons of the respective faculty regarding enrollment.
At the end of the semester, you can view your grades on selma.
On OPAL, you can enroll in courses independently. You will find course materials, information about exercises, etc., there.
Please note that some courses are not listed in OPAL.
Exam Registration
Unlike regular students at TU Dresden, Erasmus+ students cannot register for exams online via selma. Instead, you must fill out a
form
(Examinations) and submit it to the Examination Office.
For
written exams
, use the form:
“Registration for an examined assessment (Late Registration)”
For
oral exams
, use the form:
“Registration for an examined assessment (Individual date)”
Please send the completed forms to the Examination Office of the Faculty of Mathematics:

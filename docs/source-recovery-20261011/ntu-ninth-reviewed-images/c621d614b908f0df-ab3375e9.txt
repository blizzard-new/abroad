Necessary cookies
Necessary cookies are required for basic website functions. For example, during registration or when filling out multi-page forms. This ensures that the website functions properly.
All cookies
Analytics
We use the website analysis software Matomo. In doing so, we set an analytics cookie so that we can recognize you on our pages and measure the success of our campaigns. These cookies are set to collect data for statistical purposes. If these cookies are disabled or rejected, it will not affect the functionality of our website.
We do not use IP addresses or cookies to identify you personally. We use the systems with the aim of increasing the efficiency of our website and our marketing.
Marketing
On individual pages, ads are placed through our marketing partner, which may store a marketing cookie. Marketing cookies are used to follow visitors on websites. The intention is to show ads that are relevant and appealing to the individual user.

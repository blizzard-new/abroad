Frequently Asked Questions
What do I need to pay to confirm my booking?
If the property is in The Netherlands, you pay the first month's rent to confirm your booking. If the property is in other countries, you pay the first month's rent plus the one-time HousingAnywhere service fee. The listing will mention if the landlord requires a security deposit. Once the landlord accepts your booking, you can pay the deposit. We safeguard your rent and only transfer it to the landlord 48 hours after you move in.
How do I pay my monthly rent?
Your landlord can send you requests for each rental payment for the duration of your tenancy. We will send you reminders before the due date and provide you with a transparent payment overview so you can keep track of everything.

If your landlord doesn’t use our payment request feature, they will provide you with their own instructions on how to pay your rent. Note that the first rental payment is always paid through HousingAnywhere and is only paid out to the landlord 48 hours after you move in.
Is the Tenant Protection fee an agency fee?
HousingAnywhere is not an agency but a rental platform that connects tenants and landlords. The Tenant Protection fee is applied to every booking (except in the Netherlands). It ensures safe payments, the right to claim a refund, and quick support if there's a last-minute cancellation or move-in delay.

Some landlords on HousingAnywhere may charge an agency fee. This is different from the Tenant Protection fee charged by HousingAnywhere. This landlord's agency fee will be shown in the listing description, and the landlord will contact you about it after you have confirmed your booking.
I am booking an apartment with more than one bedroom. Can I split the payment with my friends when I confirm the booking?
Unfortunately, you cannot split this payment as the transaction on HousingAnywhere can only be completed at the moment of booking by one user. You can ask the landlord to add additional tenants to your rental contract if the landlord provides you with one.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why do I have to pay a commission fee?
Our fees allow us to operate our platform and services: a dedicated customer support team, secure payment processing, API integrations and fraud detection. We only charge this small commission fee when a tenant successfully books your place. And remember, on HousingAnywhere you can list an unlimited number of properties and chat with potential tenants for free.
What happens if a tenant cancels a booking?
If your tenant cancels within 24 hours of booking your place, they are entitled to a full refund. After that time, you are covered by our landlord guarantee, meaning you will still receive the first month’s rent, even if the tenant cancels. 

Note that we only payout the first month’s rent 48 hours after the tenant has moved in and provided that the reality of your place matches what was advertised in your listing.
Do you guarantee the payment of the monthly rent?
We currently do not offer this sort of guarantee. However, we do provide an online rent collection system which you can use to request the rent, deposit and any other payments from your tenants.
Do you provide insurance against damage to the property during the tenancy?
We currently do not offer such insurance and strongly recommend charging your tenants a security deposit, in order to cover any potential damage. Simply send your tenant a payment request for the deposit via our secure online rent collection system.

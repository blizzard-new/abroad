 
 
◆ Schedules before exchange students’ arrival 
As of September 2026 
The schedules before your arrival are as follows. Please note that this schedule is subject to change.  
 
Fall Quarter (September) Entry 
Mid-to-late January The application period Begins; Details will be sent to the partner universities 
March 15th Application Deadline 
April - June Internal Selection Process 
Early June Letters of Acceptance will be sent via email 
Early-to-mid June Accommodation availability at the International Residence Hall (on-campus 
dormitory) will be notified to the expected students 
July Internal selection results for JASSO Scholarship will be notified to applicants 
Mid-August Official selection re sults for JASSO Scholarship will be notified to applicants 
Mid-to-late August The Certificate of Eligibility will be sent to expected students by email; Students 
should apply for their visa and arrange airplane tickets after receiving it. 
Mid-September The on-campus dormitory allocation results will be notified to the expected students 
Late September Arrival in Japan (TUFS will inform the scheduled arrival dates in advance).

Course restrictions - undergraduate exchange students
The below list outlines courses that exchange students are restricted from taking as well as courses that may be in high demand. These restrictions are determined by the respective academic department/faculty. The following list is for information purposes only as additional restrictions may apply. You will be informed of your course approvals once your application has been processed.

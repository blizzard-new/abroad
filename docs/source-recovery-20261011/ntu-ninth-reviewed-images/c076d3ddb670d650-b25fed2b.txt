Queen's Community Housing and the Off-Campus Living Advisor are university resources available to help you while studying at Queen’s and when navigating life off-campus. Let our experienced staff help you navigate the student rental market with valuable tools and professional guidance.
Queen's Community Housing
Community Housing manages the student rental properties owned by Queen’s University, many of which are very close to campus.
Visit the Community Housing website
Accommodation Listing Service
Managed by Community Housing, this online service features properties available for rent from external landlords.
Visit the Accommodation Listing Service
Off-Campus Living Advisor
The Off-Campus Living Advisor is available to provide confidential guidance on evaluating off-campus housing, tenant rights and responsibilities, and resources for moving in/out.
Visit the Off-Campus Living Advisor website

About
OIA
The Office of International Affairs (OIA) department is Bennett University's gateway to the world. We drive global engagement through strategic partnerships with renowned universities and organizations. Our collaborative initiatives offer students and faculty unparalleled opportunities for research, study & and exchange programs, and exposure to diverse perspectives. Our goal is to empower Bennett University's students and faculty to become global leaders capable of addressing the world's most complex challenges.
Bennett Global
Network Team
Bennett University's Office of International Affairs (OIA) team propel the institution into a global network of innovation and collaboration. We forge strategic partnerships with leading universities and organizations worldwide, providing our students and faculty with unparalleled opportunities. From exchange programs and research collaborations to international workshops and internships, the OIA team unlocks a world of possibilities for Bennett University to become a key player on the global education stage.

OIA
Champions
Bennett University's Office of International Affairs (OIA) team works in close collaboration with dedicated OIA Champions from across academic departments. These champions are essential in bridging the gap between global opportunities and our student and faculty body. They actively circulate exciting international programs, internships, and exchange opportunities to their respective departments, ensuring maximum student reach. Additionally, OIA Champions play a key role in the implementation of partnerships and MOUs, providing valuable academic insights and ensuring programs align with departmental goals and curriculum requirements. This strong partnership empowers Bennett students to fully leverage the wealth of international experiences and collaborations fostered by the OIA team.

Inbound Immersion
Program
Bennett University's Inbound Immersion Program invites international students to embark on a transformative journey through the heart of India. Experience the vibrant tapestry of Indian culture, engage in thought-provoking academic programs, and forge lasting connections with peers and faculty. Our curated programs, spanning 10 days to a month, offer a holistic and customizable 360-degree experience.
Discover India's rich heritage through cultural workshops, visits to historical landmarks, and immersive interactions with local communities. Engage in specially designed short-term courses that delve into India's unique contributions to various fields. Gain valuable insights through industry visits, providing first-hand exposure to India's dynamic business landscape.
We understand that every institution has unique needs. That's why our Inbound Immersion Programs are carefully tailored to align with your partner institution's objectives. Choose from a range of focus areas, adjust program durations, and personalize the experience to create the perfect fit for your students.
Elevate your students' global perspective and ignite their passion for exploration. Bennett University's Inbound Immersion Program is their gateway to an unforgettable Indian adventure.
International
Accelerated Master's program
Bennett University's International Accelerated Master's Program offers an innovative path for ambitious students seeking a global education. This specialized program leverages our partnerships with top-ranked international universities to provide a seamless transition between your undergraduate and graduate studies. By combining 3.5 years at Bennett University with 1.5 years of focused master's coursework at the partner institution, you can earn both a bachelor's degree from Bennett and a master's degree from your chosen partner university in just five years.
This accelerated program offers a multitude of benefits. You'll gain guaranteed admission to a prestigious master's program, saving you the time and stress of a separate application process. Furthermore, by completing your degrees in a streamlined timeframe, you'll benefit from significant cost savings. Most importantly, this program fosters a truly global learning experience, allowing you to broaden your perspectives and gain valuable international exposure at two world-class institutions.

International
Transfer program
Bennett University's International Transfer Program offers a truly unique pathway to a world-class education and global career opportunities. This innovative program leverages our partnerships with prestigious international universities, allowing you to seamlessly transition your studies between Bennett University and our partner institution.
Begin your academic journey by spending the first two years at Bennett University, gaining a strong foundational education within our vibrant campus. Then, broaden your horizons by completing your degree at our renowned partner university, and immersing yourself in a new academic and cultural environment. Upon graduation, you will earn a globally recognized degree from an international university.
This program is designed with both academic excellence and affordability in mind. By carefully blending your studies between India and your chosen partner country, you benefit from significant cost savings compared to pursuing a degree entirely abroad. Furthermore, your international experience will enhance your resume, expand your professional network, and prepare you to thrive in a diverse global workforce.

Life in Sendai
Tohoku University is located in Sendai, a city with a population of about 1,000,000 the largest in the Tohoku Region. For a long time, it has been known as a “City of Trees” for its lush greenery, making it well-suited for student life and contemplation. Well removed from the Tokyo metropolitan area, rent and other costs are relatively low. The appropriate cost of living is shown below, and we believe it is a nice place to live.

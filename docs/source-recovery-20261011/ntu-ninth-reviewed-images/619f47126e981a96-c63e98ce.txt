Temporary Housing
Introduction
An important step on your pre-arrival to-do list is to secure a place to stay for your first couple of days or weeks in Illinois. Some people may be lucky and move directly into their
long-term housing
, however, most people will need some form of temporary housing to bridge the gap between arrival and move-in day. Whatever you arrange, it is strongly encouraged that you confirm temporary housing prior to your arrival as accommodations may be limited and more expensive if you try to book upon arrival.
Please note that this page is for informational purposes only and the Office of International Services does not endorse any of the businesses listed below. Rates depend on availability and are subject to change.

Hostels

Hostels are not as common in the U.S. as in other parts of the world but there are a few options in Chicago. Hostels are typically less expensive than a hotel and booking a bed in a shared room can reduce the cost even further.

UIC Guest Housing
Copy link
UIC Housing offers temporary guest housing in the Single Student Residence (SSR) on West Campus in Chicago. Daily and weekly rates are competitive with local hotels. Space is limited, however, and bookings must be made in advance and prior to arrival in Chicago.

Family & friends
Copy link
For some, staying with friends or family might be an option for temporary accommodation upon arrival in Illinois. Other new international students and scholars have reported finding temporary accommodation with fellow internationals from their home country or region as well. If you plan to stay with a friend or family member, be sure to confirm the accommodation shortly before arrival and carry their exact address in your hand luggage during your journey to the U.S.

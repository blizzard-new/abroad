Our Partners around the World
Tel Aviv University has more than 400 partnerships with world-class academic institutions around the world.
Partnerships include student and faculty exchanges, dual degree programs, and executive education programs.
The Lowy International School manages these partnerships, develops international opportunities for students and faculty, hosts international delegations of visitors, and cultivates global connections.

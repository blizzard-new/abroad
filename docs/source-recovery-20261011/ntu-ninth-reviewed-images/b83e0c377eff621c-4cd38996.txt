UQAM’s Desjardins Medical Insurance
As an international student, you are required to subscribe to UQAM’s Desjardins medical insurance, and
no other insurance will be accepted unless you obtain medical coverage from RAMQ or medical coverage from a cooperative organization for scholarship holders
.
Free students and auditors are billed only for the term of enrollment. Exchange students are billed for one (0473, 0475, 0478, 0481) or two terms (0474, 04760482, 0483), depending on the chosen program. Regular program students are insured for the year. Note that only enrolled students are insured.
When you leave Quebec for your country of permanent residence and request a cancellation of insurance fees for the summer, be aware that you will be considered a new student upon your return, i.e., clauses for pre-existing conditions (or pregnancy), which may develop during the insurance interruption, could apply (see limitations in your brochure) and lead to a significant limitation of coverage.
We strongly advise you to evaluate all situations that could restrict your medical coverage, or even lead to coverage limitations and make you ineligible for certain expenses upon your return to Quebec (examples: pre-existing illness, pregnancy).
The brochure details all the exclusions applicable to the contract.

FAQ
I am an international student and had to cancel my summer term. Am I still insured?
Yes, if you are enrolled in a regular program and your Montreal address appears in your file. It is therefore important to update your contact information on
My Portal
.
I am arriving in Quebec on August 1, will I be covered by Desjardins insurance?
Your Desjardins medical coverage covers you from
August 15
, before the start of classes. If you arrive before this date, you will need to have private insurance.
I am eligible for RAMQ, but I would prefer to take Desjardins insurance for its repatriation coverage; is that possible?
Yes, you just need to notify us of your decision by email:
sei@uqam.ca
.
I need an eye exam, does my medical coverage cover the exam and lenses?
You are entitled to one exam per year for a
maximum amount of $75
. However, lenses are not covered.
I need to see a dentist, does Desjardins insurance cover dental care?
No, dental care is not covered. However, if you have kept the ASEQ insurance, you might be eligible for a discount.
Check their website
for more information. The
following video clip
will let you know about the different types of insurances.
How can I be reimbursed for medication bills?
On the Desjardins website, you can submit your
reimbursement request online
.
I need to go to the clinic for a medical consultation, but I have not received my Desjardins health insurance card. How should I proceed to obtain it?
If you have paid your insurance premium, it is possible to obtain your certificate number by sending us an email at
sei@uqam.ca
.
Is it necessary to change my address with the Desjardins insurer?
No, but it is even more important to update your contact details in your
UQAM student file
. Desjardins uses the contact information we have.
I came with my spouse, who is a student, and I do not have insurance. Is it possible to obtain the same medical coverage?
No, Desjardins only insures students. You must take personal health insurance. The
Ombudsman for Life & Health Insurance website
can help you find an insurer.
I came with my spouse, who is a student, and we purchased family insurance before leaving our country. Can I cancel Desjardins coverage?
Unfortunately, Desjardins Financial Security has exclusive insurance terms with UQAM students.
I need to go to the emergency room, I don’t have a card and I’m not sure if I have paid the fees for insurance coverage. What should I do?
If you are registered at UQAM, insurance is mandatory and you are automatically insured.
Contact us
to find out what to do. Do not hesitate to watch the
following video
to help you.
FAQ – Pregnancy
I am a student at UQAM and I am pregnant. Am I covered by Desjardins health insurance?
Yes, you are covered, as long as your delivery is scheduled more than 30 weeks after your insurance enrollment. See the
Document on the group insurance plan for foreign university students
for specifics.
I am pregnant and should deliver within the next 2 months. I have not received my insurance card. How should I proceed?
You just need to provide your certificate number when you are admitted to the hospital. They will take care of contacting Desjardins to confirm your enrollment in the medical coverage. Your certificate number remains the same year after year.
I am pregnant and want to schedule an appointment for an abortion. Will I be insured by my Desjardins medical coverage?
Yes, voluntary termination of pregnancy is covered by Desjardins insurance. If you need more information, contact the
Quebec Federation for Family Planning
.
Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ)
Students with nationality from one of the countries that have signed an agreement with Quebec must absolutely obtain the social security affiliation attestation form
before leaving their country of origin.
Upon arrival in Montreal, you must make your
request online
at the Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ) by following this link:
Register for health insurance | Régie de l’assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ) (gouv.qc.ca)
and by submitting the following PDF documents: affiliation attestation form, proof of full-time enrollment, CAQ, study permit, or passport.
Exchange students from France do not have to provide proof of enrollment in their RAMQ request
.
Students accompanied by family members will always need to use a paper form, which they can obtain by calling RAMQ.
The procedure is the same for extending your health insurance coverage.

Some Quebec and Canadian cooperation organizations offer medical insurance coverage to their scholarship holders. To obtain an exemption from the insurance fees charged on your bill, you must scan your insurance card and send it by email to
sei@uqam.ca
.
ASEQ Supplementary Insurance
UQAM’s student associations offer a supplementary insurance plan (including dental) for which you are automatically billed. Details of the fees can be found on the UQAM Financial Services website. This insurance is not mandatory, and you have the option to opt out by changing your coverage directly on the ASEQ website.
Additionally, for students covered by RAMQ, you can request full coverage (including health and optical) by directly contacting ASEQ by phone. Be informed about the period for withdrawal and coverage change!
Please note
, the deadlines are tight: remember to complete the procedures upon your arrival.
N.B. Exchange students and independent students are not charged for this additional ASEQ insurance.
For information about the coverage of this health and dental care plan, reimbursement, out-of-pocket costs, your withdrawal or modification of coverage, etc., you can visit the ASEQ (Alliance for Student Health in Quebec) website, under your faculty student association section:

Home Insurance: Fire, Theft, and Liability
Unlike medical coverage, home insurance is not mandatory. However, even if you believe the value of your belongings is minimal, this type of insurance provides additional protection, such as liability coverage for damages caused to others. Check with private insurance companies available in Quebec for home insurance.

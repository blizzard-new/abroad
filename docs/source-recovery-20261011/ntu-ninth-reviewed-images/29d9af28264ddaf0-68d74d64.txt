Kobe University International Residence
The Kobe University International Residence, located on Port Island, was built in March 1981 as a residence hall for international students and researchers. In March 1986, a new annex was built. In March 2014, both buildings were totally renovated. The five-story reinforced concrete facility has 109 single rooms, 8 married couple rooms, and 4 family rooms. All rooms have unit bath, toilet, mini kitchen and air-conditioner.

Rent: Single room: ¥18,000 (D type), ¥21,000(E type)
Married Couple's room: ¥45,000 (B type)
Family room: ¥49,000 (A type)
Common-area charge: ¥10,800 (D type/E type) , ¥14,900 (B type) , ¥16,300 (A type)
Other Charges
Utilities:electricity(actual cost)
Cleaning fee to ready the room for the next occupant (once only): ¥11,000 (D type), ¥12,000 (E type), ¥20,000 (B type), ¥27,000 (A type)

Common room, laundry, library, Japanese-style (tatami) room, meeting room
Individual facilities
Desk, chair, bed, bedclothes, wardrobe, shoe box, desk lamp, IH-hob, mini-refrigerator, internet (Wi-Fi)
*Vary according to room type.

Kobe University Sumiyoshi International Student House
The Kobe University Sumiyoshi International Student House was built in March 1997 to provide a good environment for study and to promote international exchange. The five-story reinforced concrete facility is a residence where Japanese and international students live and study together. There are 47 single rooms (30 rooms for male, 17 rooms for female) in the residence available for international students only.
Monthly Rent
Rent: ¥4,700 / Common-area charge: ¥6,000
Other Charges

Electricity: Individual contract with Kansai Electric Power Co.,Inc.

Kokui Residence
Kokui Residence was built in 1966 as the Japanese male students' residence. It has been reinforced with earthquake-resistant construction and reopened as the international and Japanese students unisex residence in October 2011. The five-story reinforced concrete facility has 111 rooms for international students. All rooms have unit bath, toilet, minikitchen and air-conditioner.

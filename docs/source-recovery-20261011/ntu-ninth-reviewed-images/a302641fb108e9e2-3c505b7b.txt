Housing
Exchange students have several options for housing in Raleigh. On-campus housing requires an application through MyPack Portal.
Residence Halls
There are over 20 residence halls on campus. Some are traditional-hall style, others are suites, and some are hotel-style; rooms are shared between two people. On-campus housing is not guaranteed, and space may be limited. Students are encouraged to apply for housing as soon as they receive their Unity ID.
Find a Residence Hall
Global Village
An NC State “Village” is a community of students with shared academic, personal, or lifestyle interests. Most Villages are reserved for first-year or degree-seeking students. However, the Global Village is open to exchange students. The Global Village is a living and learning community for those interested in experiencing different cultures.
Learn more about the Global Village
On-Campus Apartments
On-campus apartment provide students with the convenience of living on campus with some extra privacy and independence. Rooms are private with a shared kitchen and fully-furnished living area.
Find an On-Campus Apartment
Off-Campus Housing
Exchange students are not required to live on campus. There are several student-only apartment complexes near campus where students may make their own arrangements

Simon Fraser University is consistently ranked as one of Canada’s top comprehensive universities. By selecting 
SFU as your exchange/study abroad destination, you will experience our commitment to academic freedom, 
interdisciplinary research, quality student services, and community engagement. Our location in Metropolitan 
Vancouver offers the advantages of a large and cosmopolitan city on the beautiful west coast of Canada, and 
our academic programs offer a wide choice across many disciplines in eight faculties.
Please note that the School of Medicine and Faculty of Graduate Studies are not open to exchange or study abroad students.

TERM DATES
SFU operates on a unique trimester system, comprised of 
3 equal full-time terms per year. Exchange and study 
abroad students are welcome to start at
 SFU in the Fall or Spring term, and normally stay for either 1 or 2 term(s).
Spring Term
Orientation: Early January 
Classes: Early January - Early April (13 weeks) 
Exams: Early April - Mid April (2 weeks)
Fall Term
Orientation: Early September 
Classes: Early September - Early December (13 weeks) 
Exams: Early December - Mid December (2 weeks)
Summer Term
Orientation: Early May 
Classes: Early May - Early August (13 weeks) 
Exams: Early August - Mid August (2 weeks)
*Exchange/study abroad students cannot start in Summer term, however may stay in Summer if started in an earlier
term.
APPLYING
Your home institution must nominate you to participate in a formal exchange or study abroad term at SFU. 
Contact your home institution’s exchange oﬃce to ask about deadlines and application procedures.
For more information visit : www.sfu.ca/students/exchange.html

Academic Information
The Academic Calendar lists class periods, registration dates, and exam periods. The annual course offerings and each semester’s timetable are announced each year and each semester, and are constantly updated. The syllabus for each course introduces the course outline and objectives, textbooks, evaluation methods, and the content of each class.

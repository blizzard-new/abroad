Language Requirements No language proficiency test result is required, but KNU respects its partners’ nomination criteria.
Credits Undergraduate
1 course is typically 3 Korean credits, but some courses are only 1 or 2 credits.
Undergraduate students can take a maximum of 18 credits (6 courses) per semester, and the 
minimum requirement is 6 credits (2 courses per semester.)
Graduate Master’s students can take a maximum of 10 Korean credits, which is usually 3 courses.
Application Documents
1) Online application: http://en.knu.ac.kr/admission/exchange_app2.htm
2) Letter of nomination from the applicant’s home university
3) Official certificate of enrollment from the applicant’s home university (an apostille or 
notarized certificate from the Embassy of South Korea is mandatory for non-OECD countries)
4) Official transcript from the applicants’ home universities
5) Copy of a passport (face/data page, valid for at least 6 months beyond the day of departure)
6) Color ID photo (not ID card but a face photo)
7) Bank statement with an amount of at least KRW 8,000,000 (for one semester)
 * For non-OECD countries, the original forms of the 3) official certificate of enrollment and 
7) bank statement must be submitted by post right after the online submission and 
received at KNU before traveling to Korea.
Course List &
Course Registration
Course List: http://en.knu.ac.kr/admission/exchange01.htm#con08  
Students must register for courses online during the course registration period before the 
semester starts, and the procedure is explained by email. Course lists are updated in the 
middle of July for the fall semester, and in the middle of January for the spring semester.
Scholarships
KNU has several scholarship programs for international students, such as the Global Korea 
Scholarship (GKS), ASEM-DUO, dormitory scholarships (by special agreements, for graduate 
level students or students from unbalanced institutes). Each applicable program is described in 
the welcome letter and/or subsequent emails.
Visa &
Alien Registration in 
Korea
Students must get a Student Visa (D-2) before traveling to Korea. Once accepted from KNU 
with the certificate of admission, students must apply for the student visa within one month 
after receiving the certificate of admission. Please confirm the visa requirements with the 
local Korean embassy or consulate.
Applying for the Alien Registration Card (ARC) is mandatory after arrival in Korea. Students 
will get more information about this process from KNU at the orientation.
Vaccination Vaccination and its certificate submission are no longer mandatory for traveling to Korea.
Arrival Date
Arrival in Korea a week before the beginning of the semester is recommended for the 
compulsory orientation. Students receive welcome kits necessary university-related information 
and practical information including the ARC application to the Korean Immigration Office.
Buddy Program On average, two exchange students will be paired with one Korean KNU student buddy to 
assist with settling into their new lives in Korea, communication, and cultural exchange.
Health Insurance
All international students in Korea are subject to mandatory enrollment in the National Health 
Insurance Service (NHIS) while in Korea. The estimated cost is approximately KRW 75,490 
(USD $60) per month. Once enrolled, students will receive various benefits, including dental, 
traditional Korean medicinal treatment, health exams and check-ups, and pregnancy and other 
childbirth-related medical coverage, just as Korean citizens receive.
Campus Dormitory
Students can apply for on-campus housing to be assigned to a shared room in one of several 
on-campus dormitories. The dormitory fee for one semester can range from around USD $500 
to $1,000, depending on the meal plan (which is optional). This application should be 
completed separately by online application before the start of the semester.

Dormitory
If you are interested in a place in a dormitory, you will find a quite wide range of accommodation at the Studierendenwerk and the Bürgermeister Reuter Stiftung.

Berlinovo
Guesthouse of TU Berlin
International guest researchers have the opportunity to rent a flat in the TU Berlin Guest House. The house has a total of 50 single- and multi-room flats at two different locations with areas between 31 sqm and 62 sqm.
Housing Platform
With the Housing Platform you can find a flat for your short-term or long-term stay in Berlin or, as a member of TU Berlin, you can offer your flat for (sub)lease.
Further Guesthouses and Housing Platforms
Another inexpensive type of housing is a shared apartment. If you prefer private accommodation, you can find various online providers in the list "Mitwohnzentrale".

AGU oﬀers level-appropriate classes for exchange students who wish to learn  
Japanese. Using the placement test, students with a Japanese language  
proﬁciency above a certain level will be assigned to the most appropriate class from a number of levels, and receive 
credit. On the other hand, students who have not reached a certain level will not receive credit, but will be provided
with classes taught in English by external instructors. In addition to Japanese language classes, there are several 
courses designed to help students develop a comprehensive  understanding of Japan,  including Japanese culture, 
politics, and history.
GLOBAL LOUNGE
The Global Lounge is a space, open to all international
students, to interact with AGU students. Here, you can 
discuss homework with friends from the same class, talk 
about club activities, teach each other about the culture 
and customs of your own country that are diﬀerent from 
Japan’ s, or get excited talking about your favorite anime 
or manga…let ’s promote international exchange in a 
relaxed atmospher e !
STUDENT DORMITORIES
AGU has several aﬃliated 
student dormitories, and 
exchange students are given 
priority for admission. 
They are located approximately 
30 minutes to one hour from 
the university.

AGU oﬀers level-appropriate classes for exchange students who wish to learn  
Japanese. Using the placement test, students with a Japanese language  
proﬁciency above a certain level will be assigned to the most appropriate class from a number of levels, and receive 
credit. On the other hand, students who have not reached a certain level will not receive credit, but will be provided
with classes taught in English by external instructors. In addition to Japanese language classes, there are several 
courses designed to help students develop a comprehensive  understanding of Japan,  including Japanese culture, 
politics, and history.
GLOBAL LOUNGE
The Global Lounge is a space, open to all international
students, to interact with AGU students. Here, you can 
discuss homework with friends from the same class, talk 
about club activities, teach each other about the culture 
and customs of your own country that are diﬀerent from 
Japan’ s, or get excited talking about your favorite anime 
or manga…let ’s promote international exchange in a 
relaxed atmospher e !

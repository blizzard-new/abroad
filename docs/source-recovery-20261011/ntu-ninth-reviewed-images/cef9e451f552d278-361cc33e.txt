Application  After receiving the nomination, University of Hyogo will send the students the link to 
the online application, and a set of the format (documents) required to be uploaded.  
This mail will also be sent to the student’s home institutional coordinator. 
 
Please prepare  and upload the following application materials  by application 
deadline below. 
 
1. Online application 
2. Transcript in English 
3. Letter of recommendation in Japanese or English.  
4. Copy of Passport 
5. Application for Certificate of Eligibility  
6. International Student Financial Statement 
7. Bank Deposit Balance Certificate for student’s personal or Expense  
supporter's account in English 
8. Data of identity photo  
9. Reason for Application

KOBE 
Kobe is the capital city of Hyogo Prefecture, and the 6th largest populated city 
in Japan.  The city was one of the cities to open for trade with the West 
following the 1853 end of the policy of seclusion.  Since then, it has been a 
cosmopolitan city that is home to global companies such as ASICS, Kawasaki 
Heavy Industries, and Kobe Steel as well as foreign-affiliated corporations.

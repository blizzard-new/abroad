Disaster and Accident Insurance for Students (GAKKENSAI) (Gakusei Kyoiku Kenkyu Saigai Shougai Hoken)
There is no way students can know when an unexpected accident may occur during regular curricular activities, school events or extracurricular activities. In particular, given the quantity of experiments and practical training conducted at Tokyo Tech, chemical burns, injuries resulting from machinery and other kinds of accidents occur relatively frequently. Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (JEES) administers Disaster and Accident Insurance for Students (GAKKENSAI) as a system for providing aid to students involved in such accidents. GAKKENSAI comprises a nationwide accident and disaster compensation system that was established as a measure to assist students who suffer various accidents during their study and research activities, and
all Tokyo Tech students are required to join this insurance.
A guide to joining this insurance system is enclosed with other regular admission documents (also distributed by the Student Support Division). Please note, you will need to re-join this insurance system each time you advance to the next stage of higher education (e.g. from a research student to a master's student; from an undergraduate student to a master's student; and from a master's student to a doctoral student).
Disaster and Accident Insurance for Students (GAKKENSAI)
Personal Liability Insurance for Students - supplementary to the Disaster and Accident Insurance for students (GAKKENBAI) (Gakkensai Futai Baisho Sekinin Hoken)
Personal Liability Insurance for Students (GAKKENBAI) is another type of insurance that indemnifies student liability (compensation paid to injured third parties or for damaged property) arising from accidents occurring during official Tokyo Tech activities (excludes some activities), and all students are also required to join this as it is regarded necessary for life as a student. GAKKENBAI cannot be bought independently of the abovementioned GAKKENSAI insurance, so please buy them together.

Certificate of Enrollment
Upon your participation in an internship, you may be requested from a company or institution to submit a certificate of enrollment of Personal Accident Insurance for Students Pursuing Education and Research (PAS) and Liability Insurance coupled with PAS.
Should you need one, please submit a request form at Student Support Division, or by campus mail or e-mail.
The certificate will be issued in a week from your request.

Claiming Indemnity
Should you have the misfortune to get injured or have an accident, you will need to claim indemnity. Please refer to the following regarding the procedures to be taken. Claim forms can be obtained from the Student Support Division. Always confirm that you do have insurance coverage at the Student Support Division before claiming indemnity.

[ GAKKENBAI ] Procedures for Claiming Indemnity
1) Criteria for claiming indemnity
Students are indemnified for personal liability provided for by law for injury caused to a third party or property damaged in Japan during regular curricular activities, school events and while commuting to and from Tokyo Tech.
2) Claiming indemnity
1.
Should you have an accident, please telephone School Insurance Desk, Tokyo Marine Nichido and report the following information.
Tel:0120-868-066
Your name, age and university
Date and time the accident occurred
Where the accident occurred, and the name and age of the injured person
Cause of the accident, extent of liability and extent of damage
*
The student who caused the injury is responsible for reaching a settlement with the injured party.
(Please consult with the insurance company carefully before reaching a settlement.)
2.
Students must get the prescribed form from Tokyo Tech, fill in the necessary information, and submit it to the Student Support Division, after which it will be sent.
3.
Indemnity will be transferred to your specified bank account after inspection by the insurance company.

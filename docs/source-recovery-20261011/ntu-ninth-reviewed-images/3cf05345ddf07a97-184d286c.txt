Find Housing
While living off-campus can be a great experience, finding good housing can be challenging. Northwestern University community members can use the Off-Campus Housing website (
https://offcampushousing.northwestern.edu
), which provides off-campus housing solutions for post-secondary students, faculty, staff and other community members.
Access a tutorial on how to use the website here
!
All services are free for students to use! You can:
View rental listings
View sublet listings
Find a roommate
Post a FREE sublet ad or 'roommates needed' profile
Students may browse housing listings without logging in, but contacting a housing provider, or posting a sublet or roommate listing, requires logging in using one's NetID and password.
Faculty and staff have the same access as students do, however, faculty and staff pay the usual fees for listings that a housing provider would.

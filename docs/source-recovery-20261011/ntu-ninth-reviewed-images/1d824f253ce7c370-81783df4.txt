Student housing for exchange and visiting students
A limited number of rooms in student housing are available for exchange students to apply for. The International Office also supports with finding a private room.
Applying for student housing
The
Munich Student Union
provides a very limited number of places for exchange students in
student housing
. This is referred to as the
service package
. The International Office is responsible for allocating student housing. It is not possible to apply directly to the Student Union.
Please note that applications received after the
application deadline
will not be considered for student housing.
Rooms are rented from September to the end of February (winter semester), as well as March to the end of July (summer semester); usually with a maximum rental period of one semester. The average rent is between 400 € and 600 € per month (depending on the location / student dorm).
If you are a Bachelor or Master student, and going to study at LMU as an
Erasmus+, UK, SEMP or LMUexchange student
, you can apply for a room in student housing directly in your
online application
(by ticking the corresponding box).
If you are studying at LMU for one or two semesters as part of a
double degree
or
Erasmus Mundus
program, you can ask your coordinator about student housing options.
If you are a PhD student, or do not belong to the group of above named exchange programs, or do not wish to apply for a place in student housing, please look for accommodation yourself as early as possible. We have prepared useful information about finding private accommodation in Munich below.
Please note that rooms can only be guaranteed for
approximately half of the students
! Due to the challenging housing market in Munich, please start looking for accommodation as soon as possible, while simultaneously stating in your online application that you are applying for student housing. The International Office will support you with additional resources to help you find private accommodation. We recommend to make use of this support to avoid getting scammed.
Receiving a student housing offer
If you are offered a room in student housing, the Munich Student Union will send you an email with more detailed information about the accommodation as well as a document called an
Annahmeerklärung
(declaration of acceptance). Please sign this document, return it to the Munich Student Union and transfer three months’ rent plus the deposit. You can find the exact amount in your acceptance letter. The room is reserved for you once the Munich Student Union has received the signed document. Please note that most of the rooms will be given for one semester only for winter term, however, for summer term only for one semester without exception.
Useful information for finding private accommodation
Unfortunately, we cannot guarantee you a room. If we are unable to provide you with student housing from the Munich Student Union, the International Office can assist you in finding a
private room (PDF, 100 KB)
.
Please send a completed
application form for private accommodation (PDF, 148 KB)
to
housing-incoming@lmu.de
.
The
Munich Student Union
or the
Jugendinformationszentrum (Youth Information Center)
also have resources that can help you find a place to stay.
In the
overview information on cases of fraud (PDF, 143 KB)
you can find further information and how you can best recognize those cases.

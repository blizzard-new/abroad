Exchange studies
Has your home university nominated you for exchange studies at Uppsala University? Congratulations! It is now time to start your application.
Apply to Uppsala University
To attend Uppsala University as an exchange student, your home university must have a current exchange agreement with Uppsala University. There are two types of exchange agreements: university-wide agreements and department or faculty agreements.
If you are unsure of which type of exchange agreement your home university has, ask your home university exchange coordinator.

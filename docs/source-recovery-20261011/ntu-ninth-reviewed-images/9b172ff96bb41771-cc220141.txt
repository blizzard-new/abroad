CSU Systemwide Student Exchange Program 
Fact Sheet for 2025-26 
 
The California State University (CSU) International Programs Office (CSU IP) administers the  CSU Systemwide Student 
Exchange Program and has student exchange agreements with over 60 institutions worldwide. Our office is located at the 
CSU Office of the Chancellor in Long Beach, California, which is the headquarters office for the CSU system.

Eligibility and Requirements 
Who is eligible to apply for this 
exchange program? 
Degree-seeking undergraduate or postgraduate students from exchange partners 
who wish to study at the undergraduate level and who are citizens from a country 
outside of the US. Students may be dual citizens with the US.  
Who is not eligible to apply for 
this exchange program?  
U.S. citizens who do not hold a citizenship from another country.

Application Go to our website for information and additional resources. 
Campus selection • Nominated applicants are asked to list up to four CSU campus choices on the 
CSU Systemwide Student Exchange Application.  
• Applicants are applying to the CSU system and can be placed at any one of 
our campuses based on available places at a campus, academic match, and 
other factors.  
• Applicants must be prepared to attend any of the campuses listed on their 
applications since their top choice(s) may not be available.  
• Some campuses are very competitive, so it is important for students to be 
flexible with both campus choice and subject selection.  
• The CSU International Programs will have the sole discretion in selecting an 
appropriate campus for each student. To select campuses for exchange, 
students should explore the many campuses available to them.  
Subject/Course selection • Students must list at least eight subjects (courses) that they are interested in 
taking for each campus listed.  
• Students must meet prerequisites for the subjects listed.  
• Courses listed on an application does not guarantee access to those subjects; 
therefore, students must be flexible with selection of subjects. 
Business or Film 
Studies/Production students 
• Must have successfully completed the equivalent of one semester’s worth of 
subjects based on full time studies. 
• Must be enrolled in a degree program in that subject area at home 
institution. 
• Must meet subject prerequisites for subjects listed on their application. 
Application material  Students are required to submit the following items when applying to the CSU 
Systemwide Student Exchange Program: 
• CSU Systemwide Student Exchange Application. 
• Copy of English proficiency test score not more than two years old. The host 
CSU campus may request original test results. 
• Copy of student’s passport showing student’s photo, full legal name and place 
of birth. 
• Personal statement 
• Copy of transcripts from all post-secondary institutions/universities attended. 
The host CSU campus may request original, official transcripts. 
• Declaration of Financial Support Form. Financial documents must be sent to 
the host CSU campus after a student has been placed.  
• Portfolio may be requested for students who wish to study studio art or 
architecture. An audition video may be requested for students who wish to 
study music or dance.

Academic calendar information • All campuses operate on the semester system, except the San Luis 
Obispo campus, which operates on a quarter system. 
• Three quarters are equal to two semesters or one academic year.  
• Summer courses are not offered as part of the CSU Systemwide Student 
Exchange Program. 
• Refer to the most recent academic year found on our website. Calendar for 
the upcoming academic year will not be available until the July before the

Course levels • Generally, course levels correlate with the difficulty of the course although 
students may experience challenging courses in each level.  
• Undergraduate courses are either lower division or upper division rather than 
categorized as first, second, third, or fourth year courses.  
• Lower and upper division courses pertain only to undergraduate degrees 
• Graduate level courses pertain to graduate (post-graduate) degrees. 
• Exchange students must select undergraduate level courses.

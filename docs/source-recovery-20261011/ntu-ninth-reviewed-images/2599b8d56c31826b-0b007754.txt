European exchange students (Erasmus students starting with at least completed A2 level) and overseas exchange students (all levels) can attend one of the pre-semester 80-hour
German intensive courses
offered at different levels by the University’s
Modern Language Center
(ZEMS)
.
Courses take place over 20 days at four hours per day in groups of approximately 15 students.
6 ECTS are available to students who successfully complete a course.
Registration:
From early July 2026 on via the
website of the ZEMS
Language course orientation:
August 17, 2026 (online)
Compulsory placement test:
September 2, 2026 at the Modern Language Center
Course duration:
September 4 - October 1, 2026
Course fee:
€400

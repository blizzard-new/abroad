Billing & Payment
As an exchange student, you are not responsible for paying tuition to Northwestern. You will only be billed by Northwestern for the cost of room and board (if you live on campus) and the NU-SHIP health insurance. Consult the
Student Financial Services
site for information about billing, payment methods, and payment due dates.
Below are some additional guidelines for exchange students:
Review information about
payment methods
. Since exchange students often do not have a U.S. bank account set up prior to the first payment due date, many choose to use the "International Currency" payment option through
Flywire
.
Note: When you use the Flywire service, the name of the charge will be "Northwestern University Tuition Payments (Illinois)." Even though the charge description indicates "tuition," it will be credited toward any charges on your student account, including health insurance, housing, and meal plan.
If you are studying at Northwestern for more than one quarter, you will be charged for one quarter of housing and meal plan at the beginning of each quarter.
All students will be charged in full for NU-SHIP health insurance coverage up front. It is not possible for this fee to be assessed quarterly.
If your bill is not paid by the due date, you may incur
late fees or registration holds
.
Your student account includes all transactions in real time, so you will be able to view daily activity as well as your eBill (monthly snapshot).

Arrangements Upon Arrival
New incoming students are required to:
Report to the Office of Global Education (OGE) upon arrival (
typically one week before classes start
). OGE will provide pick-up services on designated dates.
Attend the Orientation Programme.
Settle into student hostels.

Travel Documents
Keep these important documents with you until you arrive at Lingnan:
Valid passport
Student visa/entry permit from the Hong Kong Immigration Department
Admission documentation (acceptance letter)
Travel insurance certificate (if applicable)
Airline tickets
Make photocopies of important documents (e.g., student visa and entry visa stamp) and keep them in a safe place.

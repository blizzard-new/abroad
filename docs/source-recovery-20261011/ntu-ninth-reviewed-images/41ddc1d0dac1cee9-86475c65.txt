Global House (KU G-House)
This co-ed dormitory is located on the Suita Mirai Campus, about a 3-minute walk from the Kansai University Suita Mirai Campus bus stop on the Hankyu Bus.  In addition to single-gender units, all-gender units are available.  All dormitory rooms are single occupancy with toilet and unit bath and are equipped with a shared kitchen/dining room, lounge, and laundry room. A shuttle bus runs between the dormitory and Senriyama Campus.(The number of shuttle buses and seats are limited.)
*Breakfast and dinner are available at the campus cafeteria for dormitory students.

Minami-Senri International Plaza International Students Dormitory(Co-ed)
Minami-Senri International Plaza International Student Dormitory is a co-ed dormitory located about a 15-minute bike ride from Senriyama Campus and a 5-minute walk from Minami-senri Station on the Hankyu Railway Senri Line. In addition to gender-specific units, all-gender units are also available. All rooms are single occupancy equipped with a bathroom and toilet, and each unit is composed of 6-7 rooms, sharing a kitchen and lounge so that students can spend time together.  Dormitory residents are also given opportunities to experience multicultural exchange through activities with local people.

Affiliated Dormitory KU I-House
KU I-House is an affiliated co-ed international-student dormitory, operated by Kyoritsu Maintenance Co., Ltd. It is located within a five-minute bike ride of Senriyama Campus, close to convenience stores and supermarkets, which enables convenient living with a relaxed atmosphere.  There are two buildings and three different types of rooms in each that students can select from based on their life style.

Dormitory Senri-Gaifu-ryo
Dormitory Senri-Gaifu-ryo is male-athlete student dormitory, which was built through donations from the Kansai University Parent's Association in February 2004.  It is a three-story steel reinforced concrete building, located 20 minutes away on foot from Senriyama Campus.  All rooms are single occupancy, equipped with a shower room on the second and third floors.  A bathroom, drying room, and coin laundry are available on the first floor.  The maximum period of residence is four years.

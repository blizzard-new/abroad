You should budget at least 1,100 euros per month for living expenses when studying at RWTH Aachen University:
Monthly Expenditure
Minimum Costs per Month
Accommodation and associated costs
between 400 and 600 euros
Food
between 200 and 300 euros
Health insurance
between 100 and 150 euros
Study Materials (including books, printing etc)
between 20 and 40 euros
Internet and Mobile Phone
between 20 and 50 euros
Additional costs (clothing, hygiene etc.)
between 50 and 200 euros per month
A single person must prove a minimum monthly income of 992 euros to the Immigration Office in Aachen in order to have their residence permit extended.
With the increase in the BAföG maximum rate to 992 euros per month, the amount for the proof of financial support to be provided by international students was increased, see
section 2, paragraph 3, sentence 5 of the Residence Act
.
The embassies and consulates follow the instructions of the Federal Foreign Office; here, the amount for the proof of financing per blocked account, which has risen accordingly to 11,904 euros per year, will be applied from January 1, 2023 when the visa is issued, see the
Federal Foreign Office website
.
Don’t forget to factor your semester contribution into your budget, as you will need to pay this as part of the re-registration process that takes place twice a year.
Semester Fee
The semester fee comprises the student activity dues, a transportation/mobility fee, and a social services contribution. For a detailed breakdown of the current rates, please visit the
AStA (Student Government) website
.

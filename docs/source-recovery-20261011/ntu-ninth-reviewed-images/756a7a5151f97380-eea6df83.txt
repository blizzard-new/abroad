About Utah
Nestled beneath the majestic Wasatch Mountains, the University of Utah offers inspiring
                                    views of the Salt Lake Valley.
Ranked as the best state in America by U.S. News & World Report, Utah has a robust
                                    economy, high-tech sector, vibrant urban area, abundant recreation opportunities,
                                    and low crime rate.
In the foothills above campus are miles of pristine hiking and biking trails. Within
                                    a 30-minute drive are seven world-renowned ski resorts and within five hours you can
                                    visit any of Utah's five breathtaking national parks.

Accommodations
On-Campus Living
All Exchange students are required to live on campus. The Exchange Team will reserve
                                 housing based on your preferences indicated in your application, though placement
                                 is subject to availability.

Pack essentials for your first night, such as bedding, toiletries, and water. You
                                 can also pre-order items through

Mailing Address
You will receive your mailing address after being assigned to housing. You can request
                                 a mailbox through the Optional Services page in your Housing U application.
Meal Plans
All students can choose from various meal plans for on-campus dining.
Meal plans
typically begin a few days to a week before the semester starts, so plan accordingly
                                 if you arrive early.
Transportation, Phones, and Money
Public Transportation
The University of Utah
partners with UTA
to provide free access to various public services like buses, TRAX, and FrontRunner.
Banking in the U.S.
Consider opening a U.S. bank account to manage your finances. Here are some popular
                                 options:
University of Utah Credit Union: Located in the University Bookstore, (801) 481-8800
Wells Fargo Bank: 235 South 1300 East, (801) 582-1235
Bank of America Advantage Banking
Make sure to understand the terms, avoid fees, and close your account before leaving.
Cell Phone Plans
Getting a phone in the U.S. can be tricky, especially without a Social Security Number.
                                 Prepaid options are available, allowing you to pay for what you use. Explore options
                                 with AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and Cricket.

Erasmus+ Student Exchange
Erasmus+
is the European Union's programme to promote cooperation and mobility in the field of education. The University of Vienna has bilateral agreements with
around 370 European universities
and thus provides its students with around
2,800 Erasmus+ study places
at European partner universities.
What does the Erasmus+ programme offer?
Students are admitted to the partner universities through a simplified procedure, receive a mobility grant and are exempt from paying tuition fees. Courses completed abroad can be recognised at the University of Vienna after consultation with the directorate of studies (SPL).

Funding
The grant consists of:
Mobility grant
EUR 470-520/month depending on the country
Travel support
depending on the distance from your home institution; higher support for using environmentally friendly means of transport
Financial Top-Ups
for students with fewer opportunities (e.g. with disabilities, with child/ren, with health issues)
Inclusion support
A grant is only guaranteed during the main application round. If awarded, the funding period may be shorter than the actual stay. The Erasmus+ mobility grant is a financial support and does usually not cover all costs.

You must provide proof of language proficiency
already when applying
for an Erasmus+ place.
You can check in the portal which level of proficiency in which language you need to provide proof for the respective exchange places.
If two languages are listed, it is sufficient to provide proof of proficiency in only one of the two languages. However, please check whether your host university offers enough courses in the respective language.
Please note:
The levels specified for the exchange places are the minimum level required when you apply for the relevant place as a student of the University of Vienna. However, the partner university may specify higher language level requirements or insist on a specific certificate (e.g. TOEFL or IELTS). If you do not fulfil these requirements, you may not be admitted to the partner university. Therefore, obtain information in advance about the requirements specified by your host university. In the portal you will usually find fact sheets with current information about the host university as well as a link to the relevant website.

Language-competency certificate
In addition to
foreign language courses
, the Language Center of the University of Vienna also offers
preparation courses for Erasmus+ stays
and
language-competency certificate exams
, which are accepted as proof of language proficiency.
If you are nominated for an Erasmus+ place, you can apply for a refund of EUR 40.00 for completed language courses and language-competency certificates. Please note that you can only receive a refund for language courses from level B1, phase 3 onwards. Recipients of the federal aid for students receive a refund of EUR 81.00 and can receive this refund for up to 2 language-competency certificates or courses. You can receive a refund for all courses and language-competency certificates of the Language Center if the language level achieved is specified on the certificate and if they took place within the last three semesters.
The time of your nomination determines the refund period: Students who apply for a stay abroad as part of the main application round can get a refund from 1 May to 30 June of the respective year. Students who apply for a stay abroad as part of the second application round can get a refund from 10 November to 10 December of the respective year. The refund is processed directly at the Language Center upon presentation of an ID document. You can only apply for the refund during the refund period that immediately follows your nomination.

If you are admitted to several degree programmes, you can apply for a stay abroad for each programme separately. Create a separate online application for each degree programme.
Please inform your mobility coordinator when you submit your application that you have also applied for a stay abroad in another degree programme. You can only be nominated for one place, i.e. only for one degree programme.
If you wish to complete courses in a different degree programme as part of your stay abroad, please check with your host university whether it offers relevant courses and whether you can also complete courses in degree programme Y as an Erasmus+ student of degree programme X. Clarify with the responsible directorate of studies (SPL) in Vienna whether the courses will be recognised in the relevant degree programme.
Only the ECTS credits of the degree programme in which you have been nominated count as proof of performance. (Exemption: students of Teacher Education)
You cannot take a
leave of absence
for your second degree programme at the University of Vienna for the duration of your semester abroad. For further information, please contact Teaching Affairs and Student Services.
Are you enrolled in a second degree programme at another Austrian university? Notify the other university of your planned stay abroad to be re-enrolled (for the semester of your stay abroad) and find out whether you can take a leave of absence for the duration of your Erasmus+ stay.
Students enrolled in the Teacher Education programme can apply for a stay abroad via their subjects (fields of study) as well as via the study field  “General Teacher Education”. To view available places, enter "General Teacher Education" as the field of study in the
portal for student exchange
. Create a separate online application for each field of study.
You may be able to complete additional courses in the individual subjects, depending on the courses offered by the host university. The ECTS credits of all courses count as proof of performance in the Teacher Education programme.
Switzerland does not participate in the Erasmus+ programme. However, it is possible to complete a stay abroad in Switzerland as part of the Swiss-European Mobility Programme (SEMP).
The International Office of the University of Vienna is responsible for the organisation (in the same way as for Erasmus+ stays).
In contrast to Erasmus+ stays abroad, however, no financial support is granted via the Erasmus+ programme. Instead, the universities in Switzerland offer scholarships to incoming students.
For further information, please contact the respective Swiss university directly.
At the University of Vienna you are exempt from paying tuition fees for the duration of your SEMP stay. Stays abroad as part of SEMP are not deducted from your Erasmus+ quota (12 months per study cycle).

Learning Agreement
The Learning Agreement is the central document for every Erasmus+ stay. You prepare and edit the Learning Agreement directly in Mobility Online.
In this document you specify which courses you will be completing at the host university. At the same time, you agree with your directorate of studies (SPL) for which courses these courses will be recognised in your degree programme at the University of Vienna. All three parties (student, SPL, host university) must sign the Learning Agreement.
It is
recommended
to include courses corresponding to
30 ECTS credits
in total.
Obtain information in good time about the regulations regarding entry and residence applicable in your host country. For further country-specific travel information, please see the
website of the Federal Ministry for European and International Affairs
. For detailed information, please contact the embassy/consulate of the respective country in Austria (
contact details of foreign representations
).
Please note that third-country nationals are subject to separate regulations regarding residence and may need to apply for a visa for their stay abroad in good time. If you have questions, please contact your host university.
A nomination for an Erasmus+ stay abroad does not include insurance coverage. Therefore, please make the necessary arrangements in good time before the start of your stay abroad (e.g. consult the relevant health insurance provider; obtain information about any co-insurance that you may have, any existing travel insurance via your credit card; obtain health, travel and/or accident insurance, if necessary).
Depending on the host country and city, it is a good idea to start looking for accommodation as soon as possible. For support and information about finding accommodation and student residences in your host country, please contact the international office at your host university.
The International Office of the University of Vienna is happy to put you in touch with former outgoing students who have already been to your host university. Seize this opportunity to obtain information and tips. Please contact us by e-mail if you want to reach out to former outgoing students.

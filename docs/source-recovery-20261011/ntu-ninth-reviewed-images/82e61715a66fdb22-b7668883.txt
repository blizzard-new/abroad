Information on student exchange: General Aspects 
Housing
 Housing is guaranteed (International House, the dormitory). 
https://ewww.kumamoto-u.ac.jp/en/prospective/accommodation/internat
ional_house/ 
You may rent a public apartment if you wish. 
https://ewww.kumamoto-u.ac.jp/en/prospective/accommodation/others/ 
Exchange students from partner schools are not required to live on campus 
and may find their own accommodations in the area, but should be aware 
that finding off-campus housing, especially for only one semester, can be 
challenging. Spots in campus housing are reserved for exchange students. 
Room Type at International House Building C or D: each unit with 4 rooms of single occupancy 
- Living room, Shower room, Toilet, and Laundry are shared 
- Each room is furnished with a single bed, desk, chair, and telephone 
(incoming calls only) 
Fees: 17,000 JPY/monthly rent + 10,000 JPY one-time deposit 
Cost of Living
 Approximately, 80,000 JPY/month if you choose to live in the dormitory. 
Approximately, 100,000 JPY/month if you choose to rent a public apartment. 
Health Insurance
 The mandatory Japanese health insurance can be procured upon arrival in 
Kumamoto (approx. 3,000 JPY per month).

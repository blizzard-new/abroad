About
1 Full Academic Year Exchange Program with Exceptional Schoalrship Package
Global Young Leaders Program is a student exchange program fully funded by POSCO & POSTECH. POSCO Group's overseas subsidiaries used to fund the POSCO Global Young Leaders Program to train and cultivate next-generation global talent and from Class of 2024-2025, POSTECH also joined to fund and give more opportunities to become Global Young Leaders under the Program in POSTECH and Korea.
All expenses throughout the stay in Korea will be covered by POSCO & POSTECH for selected students. As a Global Young Leaders Student, you will be able to take courses and participate in research as well as experience Korean culture and industry.

Program Structure
LEAD+
LEAD+ stands for Leadership, Engagement, Achievement, and Development.
This is a signature program designed exclusively for GYLP Students, integrating all cultural and educational activities that aim to support you grow as a Global Young Leader.
LEAD+ is classified into two parts, Educational Sessions and Cultural Excursions. Details of each session and excursion may change every year.
Until now, we have done: Jeju ESG Discovery, Seoul Heritage Excursion, Leadership & Communication Discussion, and else.
Academic Courses
Since GYLP is under Academic Exchange, students should take courses from POSTECH following all regulations of Academic Exchange Program.
Furthermore, GYLP Students are required to take minimum six major courses (excluding general/liberal arts).
Korean Language Courses
Before the program starts, once the student is accepted, he/she should complete online Korean Langauge Courses during summer break.
During the Program, students must take an offline Korean Language Courses offered by PICL (POSTECH Institute of Culture and Language).
Winter Research
During the Winter Break, students must participate in research for at least four weeks (minimum 20 hours per week).
Students are responsible for finding a research advisor during the Fall Semester on campus.

Process
1. Prepare Required Documents & Submit Application
Students should submit application through our IR Portal with Required Documents. During Application period, students may receive system emails to supplement the documents. If you do, please ensure to submit the updated or revised one as soon as possible.
2. Evaluation & Selection
POSTECH International Relations Office will evaluate all applications one by one. And if needed, online interview may be required for some students. Usually evaluation and selection process takes 4-5 weeks after the application deadline.
POSTECH will evaluate and review all details on each student's application including academic, research background, personal statement, study plan at POSTECH, and recommendations. Language Proficiency and overall fit for the program will also be taken into consideration.
Final selection will be made based on the overall quality of the application and the availability of placements within the program.
3. Admission Result
Admission Results will be announced via email with Admission Package including Global Young Leaders Guide.

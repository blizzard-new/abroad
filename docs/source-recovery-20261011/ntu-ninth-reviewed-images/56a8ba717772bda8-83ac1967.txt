Transportation
Transit system
UVic is a
major transit hub
. Plan your commute on
Victoria's transit system
which operates 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
Most students registered in at least one on-campus course for credit will be enrolled in the
U-Pass program
.
Starting September 2023, you'll need to use the new Umo mobile app or a reloadable Umo card to show proof of payment on all buses in the Victoria Regional Transit System. The UVic ONECard will no longer operate as your bus pass.
You'll need to activate your U-Pass every term with that term's unique-to-you
UVic benefit code
.
For more information on how to activate and use your U-Pass, visit the
U-Pass program website
.
Exchange and visiting research students
Exchange and visiting research students are not automatically enrolled in the U-Pass program. You can opt in by purchasing a
University of Victoria Students' Society
or
Graduate Students' Society
membership package.
If you prefer not to purchase a UVSS or GSS membership, you can access the transit system using cash, purchase tickets from a
fare product vendor
, or pay via
Umo
which is a contactless fare payment system. To use Umo, you can download the Umo app or get a free reloadable Umo card from
fare product vendors
around Victoria. On campus, the UVSS, GSS, and UVic Campus Pharmacy can provide Umo cards. You can then buy a pass or load cash through the app, online at
ca.umopass.com
, at a vendor location, or through Umo’s customer service toll-free at 877-380-8181.

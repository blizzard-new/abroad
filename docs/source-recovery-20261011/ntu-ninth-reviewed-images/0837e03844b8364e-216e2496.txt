UPEI Homestay Program
As part of the program, participants are placed with UPEI host families living within the local community.  The UPEI Homestay program maintains close communication with students and families to ensure a happy experience for all. Host families are prepared to welcome students as members of the family and are eager to assist in learning English and sharing Canadian culture. For more information on Homestay or how to become a Host Family, please visit the
Homestay webpage.
Residence (summer months only)
Groups can choose to stay on campus in UPEI Residence accommodations.   Local shopping, sightseeing and Charlottetown’s historic downtown area are just minutes away from the campus.

Insurance
All KTH students are insured through personal injury insurance on KTH's premises and during travel to and from KTH. However, it is crucial to make sure you have additional medical insurance coverage in case of an emergency or if you fall ill during an off-campus activity.
Recommended insurance coverage
We recommend that all international students have comprehensive insurance to avoid high costs in case of illness or accidents. Some students groups receive additional insurance through KTH, while others must arrange insurance themselves. However it is important that all students check their insurance coverage before coming to KTH so that you are fully covered.

If you have a residence permit valid for one year or more, you can register in the Swedish population register and receive a personal identity number. Once you have received your Swedish personal identity number you will be entitled to access public medical services for Swedish patient fees.

Changed fee status and prolonged studies
If your fee status changes during your studies you will not be covered by FAS+ and are strongly recommended to find out if you need to purchase additional insurance coverage for your remaining stay in Sweden. This also applies for students who have not completed their studies within their stipulated study period.

EU/EEA degree programme student
Apart from the personal injury insurance that applies to all students at KTH, master's students from an EU/EEA country who are exempted from paying tuition fees are not provided with any additional insurance coverage through KTH. Insurance coverage needs to be purchased by the students themselves.
European Health Insurance Card (EHIC)
Students from EU/EEA member countries should bring the European Health Insurance Card, filled out by the social insurance office in their home country. With this card you are entitled to the same standard patient fees as Swedish citizens for emergency health care and certain types of non-emergency health care. For more details please consult the social insurance office in your home country. Bring the card when you seek medical care.

Home insurance
Most people in Sweden have home insurance (hemförsäkring), and we strongly recommend getting one during your stay in Sweden. You are responsible for damage or theft in your accommodation. Home insurance usually covers property, liability, legal expenses, and travel protection.
If you are an international fee-paying student, FAS+ covers aspects of a good home insurance plan, even though it is not a home insurance per se. However, you must check your landlord's requirements for a home insurance plan to know if you need to complement FAS+ to get full coverage. Exchange students are covered under Student IN and should ensure that their insurance plan offers complete coverage regarding their landlord's requirements. If you belong to another student group, you need to get an additional home insurance plan.

Housing Information -Dormitories-
All exchange students are required to live in our dormitories. Please go through the information on each housing facility and choose the dormitory that best suits your needs!
You will be asked which dormitory you wish to stay in at the time of application. However the International Office will decide which dormitory you will be placed in and will not accept requests for changes afterwards. We will do our best to allocate you to your first or second choice, but there is a possibility that you may be allocated to a third choice due to capacity.
NUFS International House (I-House)
There are about 60 rooms in a two-storey building with a central hall and courtyard. All rooms are private and each room has its own bathroom. The kitchen is shared. As every step out of a private room opens onto the hall, interaction between residents is easy and active.

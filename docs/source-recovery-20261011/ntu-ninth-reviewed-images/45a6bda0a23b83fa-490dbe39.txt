Students may also choose to arrange their own accommodations. We will provide a list of possible accommodations that have recieved positive reviews from past international students for reference.
Dormitory Application Process
● Step 1: Select "Y" for the dormitory option in the online application system
● Step 2: Submit your program application and all required documents by the deadline, May 31/November 30.
*Note: Physical(hard) copies must also arrive at the office by the deadline.
● Step 3: During the first week of June/December, check your email for the dormitory application newsletter (sent only to studetns who completed Steps 1 & 2).
● Step 4:
On June 8
, the dormitory application form will be made available. Rooms will be reserved on a first-come, first-served basis.
● Step 5: Applicants who secured a dormitory spot (through Step 3) will receive dormitory fee payment instructions. Please make the payment promptly once the payment window opens; otherwise, your spot will be given to the next applicant in line.
● Step 6: Wait for 1-2 weeks to receive confirmation of dormitory fee payment.
● Only cancel/refund requests submitted by June 30/December 31 will be processed. After this date, cancellations and refunds will not be possible under any circumstances.

● Room Assignment
- Dormitory and room type assignments are determined entirely on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Roomates will be assigned automatically
- Room or roomates changes are not permitted
● Facilities
- Mattresses are provided, but students must bring their own bed linenes and pillows
- Dormitory buildings are separated by gender
- Access to each building is restricted to residents, controlled via student ID cards
- Each dormitory building has a common area equipped with microwaves and refridgerators
- Each dormitory building has laundry facilities, study areas, and a gym
- There are no kitchen facilities, as cooking is strictly prohibited

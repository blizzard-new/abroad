HECTOR School Accommodation
Stay close to your place of learning
Living at the Heart of Karlsruhe
HECTOR School offers participants convenient accommodation options in Karlsruhe, designed for the periods spent on campus. The accommodation is located close to the HECTOR School and combines furnished living spaces with practical services for a comfortable stay.
Accommodation at Special Rates
The international department offers HECTOR School participants accommodation at special rates, providing a convenient place to stay during their on-site phases. The options include 18 fully furnished single rooms as well as two- and three-bedroom apartments, which can also be shared.
All accommodations feature private bathrooms, modern kitchens with dishwashers, Wi-Fi and shared living and dining areas. Additional services include bed linen, all utilities, cleaning between modules and washing facilities.

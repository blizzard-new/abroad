Semester Exchange Programmes
provides opportunity to study at UM partner university (host university) for one or two semesters without being required to pay international student fees. Students will pay the regular UM tuition fee when going on exchange while credits earned abroad may be transferred and counted to your UM degree.
Eligibility
Specific requirements for exchange vary according to the exchange programme. Here are some of the basic requirements.
Full-time Students with good academic and disciplinary standing;
Undergraduate student must complete one year of study at UM by the time of departure;
Postgraduate student must complete one semester of study at UM by the time of departure;
Satisfactory English language proficiency as required by both UM and the partner institution; and
Specific criteria set by faculty and host institution, if any.

Steps to Apply
How to Submit your Exchange Application?
Students who are interested in applying Exchange or Study Abroad Programmes (including University Level and Faculty Level)
should make applications via the “
Outbound Exchange and Scholarship Application System
”
before application deadline.
University Level Exchange Programme (Operated by GAO)
University Level Exchange Programmes are open to all UM students.
Log in to the Outbound Exchange and Scholarship Application
https://isw.um.edu.mo/seas
Fill in the requested information together with the following supporting documents
Curriculum Vitae
Learning agreement
[Download Form]
Travel document to Mainland & ID copy (for exchange to Mainland Universities only)
Other supporting documents, if any (e.g. certificate, awards, language proficiency)
Note:
Hard copy or e-mail submissions will NOT be accepted.
Faculty Level Exchange Programme (Operated by Faculties)
Students may also consider joining the exchange programmes offered by your Faculty/Institute. Applications should be make via the
“
Outbound Exchange and Scholarship Application System
”
. Application arrangement is subject to latest announcements from respective Faculties/Institute.
Programme Coordinators

International Student ID Card
To support students' active international exchanges and activities, SKKU offers a UNESCO-certified International Student Identity Card (ISIC) service. Use the information below to apply for a card.
APPLY FOR ISIC
A GUIDE TO ISIC
ISIC EXAMPLEAPPLY FOR ISICGUIDE TO ISIC
Eligibility
Undergraduates, graduates, and students on a leaves of absence are eligible for cards. 
							However, graduate students who have completed their courses (graduate certification completers) are not eligible.
ISIC EXAMPLEAPPLY FOR ISICGUIDE TO ISIC
ISIC EXAMPLEAPPLY FOR ISICGUIDE TO ISIC
How to Apply
Click "Apply for Issuance of International Student ID Card" → Fill out online application → Pay issuance fee
(Payment Method: specified in online application) → Visit Student Support Team on either campus
Issuance and Pick-up Location
The ISIC is issued by the Student Support Team at either campus.
Expiration Date
The ISIC is valid for a period of 12 months from the date of issuance.
Discount Benefits
Flights, transportation, accommodation, museums, historic sites, cultural asset tours, etc.

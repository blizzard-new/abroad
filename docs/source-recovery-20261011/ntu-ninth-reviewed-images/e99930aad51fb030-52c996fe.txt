Ooka International Residence
Ooka International Residence is a quality housing complex for international community of students and faculty and administrative staff of YNU, aimed to promote multicultural exchanges and mutual understanding.
1R rooms are divided into 
                    men's (1st to 4th floor) and women's (5th and 6th floor).
1K rooms are on the 1st to 5th floor,mixed with men's and women's.
Address
2-31, Ooka, Minami-ku, Yokohama 232-0061
Nearest station
Blue Line Gumyoji Subway Station. 3 minute walk from Gate 1.
Eligibility
Students※ faculty and administrative staff of YNU
※Japanese students: except Research students, Accredited auditors and Auditors.
International students: except Accredited auditors and Auditors.
Tenancy
4 years for undergraduate and 5 years for graduate at the longest.
Number of Units
310 (281 for students, 29 for faculty and administrative staff)
Single rooms for International Students (252 Units)
※Management and operation of Ooka International Residence are entrusted to Ichii Corporation,LTD.

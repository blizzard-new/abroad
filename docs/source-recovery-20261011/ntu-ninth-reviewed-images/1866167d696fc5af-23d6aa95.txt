Canceling Storrs Housing
Upon your commitment to your program, Experiential Global Learning will communicate your information to UConn Residential Life. Once you are fully committed to your program, you can log in to the MyHousing portal and cancel your housing. If you are participating in an Experiential Global Learning program for the fall semester, pay your ResLife room deposit if you are not yet committed to your program. Your deposit will be refunded once you commit to your program and ResLife has your information.
Registering for Housing for When You Return
If you are participating in an Experiential Global Learning program in the fall semester, ResLife will determine your spring assignment based on the information you provide in your completed housing application. You will be able to access your assignment on-line in early January. ResLife will send specific information to your UConn email account to keep you up to date on the process.
If you are participating in an Experiential Global Learning program in the spring semester, you must select your own room through the on-line housing selection process. In February you will receive information through your UConn email account outlining the specific process. If you are unable to select a room during your scheduled selection time, ResLife staff will serve as your proxy and access the system at that time to pick your room and notify you of the assignment through email.

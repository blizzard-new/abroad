*Below are instructions to complete the
USC application
. You will also be required to submit an application with your
host institution
. After you are accepted through USC’s application, the Education Abroad Office will
                                 send you instructions for completing your application with the host institution.*
Students applying to
Bamberg
must submit
2 faculty recommendations
to our office before applications are accepted. Bamberg applicant recommendations
                                 should be from German department faculty who can attest to language ability.
Step 1
: Go to the
program directory
If this is your first time logging into Via, the application database, you will be
                                    prompted to sign in using your USC username and password.
Please choose "go to portal" upon signing in.
You’ll also be directed to a data intake form as well as a program match quiz to help
                                    match you with a program.
If you already know what program you want to do, locate it in the
program directory
and click the "apply now” link on the top right of the program page.
Step 2
: Complete your application requirements
Prior to starting the application, you will be asked to choose an alternate. Please
                                    choose an alternate program in case your exchange program is full.
Begin filling in the application information. Note that if some fields are gray and
                                    cannot be edited, they will be automatically updated once your data is retrieved from
                                    the USC Banner System.
Upload your USC transcript (unofficial transcripts are accepted)
Complete the required essay questions
Read and acknowledge the following agreements:
Student Agreement and General Statement of Responsibility
Mandatory Overseas Medical Insurance Confirmation
Housing Responsibility Notice
Visa Responsibility Notice
Pay the Education Abroad Application Fee $225 (Undergraduate students only)
Submit your application
Step 3:
Education Abroad will review your application and mark it as
“conditionally approved”
. You will receive an email confirming your approval with a list of forms to complete. Please
                                 note that exchange applications are reviewed after the application deadline of March
                                 1 (fall and academic year programs), May 15 (priority spring applications) or September
                                 15 (spring programs).
You will be notified after this date of your acceptance and will be sent a checklist
                                    from an education abroad advisor with next steps.
Step 4:
Log into your application and complete the required forms.  You will receive an email
                                 confirmation for each submitted form.
Financial Aid Pre-Screen Form
Passport Upload
Medical Information
Proof of Application to Host Institution
Education Abroad Approval Form
Pre-Departure Information
Step 5
: Education Abroad will review your forms. You will receive an email confirmation
                                 for each accepted form. Incomplete forms will be sent back to you with directions
                                 for completion. When forms have been reviewed and are complete, your application will
                                 be moved to
“committed”
status. Please submit all forms by the stated deadline.
Step 6:
Complete the mandatory Safe Check Enrollment. An automatic email will be sent prompting
                                 you to complete this step. Additionally, you can keep track of your travel in the
                                 Travel Plan section (optional) of your dashboard.
Step 7:
After receiving clearance from the Office of Student Conduct, the Education Abroad
                                 Office will enroll you in the
mandatory overseas insurance coverage
as well as the
INTL Placeholder Course
which keeps your USC enrollment active for your time abroad. When you are enrolled
                                 in insurance, you will receive an email from CISI, the insurance provider, with documentation
                                 as well as an email from our office with details of your INTL enrollment.
All emails will come from our application database, Via TRM. Please be on the lookout
                                    for emails from messages@Via-trm.com. The application system works best on a laptop
                                    or desktop computer and with the Google Chrome browser.
Requirements
Students must have a 3.0 GPA to apply for a university exchange program. Most programs
                                    require a sophomore standing by the start of the program.

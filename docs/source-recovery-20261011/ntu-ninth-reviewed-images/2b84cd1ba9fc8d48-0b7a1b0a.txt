Okayama Prefecture is located in the center of West Japan and is particularly bless-
ed with fine weather. As the climate is very mild, with low rainfall— a typical Seto 
Inland climate, this area has thriving agriculture, specializing in fruit products such 
as Muscat grapes and white peaches. Rich marine products are provided by the Seto 
Inland Sea, which offers picturesque view in the region. Conveniently 
located, Okayama has thrived, and is known for its history and cultural 
achievements.

LIVING IN OKAYAMA
All EPOK students stay at the International Student Shared House on 
campus. Students share a unit with one Japanese student and two other 
international students. Every unit is single sex, has a common room with 
kitchen facilities, a bathroom, a toilet, and four private rooms. Not only 
is it within ten minutes walking distance from T sushima Campus, it also 
offers a great opportunity to experience customs and cultures of both 
Japan and other parts of the world.

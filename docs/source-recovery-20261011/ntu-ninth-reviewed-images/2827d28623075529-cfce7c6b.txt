U.S. Housing
If you are living in
on-campus housing
, and studying abroad in the Spring, you can terminate your contract early by taking a copy of your acceptance letter to your residence hall director.
If you live
off-campus
, you may need to sublease your Champaign-Urbana apartment, or find a sublease for yourself for the semester you’ll be on campus. Utilize resources such as online marketplaces, community group ads, your college’s posting areas, or the
study abroad housing board
to find solutions. Make sure that you review the policies within your lease and consult with your landlord to make appropriate arrangements.

Housing While Abroad

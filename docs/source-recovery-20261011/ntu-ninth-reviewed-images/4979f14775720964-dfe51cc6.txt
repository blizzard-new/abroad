  INCOMING STUDENT QUOTA
Length of the study abroad/exchange period at Temple: one (1) year or one (1) semester
Study Abroad: Typically, up to 10 students from one institution at any given time
Exchange: Typically 4 semesters per academic year (Fall-Spring)*
*Please conﬁrm with Global Programs for any questions regarding student quota
  APPLICATION
Online Application: https://connect.temple.edu/portal/global_applytoday
Fall priority and Temple Japan deadline: April 1; Fall ﬁnal deadline: April 15
Spring deadline: October 15 (Temple Japan Deadline September 1)
Application Process
1.  Home university nominates student
2.  Global Programs contacts student with application link and instructions
3.  Student creates online account, completes application and submits supplemental documents online
4.  Global Programs reviews application
5.  Global Programs sends electronic acceptance packet to student
Supplemental Application Documents:
List of current semester courses
Oﬃcial language scores
Course registration process form
Oﬃcial transcript(s)
Housing agreement form (if applicable)
I-20 application (after acceptance)
Financial documentation
Copy of biographical page of passport


  ADMISSION CRITERIA
Undergraduate (bachelor’s) students
Must have completed at least 2 semesters of full-time study at home university before studying at 
Temple
GPA:
Exchange Applicants: 3.00/4.00
Study Abroad/Fee-Paying Applicants: 2.50/4.00
English requirements: TOEFL (4.0 iBT, 550 ITP), IELTS (6.0), Duolingo English Test (110), 
CEFR (B2), or TOEIC Listening & Reading Test (390 in each section)*
Graduate (master’s) students
Varies by academic program, please contact us for more details regarding master's students. PhD 
students are not eligible to study at Temple on exchange.
*English language instruction is available through the Temple Center for English Language and Culture and may be 
required for students who do not meet minimum language requirements: https://tcalc.temple.edu/
  ACADEMICS
Course Schedule: www.temple.edu/courses
Course enrollment is based upon availability. 
Students must meet all course pre-requisites 
and must maintain a 2.00 GPA while 
enrolled at Temple. 
Any graduate (master’s) class requests must 
be approved by professor or chair of the 
academic department at Temple University.
Restricted Departments: The following departments are restricted. This means students will most 
likely be unable to register for any courses in these majors.

Computer & Information Sciences Professional Schools (Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Pharmacy, 
Podiatry)
Business (must be a business major) Visual Arts (Film/Media Arts, Graphic Design, Fine Arts, etc.)
Education Sport, Tourism and Hospitality Management

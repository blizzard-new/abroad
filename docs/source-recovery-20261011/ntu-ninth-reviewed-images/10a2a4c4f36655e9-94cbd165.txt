offered by Global Education Center (GEC)
For Exchange Students to Kobe University

Do you know that the GEC is on the list of affiliated schools 
that exchange students* may enroll at?

*NOTE: This applies to exchange students from our partner universities listed here.

Any international student enrolled at Kobe University can take a Japanese language course offered at 
GEC, but exchange students belonging to the GEC will have several advantages: 
神戸大学所属の留学生ならだれでもGECの日本語科目を履修することはできますが、GEC所属生になれば、
次のようなメリットがあります。
✓ Study guidance from GEC academic staffwho are experts in Japanese language and culture. 
日本語・日本文化を専門とするGECの専任教員による指導が受けられる。
✓ More opportunities for hands-on experience of Japanese culture and exchange with local 
Japanese people
日本文化や地域の日本人との交流を体験する機会が増える。
✓ Support by Japanese students from Kobe university
神戸大学日本人学生のサポートが受けられる。
Eligibility 資格（対象者）: Please also refer to the *NOTE above.
-Exchange students* majoring in Japanese language or Japanese Studies at their home universities
所属大学で日本語や日本事情を専攻している交換留学生
-Exchange students* who wish to prioritize studying Japanese language and culture during their stay 
at Kobe University
神戸大学留学中は、日本語や日本文化の学習を優先的に行いたい交換留学生
To the prospective exchange students* who wish to belong to the GEC
GECへの所属を希望する交換留学生*のみなさんへ:
In order for GEC students to gain a deeper understanding of Japanese language and culture, as well as the Kobe 
region, we offer the following guidance:
GEC所属の学生には、留学期間中に日本語や日本文化、そして神戸地域に関する理解を深めてもらうことを期待し、次のような指導を行いま
す。
✓ GEC students will be allocated a supervising professor, after the official acceptance. You will meet regularly 
with your professor, and submit monthly reports (in Japanese or English) as per instructions.
受け入れ後に指導教員を決定します。定期的にその指導を受けてください。また、指示に従ってマンスリーレポート（日本語ないし英語）を
提出してください。
✓ We will help you devise a study plan to include the following courses in the minimum 10 hours of classes 
(which is equivalent to 6 or 7 courses in Kobe University) that you are required to take per week.
在籍中は、履修が必要な週10時間（神戸大学では6⁻7科目相当）の授業に、下記の科目を含めることを推奨します。

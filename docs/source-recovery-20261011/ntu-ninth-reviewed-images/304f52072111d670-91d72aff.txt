Why study at UTokyo—Tradition and Innovation 
Founded in 1877, the University of Tokyo (UTokyo) is the oldest national university in Japan. Over the course of its long 
and distinguished history, the university has produced some of the most outstanding scholars, scientists, and social and 
political leaders of Japan and the world, including 10 Nobel laureates, 15 prime ministers of Japan, and one Fields Medal 
winner. As Japan’s most comprehensive research university, UTokyo has 10 undergraduate faculties/college, 15 graduate 
schools, 11 affiliated Institutes, and a number of University-wide Centers. More than 5,800 faculty members teach 
approximately 14,000 undergraduate students and almost equal number of graduate students at three main campuses in 
the Tokyo region and additional research facilities across Japan. UTokyo is committed to excellence in research and 
education as well as establishing a truly global campus to produce the next generation of leaders of the world.  
 
About USTEP 
UTokyo accepts exchange students from partner universities with a Memorandum of Understanding concerning student 
exchange. Exchange students can choose between the Type U and Type G programs.  
The Type U program is designed for undergraduate students to take courses given in English and/or Japanese at the 
undergraduate College and Faculties. The Type G program is designed for graduate students who will belong to one of 
the Graduate Schools and study or research under the guidance of faculty members there.

Scholarship 
In principle, scholarship is not available for USTEP students. For those who may encounter financial difficulties, please 
make sure to secure your funding by applying available scholarships at your home university or your home country. In 
accordance with Japanese visa requirements, applicants will be asked to submit a bank statement demonstrating a balance 
of 120,000 JPY per month for each month of enrollment.  
Housing 
Exchange students may apply for University Housing arranged by UTokyo and live in one of the university’s residential 
facilities depending on the availability. About 90% of students are successfully accommodated for the university housing. 
For those interested, please find more detail in “4. Additional Information” on the website.  
Application Procedures and Deadlines 
1．Apply at your home university. Prospective applicants must contact the office responsible for student exchange at
their home university and check the application procedures for the exchange program to study at UTokyo.  
2．Once we receive nominations from your home university during the application period, you will receive application 
instructions including the link to access an online application form.  
3．Make sure that you meet the eligibility criteria for USTEP: See eligibility noted in “1. Qualifications.”

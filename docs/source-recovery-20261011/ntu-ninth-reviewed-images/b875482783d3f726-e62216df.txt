Living in Sweden as an exchange student
On this page you can find information about important practical aspects to consider before arriving to your exchange in Sweden. You can also get inspired by getting a glimpse of the student life in Uppsala and Visby.
Housing
As an exchange student, you get prioritised as long as the application is submitted on time.
Financing
You must be able to cover all of your living expenses during your exchange studies.
Health care
Information about the health care system in Sweden and what to do if you fall ill.
Insurance
Exchange students are covered by the insurance "Student IN". Find out what coverage you have and what you might need to add.
Visa and residence permit
If you have a non EU/EEA citizenship, you have to apply for a residence permit. You can find information on the Swedish Migra­tion Agency's website.
Student life at Uppsala University
During exchange studies, you will be able to experience one of the two most vivid student contexts and unique historical settings Sweden has to offer. Both Uppsala and Visby promise a great exchange.

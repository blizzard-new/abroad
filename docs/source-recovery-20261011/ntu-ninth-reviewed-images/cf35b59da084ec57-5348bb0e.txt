You would like to apply for a study visit outside of certain
programs
or for an internship at TU Dortmund University and are enrolled at a university outside Germany? Here you can find out how to enroll.
Enrollment is possible at any given time.
Instructions: Apply for Enrollment
To register, please send an e-mail to:
international.enrollment@tu-dortmund.de
Subject:
Application for enrollment as an exchange student
Contents:
Full name (as in passport/ID card)
Degree program or subjects for which you wish to enroll
Attachments:
List of required enrollment documents
Permitted file format:
pdf
Not permitted: Cloud links, zip files and similar
List of Required Enrollment Documents
Registration for Enrollment
Valid identification document, e.g. passport
Confirmation of supervision issued by the department stating the duration of your stay
For enrollment as a foreign language assistant at schools in NRW:
Registration for Enrollment
Valid identification document, e.g. passport
School assignment stating the duration of your employment
You will immediately receive an automatic confirmation of receipt of your first e-mail (please also check your spam folder). Processing is carried out strictly according to the date of receipt and can take a couple of weeks.
We cannot give priority to individual e-mails!
We will request missing documents by e-mail.
We will then send you the provisional enrollment certificate with Campus Portal login, university e-mail address and information on the semester contribution.
A payment confirmation for the semester contribution will not be sent
. As soon as your semester contribution has been booked and your health insurance registration has been received, you are
automatically fully enrolled
and can download your certificate of enrollment from the Campus Portal.

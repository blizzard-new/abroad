Student Health Insurance Plan
Student Health Insurance: Fall Semester Enrollment begins August 1, 2026 and runs through October 2, 2026.
New Student
The carrier for the 2026-2027 Temple University student health insurance plans is
Aetna Student Health
.  Aetna Student Health offers comprehensive coverage, competitive pricing and a robust provider network, so you can get the care you need when you need it.
As in the past, the plan you chose also includes pharmacy, dental and vision benefits.
Students will still have the opportunity to choose from the two medical plan options:
PPO High Plan
PPO Deductible Plan
Addition Resources
The coverage period is either 12 months (September through August) or 8 months (January through August). At the end of the coverage period, insurance coverage will automatically terminate effective August 31. Students must re-enroll during the open enrollment period at the beginning of each new academic year. Eligible students will receive an email notification regarding student health insurance enrollment.
If you enroll for the Fall term, coverage will remain in effect from September 1, 2026, through August 31, 2027.
If you enroll for the Spring term, coverage will remain in effect from January 1, 2027, through August 31, 2027.
Please note
: Aetna does not allow early termination of coverage. If you enroll during the fall or spring, your policy will remain active for the full coverage period for which you enrolled.
All full-time students have the opportunity to purchase student health insurance at reduced group rates.  There are three plan options offered by the university through Aetna.
Some student classifications are required to have health insurance coverage in accordance with Temple University’s student enrollment policies.

Monthly Premium Billing
Aetna is responsible for generating and sending the monthly bill for health insurance coverage. The bill will be sent to the address you provided during the enrollment process. You are responsible for paying the bill directly to Aetna. For questions regarding your bill, contact Aetna at 1-877-626-2308.
Transparency in Coverage
The Transparency in Coverage Rule (TCR) requires health plans and plan sponsors to make in-network and out-of‑network machine‑readable files available to the public by July 1, 2022.
Transparency in coverage helps consumers know the cost of a covered item or service before receiving care.

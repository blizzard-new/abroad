Eligibility  
Students  who  wish  to  study  at  AIU  for  one  or  two  semesters  must  meet  all  of  the  requirements  ( Enrollment  Status ,  GPA ,  English  Proficiency )  listed  on  the  following  webpage.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/semester-year-programs/eligibility/  [Note  of  Caution  about  Enrollment  Status]   Students  must  have  completed  at  least  two  semesters  at  their  home  university  (with  grades  shown  on  the  submitted  official  academic  transcript)  by  the  application  deadline.   *Students  in  a  3-year  program,  a  Master's  program,  and/or  transfer  students,  please  refer  to  our  Eligibility  page for  requirements.  If  there  is  any  uncertainty  regarding  a  student's  eligibility,  the  home  university  must  contact  us  BEFORE  submitting  the  nomination.

Courses  Offered  to  International  Students  
All  subject  matter  courses  are  taught  in  English  and  are  available  to  international  students.   *International  students  may  NOT  enroll  in  preparatory  English  language  courses  or  certain  courses  that  are  required  for  and  restricted  to  degree-seeking  students  only.  Be  sure  to  check  the  restrictions  indicated  in  the  Course  Offerings  available  at  the  following  link.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/course/ 
 
Course  Registration  
All  new  students  register  for  courses  after  arrival  at  AIU.  Please  note  that  the  course  list  students  submit  in  their  application  forms  is  for  our  screening  committee’s  reference,  NOT  for  official  course  registration.  
Tips  on  Course  Selection  
 
-  We  strongly  recommend  students  to  obtain  approval  for  as  many  alternatives  as  possible  prior  to  arriving  at  AIU,  in  case  they  cannot  get  into  their  first-choice  approved  courses.   -  Our  class  sizes  are  small  in  order  to  ensure  maximum  interaction,  so  the  capacity  of  each  course  is  limited.  Please  understand  there  is  a  chance  students  will  not  be  able  to  get  into  their  first-choice  courses.  Seats  for  courses  that  exceed  capacity  are  determined  by  lottery  and  a  waiting  list  system.   -  Students  are  NOT  able  to  register  for  courses  which  overlap  in  the  timetable.  Course  Offerings,  Class  Schedules,  and  Syllabi  are  updated  in  early  June  (for  Fall  semester)  and  in  mid  October  (for  Spring  semester).  If  any  further  slight  changes  occur,  these  will  be  updated  again  in  late  August  (for  Fall  semester)  and  in  late  March  (for  Spring  semester).  Please  be  sure  to  check  the latest  information on  Class  Schedules before  course  registration.   
Attendance  Policy  
AIU's  education  model  emphasizes  student  interaction  in  the  classroom  including  group  and  pair  work  activities,  so  attendance  is  mandatory.  Each  instructor  sets  their  own  attendance  policy  and  in  some  classes,  a  certain  number  of  absences  can  result  in  an  automatic  failing  grade.  
Academic  Transcript  
An  official  academic  transcript  will  be  sent  to  the  international  office  of  the  student’s  home  institution  by  the  Division  of  Student  Records  within  2-3  weeks after  the  end  of  final  exams.

Access  to  the  University  
The  AIU  campus  is  approximately  a  10-minute  drive  from  Akita  Airport  and  a  30-minute drive  from  the  JR  Akita  Train  station .  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/access/ 
Arrival  Date  and  Time  
 All  students  must  arrive  on  campus  during  the  designated  date  and  times  (no  exceptions)  in  order  to  complete  necessary  paperwork  related  to  residence  registration,  mandatory  insurances,  etc.  that  will  need  to  be  done  on  the  date  of  arrival.   If  students  cannot  find  a  flight  that  arrives  in  Akita  during  that  time,  they  must  arrive  the  night  before  and  come  to  campus  during  the  designated  date  and  time.  Accepted  students  will  be  informed  of  the  designated  date  and  times  of  arrival  at  the  time  of  the  application  results  announcement.   
Pick-up  Service  
 As  public  transportation  is  very  limited  in  Akita,  we  ask  that  students  use  AIU’s  free  pick-up  bus  service  from  Akita  Airport  or  JR  Akita  Train  Station  at  the  designated  times  on  the  arrival  date.   For  more  information,  please  refer  to  the  following  webpage.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/semester-year-programs/transportation-and-hotel/  
Things  to  Know  About  Living  at  AIU,  in  Akita  and  Japan  
 There  are  a  few  things  that  may  surprise  international  students  upon  arriving  in  Japan,  Akita,  and  AIU.  Please  familiarize  yourself  with  the  rules,  etiquette,  and  daily  life  in  Akita  and  at  AIU  to  be  well-prepared.    For  more  details,  please  refer  to  the  following  webpage.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/campuslife/living/

Medical  Information  Form  
 
All  admitted  students  are  required  to  submit  a  completed  Medical  Information  Form,  which  is  available  in  their  Online  Application  Portal  after  acceptance.  The  Medical  Information  Form  consists  of  three  parts.  Part  I  is  to  be  completed  online  by  students.  After  completing  the  online  part  (Part  I),  they  must  bring  Part  I  and  Part  II  to  their  physician  who  understands  their  mental  and  physical  condition  for  completion,  have  their  parent,  guardian,  or  next-of-kin  complete  Part  III,  then  submit  ALL  THREE  PARTS  TOGETHER  by  email.   Students  and  their  home  university  coordinator  are  asked  to  go  over  the  Medical  Information  Form  together  before  submission.  Home  universities  should  also  know  about  any  conditions  students  have  so  that  we  can  work  together  to  provide  support  in  case  of  an  emergency.  
Support  on  AIU  Campus  
 "Health  and  Medical  Care  Room",  "Counseling  Room"  There  is  a  "Health  and  Medical  Care  Room"  and  a  "Counseling  Room"  on  campus.  Please  note  that  there  is  no  medical  doctor  stationed  on  campus.  For  more  information,  please  refer  to  the  following  webpage.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/campuslife/health/  “Reasonable  Accommodations”  If  students  require  any  Academic  and/or  Physical  Support  (such  as  housing  considerations)  while  at  AIU,  applications  must  be  submitted  by  the  specified  deadline.  For  more  information,  please  refer  to  the  following  webpage  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/campuslife/support/ or  contact  the  Study  Support  and  Student  Wellbeing  Coordinator  (studentwellbeing@gl.aiu.ac.jp).   
Bringing  Medications  
 
Students  who  intend  to  bring  medications  or  continue  using  medications  in  Japan  should  prepare  to  bring  all  required  medicines  with  them,  as  it  may  be  difficult  or  impossible  to  acquire  the  same  medicines  in  Japan.   However,  it  is  illegal  to  bring  some  prescription  and  over-the-counter  medicines  commonly  used  in  the  United  States  or  other  countries  to  Japan,  including  certain  inhalers  and  certain  allergy  and  sinus  medications.  For  more  information  on  how  to  bring  medications  to  Japan,  please  refer  to  the  following  webpage.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/semester-year-programs/health-and-medical-care/  
Hospitals  and  Clinics  Situation  in  Akita  
 There  is  little  medical  support  available  in  English  at  clinics  and  hospitals  in  Akita.  Students  must  ensure  that  they  can  access  any  necessary  ongoing  medical  services  during  their  stay  in  Akita  prior  to  traveling  to  Japan.  In  general  AIU  does  not  provide  hospital  escort  or  interpretation  services.  Please  refer  to  the  following  webpage  for  some  common  misconceptions  regarding  medical  preparation.  https://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/semester-year-programs/health-and-medical-care/  
Overseas  Travel  Insurance  
International  students  are  required  to  purchase  overseas  travel  insurance  before  departure.  Please  read  the  detailed  information  on  the  following  web  page:  http://web.aiu.ac.jp/en/short-term/semester-year-programs/insurance/

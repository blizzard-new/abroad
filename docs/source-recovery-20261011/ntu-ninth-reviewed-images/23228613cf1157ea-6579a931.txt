Housing Information
Technion has two separate campuses – the medical school complex, which is adjacent to the Rambam Medical Center (located in Bat Galim), and the science and technology complex located at the main Technion campus in Neve Shaanan.
Dorm rooms are extremely limited. Request for on-campus housing must be submitted at least three months in advance.
Applications for Technion International BSc Program (Mechanical Engineering in English)/ Sstudy Abroad Program/ Research Students: please contact
int.students@technion.ac.il
Graduate School: please contact
grdorm@technion.ac.il
Post-doctoral fellows: please contact
postdoctoral@technion.ac.il

Winter International Student Experience
Program Overview
The SKKU Winter International Student Experience (WISE) is a three-week in-person academic program designed to provide global students with a meaningful and immersive study-abroad experience during the winter season. Participants take credit-bearing courses taught in English across diverse fields such as Business & Economics, Social Sciences & Humanities, Computer & Data Science, and Korean Studies & Korean Languages, all held to the same academic standards as regular semesters at SKKU. In addition to academics, students participate in hands-on cultural immersion activities such as ski trips, cooking workshops, and K-pop experiences. An optional online Intensive Korean Language Program is also offered prior to the in-person session, allowing students to build basic Korean language skills at their own pace.

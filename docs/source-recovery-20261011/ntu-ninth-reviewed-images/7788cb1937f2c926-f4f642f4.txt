Tokiwadai International Residence
Tokiwadai International Residence is a new housing complex which opened on March 2019. It is a new type of residence where students and researchers live together while promoting multicultural exchanges and mutual understanding among them.
In a Shared Unit, eight students from different nationality, class and college forms one unit, and lives together in a “shared house” type residence which includes a living room and a kitchen. Apart from this, a fully-private type room and researcher’s accommodation are also provided.
Both Shared-Units and Private Type Rooms are divided into men's and women's areas in the building.
Eligibility
Students※ and international researchers of YNU
※Japanese students: except Research students, Accredited auditors and Auditors.
International students: except Accredited auditors and Auditors.
Tenancy
Students: Within 4 years for undergraduate student and 5 years for graduate student.
Researchers: Within the period accepted as international researcher at YNU.

French language courses
Exchange students can enroll in French language courses offered by the
École de français
of UdeM’s Faculty of Continuing Education. They have two options:
1. Full-time enrollment at the École de français
This opportunity is available to students who have a knowledge of the French language. When applying online for the exchange program, choose the option "Français lang. sec. non-franco.". A semester of French language courses consists of:
4 to 5 French language courses of 3 credits each (for a total of 12 to 15 credits)
Please note that a full-time enrolment to French-language courses may not be guaranteed as it depends on many factors such as: student’s level of French, course offering, course schedules, etc. Certain courses may also be cancelled shortly before the beginning of the term due to low enrolment. Since Université de Montréal cannot refund any fees students may have undertaken, it is essential that students plan for alternative courses (other language course or courses in English Studies).
2. French language courses for students enrolled in another study program
You can take one 3-credit French language course per semester in addition to the courses in your study program.
See our French courses
Placement test and enrollment
To enroll in French language courses, you will need to complete the mandatory online placement test after being admitted to the exchange program. The information regarding the test will be sent to you after your admission.
Please take the time to answer the questions on the placement test carefully. Using external help (such as reference documents, the internet, or another person) is prohibited. The test is not eliminatory; its purpose is to allow the École de français to place you at the level that best matches your proficiency. Note that students who use external help risk being placed in courses that are too difficult for their skill level. It will not be possible to change your registration afterward.
After completing the test, you will receive an email with your proficiency level (QUM) in both oral and written skills, along with a list of courses you can enroll in.
You will need to contact the
Student Technician
for French language courses to proceed with your course registration.
Fee: For a 3-credit course, a fee of $77,25 CAD will be charged to your student account for course materials.

Living costs
In Germany, living expenses can vary quite a bit, especially when it comes to housing. To help you plan your student budget, we've outlined what you should roughly budget for monthly living costs in Munich.
In the capital of Bavaria, you should calculate with about 1,500 euros per month. Here’s a basic outline of what you should budget for monthly living costs:
Rooms in private housing including utilities: 700 - 1,000 euros
Rooms in university housing including utilities: 400 – 600 euros
Food, health insurance, leisure activities: approx. 500 euros
„Deutschlandticket für Studierende“ (public transportation): 43 euros /month
LMU semester fee: 97 euros

Attached is the Factsheet for the 2026 Spring Semester (March ~ June, 2025).

The nomination and application period for the 2026 Fall Semester is expected to begin in October 2025. 
(Nomination deadline: October 15th, Application deadline: October 31st)

In the meantime, please review the attached factsheet for detailed information.

*Please note that certain information in this factsheet has been updated from the previous semester, including details on dormitory accommodation and the RKP Korean Language Program. Please be noted.

If you have any questions, feel free to contact us at incoming@skku.edu.

Thank you.

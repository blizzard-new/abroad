Application Guidelines for Exchange Students
to Ochanomizu University (Fiscal Year 2027)
The most distinguishing feature of the educational programs Ochanomizu University
offers is the adoption of highly advanced curriculums taught in small class units. In the
specialized education curriculum in particular, the intellectual capacity that each student
aims for is cultivated through small seminars, labs and practical training.
Another pivotal focus of the university's educational platform is "leader education"
firmly grounded into the current trend toward globalization. In the quest to foster
international caliber knowledge and abilities, we make keen efforts to instill superior
language skills and cultural literacy applicable on the global front, in tandem with the
development and practical application of educational research keyed to the lifestyle of
modern women.
True to our mission statement to excel as "a place where all women who are motivated
to learn can realize their earnest dreams", Ochanomizu University will continue to
fulfill our role as an institution of higher education.
For details, please refer to　https://www.ocha.ac.jp/intl/studyjp-en/index.html

・GPA: Applicants are recommended to have the latest GPA score of 2.5 or above on a

by case basis. If this is the case, please consult before submitting the application.  If the
4.0 scale doesn’t apply to the grading system at their home university, pleease convert it
to the 4.0 scale.
・Language Proficiency:
Applicants are expected to have the Japanese language proficiency necessary to
participate in lectures, seminars and tutorials at Ochanomizu University.
Those who choose the status "(b) Exchange Research Student" are not required to be
proficient in Japanese if they have the English language proficiency necessary to
conduct their research with approval from their academic advisor.
・Exchange students should return and graduate from thier home university after their
period of study/research at Ochanomizu University.

E. Scholarship
Japanese
Based on a credit unit system
Academic achievements are evaluated by  S, A, B, C or D grades although S is
applicable only to the Undegraduate Courses not to the Graduate Courses.
Grade of “S” is given for excellent performance, “A” for good performance, “B” is for
above average, “C” for average and  “D” for failure.
https://www.ocha.ac.jp/en/education/index.html
The Japanese language classes are optional.
There are two classes, “Seminar: Japanese” and “Seminar: Japanese Culture”, each of
which corresponds to 2 credits and is offered at 5 different levels from beginning to
advance.
There are also Tokusetsu Nihongo (literally translated as “Special Japanese”) classes
ranging from "beginner level" to "advanced" level, such as “Conversation”, “Writing”,
“Chinese Character”, “Grammar”,“Reading”, and “Japanese Language Proficiency
Test”. These classes are not regular courses. Students who wish to tranefer credits
should contact their own universities in advance.
Students will be advised to take classes based on the result of a placement test required
to take after application.

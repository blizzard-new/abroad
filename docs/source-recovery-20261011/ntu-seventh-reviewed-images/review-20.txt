Guideline for how to search for rental properties and how to live there
Important notes
At the time of making an agreement and of moving in
(1) Someone as guarantor is required.  In case you cannot find any appropriate persons as guarantor in Japan, the official guarantee companies will be your guarantor.
(2) Figure out how much you are supposed to pay for deposit, key money, agentʼs commission, rent, common service charges, equipment and so on.
(3) Check the condition of the property carefully at the time of moving in. You should let the landlord know if you are aware of any stains or malfunction of the facilities.
(4) Visit to greet your neighbors within a couple of days after move-in and let them know that you are the new neighbors.  It is important for you to communicate well with your neighbors and landlord.
(5) Contact the utility companies such as electricity, water, landline phone (if necessary) and so on to start their services.
(6) Keep the contact details of your landlord and your guarantor.  You should know when, where and how to dispose garbage.
(7) Read the lease agreement carefully (whether or not you are allowed to keep any pets, to put nails in a wall and so on).
At the time of moving out from the property
(1) Let your landlord know one or two months earlier if you will terminate the lease contract.
(2) Contact electricity, gas and water companies to terminate their services. Submit both YNU and the ward oﬃce the address change notice.
(3) Clean up the property as it used to be. Leave none of your belongings in the property.
(4) Return the keys to the landlords.
(5) Deposit may be refunded by your landlord. Settle default of rent or unpaid bills if any.
Basic rules to follow during the contract period
(1) Only the persons under the lease agreement are allowed to live in the property.
(2) Do not bother your neighbors by making noise.
(3) Let your landlord know when you are away for a while for holiday or for home leave.
(4) Pay the rent before the due date of each month.
(5) Dispose garbage in the requested date and manner. (See appendix for details.)
(6) Pay utility bills before the due date of each month. The services might be terminated in case of delay of payment.

Advice for searching for rental properties
Advice from the senior international students
1. When you visit the local real estate agencies, you had better wear neat clothes for a good impression.
2. If you feel your Japanese is insufficient, it is better for you to take someone with adequate Japanese proficiency with you.
3. You should tell your requirements clearly to the real estate agencies, such as:
・Rent, the area you want to live in, type (public or private apartment, a house), the nearest station
・Floor plan (single room, 1K, 1DK, 1LDK), washing room/bathroom, airconditioning/heater
・Can the property be shared with someone else? Are joint guarantors required? When is the property available to move in?
4. Have your requirements in detail. Try to get as much information as possible from websites or magazines in advance about average rent or the area you want to live in.

Junior Year Overseas at Yokohama National University (JOY) Program
In October 1997, the International Student Center started the JOY Program for students from partner universities outside Japan to give them the opportunity to experience Japanese university life for up to a year. The outline of JOY is as follows:
JOY Program Overview
Objective
To assist international students coming to Yokohama National University under student-exchange agreements by providing them with the opportunity to acquire a good understanding of Japan and its role in the international community.
Eligibility
Students must be enrolled full-time in an
undergraduate degree program at a university that has signed a mutual student exchange agreement with YNU.
Students must be proficient in English (
TOEFL iBT 80(Pre-January 2026 scoring system)/ 4.0(Post-January 2026 scoring system), IELTS 6.0 or higher*
) or Japanese (
JLPT N2 or higher
).
*English Certificates other than official TOEFL and IELTS scores are accepted with a letter from the home university certifying the English proficiency equivalent/higher to TOEFL iBT 4.0, IELTS 6.0 or CEFR B2 level.
Educational Program
Courses are offered in varieties of disciplines covering humanities, social sciences, and engineering in English specifically for JOY Program students. They may also attend other courses offered by the faculties, provided they have adequate language skills and approval from the lecturers in charge.
Scholarships
JOY students are eligible to apply for the Japan Student Services Association (JASSO) scholarship. The application form is included in the JOY Program application packet. The JASSO scholarship consists of JPY 80,000/month stipends.
Given the limited number of JASSO scholarships and the increasing number of JOY applicants, this scholarship is increasingly competitive. If available, students should pursue scholarship opportunities at their home universities.However,
JASSO rules stipulate that students cannot simultaneously receive the JASSO scholarship and any other study abroad scholarship exceeding JPY 80,000/month (the sum of other scholarships excluding travel support).
Nomination
Students need to be first officially nominated by their home university.
We will inform partner universities of the details for the nomination procedures and deadlines.
Application
After receiving the nomination, we will send the application link directly to the student.
Admission
Students will be selected and admitted on the basis of the quality of their application documents, and on the student exchange balance between their home universities and YNU.

On Arrival Campus Tour of 2026 Fall Exchange Program
On-arrival Campus Tour of Exchange Program for the 2026 Fall was held on October 1st (Thu).
Forty exchange students from thirty universities in eighteen countries/regions started the 2026 Fall Exchange Program at Yokohama National University.
During the On Arrival Campus Tour, the exchange students were divided into small groups and walked around the campus together with students of Yokohama National University and continuing exchange students from the 2026 Spring.
Through the On Arrival Campus Tour, they prepared for their new life at Yokohama National University.
Congratulations on completing the 2026 Spring JOY Program!
Forty-five exchange students from thirty universities in seventeen countries/regions completed the 2026 Spring JOY Program and go back to their home universities after this semester.
On August 5th (Wed), the last day of the program, each of JOY Program students gave a one-minute speech at the International Student Center to share their memories and experiences during the exchange.
It seemed that they still do not want to say goodbye to Yokohama National University and many of the valuable encounters they had in Japan.
JOY program student introduced his country's culture
Mr. Heinrich of the JOY program student introduced German culture to YNU students and faculty staff.
He introduced about transportation in Germany, German food and drinks and party games with a a lot of pictures and gestures.
He also shared his university life, including classes and the cafeteria.
About 30 participants enjoyed learning about the differences between German and Japanese culture and university life.

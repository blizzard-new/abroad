EXCHANGE PROGRAMS FOR PARTNER UNIVERSITIES 
STUDENT EXCHANGE PROGRAM JAPANESE LANGUAGE AND CULTURE PROGRAM (JLCP) 
Program Outline 
One semester/year program where the students apply for our 
Faculty/School relevant to their study/research fields at their home 
universities. Our undergraduate schools offer lectures (credit-able) 
and our graduate school offers research guidance (non-credit). Our 
graduate schools do not provide lectures (credited-courses) for the 
exchange students. Applicants are only able to apply for the same 
academic level as in their home universities: ex., graduate level 
applicants must apply to graduate level at NU. 
One semester/year program for the students at our partner 
universities who wish to learn Japanese language and culture. 
Participants will be classified by the level of their Japanese 
language proficiency which is determined by the result of the 
placement test. Participants of this program are expected to 
take 7 credit hours per semester . 
Qualification 
-Must be enrolled as a full-time student at one of our partner
universities and remain enrolled throughout the mobility period.
(until Mach 31 or until September 30)
-Must meet the requirements of the Faculty/School of which
applicant applies to. 
-Must be enrolled as a full-time student at one of our partner
universities and remain enrolled throughout the mobility period.
-Must have passed N3 level of Japanese Language Proficiency Test or
equivalent.
-Must be a non-Japanese national

PRACTICAL INFORMATION 
Language Program 
Non-credit Japanese language program is available for participants of the Student Exchange Program. The level is varied 
from elementary to advance. The result of a placement test determines the language level. 
In the event that the number of applicants exceeds the course capacity, we will be using a lottery system to determine 
the enrolled students, which may result in some students being unable to register for their desired course.  
Tutorship System Tutors will be allocated to the Student Exchange Program students in order to make their life at NU easy and comfortable. 
Accommodation Exchange students have priority to reside in the university dormitories. 
http://www.liaison.nagasaki-u.ac.jp/en/?page_id=194 
Visa 
Exchange students must obtain a student visa. Please apply for a student visa at the nearest Embassy/Consulate of Japan 
where you live. For visa a pplication, you need to have the Certificate of Eligibility, which NU will send to the partner 
university. The 
Health Insurance 
All international student who stays in Japan for more than three months must enroll in the National Health Insurance System. 
This system covers 70% of the medical expenses in Japan a nd is covered by the government. Exchange students can receive 
assistance with the application after their arrival in Nagasaki. National Health Insurance coverage begins from the registration 
date as a resident in Nagasaki City. Therefore, it is strongly recommended to obtain overseas travel insurance that covers the 
first few days after arrival in Nagasaki, starting from the departure date.

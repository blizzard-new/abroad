1. Brief Outline of the Program 
JLCP provides an opportunity to learn Japanese language and culture at Office for 
Global Affairs , Nagasaki University . The objectives of this program are to improve 
participants’ Japanese language skills, enrich their knowledge of Japanese culture, and  
deepen mutual understanding between international students and Japanese students. 
Based upon the Memorandum of Understanding on Student Exchange and Academic 
Cooperation Agreement between Nagasaki University and partner universities, JLCP is 
classified into two courses depending on tuition paying type. There are “Non-Fee-Paying 
Course” and “Fee-Paying Course”, and “Fee-Paying Course” will be applied when the 
number of exchange students exceeds the number written in the Memorandum. 
 
 
2. Eligibility 
Applicants of JLCP must be regular students who are currently enrolled at the partner 
universities of Nagasaki University. They must also satisfy the following requirements: 
1) Applicants’ major or minor field of study must be Japanese language and culture. 
If not, their main purpose of the study abroad must be learning Japanese language 
and Japanese culture. 
2) Applicants must be recommended by the partner university of Nagasaki University. 
3) Applicants must stay enrolled at their home university during their study abroad 
period.  
4) Applicants must have passed N3 or higher level of Japanese-Language Proficiency 
Test (JLPT), or equivalent test score to JLPT N3 level. 
5) Applicants must have achieved academic excellence in their home university. 
6) As a general rule, applicants must have non-Japanese nationality.

2) General Education Courses Available for Exchange Students 
These courses are available only for those undergraduate students whose Japanese 
language level is classified as “Advanced JapaneseⅠ” or “Advanced JapaneseⅡ.” The 
English mediated general education courses are opened for undergraduate students 
regardless of the placement test results.  Graduate students cannot take any general 
education courses.  
Students may choose and attend the general education courses from “Course Lists of 
General Education” posted on the website below. Qualified students must consult with 
their supervisor and follow the registration instructions provided by the supervisor.

(6) Students with Disabilities or Special Needs 
Students with disabilities or special needs who would like to request reasonable 
accommodations or academic adjustments should consult with the Student 
Exchange Support Division  before applying to the JLCP. When contacting the 
Student Exchange Support Division , please submit the information listed below, 
along with documentation from a medical doctor or other qualified and appropriate 
professional that lists the student ’s diagnosis (or diagnoses) and describes the 
area(s) in which the student experiences difficulty. 
 
Note: Under no circumstances will a student experience discrimination or be 
disadvantaged in the application/admission process as a result of consulting with 
the Student Exchange Support Division  regarding a disability or special nee ds. In 
addition, please note that it may not be possible to provide reasonable 
accommodations or academic adjustments if the student does not consult with the 
Student Exchange Support Division prior to applying. 
 
[Information to be submitted by the student] 
a. Full name 
b. Name of home university 
c. Student’s e-mail address 
d. Student’s phone number 
e. Type of disability or special needs 
f. Desired reasonable accommodations or academic adjustments 
g. Reasonable accommodations or academic adjustments provided at home university 
h. Aspects of the student’s daily life that are affected by the disability or special needs 
 
If necessary,  the Student Exchange Support Division  may request an online or 
telephone interview with the student and/or their home university representative, 
etc.

7. Grade 
Grades of subject provided by Office for Global Affairs  will be determined on the 
performance including quizzes, exams, reports, attendance, and so on.  On the academic 
record sheet, each grade (AA to D: 5 grade evaluation) and the score (perfect score: 100) 
will be printed with signature by Office for Global Affairs. As a general rule, an academic 
record will be issued as follows: 
For the participants who accomplish this program at the end of Fall Semester 
: After the end of March 
For the participants who accomplish this program at the end of Spring Semester 
: After the end of September 
Grades of general education courses will be issued around the same time in a different 
format. 
 
 
8. Certificate of Completion and the Issuance Time 
A completion certificate of JLCP will be issued for those who have completed taking 
the required credits (7 credits a semester, 14 credits annually)  of subjects provided by  
Office for Global Affairs (credits earned by taking general education courses shall not be 
included). A certificate of completion will be issued as follows: 
For the participants who accomplish this program in the Fall Semester 
: After the end of March 
For the participants who accomplish this program in the Spring Semester 
: After the end of September 
 
 
9. Tuition Fee for “Fee-Paying Course” Students 
Fee-Paying Course students from partner universities of Nagasaki University are 
required to pay 14,800 yen per credit. Please note that there is a possibility of change in 
tuition fee.

10. Accommodations 
Exchange students have priority to stay in the university dormitory. In case there is 
no vacant room in the dormitory, Nagasaki University shall help  to find private 
accommodations. 
 
 
11. Scholarship 
Nagasaki University will apply to  JASSO (the Japan Student Services Organization) 
Student Exchange Support Program  for JLCP. Only when it is selected by JASSO, the 
scholarships will be provided to the selected JLCP participants*. The potential scholarship 
recipients will be selected based on their academic records at their home university and 
other conditions, and will be nominated to JASSO. Requirements of this scholarship is 
available in JASSO’s website: 
http://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/tantosha/study_j/short_term/index.html 
 
* JLCP was not selected for the year of 2026-2027.

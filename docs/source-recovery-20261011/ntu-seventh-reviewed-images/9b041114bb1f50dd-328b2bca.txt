National Health Insurance Service (NHIS)
All international students in Korea are automatically registered for the
National Health Insurance Service (NHIS)
once they apply for their residence card (formerly ARC). NHIS is Korea’s national healthcare system that helps cover a wide range of medical services, treatments, and selected prescriptions (For more information, please refer to the
NHIS website
).
While highly recommended, NHIS coverage is
not mandatory
for all international students.
Students staying less than six months (one semester) may apply for an exemption.
Students staying
over six months
are
required to enroll
and pay the monthly premium.
[For stays of 1–4 months]
Students staying in Korea for less than four months
may apply for an exemption
under the following conditions:
You must have valid
private insurance
purchased outside Korea with
unlimited or KRW 1 billion+ coverage
.
The insurance must cover emergency care and hospitalization abroad at a level comparable to NHIS.
Students from France and Japan are eligible for exemption from NHIS under provisions between governments and insurance policies.
Please note:
Exemption applications are
often denied
if they do not meet NHIS criteria.
Individuals may still be required to pay NHIS fees even after applying for exemption.
Read the official NHIS guide carefully before applying.
How to Apply for Exemption
Complete the following forms:
Application for Exclusion from National Health Insurance for Foreigners
Health Insurance Regional Subscribers’ Declaration of Qualification Loss
Translate key sections of your private insurance policy (coverage details) into
Korean
.
Submit the documents at your
local NHIS branch or foreign resident center
.

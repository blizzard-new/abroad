Application Procedure
01
Online Nomination
Applicants must be nominated by their home/sending institutions prior to the nomination deadline.
Nominations received after the deadline may not be accepted.
A new nomination link will be sent to partner institutions every semester when applications open. If your institution has not received the new link, please have them email the KGU Incoming Team (
) as soon as possible.
02
Gathering all Application Documents
A complete application may contain up to 14 documents. Please access the below website to find details about the application documents and their format.
2027 Spring Semester Intake - Application Website
All documents must be written in English or Japanese. If the document is written in another language, it must be accompanied by an English or Japanese translation.
Hand written forms (except the "Certificate of Health") are NOT accepted.
Use the designated format when specified.
03
Submission of Required Documents
Before submitting the soft copies to your home university's international office so they can send your full application to us, please make sure you followed all the directions.
All files available must be submitted in PDF format except the digital ID photo that can be submitted as a JPG file.
If your professor followed the dedicated procedure, document number 05 has already been uploaded to our platform and
does not need to be submitted again
. You will receive a confirmation email once the submission is completed.
Please label your files 'Application Number - Document Number Form Name.pdf', eg. 'S27-999 - 01 Application Form.pdf'
Once you have checked all your application documents,
send them to your home university's exchange coordinator for review
and submission ahead of the deadline. Your home university's exchange coordinator
has received a submission link
to use for this purpose.
⚠️Application Deadline: November 15

Regarding Accommodations
All nominated students are eligible to apply for KGU-arranged accommodation. While we cannot at this time guarantee that they will receive a housing placement, we assure you that the KGU Residence Center will make every effort to accommodate as many students as possible.
The KGU Residence Center will be in touch by early November with details about the Accommodation Application/Request process but in the meantime, you can find a quick overview of the accommodation options we offer on the page below.
About Accommodations
Any student who would rather arrange for their own accommodation or who would not be successful in obtaining a KGU-arranged accommodation as a result of the housing application process can review the information available on the above page for guidance on how to find a suitable self-arranged accommodation in our area and a few recommendations.

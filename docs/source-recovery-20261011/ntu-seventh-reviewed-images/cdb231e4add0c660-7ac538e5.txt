Documents
All documnets must be prepared in English or Korean in designated format.
Standard ID Photo
[JPG format] 3 × 4 cm
Passport Copy
[JPG or PDF format]
Color scan of the bio page (showing name and passport number, as well as machine-readable zone)
Official Academic Transcript (with cumulative GPA)
[PDF format]
If the home university does not use a GPA system, an official letter from the international office confirming that the applicant meets Ewha’s eligibility criteria must be submitted.
Student Agreement
[PDF format] downloadable from the online application page
Must be signed by both the applicant and the home university advisor

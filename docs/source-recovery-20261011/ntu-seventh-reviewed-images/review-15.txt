Kindai Overview
Kindai University was founded in 1925. Now one of Japan’s largest universities, it has six campuses in western Japan and boasts research facilities across the nation - from Hokkaido in the north to Kagoshima in the south. Currently, Kindai University comprises 16 faculties with 50 departments, 14 graduate schools and 1 transdisciplinary graduate school, 18 research facilities, two junior colleges, 18 associated primary and secondary schools, and two teaching hospitals. The university has over 30,000 students and more than 580,000 alumni.

Exchange Program FAQ
When and where can I find the application information?
Information regarding nominations and applications will be sent directly to
your home university's exchange program coordinator.
Spring Program: Around August
Fall Program: Around January
​
To know more about how our program works, please check the link below.
【Kindai Exchange Program Flow】
When will application results be confirmed?
Spring Program: Around December
Fall Program: Around May
​
Can I only take courses related to my major?
Kindai does not restrict the fields of study for course selection. Program A students can take courses from List A across multiple disciplines. Program B students can take courses from List B in the same way. Additionally, due to visa regulations, you must take at least seven courses per semester.
​
How many ECTS credits is one credit at Kindai University equivalent to?
1 credit at Kindai is considered equivalent to 2 ECTS credits. However, the method of credit transfer calculation may vary depending on the partner institution, so please make sure to confirm with your home university.
About our grading system, please visit
here
.
​
Where can I find the housing information?
At Kindai University, we guarantee 100% placement in off-campus housing.
Details about the accommodation will be provided only to accepted applicants, and students are required to stay in the designated housing.
​
Are there any scholarships that Kindai offers to exchange students?
Unfortunately, we do not offer any scholarships for exchange students. We are also unable to support any scholarship applications.
​
Will Kindai University accept a free mover (fee paying) student?
No, we do not accept free mover (fee paying) students.

①Nomination
Students who wish to participate in the Kindai University Exchange Program must be nominated by your institution's coordinator.
Information regarding the nomination process will be sent via email to the partner institution coordinators in August for the spring semester intake and in January for the fall semester intake.
*We are unable to respond to individual inquiries from students before the nomination submission. Please direct any questions through your institution’s coordinator.
近畿大学交換留学プログラムに参加したい学生をあなたの担当者から推薦してもらいます。
Nomination方法等のお知らせは春学期受入は8月、秋学期受入は1月に協定校担当者宛にメールでお知らせします。
※Nominationの提出以前の学生からの個人的な問い合わせは回答しかねます。協定校担当者を通して質問をしてください。
【Required Information　必要情報】
・Student's full name　学生名
・Student's academic performance　学生の成績
・Student's language proficiency　学生の語学能力
・Participation program and period　参加希望プログラムと参加期間

Nomination締切後、協定校担当者から推薦された学生対し、Application方法の案内を個別にお知らせします。
Application締切後の提出は一切受け付けませんので、予め下記の情報や書類を準備しておいてください。

③Pre-arrival
Students who pass the application process will be instructed to complete the following procedures.
*All students are required to reside in the university-designated accommodation.

なお、本学が指定する宿舎に必ず居住をするようお願いします。

④Visa Application
Students must apply for a visa at the nearest Japanese consulate to enter Japan.
The Certificate of Eligibility (COE), which is required for the visa application, will be sent by Kindai University.
Once you have obtained your visa, you will be ready to enter Japan.
*The permitted entry date for Japan will be announced during the Pre-arrival process.
日本に入国するためのビザ申請を近くの日本領事館で行ってもらいます。
ビザ申請に必ず必要になるCOE(Certificate of Eligibility)は本学よりお送りします。
ビザを取得することができたら、いよいよ日本への入国が可能になります。
※日本への入国可能日についてはPre-arrival時にお知らせします。

During the course registration period, students will select the subjects they wish to take at Kindai University.
At Kindai University, students can attend classes before finalizing their course selection.

近畿大学では授業に出席しながら受講する授業を決めることができます。

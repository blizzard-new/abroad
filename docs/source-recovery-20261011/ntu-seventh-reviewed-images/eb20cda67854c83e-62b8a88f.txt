COURSE INFORMATION
Courses are offered in English in Business Admi-
nistration, Economics, International Affairs, Social 
Sciences and Law. The definite course programme 
is available approximately one month before the 
semester starts. Guest students are strongly recom-
mended to attend courses that correspond to their 
level of study at their home university.
Bachelor
Bachelor students attend bachelor courses and 
may only attend master’s courses if necessary and if 
prerequisites are fully met. Master’s core courses are 
not open for guest undergraduate students.
Master
Master students attend master courses and may 
attend Bachelor courses if necessary.

REQUIRED WORKLOAD

credits. Guest students are required to take at least 
16 ECTS credits and may not exceed 40 ECTS cre-

Presentations/course participation may also constitu-
te part of the final grade. The examination format is

HSG Course Catalogue
EXAMINATION PERIODS 
At the HSG there are two different types of exami-
nation periods:
• Decentral and MidTerm examinations take place
within or at the latest at the end of the lecture
period.
• Central examinations take place after the end of
the lecture period.
Guest students are entitled to take all examinations 
within the lecture period (= same time frame as 
decentral examination period).  
The examination type of each course is indicated in 
the course information sheet.

TRANSPORTATION
In Switzerland, travelling by public transport is very 
convenient and encouraged. Students are advised 
to obtain the Swiss Federal Railway half-fare card 
to save 50% on all tickets for trains, buses, trams, 
ferries. It is available from CHF 120 
(under 25 years old) and valid for one year.

LANGUAGE OF INSTRUCTION
G
erman and English (course availability will vary

LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS
German
For courses taught in German, no formal German 
certificate is required, but guest students are expec-
ted to have a language level equivalent to B2 (CEFR). 
English 
All programmes (except ISP): For courses taught in 
English, no formal English certificate is required, but 
guest students are expected to have a language level 
equivalent to B2 (CEFR). 
ISP: For the ISP, non-native English speakers must 
provide a valid TOEFL score of 89 (iBT) or IELTS 
score of 6.5. This requirement is waived for students 
enrolled in an English-taught MBA at their home uni-
versity.
LANGUAGE COURSE
A variety of languages is available — such as Chinese, 
English, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, 
and Russian — and these courses are open to guest 
students.
Incoming exchange students are welcome to enrol in 
regular language courses offered by the University of 
St.Gallen during the semester.

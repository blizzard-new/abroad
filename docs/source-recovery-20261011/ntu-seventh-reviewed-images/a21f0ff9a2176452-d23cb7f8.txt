3. Admission Requirements 
Minimum GPA 3.0 out of 4.0 
Language requirement For English*: Minimum TOEFL iBT score of 79, IELTS score of 6.5 or equivalent is required. 
For Japanese**: Minimum JLPT score with grade N1 or equivalent is required. 
Available exchange period  One semester or One year

6. Other 
Courses for KUINEP 
The Institute for Liberal Arts and Sciences (ILAS) offers wide variety of courses in English 
(approx. 350+) for both Japanese and international students, which cover 
Humanities/Social/Natural Sciences and are designed for mainly 1st and 2nd year students. 
Japanese Language Classes 
Variety of courses (absolute beginner/intermediate/advanced) to learn and improve 
Japanese proficiency are available for international students. The maximum number to be 
registered is 4 courses (8 credits) for KUINEP and GEA students. GESR students can take only 
non-credit classes. 
Advising Lounge 'KI-ZU-NA' Advisors offer consultation services to international students.

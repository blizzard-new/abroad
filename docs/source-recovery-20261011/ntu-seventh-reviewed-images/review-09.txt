Student Exchange Programme 
Semester Fall Semester – Early September to late December (including 
exam) 
Spring Semester – Jan to late May (including exam) 
Application Method HSUHK will only accept exchange students nominated by partner 
institutions. Applicants are required to complete a HSUHK 
Admission Form and submit relevant documents to HSUHK for 
review via partner institutions. 
Nomination & 
Application Deadline 
Fall Semester – Early to mid-April 
Spring Semester – Mid-October to late October 
Language 
Requirements 
Applicants are required to complete at least one term of study in 
home institution and attain a minimum cumulative Grade Point 
Average of 2.5 (on a 4-point scale) or equivalent. 
For those who are not native English speakers, they are required 
to possess one of the following qualifications: 
■ a minimum score of 6 in IELTS; or 
■ a minimum score of 550 (paper-based)/213 (computer-based)/ 
79 (internet-based) in TOEFL 
Proof of language capacity may be satisfied if the student has 
completed substantial university-level course work in English. 
Useful Information 
Course Registration Inbound exchange students are required to complete 12 credit - 
units (or 4 courses) min. to 18 credit-units (or 6 courses) max. No 
specific course restrictions apply but students are advised to check 
prerequisite(s) of individual courses. Exchange students can select 
courses from different programmes as long as the timetable 
allows. 
HSUHK would provide students the course list in July for Fall 
Semester and in December for Spring Semester. The up-to-date 
course list can be found at: https://gao.hsu.edu.hk/en/inbound- 
exchange/academic-studies/

Accommodation Places in Residential College on campus are reserved for inbound 
exchange students who will be invited to submit applications in 
June for Fall Semester and November for Spring Semester. 
Estimated Living 
Cost 
Accommodation: ranges from HK$1,900 to HK$2,300 per month, 
depending on the choice of the room. 
Meals: around HK$4,000 per month 
Personal Expenses: around HK$2,000 per month 
Actual expenses depend on your personal spending habit 
Health Insurance Exchange students are required to purchase insurance and submit 
proof of insurance coverage prior to arrival. The insurance must 
cover travel, personal accidents, medical expenses (accident and 
non-accident), hospitalization and emergency evacuation/ 
repatriation. 
Buddy Programme A Buddy will be assigned to each inbound student and pick-up 
upon arrival will be arranged by the respective Buddy. 
Orientation All inbound exchange students are required to attend an 
Orientation which usually takes place a week prior to the 
commencement of the semester.

Eligibility
Thank you for your interest in studying at HSUHK. We only accept nominations and applications from our partner institutions. Please contact the international office at your home institution for more information.
Applicants are required to complete at least one term of study in the home institution at the time of application and attain a minimum cumulative Grade Point Average of 2.5 (on a 4-point scale) or equivalent. For those who are not native English speakers, they are required to possess one of the following qualifications:
minimum score of 550 (paper-based)/ 213 (computer-based)/ 79 (internet-based) in TOEFL; or
minimum score of 6 in IELTS
Proof of language capacity may be satisfied if the student has completed substantial university-level course work in English.
Exchange students are required to complete 12 credit-units (or 4 courses) min. to 18 credit-units (or 6 courses) max. No specific course restriction applies but students are advised to check pre-requisite(s) of individual subjects.
How to Apply
HSUHK only accepts exchange students who have been officially nominated by its partner institutions. Nominated students are required to complete the online application form and submit all relevant documents to HSUHK for consideration.

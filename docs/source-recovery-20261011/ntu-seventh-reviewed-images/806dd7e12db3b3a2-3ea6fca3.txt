02. Pre-Application Preparation 
(0) Changes from the 2026 Fall Semester 
⚫ Language Requirement 
Beginning with the 2026 Fall Semester, the language requirements have been revised. Please refer to the document for details: 
https://en.ritsumei.ac.jp/skp/apply/pdf/LanguageRequirementsENG.pdf 
 
⚫ Scholarships 
Please note that the application form still contains information about the RUSSES Scholarship. However, the RUSSES 
Scholarship Program will end at the conclusion of the Fall 2026 semester and will not be offered from the Spring 2027 
semester onward. Students may apply for the JASSO Scholarship; however, it is available only if the program is selected for 
funding by the Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO). Please note that the JASSO Scholarship is not guaranteed and 
may not be awarded depending on the academic year, semester, funding availability, and selection results. 
 
⚫ Changes to the COE (Certificate of Eligibility) Application Process 
Applicants must submit a separate COE (Certificate of Eligibility) Application Form in addition to their program application. 
Starting from Spring 2027, COE applications will be processed through an administrative scrivener corporation appointed by 
the university. Applicants are required to submit the necessary information through a web form that will be provided separately 
by the administrative scrivener corporation at a later date. For details, please refer to " 05. Certificate of Eligibility (COE) 
Application Submission" 
 
⚫ Establishment and Collection of the SKP Student Support Fee (Applicable to All SKP Students) 
Starting from Spring 2027, all SKP students, including both exchange and self-sponsored students, will be required to pay the 
SKP Student Support Fee (JPY 50,000). The fee will be used to cover various services and support provided to SKP students, 
including the following: 
・ Medical assistance and emergency support services required for SKP students 
・ Enrollment in university -designated insurance plans, including Gakkensai (Personal Accident Insurance for Students 
Pursuing Education and Research) and Gakkenbai (Liability Insurance Coupled with Gakkensai) 
・ Application and administrative fees related to immigration procedures, including the Certificate of Eligibility (COE) 
・ SKP programs, activities, and events 
・ Other expenses deemed necessary by the university to support SKP students 
 
* All students are required to make a full, one -time payment of the SKP Student Support Fee  within two weeks from the 
announcement of acceptance.  Payment must be made through Convera, the university's designated international payment 
service. In case of withdrawal, the refund will only be given if the applicant notifies the SKP Staff of their decision by the 
withdrawal deadline (March 31, 2027) set before the start of the semester . Please note that any refund will be issued after 
deducting the bank transfer fees incurred by the university. 
* Costs for government or local government programs (e.g., Japan’s National Health Insurance), as well as insurance plans or 
services chosen by the student (e.g., University Co-op), are not included. Students are responsible for enrolling in and paying 
for these separately after arriving in Japan. Further details will be provided upon admission and during orientation.

<IMPORTANT NOTES> 
⚫ SKP students can select courses only from the course lists for each track and campus distributed at the 
beginning of the course registration period. Students cannot choose from the full list of courses offered to regular 
students. Research guidance is not provided to SKP students. 
 
⚫ Undergraduate students can only choose courses from the SKP course list designated for undergraduates. Graduate 
students can register for courses from both undergraduate and graduate SKP course lists.  
 
⚫ International students are required by Japanese law to attend a minimum of 10 hours of classes per week. As each class 
period lasts for 95 minutes at Ritsumeikan University, students must take at least 7 class sessions per week. You can 
register up to a maximum of 20 credits per semester, including Japanese classes. 
 
⚫ Some courses have an enrollment limit. If oversubscribed, a lottery will be held. For such, we cannot guarantee a seat 
for particular courses and all your desired courses. 
 
⚫ SKP students can generally choose courses offered at their home campus. If space is available, students may apply for 
courses at other campuses. However, since the priority is given to students from home campus, enrollment is not 
guaranteed. Additionally, travel between campuses takes approximately 1 hour and 30 minutes, so course registrations 
that do not allow sufficient travel time will not be approved. Students should carefully review the course lists from 
previous semesters and choose their campus carefully. 
 
⚫ IJL track Japanese Language Courses require significant self-study. IJL students willing to take courses offered in English 
on top of the pre -fixed IJL Japanese Language Courses are advised that they have a flexible study plan. IJL students 
may register for up to two (2) English courses.  However, since RCE students have priority, IJL students may not be 
able to register for their preferred courses in English. 
 
⚫ For questions or concerns, please first refer to 【SKP】LINK for Application Guidelines FAQ 
 
⚫ At Ritsumeikan University, each class session is 95 minutes long, and each semester consists of 14 weeks (14 class 
meetings). For more details, please refer to the following page: 
Changes in Class Hours from AY2025 ｜Ritsumeikan University 
 
⚫ Courses offered by the College of Design and Art, opening in April 2026, will not be available to SKP students for the 
time being.

03. Scholarships 
 
SKP applicants may apply for the JASSO Scholarship. However, the JASSO Scholarship is available only if the program is 
selected for funding by the Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO). Please note that although information about 
the RUSSES Scholarship is still included in the application form, the RUSSES Scholarship Program will end at the 
conclusion of the Fall 2026 semester and will not be offered from the Spring 2027 semester onward. 
Please note that the JASSO Scholarship is not guaranteed. Depending on the academic year, semester, availability of 
funding, and selection results, no scholarship may be awarded. 
 
 JASSO Scholarship 
Outline The Japan Student Services Organization (JASSO) provides this scholarship to exchange students 
through Ritsumeikan University, and applicants are screened by Ritsumeikan University. 
Monthly 
Stipend 80,000 yen 
Period One semester (4 months) or two semesters (10 months) 
 The funding will start from April for Spring Semester/ from October for Fall Semester. 
Eligibility 
Exchange students from partner universities  that will reside in Japan under a student visa while 
participating in SKP 
Students who stay enrolled at Ritsumeikan University and participate in the SKP until the Program End 
can apply. Therefore, students who have the possibility of leaving the program and departing the country 
midway or departing before the university's designated final scholarship disbursement date (Spring 
Semester: around July 27, Fall Semester: around January 20) cannot apply.  
Application 
Process 
Complete all required sections of the “Scholarship Application” in the SKP Application Form. 
*Students can only apply for scholarships at the time of program application. 
Ineligible 
Students 
a) Students who are receiving any other scholarship(s) that total over 80,000 yen/month* during 
their study period in Japan 
b) Students with a GPA below 2.3 on JASSO’s 3.0 scale 
c) Students with Japanese citizenship, including those with dual citizenship 
d) Students with a Japanese visa other than the student visa 
e) Students who cannot submit an official transcript with all required information 
 
* Students who receive other scholarships totaling more than JPY 80,000 per month are required to 
decline the JASSO Scholarship.

⚫ Please provide accurate and truthful information regarding any scholarships you are expected 
to receive or have applied for, as well as any financial support you receive from family or others.

04. SKP Application Submission 
 
Important Notes 
⚫ All application procedures must be completed online. 
⚫ Applications containing plagiarized, third-party, or AI-generated content will be invalid. 
⚫ Please fill in all the required information and upload complete and accurate documents. Incomplete documents or 
applications will not be accepted. 
⚫ After submission, the application details (e.g., Study Track, Campus, Participation Period, etc.) CANNOT be 
changed.  
⚫ Please submit your application ON TIME. Late submissions will not be accepted. 
⚫ You can save your application at any time and resume it later. 
 
Document Guidelines 
⚫ Please upload all the application documents as digital files in either PDF or JPG format.  
⚫ All documents must be written either in Japanese or English. If a document is written in another language, 
please attach a Japanese or English translation of the required sections. You may translate it yourself. 
Documents without a Japanese or English translations will not be accepted. 
 
Login Info 
⚫ The application link and student’s ID password will be sent to the email address registered by your home university’s 
exchange coordinator. They should be sent within a week after the nomination submission. Please confirm with your 
coordinator when your nomination has been completed. 
⚫ The ID and password for the application system will be sent by email from skp@ritsumeikan.sakura.ne.jp. 
Please check your email security settings in advance to ensure that you receive messages from this address and be 
sure to check your spam/junk folder as well. 
⚫ If you have any questions, please first consult with your home university’s exchange coordinator. If further assistance 
is needed, please have them contact SKP Staff directly. 
⚫ Please keep a record of the email address used to log in to the application form, as it will be used for future 
communications and various administrative procedures throughout the program.

05. Certificate of Eligibility (COE) Application Submission 
 
Student Visa Applicants 
Applicants who do not hold Japanese nationality and do not possess a valid Residence Card must apply for a Certificate of 
Eligibility (COE) to obtain a student visa. COE applications will be processed through an administrative scrivener 
corporation appointed by the university. Detailed instructions will be provided separately by the administrative scrivener 
corporation at a later date. Please submit the required information through the designated web form. 
 
Japanese Nationals 
Japanese nationals are not required to apply for a COE. However, after the admission results are announced, you will be 
required to submit a family register extract (koseki shohon) and an ID photo for the issuance of your student ID card. Please 
prepare these documents in advance. All Japanese nationals, including dual nationals, must enter Japan using a 
Japanese passport. Failure to do so may affect immigration procedures and participation in the program. 
 
Residence Card Holders 
Applicants who currently hold a valid Residence Card should submit a copy of the front side of their Residence Card, with 
all four corners clearly visible, through the application form. Whether a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) application is 
required will be determined after we review your current residence status and related information, and we will contact you 
separately.

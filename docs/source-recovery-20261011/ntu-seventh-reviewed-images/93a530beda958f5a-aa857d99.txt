Student Nominations 
Exchange Student Nominations are two times per year. Partner institutions 
must nominate exchange students to the Office of International Student 
Services by the deadline.  
SEMESTER OF STUDY NOMINATIONS DEADLINE 
Academic Year or Fall (only) March 15th 
Calendar Year or Spring (only) September 15th 
 To attend OU as an exchange student, applicants must have completed 
their first year and be in good academic standing. Home university 
coordinators must use OU’s Nomination Form  to nominate students each 
term. After nomination, students receive a confirmation email, followed by 
the application link and instructions.  
Please view our informational brochures about the Exchange Student 
Program online!  
The Incoming Exchange Student Website is the best source of current 
information. 
Application Requirements   
After nominations close, the Exchange Student Coordinator 
will email each nominated student an application link with 
instructions. Students must check their email regularly during 
this process. 
Applicants must submit the following online (no hard copies 
required)  
1) Biographical page of passport  
2) Evidence of English proficiency  
3) Financial support documentation  
4) University transcripts (must be translated into English) and 
current course enrollment  
5) Course preferences (requested later) 
 
 
  
English Proficiency Requirements  
Applicants whose home university does not use English as the primary 
language of instruction must submit valid English proficiency scores with their 
application—OU cannot admit students without this documentation. 
Accepted tests: TOEFL (paper or iBT), IELTS, PTE, or Duolingo (DET). 
TOEFL ITP is not accepted. 
Minimum scores must be met for admission. This requirement may be waived 
if the student’s university teaches all courses in English.

Course Enrollment Process  
• ISS partners with academic departments and 
advisors to enroll exchange students. 
• Enrollment isn’t guaranteed pre-arrival; be prepared 
to adjust preferences. 
• All exchange students must take a full-time load  
(12 undergrad, 9 graduate) * – 15 OU credits, most 
courses are 3 credits). 
• Maximum allowed is 15 credits. 
Academic Expectations  
• Attend all classes and strive to meet course 
requirements. 
• Failure to participate or make satisfactory progress 
may jeopardize your stay at OU. 
 
 
Arrival, Departure & Orientation 
• Arrival: Monday or Tuesday the week before classes start 
• Depart: Saturday after final exams 
• Students can move into campus housing upon arrival; details 
will be emailed. 
• Limited airport pickup is available before classes begin; 
students must arrange their own transport at semester’s end. 
• Students may enter the U.S. up to 30 days before the 
program start and stay up to 30 days after it ends. 
• Orientation (NISO) 
 
All exchange students must complete the four-part New 
International Student Orientation: 
o Online Pre-Departure Orientation (immigration, 
health, academics) 
o Airport Transport (if available) 
o ISS Arrival document submission 
o NISO Connection in-person orientation on campus  
 
Housing 
Considerations for Off-Campus Housing 
✓ Is a short-term lease available for the duration of 
your study period? 
✓ Is a deposit required, and if so, how much? 
✓ Is the apartment furnished? 
✓ Which utilities are included in the rent, and which 
are not? 
✓ What are the distance and transportation options 
(walking, shuttle, public transit) to campus? 
 
Estimated Living Expenses (per semester): 
✓ Food: $400–600/month (meal plans available) 
✓ Books: $250–800 
✓ Health Insurance: $1,132 (fall) / $898 (spring) 
✓ Personal Expenses: $400–800+/month 
✓ Other Fees: Sooner Card, program/orientation fees, 
transcript fees 
All costs are subject to change.

English Language Requirements
If you are an international applicant to the Exchange, Hoakipa, or Independent visiting student program and your first language is not English, you may need to provide evidence of your English proficiency. Students who do not meet minimum requirements may be required to enroll in English Language Institute (ELI) courses alongside regular university courses.

Undergraduate and Postgraduates: English Language Institute (ELI) Placement Test & Course Requirement
If your English test score is below the level required to waive the
ELI Placement Test
—and you do not qualify for an exemption—you must take the ELI Placement test before the semester starts. This is
mandatory
.
The test includes:
2 listening sections
2 reading sections
1 writing section
Depending on your results, you may be placed into
1 to 3 ELI classes
(intermediate or advanced). These classes will be taken
with your other university courses
.
If you have questions, please contact the ELI Office team or
check their website
.

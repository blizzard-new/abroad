Class format is based on lectures, readings, class 
participation and self-study. Students are expected 
to attend all lectures, to complete all course-related 
assignments and readings, and to sit exams in order to 
receive a course grade.
• The academic semester has 16 weeks of class;  
however, some courses are taught in an 8-week 
period.
• 1 credit equals 48 hours of course work (in and 
out of class). It is important to remember that the 
ratio of classroom work vs individual work is 1 to 3 
for master students and 1 to 2 for undergraduate 
students.
Students interested in enrolling in any of the School of 
Management master’s programs can only apply if their 
home institution has an agreement with one of the 
School of Management master’s programs at Uniandes. 
For further information, please contact the Centro de 
Servicios Académicos at csa.adm@uniandes.edu.co.
On the following link you will find our historic catalog of 
courses. Please keep in mind that the official catalog for 
the Exchange semester will be published 1 or 2 weeks 
before the course registration. http://bit.ly/41auwM2
Students are responsible for choosing the courses 
according to the necessary requirements. If the student 
does not meet the criteria needed, they should not ask 
to be enrolled in the class.
It is important to remember that the University applies 
the “first come, first served” rule. If you do not register on 
time, it will be difficult to find places on the courses of 
your choice.  https://bit.ly/3L7rDn6
Students must register for a minimum of 3 credits 
and a maximum of 20 credits. They should adhere to 
the minimum credit requirements set by their home 
university.
Each credit corresponds to 48 hours of coursework, 
including both in-class and out-of-class work. It’s 
important to note that the ratio of classroom work to 
individual work is 1:3 for master’s students and 1:2 for 
undergraduate students.

Visa Information 
For students whose nationality does not require a
tourist visa:
The immigration officer at the port of entry should 
stamp the student’s passport with the PID (Student 
Entry Permit) upon presentation of the admission letter 
(for stays up to 180 days).
• This permit is valid for 90 days and must be 
renewed at Migración Colombia for an additional 90 
days.
• For stays longer than 180 days, a student visa is 
required.
For students whose nationality requires a tourist visa:
• Regardless of the length of stay, a visa needs to 
be obtained prior to arriving in Colombia. Further 
information: https://bit.ly/2AsD6ug
• When arriving on a visa, students are required to 
obtain an ID card (Cédula de Extranjería) within 15 
days of arrival in Colombia. This procedure costs 
approximately $90 USD. All information about this 
process can be found https://bit.ly/3wAxsq1
Please remember to check the entry requirements 
for Colombia in advance. You can verify them with 
the Colombian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, “Migración 
Colombia” or with the airline you will be using to travel 
to Colombia. 
All students are responsible for obtaining their 
respective immigration documents. To be enrolled and 
registered at Uniandes, students must have either the 
PID stamp, a visa/Cédula de Extranjería, or a Cédula 
Colombiana (for Colombian students).

International Exchange / Visiting Students
Eligibility
Full time male and female undergraduate/graduate students in an accredited university or equivalent
Students who have completed at least one semester at home university, with
cumulative GPA of 2.5/4.0(75/100)
or above
Students with language ability to take courses in either English or Korean
Transcripts:
- An official transcript must be ready at the stage of application.
- Graduate students must provide official transcript of graduate-level studies.
Nomination
Exchange students are nominated by Ewha’s partner institution with exchange agreements. Nomination must be received prior to the student's application.
Visiting Program
Students who are not nominated by their home university, including those from non-partner institutions, may apply through the Individual Visiting Program. All eligibility and academic requirements are the same, except that this program requires a different visa type and involves tuition fees.
For details on the visiting program fees, please refer to "Fee" section.

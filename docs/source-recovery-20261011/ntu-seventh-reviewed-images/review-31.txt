Semester Exchange Programmes
Students from partner institutions are welcome to study as exchange students at UM. As an Incoming Exchange student, you can study at UM with tuition-waived for one or two semesters and the credits you earn here may be transferred back to your home institution.
To apply for the incoming student exchange programme, you must be nominated by one of our partners which have a formal student exchange agreement with UM. Please contact your exchange officer at your home institution for more information.
Students coming on exchange via a faculty level exchange agreement are restricted to enroll in the courses provided by specific faculty that has the agreement with your university. Students coming on exchange to UM via a university level agreement are able to choose courses from across the university, provided that you meet the pre-requisites and there are quotas availability. Students should consult your academic advisor at your home institution or contact your International Office for credit transfer and academic consultation.
Nomination
We only accept nominations from our exchange partners. Individual applications will not be accepted.  Students interested in applying for exchange to UM should approach your exchange officer at your university for more information. A set of the following nomination documents should be submitted to the Global Affairs Office / Host Office at UM via your International Office/ Department Office:
Academic Transcript in English which shows the accumulative GPA of the nominees;
Programmes & Year of Study the nominees are applying
Course Selection Form
CV
Personal Statement
Recommendation Letter from home university
The research project for Postgraduate Students if they would like to take research-based programmes in UM
Application
Accepted nominee will receive an online application link for course pre-enrollment and documents upload. Students who do not meet the online application deadline cannot do the course pre-enrollment, course enrollment can only be done upon arrival at relative faculties.
Please prepare the below documents to be uploaded onto the application system.
1 Colored 1.5 Inch White Background Photo
Copy of ID Card & 港澳通行証 (For Mainland Exchange Students only)
Copy of Passport
Latest Academic Transcript in English (issued by Home University)
Copy of Medical Insurance which covers the period of stay in Macao (if you would not like to join our University Medical Scheme, not applicable to Mainland Exchange Students)
Registration upon arrival
A set of the below documents should be submitted upon your arrival:
Designated Medical Examination Form sent by UM Registry
Remarks:
The Medical check-up has to be done within three months before coming to Macao (except Mainland China Exchange Students)
The Medical check-up done at home country must be signed by home doctor for validation.
English Report/ Film of the X-Ray Test on Lungs
Copy of Tetanus Vaccination Record
3 Colored 1.5 Inch Passport-sized Photos (with white background) for student visa application, UM registration and accommodation check-in
More information can be found from the below website:
https://sds.sao.um.edu.mo/whole-person-nurturing/international-exchange-students/
Visiting Student Researcher Programmes
The Visiting Student Researcher Programme provides opportunities for postgraduate students to conduct research at UM. Students who participate in the Programme will be attached to a Faculty/ Institute and engaged in research activities under the supervision of UM’s academic member(s), with a minimum duration of 3 months and a maximum of 12 months.
To apply for the Visiting Student Researcher Programme, you must be nominated by one of our partners which have a formal visiting student researcher agreement with UM.
Students coming via a faculty level visiting student researcher agreement are limited to conducting research under the supervision of a specific faculty/institute that hosts the agreement. Students coming to UM via a university level agreement may conduct research at one of UM’s faculty/ institute. The student’s research direction should match that of the proposed host supervisor and must meet the academic and/or research requirements of the proposed host supervisor. Students should consult your supervisor or relevant advisor at your home institution for your research plan at UM.
Eligibility
Postgraduate students (including Master and PhD students) who meet the following requirements are eligible to apply for the Visiting Student Researcher Programme.
complete at least two semesters of study of their current degree programme at home university before the commencement of the research attachments;
fulfill the minimum language requirement set by UM;
specific requirements of applied faculty/ institute (if any).
How to Submit your Application?
The application will be opened twice a year.
Application Period
Visiting Period
March to April
For research attachments that commence within July to December of the current year
September to October
For research attachments that commence within January to June of the subsequent year
We only accept nomination from our partners, individual applications will not be accepted. Students interested in applying for visiting researcher programme to UM should approach your officer/ advisor at your home university for more information.
A set of the following nomination documents should be submitted to the Global Affairs Office / Host Office at UM via your International Office/ Department Office:
Application form
ID Copy (both sides of the ID card must be copied on the same page of an A4-size paper)
Passport Copy (applicable to foreign applicants only)
Latest academic transcript
Official Testimonial
Curriculum Vitae (including academic qualifications, research activities, publications, awards, etc.)
Research plan
Language proficiency certificate, if any
Visiting researchers can apply the Postgraduate House (on-campus accommodation) during their stay at UM, details please visit
https://srs.sao.um.edu.mo/pgh-mangement/
.
IAESTE Internship Exchange Programme
The International Association for the Exchange of Students for Technical Experience (IAESTE A.s.b.l.) is an independent, non-profit and non-political student exchange organisation, as well as the largest organisation of its kind in the world. IAESTE provides paid, course-related internships for students in the science and engineering disciplines, and high quality trainees for long or short term projects for employers. Currently, there are over 80 countries and regions involved and over 4000 internships exchanged per year globally.
IAESTE activities in Macao are administered by Global Affairs Office of University of Macau and jointly managed by Faculty of Engineering (FEG), Faculty Information Science and Computing (FIC), Faculty of Medicine (FMD), Faculty of Science (FSC) and Institute of Chinese Medical Sciences (ICMS). The IAESTE Macao team looks forward to hosting you in this small and vibrant city, one of the most important if not the first hub where the East meets West. Here, you will experience a combination of our rich traditional culture and exciting modern lifestyle.
Learn more about about IAESTE A.s.b.l.:
https://iaeste.org

Application
IAESTE Macao will only consider nominations sent by the official IAESTE offices through the official IAESTE Exchange Platform (EP). If you wish to apply for IAESTE Internship in Macao, please consult the IAESTE National or Local Committees in your country or region.
List of documents that we require for nomination on EP:
Student Nominated Form (SN Form)
C.V. / Resume
Motivation letter addressed to the employer
Official Certificate of Enrolment
Official Transcript
Recommendation letter
(optional)
IAESTE English language certificate (if English is not the language of instruction in your university)
Other supporting documents or certificates related to your application
(optional)
Passport Copy
Photo
The documents should be combined into one file according to the order above (in .pdf format and should not exceed 2MB) and upload to the IAESTE Exchange Platform (EP). Please consult your IAESTE Committee for document templates and more information.

Erasmus+
Erasmus+ is a mobility programme commissioned by the European Commission to facilitate mobility and exchange of students and professionals in the education sector within and beyond Europe. UM as one of the participating institutions in Macao SAR, we have sent and received students, professors and administrators to/from higher education institutions in Europe for academic endeavours and exchange of experience to promote mutual understanding and professional growth.

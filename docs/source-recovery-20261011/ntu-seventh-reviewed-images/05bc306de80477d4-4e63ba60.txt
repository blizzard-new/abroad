Application Procedure
The CJP application is a three (3) step procedure.
Step 1: Online Nomination to be completed by the home university/sending institution
Step 2: Online Application and Other Application Document to be filled out/gathered by the nominated student
Step 3: Online Submission of all Application Documents by the home university/sending institution
Once nominated, the applicant should:
Gather all the necessary application forms.
Save a copy of all documents to a hard drive.
Send all documents to their exchange coordinator so they can upload them to the dedicated platform by the Application Closing Date.

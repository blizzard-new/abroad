How to Apply: Undergraduate Students
Find out how to apply to the Exchange, Hoakipa, and Independent Visiting Student programs at the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa, and the deadlines for submitting your application.
Nomination & Application Deadlines
Fall Semester (August – December)
Nomination Period: December 1 – February 1
Application Period: December 1 – March 1
Spring Semester (January – May)
Nomination Period: July 1 – September 1
Application Period: July 1 – October 1
Applying as an Exchange or Hoakipa Visiting Student
Our Exchange program and Hoakipa Visiting Student program are open to students who are currently enrolled at one of our 90+ partner universities around the world. If your home university does not have a formal
Exchange or Hoakipa agreement
with the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa, please scroll down to learn about applying as an Independent Visiting Student. Please note that if you already have an undergraduate degree you must follow the application instructions for
Postgraduate Students
instead, even if your current program is also at the undergraduate level. Students with bachelor’s degrees are considered postgraduate applicants by UH Mānoa.
Before you apply:
NOMINATION
Applicants for the Exchange or Hoakipa Visiting Student programs must first be nominated by one of our
exchange partner institutions
before they can apply to the MIX Program. To begin the process, students should contact their home university’s exchange or international office. Home university representatives may request access to the nomination form by contacting us at
inbound@hawaii.edu
.
ELIGIBILITY
In order to be eligible for the undergraduate Exchange or Hoakipa Visiting Student program, applicants:
Must be currently enrolled at one of our partner universities
Must be nominated as an Exchange or Hoakipa visiting student by your home university exchange office
Must meet our
English language requirements
Have a cumulative GPA equivalent to at least 2.5 on a 4.0 scale
Cannot hold any previous bachelor degree (students pursuing additional bachelor degrees may enquire about applying as a Postgraduate Applicant instead)

Once your application is complete:
ADMISSION AND NEXT STEPS
After you have completed the application process, please allow some time for your applications and materials to be reviewed. Upon admission, you will receive a series of emails from
inbound@hawaii.edu
, the inbox for our Mānoa International Exchange (MIX) Inbound team:
Acceptance Email
– this is considered your official acceptance letter and includes your assigned UH Student ID Number. A PDF version is available upon request.
Welcome Guide
–
includes information on your next pre-arrival steps, housing, course registration, and program expectations.
Form I-20 Delivery & Instructions
– If you need a U.S. student visa to study at UH Mānoa, you will receive the Form I-20 via email within 5-7 business days after your Acceptance Email. You will use the Form I-20 for the F-1 student visa status. For more detailed information on the visa application process, please see the
Visas
page on the ISS website.
Note: If you are a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, or have confirmed attendance through another visa type, you will not be issued an I-20.
You will continue to receive emails from
inbound@hawaii.edu
throughout your MIX Program.
If you have any questions, or do not receive any emails as intended (make sure to check your spam folders as well), please contact us at
inbound@hawaii.edu
.
Applying as an Independent Visiting Student
If your home university does not have a formal
Exchange or Hoakipa agreement
with the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa, you can apply for our Independent Visiting Student program. Students can apply for this program independently or with the assistance of their home university. Postgraduate Students
instead, even if your current program is also at the undergraduate level. Students with bachelor’s degrees are considered postgraduate applicants by UH Mānoa.

English language requirements

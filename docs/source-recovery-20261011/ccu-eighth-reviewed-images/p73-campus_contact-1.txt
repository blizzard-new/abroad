Infrastructure that Inspires Excellence
In order to achieve its ambition of being a world-class education provider, Chitkara University is committed to significant enhancement of its facilities and buildings, essential for world-class research and all-round development of students. The University has an extremely modern and state‑of‑the‑art infrastructure that goes a long way in facilitating a number of services for the students as well as the staff members.
Academic Blocks
The academic blocks bring together state‑of‑the‑art classrooms, department specific laboratories, dedicated libraries, studios, and amphitheatres to create a comprehensive learning environment. Classrooms are equipped with modern audio-visual systems and digital teaching technologies that support engaging lectures, presentations, and discussions. Complemented by specialised facilities and subject-focused learning spaces, these blocks encourage exploration, collaboration, and deeper academic engagement. Together, they support both theoretical understanding and hands-on learning.
02
Central Library
The libraries across the campuses serve as vibrant knowledge hubs, offering spacious reading halls, periodical sections, group discussion rooms, and dedicated zones for accessing digital databases. They house an extensive collection of resources across multiple formats, including books, journals, multimedia, and global research databases. Skilled library professionals support students and faculty in navigating these resources, enabling them to conduct effective research, discovery, and academic inquiry.

Research Laboratories
High-tech laboratories having equipments with calibrations to ISO 9001-2000 Certifications state‑of‑the‑art workshops and numerous work stations with licensed software play an important as part of your learning experience. Laboratories are fully equipped to meet the academic requirements as per the university curriculum. To name a few: MATLAB and Simulation & Computational labs in Electrical Engineering, Microprocessor & Micro Controller Lab with Keil software, Microelectronics Lab with Cadence software, VLSI with xilinx software, CADAENCE Lab, Embedded System Lab, DAEC Lab, Communication and Texas Instruments Lab and MVI Lab equipped with LabVIEW software in Electronics and Communication Engineering and CAD/CAM and Aauto Synch lab in Mechanical Engineering are have for carrying out different experiments. A Project Lab with ultra-modern facilities has also been established.
04
Residential Facilities
At Chitkara University, you will be among students from across the country living in residences that feel like a home away from home. Separate facilities for boys and girls, caring wardens, and strong security ensure a comfortable stay, allowing students to focus on academics. Internet access and Subscriber Trunk Dialling (STD) keep students connected with family and friends, while common rooms with TVs, indoor games, and other amenities offer space to relax and unwind.

Cafeteria & Food Court
Whether it’s breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a late-night snack, the University Food Court offers something to satisfy every craving. The dining hall features a wide variety of cuisines. 07
Auditoriums & Seminar Halls
The spacious, amphitheatre-style classrooms in the University provide the most conducive atmosphere for dynamic and focused discussions. They have been designed to bring together analysis with action and are augmented with integrated audio visual teaching aids for lectures, presentation, etc. Special sessions and classes are also held on business etiquettes, negotiation skills, effective communication, leadership, teamwork and analytical skills.
Contemporary auditoriums and seminar halls serve as dynamic venues for intellectual exchange and academic engagement. Equipped with advanced acoustics and modern presentation technologies, these spaces host lectures, conferences, expert talks, and student forums, enabling dialogue, collaboration, and the vibrant exchange of ideas across the University community.

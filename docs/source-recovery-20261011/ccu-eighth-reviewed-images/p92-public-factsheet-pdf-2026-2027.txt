European Credit Transfer System (ECTS)
San Jorge University uses a local grading 
scale from 0 to 10. 
A grade of 10 represents the highest 
score, while 5 is the minimum passing 
grade required to pass an exam and 
obtain credits. Any grade below 5 is 
considered a fail.
Course Descriptions and Language of 
Instruction
Academic offer of San Jorge University: 
USJ website 
Courses offered in English: USJ website
Language requirements
The main language of instruction is 
Spanish; therefore, we strongly advise 
students to enroll in the intensive Spanish 
course for exchange students upon their 
arrival. 
The recommended language level is B1 in 
English and/or Spanish. 
All students enrolled in any Health 
Sciences degree who will participate in 
clinical practice: mandatory B2 level in 
Spanish. 
Students of the Bachelor’s in Translation 
and Intercultural Communication: 
mandatory B2 level in Spanish and/or 
English.
Accommodation
We provide assistance in finding 
accommodation for incoming students 
on campus. For more information, please 
visit: USJ website
Transport
Students can use the San Jorge 
University Bus Service, which costs 
approximately €300 / semester. 
For more information, please visit: USJ 
website
Sports Activities

participate in various sports competitions, 
events, and categories in our stateof-the-
art sports facilities through the Sports 
Service: website
Spanish Courses for Exchange Students
Free courses are offered throughout 
the semester by the Institute of Modern 
Languages, which evaluates students’ 
Spanish levels in order to adapt the classes 
according to each student’s knowledge.

SDU Skema E-mail: By pressing the “send” button, you order access to the online application form of the University of Southern Denmark. Within a few moments, you receive an e-mail with a username and password. Please be aware that it may take a few minutes before you receive the e-mail, so please do not reorder access more than once. If you have not received the e-mail within an hour from the first try, it may be our mail system that is overloaded, or the e-mail has been pickup by your spam filter (please check unwanted e-mails). If the e-mail has not been picked up by your spam filter and you have not received it within one hour, please try to order access once again. If you are still have not received an e-mail from us, please contact our Helpdesk ( servicedesk@sdu.dk ).

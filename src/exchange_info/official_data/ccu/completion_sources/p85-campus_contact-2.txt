All university buildings have disabled access, and the team at the Disability Services, or Pôle Handicap , receives disabled students and supports them throughout their studies. Healthcare and social welfare professionals are also ready to help, both at the university and on the campus in the city centre. Bordeaux Montaigne University has about 50 associations working on a wide range of missions, and some are specifically dedicated to welcoming foreign students. Wherever you may be on campus, the Aquipass card will be your key and can be used to borrow items from the university libraries , to pay for your meals in university restaurants, and so on.

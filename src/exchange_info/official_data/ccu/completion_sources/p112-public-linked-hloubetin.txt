It is a modern, closed complex of several buildings. The advantage of the area is the proximity of the metro station B - Hloubětín and thus excellent accessibility to the center of Prague. Accommodation for CZU students and employees is available in a completely new separate building. It offers a total of 125 beds in single or double rooms on six floors. Room types and price All rooms are of the studio type and therefore have their own bathroom and a kitchenette with a refrigerator. The rooms are fully equipped and provide enough storage space as well as comfortable facilities for studying. From 1 st January, 2026 One-bed studios 14,000 CZK / month 14,700 CZK / month Double studios 10,000 CZK / person / month 10,500 CZK / person / month * Prices are valid for long-term accommodation (min. 1 month) and include VAT. In addition to the stated prices, guests are required to pay a refundable accommodation deposit in the amount of one month's rent. The deposit is refundable no later than 1 month after the proper check-out from the accommodation. Provision services 24-hour reception Continuous security of the object 2 food, newspaper and magazine stores 1 restaurant Possibility to use the laundry for a fee Internet cafe WiFi internet connection in all buildings for a fee Paid secure parking Photos Reservation If you are interested in booking a room in the accommodation capacity of Hostel Hloubětín, please fill in the following form: >>> ACCOMMODATION REQUEST AT HLOUBĚTÍN <<< On the basis of the completed form, you will receive a reply to the e-mail address indicated in the request within 5 working days to confirm or reject the reservation, depending on the current available capacity. If you do not receive a reply within 5 working days, please contact hloubetin@kam.czu.cz . To confirm the reservation, a reservation deposit will then be required within 10 days of the reservation date. The amount of the deposit depends on the room type chosen and always corresponds to one month's accommodation price. The deposit is not used to pay the rent and is refundable after the end of the accommodation. For more information on payment methods, click here - The reservation is confirmed ONLY after payment of the deposit and receipt of the email - Confirmation of accommodation reservation . Before the start of your accommodation, you are obliged to sign the electronic contract of accommodation in your ISKAM profile. If you need to obtain an accommodation confirmation for your visa application, please email hloubetin@kam.czu.cz and wait for instructions. The fee for issuing the confirmation is 200 CZK. The confirmation can only be issued after the booking has been confirmed. INCOMING students at Czech University of Life Sciences please contact your faculty coordinator for the accommodation confirmation. Payment conditions The most common method of payment is via the online payment system on the ISKAM profile. All payment methods are listed in the document below. DEPOSIT PAYMENT The deposit payment deadline is 10 days from the date of booking the accommodation. A payment prescription will be available in the ISKAM student profile. PAYMENT FOR ACCOMMODATION Payment for accommodation is due by the 15th day of each month in which the accommodation takes place (For example, payment for September should be made by the 15th of September). If you start the accommodation later than on the 15th day of the month, a payment slip will be available within 1-2 working days of entry and should be paid on the same day. The late payment fee is 15 CZK for each day the payment is late. ATTENTION Payments delayed by more than 14 days is grounds for termination of accommodation. Booking cancelation To cancel the reservation please contact us at hloubetin@kam.czu.cz . The cancellation is free of charge. The deposit is returned in full. More about deposit return at For early termination of accommodation, please inform us at hloubetin@kam.czu.cz at least ONE month before the planned departure. Timely notice is the most important step for a full refund of your security deposit. Check-in procedure The address of the dormitory and the reception for check-in is Slévačská 744/1, 190 00 Prague 9 . The check-in is possible from the 1st day of the reservation from 2 p.m.. Check-in date is specified under Accommodation section in your ISKAM profile. The documents needed for the check-in: ID card (only EU citizens) or passport with visa/residence permit. The accommodation voucher, which will be sent to your email address registered in ISKAM . Without those documents you will not be able to check-in. In case of any emergency during your check-in, you can contact us by phone. ATTENTION! The room number is the subject of change. The student will get the ACTUAL room number on his/her check-in at the reception. The correct room number will be show in your ISKAM profile within 1-2 working days. If you have asked in prior to be accommodated together with a specific roommate, this will be kept. Check-out procedure The check-out must be done before 10 a.m. on the end date of accommodation stated in your ISKAM profile. ATTENTION! Check-out at even 10:01 on the check-out date is counted as the next night towards accommodation and will be covered from the deposit. How to check-out: Clean up the room – all equipment and facilities must be handed over in the condition in which it was taken over. Hand over all keys at the reception before 10 a.m. Get the check-out card with student’s name and check-out date and time. The deposit is returned within a month after check-out. The return can be done to the card/cards you have used for online payments in ISKAM or direct bank tranfer. Contact the accommodation office at hloubetin@kam.czu.cz about the way you would like to recieve the deposit.

The international campus life at THA offers many opportunities to meet people from all over the world. Whether at game nights, on excursions, at events, or in German classes—join in, connect, and share experiences together. German Classes and Field Trips Even outside the classroom, learning German becomes a shared experience. Events and activities organized as part of the German courses bring together language, culture, and social interaction. Discover Augsburg and the surrounding region together: Field trips organized as part of the German language courses offer the opportunity to explore new places and make friends with other students. International Game Nights Playing games together, laughing, and meeting new people—international game nights are all about socializing in a relaxed atmosphere. SWOP Team The SWOP team helps international students settle in and organizes various activities that bring international and German students together. Buddy Program Advanced students support exchange students who come to THA for one or two semesters by helping them settle in at THA and in Augsburg. You can sign up as a buddy for incoming exchange students with the SWOP team . Our campus life is colorful Meet new people, discover other cultures, and share unforgettable moments together.

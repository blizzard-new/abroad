Qualifications Exchange students from CUC partner universities International students of CUC Check the application guideline announced by CUC for the application process. Boarding fee Boarding fee per month (yen) ¥38,000 per month Boarding fee includes utilities (gas, water, electricity) and internet communication. Floor plan The unit consists of two or three (in the case of a triple room) private rooms for individual use and a shared kitchen, dining room, bathroom, toilet, and balcony. As a general rule, two people will live in one unit. Facilities Private room Desk and chair Air conditioner Desk light Bed frame (no mattress or other bedding is provided) Shared facility Kitchen IH cooktop Microwave oven Refrigerator Dining table and chairs Tableware and utensils Washing machine Bathroom sink Toilet Bathroom Balcony Remarks The rooms are not equipped with supplies (towels, detergent, hair-dryer, etc.). Meals The International Dormitory has no cafeteria and no meal plan, so please prepare your own meals or use the cafeterias on campus. International Cultural Exchange Room The International Cultural Exchange Room can be used for exchange and event purposes. The room is available for use by international students, including exchange students in the International Dormitory, and CUC students who support international students.

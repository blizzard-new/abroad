Read more 29.9.2026 Science Night 2026 Revealed the Hidden Secrets of the Language of the Universe On Friday, 25 September 2026, the campus of the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague welcomed around 900 curious visitors of all generations. The central theme of this year’s Science Night was COMMUNICATION, and more than 40 stations across the University’s six faculties demonstrated that humans are far from the only ones exchanging information. Plants, animals, technologies and entire ecosystems also communicate in their own ways. Science is the language through which the universe speaks to us, and Science Night is an opportunity to try to understand it.

/WEB-INF/jsp/k2web/com/cop/site/layout.jsp main_eng_JW_MS_K2WT001_S Donam Soojung Campus is the main campus of Sungshin Women's University. It is located in Seoul, the capital city of South Korea, and houses major facilities such as the Central Library, Central Museum, International Conference Center, and Student Center. It is a 10-minute walk from Sungshin Women's University's entrance on Line 4, one of the main lines of the Seoul Subway, making it very accessible. The campus has 35 departments in 10 colleges. ① Ad minis tration Building : This building houses the administrative offices. There is a central museum on the 3rd floor. ② Nanhyang Building : This building houses general lecture halls, art school labs, and exhibition halls. ③ Fine Arts Building 1 : This building is used by the Oriental Painting, Western Painting, and Sculpture departments of the College of Fine Arts. There are labs, faculty research labs, and more. ④ Fine Arts Building 2 : This building is used by the Crafts and Design Departments of the College of Fine Arts. It contains art school laboratories, faculty research labs, etc. ⑤ Music Building : This building is used by the vocal, instrumental, and composition departments of the College of Music. It contains music labs, faculty research labs, etc. ⑥ S oojung Building : This building houses general classrooms and faculty research labs. On the first floor, there are amenities such as a cafe (Blue Pot), convenience store, optician, and copy room. The International Education Center is on the 3rd floor, and the cafeteria is on the 10th floor. ⑦ S cience Building : This building is scheduled for remodeling in 2024 (currently unused) ⑧ Prim e Building : General classrooms, faculty research labs, and offices of the Academic Senate. ⑨ Sung shin Buildin g : This is the main building of Soojung Campus and houses administrative offices, classrooms, faculty labs, and conference rooms. The first floor houses the main administrative offices. The second floor has a reading room. The fifth floor houses a café (Suharu) and amenities (Soojungmaru). ⑩ Gy mnasium : A building with an indoor gym and fitness center. ⑪ Central Library( Woonjung Building) : This is the central library building. ⑫ S tudent Center : This building is used by students for various student activities. ⑬ Do rmitory S u n g m i B u i l d i n g : A state-of-the-art student dormitory built in 2020. It is located close to Soojung Campus, a 3-minute walk from the main entrance of Soojung Campus. ⑭ Dormitory Sungmiryo Building : Newly renovated in 2023, this is a state-of-the-art student dormitory. It is located very close to Soojung Campus, a 1-minute walk from the main gate of Soojung Campus. ⑮ Global Education Hall : This building houses the International Education Center and the Lifelong Education Center.

International students from partner institutions are eligible for our exchange program and their tuition at KHU is waived under the bilateral agreements. KHU currently offers over 30% of courses in English in a variety of fields including Management, Hotel and Tourism Management. Visiting Student Program Visiting Student Program allows you to spend 1-2 semesters at Kyung Hee University. We eagerly provide you one of the best international study options in Korea. Eligibility Academic Requirements - Must be an currently enrolled undergraduate student at a four-year university - Must have an overall GPA of 2.5 out of 4.0 or above - Must have more than one semester remaining at home university after the completion of visiting program Language Proficiency - TOPIK Level 3 or above (Level 4 for College of Business Administration) - in case of taking courses conducted in Korean ** Required minimum will be raised to TOPIK Level 5 or above, starting from Fall 2025** - TOEFL iBT 82 / IELTS 6.5 or higher recommended- in case of taking courses conducted in English * If applying to study in his/her native language, a proof of language proficiency is not required Tuition and Fee Students are responsible for tuition and living expenses in Korea Tuition Fee: KRW 6,536,000 / Semester (Subject to change and may vary depending on the program) Procedure 1. Apply to the exchange student program at your university's International Office : We require email  nomination from your university. * For Visiting Students Program : Please email us to express your interest in participating in the visiting student program by (Spring semester) October 20 / (Fall semester) April 20 . We require a confirmation email from your home university that you are eligible to participate in the visiting program and transfer your credits back to your home university. 2. Submit all Required documents to Kyung Hee University via online (Some may require post mail submission) - Complete list of documents required will be announced later to participating students 3. Visa Issuance 4. Book Flight Ticket 5. Course Registration 6. Dormitory Check-in 7. Study at KHU with 2 exams(Midterm/Final) 8.

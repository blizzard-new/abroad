The Welcome Desk helps international students to find their way on campus and in Bordeaux, and provide advice on everyday matters (housing, insurance, banking, student jobs, etc.). The Welcome Desk is located in the "Accueil" Building, on the 2nd floor (ACC 209) and is open from Monday to Thursday (14:00 - 17:00) and on Friday morning (9:00 - 12:00).

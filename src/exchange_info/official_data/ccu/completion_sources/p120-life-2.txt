Guide to enrolling Courses Your course is the degree or award you are enrolled in, like a Bachelor of Business.  It's also identified by a code, such as BS05.  Courses are made up of units. Units Units are individual subjects you enrol in each semester. They're identifiable by a name and a code, such as BSB110 Accounting. Classes Each semester, you'll need to register for and attend classes for each of the units you have enrolled in.  You need to enrol in your units before you can register for classes.

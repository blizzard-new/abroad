School 2026 About EdUHK Summer School 2027 Academics Cultural Activities Tuition and Accommodation Eligibility and Application Contact Us Accommodation & Cost of living Accommodation EdUHK has four on-campus residential hostels, offering two types of living quarters. Exchange students are guaranteed a place in these hostels. Students can opt to stay in a twin or triple-share room in one of the student residential halls or a 9- / 12-person apartment in the Jockey Club Student Quarters. Wi-Fi services are available in all residential halls. Please refer to Student Affairs Office website for more details. Details of accommodation application will be provided via email. Hostel Room Type Cost per semester Facilities Northcote Hall Twin/Triple Bedroom HK$5,800 - 8,290 (Twin Bedroom) HK$4,060 – 5,790 (Triple Bedroom) Each floor has bathroom/toilets, pantries with refrigerators, laundry rooms，activity rooms, etc. Grantham Hall Robert Black Hall The Jockey Club Student Quarters 9-person or 12-person flat HK$5,800 - 8,290 Double/triple bedrooms in each flat, with sitting/dining areas, terrace, kitchen, laundry room, bathrooms and individual computer ports. Estimated Living Fees Accommodation^ HK$4,060 - 8,290 (US$530-1,070) / Semester Hall Life Education Fee^ HK$220 (US$28) Visa Application and Administration Fee HK$530 (US$67) Meals* HK$3,000 - 4,000 (US$385 - 513) / Month Local Transportation** HK$500- 800 (US$64 - 103) / Month Others Various activities are organised by GAO and SAO to facilitate integration into the campus life as well as understanding of the culture and society of Hong Kong among non-local students. Participation is voluntary, and fees would be charged on some of the activities. ^ Subject to annual review. * the above figures are for reference only; actual expenses might vary, depending on individual lifestyle. Caution Money All students are required to sign a credit card payment authorisation form for HK$500 (US$64) to cover any additional expenses incurred, such as library fines or hostel damage repairs. Charges will only be made if bills remain unpaid after the student has left EdUHK. Students will be notified of the amount by email before the transaction. If no unpaid bills, the information will be disposed within 3 months after completion of the exchange term. ::: Connect with Us!

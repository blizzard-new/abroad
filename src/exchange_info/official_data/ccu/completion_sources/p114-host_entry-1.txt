Time and place: Nov. 3, 2026 8:00 AM – Nov. 4, 2026 4:00 PM , Fredrikstad Campus Add to calendar Building on the success of RESET 2025, this year's workshop brings together leading researchers, industry experts, and practitioners from around the globe to explore intelligent energy systems and AI-driven solutions for sustainable energy transition. The event is organized by the iSHINEd Society in collaboration with our Faculty of Computer Science, Engineering and Economics. Key Highlights: • Distinguished international speakers from academia and industry • Free registration for all participants • Hybrid participation for global accessibility • Networking opportunities for joint research and industry collaboration • Publication opportunities in leading journals Featured Topics Include: • Green Hydrogen Production and Fuel Cell Technologies • AI for Industrial Energy Applications • Digital Twins and Data-Driven Energy Systems • Renewable Energy Integration and Smart Grids • Hydrogen Mobility and Clean Transportation • Energy Storage, Electrification, and Power-to-X This workshop represents a significant step forward in international collaboration, bringing together experts from multiple countries to accelerate the global energy transition through innovation and knowledge sharing. Who Should Attend: • Academic researchers and students (bachelor, master and phd level) • Industry professionals • Policymakers and energy sector stakeholders • Anyone interested in tomorrow’s energy solutions and hydrogen technologies Registration: Participant registration is open until October 15, 2026. For More Information: Visit: www.ishined.com/RESET2026 Contact: manikandan.palanichamy@hiof.no , rebecca.norvang@hiof.no Join us in shaping the future of sustainable energy through global collaboration and cutting-edge research.

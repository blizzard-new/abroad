2027/2028 Meet and Greet x UKM Talent Audition (MCU) Serpong Campus 2026/2027 Home Student Affairs Activities Meet and Greet x UKM Talent Audition (MCU) Serpong Campus 2026/2027 Published by Regina Caeli Cahaya Tarsisty on October 8, 2026 Categories Student Affairs Activities Tags Meet and Greet 2026 Universitas Bunda Mulia (UBM) Tangerang, September 11, 2026 – UKM Talent (MCU) Bunda Mulia University (UBM) Serpong Campus successfully held the Meet and Greet x UKM Talent MCU Audition activity on Friday, September 11, 2026. The Meet and Greet activity took place at 11:33-13:20 WIB in classrooms 1408-1409, UBM Tower Serpong Campus. This activity was held as a forum for introductions as well as strengthening the bonding between members and administrators of UKM Talent (MCU). The opening of the event was hosted by Marlon Surya Loandy , as the moderator in the talk show session, which was continued with singing the song Indonesia Raya and the Mars of Bunda Mulia University, as well as remarks from Miss Viola Geraldine, S.Psi., MIKom . as the mentor of UKM Talent (MCU) and Clara Chyntia Boen as the Chair of the Event. Presentation of unique souvenirs in the form of snack necklaces Source: CLICK Documentation (Hansen) The series of events continued with an interactive talk show session with alumni of the UKM Talent (MCU) management for the 2025/2026 period, namely Yehezkiel Samuel Marcellino (UKM Chair), Olivia Clarissa (UKM Vice Chair), and Chelsea Ariella ( Execution Team Coordinator). This session presented various insightful points regarding management experiences and an in-depth understanding of the UKM Talent Master of Ceremony at Bunda Mulia University. The enthusiasm of the participants was increasingly felt in the QnA session, when two active questioners asked questions that lightened the atmosphere. As a form of appreciation for their dedication and sharing of knowledge, the speakers received unique souvenirs in the form of snack necklaces. The atmosphere became even more warm when the event moved on to the introduction session of the ranks of the Daily Management Board (BPH) Gen-6 for the 2026/2027 period. The introduction included the ranks of the Chair, Vice Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Coordinator, to the Events, Equipment, Content, Design, Public Relations, Talent , and Merchandising divisions. The participants welcomed this session actively and interactively. Games Session Winners Source: CLICK Documentation (Hansen) To strengthen the participants’ familiarity and knowledge about the world of Master of Ceremonies, the event continued with an interactive games session. The lively competition resulted in three winners who received attractive prizes. The Meet and Greet x UKM Talent MCU Audition series concluded with a joint documentation session, the second attendance check, the announcement of the opening of new member auditions, and the official closing by the MC. Audition Session Source: UKM Talent MCU Documentation UKM Talent (MCU) Bunda Mulia University (UBM) Serpong Campus also successfully held the “ Voice Out Your Euphoria Beyond Phobia” Audition on Monday, September 14, 2026 to Friday, September 18, 2026. The activity took place at 11:30-18:00 WIB in the Library Discussion Room, UBM Tower Serpong Campus. This activity was held with the aim of identifying, mapping, and the basic abilities of the Master of Ceremony (MC) of prospective new members of UKM Talent MCU. Audition Session Source: UKM Talent MCU Documentation The “ Voice Out Your Euphoria Beyond Phobia” audition was attended by all prospective new members of UKM Talent (MCU) with a total of 72 audition participants. The audition activity which lasted for one week was filled with dynamics between the audition participants and the judges to create a warm and pleasant atmosphere, starting from the first day of the audition until the last day of the audition. The warm and pleasant atmosphere emerged from the interaction between the audition participants and the judges during the audition process, such as when giving evaluations and the running MC process. The audition activity ended with a thank you to the audition participants for their willingness to participate in the series of activities of the “ Voice Out Your Euphoria Beyond Phobia” Audition .

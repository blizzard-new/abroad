With an insertion rate of 89%, the FGES offers a preparatory course for entry into business schools, a year of preparation for scientific studies, state-recognized bachelor's and master's degrees in the fields of economics, management, banking and finance, science and sustainable development. These courses are strongly geared towards professionalization since students spend up to 3 years in a company during the 5 years of their training, as part of internships and/or work-study programs.

About FHNW Locations and Contact Locations and Contact FHNW Cam­pus Olten Contact Getting there Opening hours FHNW car parks Special opening hours Campus Security Further links Impressions Our schools Applied Psychology, Business, Education, Social Work, Optometry The FHNW Olten Campus is a versatile hub for study, work, and social interaction, offering a wide range of programs in education, continuing education, and research. The School of Applied Psychology, the School of Education, the School of Social Work, the School of Technology and Environment and the School of Business define the campus and create an interdisciplinary environment with modern infrastructure in a central location. Learning, recreational, and dining facilities, as well as cultural and sports opportunities, contribute to a vibrant campus atmosphere. Exit the main station at track 12 and follow the signs. Getting there by car Olten is located about 15 minutes from Exit 46 (direction Aarburg/Olten) of the A1 Motorway. However, as there is often heavy traffic on this route, the journey to Olten could take significantly longer. Several fee-based car parks are located on the campus.

Important before you apply The accommodation scholarship can be sent to a Czech bank account only . Revolut is not considered a Czech bank account. Necessary step before applying You must first open a Czech bank account . You may find these websites helpful when comparing banks: iStudentZone.cz Prague University of Economics reddit.com foreigners.cz Accommodation Scholarship Application in UIS Step 1 – Log into the UIS Step 2 – Go to the Student's Portal Step 3 – Open Bank accounts Step 4 – Add your Czech bank account Fill in the following: Purpose: for benefit pay-outs Bank's country: Czech Republic Bank name Currency: CZK Account number You may also set the validity of the bank account When finished, click Add . Step 5 – Go back to the Student's Portal and open the Accommodation benefit application form Step 6 – Activate the request Click on Activate the request . The UIS will confirm that your request has been accepted.

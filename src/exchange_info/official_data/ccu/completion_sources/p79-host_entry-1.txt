Access to conferences/ seminars/ webinars are provided to the public irrespective of gender, ethnicity, disability etc. 4th IEEE International Conference on Industrial Electronics: Developments & Applications (IEEE ICIDeA 2026) October 29 - October 31 Overview IEEE ICIDeA 2026 is the flagship international conference of the Bhubaneswar IE Chapter , organized in technical collaboration with the IEEE Bhubaneswar Subsection . This international conference will be conducted fully on hybrid mode 4th IEEE International Conference on Industrial Electronics: Developments & Applications (IEEE ICIDeA) , scheduled to take place from 29th October to 31st October 2026. The main aim of this international conference is to bring researchers from all the esteemed institutes of the World. Along with researchers the professionals and executives from energy sector, manufacturing industries, electrical power companies are invited to share ideas and information pertaining to innovations focuses on techniques handling today’s challenges. Prospective authors are invited to submit full papers describing original, previously unpublished, complete work (not currently under review by another conference/journal) up to six pages in IEEE double-column format including figure, results, and references. All accepted and presented papers of the conference by duly registered authors will be submitted to IEEE Xplore Digital Library for possible publication.

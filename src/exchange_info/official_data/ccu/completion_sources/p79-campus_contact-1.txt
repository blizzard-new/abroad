                                interest, aptitude and career goals. KIIT University is one of the early
                                adopters of the Choice Based Credit
                                System (CBCS) in the country, in line
                                with University Grants Commission
                                guidelines. CBCS provides students with the choice
                                to select courses — core, elective and
                                minor — based on their own interests,
                                aptitude and career goals. Along with a wide range of courses to
                                choose from, the system is intended to
                                ensure a holistic learning experience. 03 · Industry Connect Industry Interaction Connecting curriculum, academic

                                the overall knowledge ecology and
                                produce industry-ready professionals.
                                The University has inducted
                                representatives from industry into
                                Boards of Studies and introduced
                                industry electives.                                                 has institutionalised the
                                                Tutor-Mentor system since
                                                1997, the year of its
                                                establishment as an
                                                institution. In this system,
                                                the student is termed the
                                                mentee and the faculty member
                                                is the mentor. The guidelines for mentors
                                                identify the generic issues
                                                to be addressed by mentors
                                                and define the different
                                                categories and responsibilities
                                                within the mentoring system. 1.1 Background Effective and close
                                                teacher-learner interaction
                                                has proved to be a key factor
                                                in student learning and
                                                success. In recognition of
                                                this, KIIT institutionalised
                                                the Tutor-Mentor system from
                                                its early years. 1.1.1 Issues Addressed Academics Classes, attendance,
                                                        course progress,
                                                        subject difficulties,
                                                        registration and marks. Career Placement, higher
                                                        studies and
                                                        entrepreneurship. Emotion Family, friends,
                                                        important relationships,
                                                        misunderstandings,
                                                        money and recent
                                                        happenings. Discipline Altercations, fights,
                                                        threats, bunking,
                                                        fines and complaints. Grievances Problems faced during
                                                        or after classes that
                                                        require attention. Other Issues Any matter not covered
                                                        by the categories above. 1.1.2 Mentor Mentors take care of
                                                registration, academic
                                                information, interaction
                                                with guardians, attendance
                                                status, health conditions,
                                                general conduct and etiquette,
                                                and brand-awareness promotion. They are the first-tier
                                                contacts to receive concerns
                                                and initiate appropriate
                                                steps towards grievance
                                                redressal. The Mentor Counselling
                                                Committee enables senior
                                                mentors to groom mentors and
                                                counselling mentors through
                                                periodic sessions and
                                                workshops. 1.2 Mentors' Appointment At the beginning of the
                                                academic year, the
                                                Dean/Director of each School
                                                notifies mentors, counselling
                                                mentors and senior mentors
                                                from among faculty members. A faculty member may be
                                                    nominated as mentor for
                                                    a group of students. The group size for
                                                    undergraduate students
                                                    should preferably be
                                                    thirty. For female students,
                                                    the current guideline
                                                    states that a male faculty
                                                    member will not be
                                                    nominated as mentor. Counselling mentors are
                                                    nominated from faculty
                                                    with suitable rapport
                                                    building and rational
                                                    decision-making skills. The guideline provides
                                                    one counselling mentor
                                                    for a student group size
                                                    of 200. Senior mentors are
                                                    nominated from among
                                                    senior professors. 1.3 Mentors' Responsibilities Ensure a cooperative and
                                                    supportive environment
                                                    for mentees. Hold frequent and
                                                    regularly scheduled
                                                    meetings with mentees. Keep guardians informed
                                                    about academic progress,
                                                    attendance and disciplinary
                                                    issues where applicable. Keep students and
                                                    guardians informed of
                                                    organisational achievements. Help mentees understand
                                                    academic programmes and
                                                    regulations. Identify opportunities
                                                    for discipline-specific
                                                    and extracurricular
                                                    development. Provide direction to
                                                    strengthen disciplinary
                                                    and interdisciplinary
                                                    skills. Identify the need for
                                                    counselling and arrange
                                                    interaction with
                                                    counselling mentors. 1.4 Documentation Requirement Document Responsibility Timing Purpose Notification

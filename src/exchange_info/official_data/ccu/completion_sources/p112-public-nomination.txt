Nominations sent by e-mail , EWP , or any other way are not taken into account . The nomination must be submitted by the responsible person at the student's home university . Nomination Deadlines 15 May First (winter) semester and full academic year 15 October Second (summer) semester Nominations submitted after the deadline are not taken into account . Online Nomination Form Open Nomination Form mobility@czu.cz In case you need to discuss more details about the nomination process, please contact us at the e-mail address above.

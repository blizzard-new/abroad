Notice Notice more Interview Natalia Roza Krasniewska & Anna Izabela Krasniewska Hospitality and Tourism Management We always knew that life is about experiencing new things and following the dreams. In the time when we discovered South Korea we realized that it can be perfect place to do both. As a sisters we always enjoy doing things together. That’s why two of us study in English at the same major which is Hospitality and Tourism Management in Kyungsung University. Our plan is to open polish style café to introduce our culture to Korean people. Everyone knows that main reason for going to university is to get an academic qualification, however we also think that the social side of things and developing as a person is equally important. What we love about university life is that you are left to make your own decisions and live by yourself, especially in so unique country like South Korea. It’s great opportunity to meet people from all over the world and makes friendships that will last forever. Language create boundaries between people but during ours studies we could also learn Korean which changed the whole living abroad experience. We explored Busan city by ourselves, from trying new food to talking with native people.

Study Abroad Exchange/Visiting Pre-Arrival Guide HUFS Housing HUFS Dormitories Seoul Campus HUFS offers its students various housing options. Though International Houses are technically "off" campus, they are directly across the street from the front or rear gate of campus. There is little to no difference in the commute time to various buildings/classrooms around campus. These are: On-Campus Housing GlobeeDorm Managed by HUFS (All exchange students must contact OIAM for any Globee Dorm related inquiries and NOT the Globee Dorm office) Off-Campus Housing International House A International House C International House E International Houses are all directly m anaged by the OIAM ( Contact point: visiting@hufs.ac.kr ) GlobeeDorm is a typical dormitory while International House A / C / E are apartment units. Fees for each housing option vary but a general overview is provided below on each dorm page. Dormitory Assignments You will be notified about dormitory assignments about one month before the semester begins by email. We will not be able to tell you about room availability until current students move out at the end of the summer/winter session, and know how many students are returning for the regular semester.

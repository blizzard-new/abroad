Exchange Program : Students can enroll in up to six academic credit courses. * Open to students enrolled at partner universities of SeoulTech. * The Student Exchange Program at SeoulTech allows students from partner universities to pursue their studies for one or two semesters at SeoulTech. * Students who are officially nominated by their home universities will have the opportunity to apply for the Student Exchange Program at SeoulTech. * Tuition will be waived under the student exchange agreement. - Visiting Program : Students can enroll in up to six academic credit courses. * Open to students enrolled at both partner and non-partner universities of SeoulTech. * Participants will have the chance to take credit courses to pursue their studies for one or two semesters at SeoulTech. * Students must be officially nominated by their home universities. * Although students in the Visiting Program are granted the same status as exchange students, they are not eligible for tuition waivers. - Internship-Based Inbound Program : Students can enroll in one to two academic credit courses and secure an internship opportunity at a company. * Designed for students from overseas universities interested in studying at SeoulTech for one or two semesters, with the additional opportunity of completing an internship. * Open to students enrolled at both partner and non-partner universities of SeoulTech. * Participants will have the chance to take a basic Korean language course and partake in a cultural trip exploring Korean traditions. * Although students in the Internship-Based Inbound Program are granted the same status as exchange students, they are not eligible for tuition waivers. Eligibility Students who wish to study at SeoulTech must be: - Have completed at least two semesters at their home universities. - Be enrolled as full-time students at their universities. - Be nominated as an Inbound Program student by their home institution. - Be able to study either in English or in Korean for at least one semester (4.5 months). - Not have studied at other Korean universities in the immediate preceding semester . (This requirement is due to visa issues.)

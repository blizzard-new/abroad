Study at HU Research Health Care Center The Health Care Center provides medical checkups, health consultations, and primary care for non-life- threatening conditions such as lacerations, burns, common colds, stomach aches, or the flu to students and staff of the university. Staff members at the center include physicians, nurses, and counselors.

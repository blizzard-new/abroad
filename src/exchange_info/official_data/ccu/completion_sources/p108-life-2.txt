TBU Calendar Facebook Instagram Twitter Youtube Accommodation, Catering, Catering Services Accommodation We offer accommodation in the University Halls of Residence to students, employees and guests. The accommodation is provided at reasonable prices in high quality single, double and triple rooms, including beds for disabled guests. The Halls of Residence are located within a 10-minute walking distance from the town centre. We offer free Internet access in rooms; guests may also use Internet study rooms, TV rooms or laundry rooms/drying rooms. Catering We offer meals to students, employees, as well as to the general public in four modern catering facilities. Diners are not required to order meals in advance. Payments for the meals taken are made in cash or by luncheon vouchers; TBU students and employees may also pay by a chip card. Catering & Events We provide catering services for various social events, such as cold and hot buffets, banquets, wine tasting, cocktail parties, refreshments during final state examinations, conferences, business parties and meetings; we offer catering services also for weddings, birthday parties, etc. We will organize the event according to your wishes and we will make sure that the day of your event is unique and truly unforgettable. Our professional and discrete staff will always be at your disposal, ready to fulfil your needs, no matter how non-traditional or extraordinary they are. We offer services not only to TBU students and staff, but also to the public.

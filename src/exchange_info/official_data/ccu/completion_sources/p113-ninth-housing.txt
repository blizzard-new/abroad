Practical guide Accommodation Practical Guide Accommodation Contents BUT Accommodation Facilities Private Accommodation Waste Collection Fee Accommodation Scholarship BUT Accommodation BUT provides students' accommodation for both students and employees, which is provided by the BUT Halls of Residence. Room amenities and furnishings may vary. The dormitories offer double or triple rooms. In case of availability, you can pay extra fee for a separate room. Some rooms have their own kitchen, bathroo and toilet. Some rooms share these facilities with more than one room. Within dormitories building students can use a study room, TV room, or a music room with a piano at their disposal. All student dorms are equipped with laundries and dryers for a small fee. There are approximately 6 100 beds available. International students are accommodated on campus "Pod Palackého vrchem". The Pod Palackého vrchem dormitories are the central dormitories in the middle of BUT campus (tram and bus stop Technology park). In the campus you will find a fitness centre, canteen, grocery store, hairdresser and many more. Sports facilities are provided by the Centre of Sports Activities of the BUT (CESA). Facilities Halls of Residence offers: 100 % high-speed internet connection wide range of dining services (cafeterias, restaurants, pizzerias, cafés, bars, and snack bars) modern catering system (cashless, no pre-ordering) student counselling center for all students who have any study or learning needs resulting from their healt conditions: alfons.vutbr.cz/en Contact: Brno University of Technology Halls of Residence and Dining Services of BUT Kolejní 2 612 00 Brno Website: www.kam.vutbr.cz/english Student accommodation office: Tel.: +420 541 142 930-3 E-mail: ubytovatelky@skm.vutbr.cz Private Accommodation The dorms are not for you? The dorms are at full capacity and you didn't get in?  Wanna try something else? Go private! You can find rooms and flats through an agency or on your own, usually via Facebook groups. IMPORTANT NOTE FOR STUDENTS FROM 3rd COUNTRIES: Students who apply for a Czech visa will also need an accommodation contract or confirmation of a preliminary accommodation contract to submit at the embassy to be included in the visa process! Therefore, you should communicate this necessity with the property owner as soon as possible to receive your visa on time, thus before the beginning of the semester. Waste Collection Fee Those who're staying for more than 3 months here in Brno are obliged to pay the waste collection fee: a basic fee of 670 CZK (approx 26 EUR) per payer for the calendar year. More information here: www.en.brno.cz/w/waste-tax Accommodation Scholarship Students are entitled to receive the accommodation scholarship if they are: students enrolled in Bachelor, Master and Doctoral programme Students can apply for an accommodation scholarship online via Studis section in the BUT Intraportal. The instructions on how to apply for the accommodation scholarship will be provided to all international students by e-mail at the beginning of the semester after their arrival during the Welcome week. In order for the accommodation scholarship to be paid, each student must have a bank account in CZK with a Czech bank listed in the Studis section in the BUT Intraportal.

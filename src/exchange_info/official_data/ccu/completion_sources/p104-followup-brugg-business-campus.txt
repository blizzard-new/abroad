About FHNW Locations and Contact Locations and Contact FHNW Cam­pus Brugg-Windisch Contact Getting there Opening hours Special opening hours Impressions Our schools Business, Computer Science and Information Technology, Education and Engineering and Environment Approximately 4,500 people work, study and research at FHNW Campus Brugg-Windisch. The School of Business, Computer Science and Information Technology, Education and Engineering and Environment offer a wide range of courses in basic and further education. For leisure and enjoyment, a variety of cultural and sports events are available for employees and students. The campus features a library, a restaurant, a bar and a café and is also a very popular event location for external clients from all over Switzerland. Contact Fachhochschule Nordwestschweiz Campus Brugg-Windisch Bahnhofstrasse 6 CH-5210 Windisch T +41 56 202 77 00 empfang.windisch@fhnw.ch Getting there Public transport The campus is a 2-minute walk from the train station in Brugg and less than an hour`s journey from Basel, Berne and Zurich. By car Motorway A3 to exit 19 - Brugg-Windisch. Set your GPS navigation system to Windisch. There is a public parking lot with 240 spaces below building no. 5 (P2).

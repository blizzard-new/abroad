Hanrim Dormitory Hanlim Dormitory, which opened its doors in 2002, is a modern facility that can accommodate up to 1,046 students. It offers a comfortable living environment with double occupancy rooms. Each student room is equipped with a personal desk, chair, bookshelf, bed, shoe rack, wardrobe, hanger, telephone, and LAN connection. In addition, each room's private bathroom is equipped with a toilet, sink, and shower. On each floor, there are shared bathrooms, washrooms, and lounges. In the basement, you'll find additional facilities including a dining area, study rooms, an internet cafe, a fitness center, meeting rooms, laundry, and ironing rooms.

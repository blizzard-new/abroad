Charges are pro-rated for students moving in after August 29st, 2026. This is an installment payment plan, not monthly rent. Students who vacate due to an approved release of the license agreement will be charged on a pro-rated basis. The initial payment may be made online during the application process.  The $50 processing fee is non-refundable even if the license agreement is approved for cancellation. Monthly Installment Plan: Charges will be posted to your CSUN Student Account , which can be viewed at your CSUN Portal account (not Student Housing Portal), and are due according to the following schedule. Payments received after the 10th of each month will accrue a late fee of $15 on the 11th day. Contact the Student Housing Office to make special arrangements to make payment in full for the semester or the entire academic year. Financial Aid Disbursement: If any financial aid is awarded and there are funds remaining after tuition fees are paid, these funds are applied toward other University debts on the resident’s account such as housing installment payments. Financial Aid Changes: Changes to financial aid may have an impact on the licensee’s balance due. Changes in financial aid, whether imposed or voluntary, will not nullify any obligations of this license agreement. Full and prompt compliance with all Financial Aid Office requests for information is strongly advised to ensure a timely financial aid award and disbursement. Family Housing - Village Apartments: July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 Village Apartments 2026-2027 The Village Apartments is a family housing complex of one- and two-bedroom apartments at the north end of the campus. The complex includes 56 one- and 48 two-bedroom units, each with one bath. Family housing has a community building that includes laundry facilities, mailboxes, and a large recreation room available for social gatherings. An outdoor swimming pool and modern play structure are next to the community building. Family housing is located in the block surrounded by Lassen Street, Zelzah Avenue, Devonshire Street and Lindley Avenue. The main campus is easily accessible on foot or by bicycle. There are also several bus routes. Shopping and other community services are conveniently located nearby. Apartment Features Each apartment features direct access from the outside, eliminating the need for noisy hallways. Kitchens are equipped with a refrigerator, a range, and a garbage disposal. One-bedroom apartments include a dining bar. Two-bedroom units have a separate dining area. Bedrooms contain easily accessible large closets, and there is a separate linen closet in each unit. All apartments come unfurnished. Carpeting and window coverings are provided. Ground-floor apartments have patios, and upper-level apartments have decks. All are situated on the complex's extensive green space. There are a limited number of units designed to accommodate persons with physical disabilities. Family housing offers both partially modified units with accessible bathrooms and fully modified units with accessible bathrooms and kitchens. 2026-2027 Monthly Rent Payment Schedule and Other Fees Due Dates One Bedroom Two Bedroom July 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 August 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 September 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 October 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 November 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 December 1, 2026 $1,558 $2,046 January 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 February 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 March 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 April 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 May 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 June 1, 2027 $1,558 $2,046 Total Rent Payments $18,696 $24,552 Other Fees Security Deposit $500 $500 Lost Keys $25 $25 Lock Outs $5 $5 *RHA Fee $15 $15 Monthly Rent Schedule: Charges will be posted to your CSUN Student account, which can be viewed via the CSUN Portal and are due according to the following schedule.

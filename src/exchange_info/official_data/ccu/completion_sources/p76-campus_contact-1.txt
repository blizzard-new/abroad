                                    Departments You are here: Academics » Campus of Open Learning Campus of Open Learning | Delhi University | COL DU Keshav Puram Share this Since 2006, the Campus of Open Learning of University of Delhi (COL-DU) has been offering various short-term certificate courses. From the years 2024 and onwards, following Certificate Courses are being offered, which can joined after 10+2. Students from all colleges, departments of DU and from other universities may also apply in these short-term certificate courses: Applied Psychometrics and Scale Construction (Online Mode) Counselling and its Applications (Online Mode) Medical Transcription AC Refrigerator Repairing Bakery and Confectionery Beauty and Hair Lab Stenography, Secretarial Practices Radio Jockeying, Anchoring, T.V Journalism Animation, Motion Graphics Graphic Design and DTP Photography and Video Editing Motor Driving for Girls (Car) Certificate in Basic Computer Application Certificate in Introduction to Networking Basic Drone Technology & Operations Course Assembly & Repair of Drone Python Programming Basics Address for Skill Courses (Centre): OLDC, Second Floor, ARC Building, University of Delhi, Opposite S.G.T.B. Khalsa College, Delhi-110007 Help line number: 9318354363 9318354636 Email-id: skillcentre.cisbc@col.du.ac.in Web Site :- https://sol.du.ac.in/skill_courses/index.php Registrations in these courses are open for all, including University of Delhi students. Admissions on first cum first serve basis (as per the number of seats in each course). For more information visit Campus of Open Learning website.

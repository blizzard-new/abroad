Annual Report About Us Home Life at Presidency International Engagement About us --> The Office of International Affairs (OIA), established in October 2021, leads Presidency University’s internationalisation initiatives by building strategic global partnerships and translating them into meaningful opportunities for students, faculty and researchers. Our guiding commitment is “International Exposure to Everyone.” We believe that global exposure should not be limited to a privileged few. Students differ in their academic interests, aspirations, readiness and financial circumstances. OIA therefore facilitates multiple pathways to make international academic experiences accessible to a wider university community. Through partnerships with universities worldwide, OIA supports student and faculty mobility, research internships, semester exchanges, Master’s progression, dual-degree opportunities, international teaching, collaborative research and global learning experiences on campus. Whether you are a prospective student, collaborative partner, or international visitor, we look forward to welcoming you to the Office of International Affairs at Presidency University. International Exposure to Everyone – Turning Global Partnerships into Global Opportunities Different aspirations. Different financial capacities. Multiple global pathways. At Presidency University, internationalisation is measured not merely by the number of agreements signed, but by the academic opportunities, financial benefits and institutional outcomes those relationships make possible. Our internationalisation model CONNECT  →  CREATE PATHWAYS  →  ENABLE ACCESS  →  INTERNATIONALISE AT HOME  →  DEMONSTRATE IMPACT Global Impact at a Glance 200+ International partnerships 1,500+ Students undertaking overseas opportunities 12,000+ Students reached through global academic engagements ₹15 crore+ Tuition-fee savings and scholarships 44+ Countries 160+ Incoming international students / participants 12 Dual-degree pathways ₹3 crore+ Stipends secured One Vision. Multiple Global Pathways. GO ABROAD | Funded research internships, semester exchanges, short-term immersion, Master’s progression (3+2, 3.5+1.5, 4+1) and dual-degree pathways. LEARN GLOBALLY AT PRESIDENCY | Visiting international faculty, global masterclasses and Collaborative Online International Learning (COIL). Three courses have already incorporated COIL. RESEARCH GLOBALLY | International research internships, doctoral mobility, joint research initiatives, scholarly publications and research supervision. TEACH GLOBALLY | Faculty academic mobility and funded teaching assignments at partner universities, complemented by visiting academics teaching at Presidency. Making international opportunities affordable Reciprocal tuition-fee waivers, partner scholarships, funded internships, stipends and the Chancellor’s Bon Voyage Scholarship reduce barriers to participation. Eligible students can pursue selected exchange opportunities without paying additional tuition fees to the host institution, subject to programme conditions. For Presidency, the financial value of internationalisation is measured not only by revenue generated, but also by financial barriers removed. Internationalisation for faculty and researchers More than 15 faculty members have participated in international academic mobility, including funded teaching opportunities, while 10+ PhD scholars have undertaken overseas semester or research mobility. Collaboration with international researchers supports joint publications, research supervision and proposals. International Teaching Week 2026 is planned to bring 35 international faculty to Presidency for teaching, research interaction and partnership development. Beyond MoUs: Partnerships That Deliver Partnerships are cultivated as evolving academic relationships, progressing from introductory engagement to teaching, mobility, COIL, collaborative research and deeper institutional cooperation. An in-house International Mobility Dashboard tracks students’ host destinations, programme duration, academic progress and expected return, supporting student welfare and evidence-based decision-making. Institutional impact The University’s international academic networks and global visibility have expanded alongside reported improvements in its QS and Times Higher Education (THE) rankings. Inbound immersion programmes and international academic engagement also create opportunities for international recruitment and programme revenue. Different aspirations. Multiple pathways. One promise: International Exposure to Everyone. Objectives To foster the global visibility and profile of Presidency University. To develop, facilitate, and strengthen international connections. To facilitate, assist, and support international students in all aspects. To liaise with various international universities to facilitate staff and student mobility. To enhance diversity on the campus. To enhance global career perspectives for staff and students. To provide opportunities for staff and students to learn in different environments, thus helping them to look at life from different perspectives. Single point of contact for foreign student’s registration with FRRO/e-FRRO Maintain records and disseminate information related to internationalization. Strategic Objectives International Student and Scholar Support Global Learning and Curriculum Integration Research and Innovation Strategic Objectives Strategic Objectives Global Partnerships and Networks: Establish and strengthen partnerships with leading universities, research institutions, and organizations. Develop joint degree programs, research collaborations, and exchange opportunities. Facilitate international conferences, workshops, and symposiums to share knowledge and best practices. International Student and Scholar Support International Student and Scholar Support: Provide comprehensive support services for international students and scholars, including visa assistance, orientation programs, and cultural integration activities. Develop mentorship programs pairing international students with local students and alumni. Enhance academic support services tailored to the needs of international students. Global Learning and Curriculum Integration Global Learning and Curriculum Integration: Integrate global perspectives into the curriculum across all disciplines. Promote study abroad programs, internships, and service-learning opportunities in diverse cultural settings. Support faculty in developing and delivering globally-oriented courses and programs. Research and Innovation Research and Innovation: Promote international research collaborations that address global challenges. Provide support for faculty and student research projects with an international focus. Host visiting scholars and researchers to enrich the academic environment. OIA Team Administrative Structure of OIA The OIA supports the development and growth of the Global Gateways, each of which is managed by a smaller team located at the Bangalore Campus. These leaders identify the best ways to achieve the established goals for the gateways and ensure that the gateways operate in alignment with the strategies of the University.

With a view to develop the all-round personality of the students, the institute gives equal importance to both curricular and extracurricular activities. Students have emerged with flying colors in various sports activities at college, university, state, national & International level. Both students and staff participate in various tournaments and have brought many accolades to Saintgits. There are four national level fests which are held at the campus every year Simthesis: is the national level management fest held by the college for undergraduate and postgraduate students Nakshatra: is the annual techno-cultural festival of the college, usually held at the beginning of every year. Over 4500 students from engineering colleges across India arrive at the campus to attend this mega event to perform and showcase their artistic and technical excellence in the competitions and cultural events.

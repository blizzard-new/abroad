A health center dedicated to students The University Multi-Service Health Centre (CPSU) offers general medical and gynecological consultations, nursing care and psychological support. The CPSU welcomes all students of the Catholic University of Lille to take care of each individual and ensure their well-being. General practitioners, gynecologist and psychologists consult by appointment only, by telephone, by email or on Maiia. These appointments are reserved for students of the Catholic University. Also find relaxation workshops, mindfulness meditation, positive psychology, hypnosis, by appointment or registration. Health guide Our medical teams provide you with various health sheets sorted by categories (diet, gynecology, travel, etc.) to help you find verified information. Report Mission Accueil Handicap (MAH) The University pays special attention to students with disabilities so that they can continue their studies in the best possible conditions. Our MDPH-approved doctor formulates the recommendations that are sent to the various establishments. To request an accommodation, you can contact the MAH representative at your establishment or make your request on this form . Social Security for international students Are you a foreign student? Interested in beginning or continuing your higher education in France? Registering at a French institute of higher education for the first time? Don't have a French Social Security number? You will need to register on this site for coverage of your health care expenses throughout your time in higher education. Office hours at EDHEC A nursing office is organized every Tuesday at EDHEC, from 9 am to 5 pm starting in October. A general medicine office is held there every Thursday, from 9 am to 6 pm. When the CPSU is closed In the event of a problem that does not require emergency intervention outside CPSU opening hours, you can go to the Maison Médicale de Garde Lille Métropole located on rue Desais in Lille, which is accessed via the emergency department of the Saint Vincent de Paul Hospital. It is open Monday to Friday from 8 p.m. to midnight + weekends and public holidays from 8 a.m. to midnight. More information on their website .

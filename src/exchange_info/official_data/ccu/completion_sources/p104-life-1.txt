About FHNW Every visit to us opens up new perspectives and opportunities. Searching and using We provide access to analogue and digital sources of knowledge for study, research, work and continuing education. Learning and working We pass on our expertise and act as a learning space and workplace for students, teachers, researchers and other interested parties. Researching and publishing We provide support in terms of research, data management and publication and are committed to open science and the open dissemination of knowledge. Subject areas We are happy to help you find your way through the wealth of information sources. About us The FHNW Library connects, guides and helps.

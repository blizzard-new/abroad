Breadcrumb Why Oneonta Student Life Campus Living Living on campus is comfortable, convenient and fun, with lots of opportunities to make friends and get involved. Housing rates are fixed for each incoming class. Learn about living on campus Dining Services Our dining halls offer everything from burgers and fries to made-to-order salads and vegan entrees. At My Kitchen, you can make your meal just the way you want it, with four individual kitchens stocked with ingredients that change daily. More About Dining Services Clubs & Organizations With more than 150 student clubs and organizations ranging from the A/C (Activities Council) to WIRE-TV, there are all kinds of ways to get involved and meet friends. Don’t see the club you want? Start your own! Explore Clubs & Oganizations Fraternity and Sorority Life SUNY Oneonta has 22 recognized fraternities and sororities. First-semester freshmen are not allowed to join but can attend recruitment events to learn about each organization. More about Fraternity and Sorority Life Athletics SUNY Oneonta’s 21 men’s and women’s athletic teams compete at the NCAA Division III level and are part of the SUNY Athletic Conference. Not on an athletic team but still want to play to win with friends? Or just stay fit? Join a club sports team, participate in intramural athletics, or drop in on a fitness class.

In view of the strategic goal of a gender-equitable university with equal participation of women and men at all levels, applications from women are particularly welcome." Your representative body for severely disabled employees "The Ostfalia Representative Body for Severely Disabled Persons works to integrate disabled people, takes care of prevention and advises you. We want to create a place of learning for employees and students where people with and without disabilities and in different life situations can work and study on an equal footing. If they are equally qualified, we give preference to people with severe disabilities and those of equal status."

Read More About School The School of Allied and Healthcare Professions at Presidency University has been established to cater to the growing need for a health workforce and to facilitate the needs of the ecosystem of health care which also includes assisting, facilitating and complementing the work of physicians and other health care specialists. The curriculum of the school is well aligned with United Nation Sustainable development Goal (SDGs) ensuring that students are sensitized to global challenges such as sustainability, ethical practices and responsible innovation. Additionally, the curriculum is carefully designed to align with emerging trends in Artificial Intelligence (AI). ENROLL NOW The industry relevant tools, practical exposure taught in the school effectively meets current industry demands and enhances students ’employability. HOD's Message Dear Students, At the School of Allied and Healthcare Professions (SOAHP), we are committed to shaping students into better versions of themselves through quality education and skill development. With strong guidance and mentoring at Presidency University, students are empowered to build their own vision for success. Our experienced faculty, practical-based learning, and opportunities like conferences, workshops, and global exposure prepare students to become industry-ready in the growing healthcare and rehabilitation sector. We look forward to welcoming you to our vibrant learning community. Best Wishes! Attract and develop highly qualified faculty committed to excellence in teaching, research, and healthcare innovation. Establish state-of-the-art laboratories and clinical training facilities to enhance practical learning experiences. Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration to promote holistic patient care and inter professional education. Instil leadership, ethical values, and a spirit of community service among students to meet global healthcare challenges.

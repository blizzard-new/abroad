Transversal Modules (3 ECTS) Personal Branding In today's digital environment, the growing use of various social networks and online platforms linked to personal branding is increasingly evident. A simple CV is no longer enough to stay competitive in the job market. This course addresses, from a pedagogical, practical, and functional perspective, the various aspects surrounding the need to manage one’s personal brand in a professional context . Open to exchange students from all fields of study . 3 ECTS Fall / Spring Volunteering - Society and intercultural Service Learning This course aims to show the reality of Spanish society from the margins, focusing on the data on inequality and marginalisation, while showing the reality of the social organisations in our country that are working to improve people's lives and to eliminate inequalities.

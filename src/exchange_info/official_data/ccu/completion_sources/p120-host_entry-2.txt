Your architecture career path If you are interested in pursuing registration as an architect in Australia, you can package the Bachelor of Architectural Design and Master of Architecture when you apply to QUT. Gain guaranteed entry into the Master of Architecture as a graduate of the QUT Bachelor of Architectural Design. Attain Australian Institute of Architects membership upon graduation. Secure two years of work in an architectural practice, pass your Architectural Practice Exam and become a registered architect. Join the growing architecture industry, with job growth expected to rise in the lead up to the 2032 Brisbane Olympic and Paralympic Games.

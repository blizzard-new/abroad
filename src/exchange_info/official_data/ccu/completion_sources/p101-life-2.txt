Our premium programmes support advanced start-ups and established companies in breaking down barriers, seizing new opportunities and achieving sustainable, profitable growth. If you want to refine, transform or scale your business model, you’ll find the right programmes and inspiration here. You want to accelerate your growth? Then get in touch with us – we take your business model where it belongs: forward. Silicon Valley Program Silicon Valley Program The Silicon Valley Program is a nine-month scaling and transformation programme. We support ambitious teams on their journey from a minimum viable product to a company ready for investment with a scalable business model. Find out more Spinout Program The Silicon Valley Spinout Program is a nine-month acceleration programme for corporate spin-outs. We transform internal project teams into new businesses with growth potential and minimise the risk. Find out more Masterclass The Silicon Valley Masterclass offers the chance to spend a week experiencing the world’s most significant technology ecosystem first-hand, right there in Santa Clara, whilst also spending a week working on your business model – including pitch training. Find out more Experience Week Silicon Valley Experience Week is an intensive executive programme for founders, aspiring entrepreneurs, innovation managers and tech leaders who want to understand how to think, make decisions and scale up within the world’s leading innovation ecosystem.

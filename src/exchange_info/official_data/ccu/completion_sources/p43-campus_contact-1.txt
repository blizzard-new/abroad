PNU Official Visit Request Form Request edit access PNU Official Visit Request Form Thank you for your interest in visiting Pusan National University (PNU). PNU is an open campus, visitors may explore the various parts of the university using the self-guided tour map: https://www.pusan.ac.kr/eng/CMS/Contents/Contents.do?mCode=MN053 Visitors may also hop on to the PNU internal bus shuttle services to get around the campus. For official visit, we encourage you to review the Faculties and Schools in PNU: https://www.pusan.ac.kr/eng/CMS/OrganizationMgr/Undergraduate.do?mCode=MN019 to identify your visit objectives and contact the relevant offices directly to schedule a meeting. Due to high volume of visit requests, please allow us some time to contact the relevant offices before getting back to you.

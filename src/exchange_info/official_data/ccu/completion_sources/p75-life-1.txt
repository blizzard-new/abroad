                                    extensive collection of digital resources to cater to the academic and research

                                    offer access to a wide range of online databases, e-books, e-journals, e-magazines,

                                    around the world. To further enhance user convenience and accessibility, KRC also

                                    anywhere, on any device. In addition, KRC has become an institutional member of various prestigious
                                    organizations and platforms, including DELNET databases, the National Digital
                                    Library of India (NDL), and Shodhganga. These memberships further expand the depth
                                    and diversity of our collection, enabling us to provide a more comprehensive range

APPLY NOW Discover and Connect Activities and guidance for prospective students location Campus Deggendorf The Deggendorf campus is modern and international. It’s a welcoming place for studying, teaching, and research. Located right on the Danube and at the gateway to the Bavarian Forest, science and quality of life go hand in hand here. Further Information Campus Pfarrkirchen The European Campus in Pfarrkirchen is THD’s international campus, home to over 1,500 students from around 100 countries. Its motto: “One World, One Campus.” Here, you become part of a global network and help shape a sustainable future through research and strong partnerships in over 60 countries. Further Information Campus Cham The Cham Teaching and Research Campus offers an international atmosphere, forward-looking degree programs, and cutting-edge research in mechatronics, robotics, and AI. Here, theory meets practice—benefiting the region’s economy and your future career!

The Education University of Hong Kong (EdUHK) is a publicly funded higher education institution. Retitled in 2016 and with a heritage stretching back over 160 years, the University has an education focus with leading research and pioneering multidisciplinary programmes which enable undergraduates, postgraduates, and educators to deepen awareness of regional and international trends and multicultural understanding. EdUHK aims to be a leading education university, creating an impact and defining the education landscape for not only Hong Kong, but also the Asia Pacific region. EdUHK is ranked 4th in Asia and 12th in the QS World University Rankings by Subject (Education and Training) 2025. Student Exchange Programme Students from over 100 partner institutions worldwide join us at EdUHK for one semester or one academic year through the Student Exchange Programme.

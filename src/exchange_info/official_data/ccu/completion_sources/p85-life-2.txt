At Bordeaux Montaigne University Some documents can be lent at home, others can only be read at the Literature and Human Sciences library. The service is free for the requests in other French libraries. Who can make an interlibrary loan request? Any person (fr) registered in the libraries of the Bordeaux Montaigne University. How can I make an interlibrary request? Consult our French pages for (fr) more details . Foreign reader You can't afford to come in Bordeaux and you have found an interesting document in our catalog? Ask the closest university library to make a request to our interlibrary loan service.

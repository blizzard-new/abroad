International Student Insurance All international students who enter Sungshin Women's University are required to subscribe to National Health Insurance or International Student Insurance. 1. National Health Insurance - A foreigner who has lived 3 months or longer in the Rep. of Korea (a holder of alien registration card) and wants to apply for it. - The person is eligible for the same benefits as Korean nationals when visiting a hospital or a pharmacy. - To sign up: inquire National Health Insurance Service (1577-1000, http://www.nhic.or.kr ) - Premium: paid monthly. 2. Group Insurance of International Students (available from a private insurance company) - To sign up: a student who has not signed up to the National Health Insurance MUST subscribe to Group Insurance for International Students; visit International Student Support Team to check the student's premium and pay it. - Premium: varies with gender, age, and subscription duration. - Submission Documents when Filing an Insurance Claim: passport, alien registration card, copy of bankbook, insurance claim filing form, hospital cost statements (receipt), first hospital visit records or doctor's note.

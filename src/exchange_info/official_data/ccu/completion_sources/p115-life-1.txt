Storytelling is a form of knowledge sharing in which fictional travels, people, and places can help us make sense of a complex world. As you explore the gallery, we encourage you to examine what prompts us to use our imaginations, ponder why these stories have been so popular over so many centuries, and consider the ways imaginary journeys allow us to process and evaluate our lived realities while helping us imagine the possibilities of better worlds.

Health care is rapidly changing. Advancements in AI tools, telehealth, wearable technologies and patient data management are changing how, where and when health care is delivered. Technology is allowing us to be more agile while still focused on the most important thing: people. Studying with us will develop your expertise, technical skills and cultural competencies, so you can make a meaningful difference to patients and their communities. World-class facilities Treat real patients at our QUT Health Clinics, and get hands-on experience in our purpose-built clinical simulation centre, virtual environment training rooms, labs and gyms to build job-ready skills. Real-world experiences Placement is your chance to gain real experience and make a difference sooner. We partner with more than 1300 healthcare facilities. Our students complete more than 1.3 million placement hours each year, working on real cases in the field. Industry expertise Our lecturers and researchers work in the field and our graduates are in high demand, with many being offered employment before they complete their studies.

The WCIS is here to help international scholars and their families navigate life in the Czech Republic. From relocation and visa formalities to finding schools and leisure activities, we offer full support on non-academic matters. Read more about our services. We are proud members of the European network EURAXESS – Researchers in motion , joining over 600 service centres across 43 countries. Get started with our Welcome Guide Coming to CZU?

No other courses will be available. The maximum number of ECTS studied in one semester is 30. All Learning Agreements exceeding this limit will be declined. There is no required minimum number of ECTS set by CZU. However, always confirm potential minimum requirements with your home university . It is possible to combine courses from multiple faculties ; however, one faculty must be your major faculty . At least 50% of your selected courses must come from that faculty. Bachelor students may choose Master-level courses if they are at least in the second year of their Bachelor studies. Master students may choose Bachelor-level courses if their home university agrees. Thesis and Research Activities Students who wish to work on their Bachelor’s thesis, Master’s thesis, doctoral research or another research project during their stay at CZU must find and contact a suitable CZU supervisor independently before arriving at CZU . Please note that most CZU faculties do not accept exchange students for thesis supervision due to the limited supervisory capacity of their academic staff. Acceptance, therefore, cannot be guaranteed. The CZU Erasmus Office cannot search for supervisors, contact academic staff on behalf of students or match students with potential supervisors . It is the student’s responsibility to identify a suitable academic member of staff, contact them directly and obtain their approval before the mobility begins. Students whose main purpose is to work on a thesis or participate in research are strongly advised to apply for an Erasmus+ traineeship mobility rather than an Erasmus+ study stay.

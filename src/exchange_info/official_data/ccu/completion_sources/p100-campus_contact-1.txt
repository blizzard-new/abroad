fourfold good Ostfalia is one of the largest state universities of applied sciences in Lower Saxony . A special feature is that our four campuses in Suderburg, Salzgitter, Wolfenbüttel and Wolfsburg are sometimes far apart - sometimes in the centre of the city, sometimes in the countryside. Directly to: Four locations - one university More information about Ostfalia as an employer Four locations - one university Our faculties offer a broad spectrum in the fields of engineering and computer science, business and law, media and design as well as social work and healthcare. More than 240 professors teach and conduct research at Ostfalia. A total of around 1,000 employees work for us in a wide variety of areas.

Eligible Applicants: Undergraduate, graduate students, diploma candidates, researchers(except students on leave of absence) 2. Application and Check-in Procedures 1) Check the notice on PNU International website 2) Online Application → Announcement of successful candidates → Pay the dormitory fee & Submit health checkup result(Tuberculosis certificate) 3) Apply for a roommate → Dormitory room allocation → Check-in at respective dormitory 4) Take the online dormitory orientation 3. Notes 1) Dormitory application must be conducted every semester. 2) Students who have more than 3 penalty points cannot apply for. 3) Applicants are required to submit the certificate of tuberculosis per semester before the entrance(Mandatory). 4) Penalty points will be imposed for violation of dormitory regulations, and in the event that 10 or more penalty points are accumulated, immediate eviction will be carried out.

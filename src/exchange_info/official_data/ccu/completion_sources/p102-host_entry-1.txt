THA Studies International Opportunities / THA / Studies / International Opportunities International Programs for Students at THA The International Office is a support service that coordinates THA’s international activities in collaboration with the faculties. We promote the internationalization of the university and serve as a point of contact for international students and guests, as well as for THA students, faculty, and staff. At the Center for Languages and Intercultural Communication , you can learn a wide variety of foreign languages, including German, and prepare yourself for working in international teams.

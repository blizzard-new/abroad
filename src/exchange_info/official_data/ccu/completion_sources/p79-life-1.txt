With a dedicated team, IRO ensures seamless integration, from admission to graduation, enhancing the global experience of every student. Core Activities of IRO Mobility and Guidance Assisting international students and guest researchers with academic and research pursuits at KIIT. Guiding KIIT’s domestic & International students in exploring study opportunities abroad for a global academic experience. Strategic Global Engagement Focusing on enhancing KIIT’s presence in the global educational landscape through strategic initiatives. Aligning our educational offerings with global standards to attract a diverse international student body. Student Support Services Offering a welcoming orientation and ongoing support for all international students at KIIT. Providing a range of services including VISA & immigration, local registration, and academic assistance. Fostering Academic Partnerships Building and sustaining academic partnerships with global institutions for educational enrichment. Engaging with Embassies, Ministries, and international organizations for educational and research collaborations.

Search term Frequently searched Academic Programs Services Search for persons THA University Organisation Facilities Staff Development and a Healthy University / THA / University / Organisation / Facilities / Staff Development and a Healthy University Staff Development & Healthy University The THA's Human Resources Development department guides and supports employees and managers through every stage of their careers—from their first day at the university through to ongoing professional and personal development. A Wide Range of Offerings Service With a diverse range of offerings—including onboarding, continuing education and professional development, job shadowing, networking and exchange programs, and events—we promote a successful onboarding process, a welcoming culture in action, and lifelong learning. In this way, we provide guidance, strengthen skills, and help employees successfully navigate the diverse demands and constant changes of everyday university life. Our offerings are complemented by initiatives from the Healthy University program, family services, university sports, and numerous employee perks and corporate benefits. As part of the Chancellor’s Office for Strategic Projects, we work together to shape an attractive, future-oriented work and learning culture at THA.

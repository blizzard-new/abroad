Access to conferences/ seminars/ webinars are provided to the public irrespective of gender, ethnicity, disability etc. International Conference on Innovations in Technology for Humanity – IIHTC 2026 December 17, 2026 - February 19, 2027 The inaugural International Conference on Innovations in Technology for Humanity (IITHC) 2026 is a premier global forum uniting technology and humanitarian action, with a special focus on the Indic context and underserved communities worldwide. The conference brings together researchers, policymakers, and innovators to present and discuss technology-driven solutions addressing poverty alleviation, educational access, cultural preservation, equitable resource distribution, and environmental sustainability. The proceedings of the conference will be published by Springer LNNS Series and subsequently submitted to Scopus for indexing consideration.

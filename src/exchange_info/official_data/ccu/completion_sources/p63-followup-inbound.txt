Tel +603 9101 8880 ext. 2066 Email Kuala Lumpur: [email protected] Springhill: [email protected] UCSI University welcomes students from any of their partner universities who are eligible to study at UCSI University through their exchange programme. The application and selection process for the exchange programme are run entirely by the students’ home universities. Therefore, prospective students should apply with the person-in-charge of the student exchange and study abroad programmes through their institution’s International Office. Frequently Asked Questions How long? One semester or two semesters. How to validate? Exchange students will usually earn credit for grades that were obtained at UCSI University. When? January 2024 Nomination Deadline: 14 September 2023 Document submission deadline: 5 October 2023 Course Selection: First three (3) weeks of each semester May 2024 Nomination Deadline: 11 January 2024 Document submission deadline: 1 February 2024 Course Selection: First three (3) weeks of each semester September 2024 Nomination Deadline: 16 May 2024 Document submission deadline: 6 June 2024 Course Selection: First three (3) weeks of each semester January 2025 Nomination Deadline: 12 September 2024 Document submission deadline: 3 October 2024 Course Selection: First three (3) weeks of each semester Eligibility? Registered as an active student at home university. Must have completed at least one semester of studies in order to be eligible for the exchange programme. Good academic performance. Have a good command of the English Language (Minimum: IELTS 5.5 and TOEFL 500. If English is the language of instruction at your home university, IELTS and TOEFL is exempted) Have a good health condition. Must agree to abide by the rules and regulations of UCSI or placement provider. Should demonstrate empathy and understanding of cultural difference. Should demonstrate excellent personal skills. Agrees to be responsible and accountable for the costs incurred. Medium of instruction? All classes in UCSI University are taught in English. Students will be studying alongside regular UCSI University students Activities and Support Services? UCSI Student Affairs and Alumni offer a range of support services for all our international students including the exchange students.

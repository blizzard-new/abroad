aspect of our lives, and its pace is unprecedented. The progress of globalization signifies multidimensional competition and cooperation. In such a situation, enhancing one's individual capacity through the acquisition of knowledge and skills, as well as cultivating the ability to coexist based on an understanding of other cultures, can be considered essential requirements for living as global citizens "In the face of the currents of globalization, we believe that the core mission of our International Exchange Office is to elevate the Globalization Quotient of our enrolled students. Firstly, the student exchange program will provide you with meaningful experiences in your life. Whether it's for language acquisition or academic studies, if you have specific goals in mind, we encourage you to experience the student exchange program. Dong-A University has expanded its exchange programs to include not only North America, Europe, China, and Japan but also Central Asia and ASEAN member countries, tailored to the needs of our enrolled students. Give it a try The International Exchange Office is also dedicated to promoting globalization within our campus. Having students from diverse nationalities roaming our campus and joining us enables us to naturally embrace globalization. To facilitate this, Dong-A University has expanded its operations, from running the Korean Language Institute to increasing the admission of international students at the undergraduate and graduate levels. Particularly, by expanding English and Chinese tracks, we have been able to attract outstanding international students from various countries. Based on mutual understanding, let's work together with global citizens around us to shape the future Our International Exchange Office supports your dreams Jung-Kuk Yeom, Director of the Office of External and International Affairs 2026. 02 스크롤 상단으로 스크롤 하단으로 페이스북 인스타그램 유튜브 네이버블로그 Office Of  External International  Affairs 서비스메뉴 좌측으로 Privacy Statement Maps & Directions 우측으로 Bumin Campus BC-0116~3, Bumin Campus, 225, Gudeok-ro, Seo-gu, Busan, Republic of Korea (Postcode : 49236) TEL : +82-51-200-6114 Guduk Campus 32, Daesingongwon-ro, Seo-gu, Busan, Republic of Korea (Postcode : 49201) TEL : +82-51-200-7000 Seunghak Campus 37, Nakdong-daero 550beon-gil, Saha-gu, Busan, Republic of Korea (Postcode :49315) TEL : +82-51-200-6114 COPYRIGHT(C) 2023 DONG-A UNIVERSITY ALLRIGHTS RESERVED.

The GTSD 2026 will be held in HYBRID format on 30 and 31 July, 2026 in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. The aim of the conference is to provide an international scientific research forum in theories and applications of green technology and sustainable development in industrial revolution 4.0. All the researchers, academicians, scientists, scholars and activists who are working on the fields related to green technology and sustainable are invited to submit their original research papers to GTSD 2026.

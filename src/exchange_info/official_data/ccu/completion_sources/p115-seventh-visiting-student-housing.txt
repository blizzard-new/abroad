From U.S. Highway 101 From 101 West: Take U.S. Highway 101 to Interstate 405 northbound. Exit Interstate 405 at Reseda Boulevard and turn right. Turn right on Lassen Street. Cross Lindley Avenue. The Conference Services & Guest Housing Office will be on the right. Follow signs for parking lot F9. From 101 East: Exit U.S. Highway 101 at Reseda Boulevard and turn left. Turn right on Lassen Street. Cross Lindley Avenue. The Conference Services & Guest Housing Office will be on the right. Follow signs for parking lot F9. Parking Visitors may park in the G9 parking structure or F9 and F10 parking lots. Resident parking permit or daily parking pass is required. A daily parking permit ($10.45) can be purchased at the kiosk in either the G9 parking structure or the F9 and F10 parking lots. For more information about parking at CSUN, please visit Visitor Parking Information .

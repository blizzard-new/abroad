Student Housing and Residential Life Student Housing Office 17950 Lassen St. Northridge, CA 91325 housing@csun.edu Phone: (818) 677-2160 Fax: (818) 677-4888 Office Hours Monday-Friday: 8 a.m. - 5 p.m. Saturday and Sunday: Closed How to Apply for Student Housing 2026-2027 Student Housing Application Waitlist Student Housing has started a waitlist for the 2026-2027 academic year. If you intend to live on campus, please log into the Student Housing Portal and add yourself to the waitlist. There is no cost to join the waitlist. Waitlist number: Students on the waitlist can check their waitlist number in the Student Housing portal. Please see the video below to learn more about our waitlist process. If you can't find your waitlist number, please contact our office by phone at 818-677-2160 or email . Waitlist Update The Park Apartments and Suites As of September 1, 2026 the waitlist number is: 1,435 As of June 2, 2026 the waitlist number is: 2,144 Things to know before you apply The Park Apartments and Suites are designed for undergraduate, graduate students and offer multiple housing options ideally suited for the various members of our student population. The Village Apartments (Family Housing) are designed for graduate and undergraduate students with spouses/domestic partners and/or dependent children. The Park Apartments and Suites Waitlist: CSUN has a total enrollment of around 37,000, but only approximately 3,500 bed spaces are currently available at The Park Apartments and Suites for the 2026/27 academic year. Once our application opened, spaces filled up quickly in The Apartments and we started a waitlist. There is no cost to join the waitlist and we will continue to send offers to students as space becomes available through summer — however, students on the waitlist who need housing for fall 2026 should also plan for other options in case a bed space does not become available. Submitting the 2026-2027 Application Things to know before you apply: Student Housing Portal login: To apply for Student Housing you must have a CSUN Portal account . Application fee: There is a $50 housing application fee and a $50 meal plan application fee (if applicable) Available housing options: Review your housing options to make sure you select the option that will best suit your needs. When to apply: Applications are processed on a first-come, first-served basis. Space is limited and fills up fast, so you are encouraged to apply as soon as possible. Housing applications may be submitted prior to being accepted to CSUN. Meal plan requirement: Students living in the suites and apartments without kitchens are required to purchase a 10, 12, Matty 5 or All-Access meal plan during the application process. Know the rules: Take a look through the Student Housing Rules and Regulations before submitting your application. Gender-neutral housing: Student housing offers gender-neutral housing to all students. Gender Inclusive Housing is designed for LGBTQIA+ students and their allies who do not feel comfortable expressing their identity in a traditional same-gender housing space. If you have questions about gender-inclusive housing, please email Residential Life at reslife@csun.edu . Cancellations: If you no longer plan to live in Student Housing or have decided to attend another university, you can submit a cancellation form in the Student Housing Portal . In the “ Forms ” link, go to Cancellations and select " Park Apartments and Suites: Cancel Before Move-in " form. For more information about cancelations, visit our Housing Cancelation page. The Village Apartments (Family Housing) Waitlist: There is currently a waitlist for family housing. If you are a student with a spouse and/or a dependent and require family housing, please contact Bella Dorado at bella.dorado@csun.edu . Submitting the 2026-2027 Family Housing Application Things to know before you apply: Family Housing Eligibility: The Village Apartments (Family Housing) are available to: Current CSUN students are continuously enrolled in 12 or more undergraduate or 8 graduate units. Students who are married and/or have legal custody of dependent children. Student Housing Portal login: To apply for Student Housing you must have a CSUN Portal account . After you submit your application Waitlist: Once your application is completed, you will be placed on an automatic waitlist. Please note, if you are currently on the University Village Apartments waitlist for the 2026-2027 year, offers will start to go out in late May and continue on a rolling basis through the summer as spaces become available. Current residents who meet the eligibility requirement have the opportunity to renew their license agreement for the following year. The renewal process will begin in mid-May and continue through June. We will have more information regarding our availability and new offers for the 2026-2027 year once that process is completed. Eligibility verification: You will be asked via email to submit documents to verify your eligibility to live in family housing. Space availability: Once your eligibility is confirmed, you will be notified of space availability based on your preferred move-in date. Application fee: There is no application fee to apply for Family Housing. Security Deposit: $500 Available housing options: Review your housing options to make sure you select the option that will suit you best. To check out our rates, eligibility requirements, and room options, visit Family Housing . When to apply: Applications are processed on a first-come, first-served basis. Space is limited and fills up fast, so you are encouraged to apply as soon as possible. Housing applications may be submitted prior to being accepted to CSUN. Know the rules: Take a look through the Student Housing Rules and Regulations before submitting your application. Cancellations: If you no longer plan to live in Student Housing or have decided to attend another university, contact Bella Dorado at bella.dorado@csun.edu or housing@csun.edu to cancel your housing license agreement. For more information about cancelations, visit our Housing Cancelation page.

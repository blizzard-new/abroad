Student and Campus Safety Resources Stay Connected Safety Map University Policies More section menu items Our Commitment Campus leaders are committed to doing everything possible to prevent sexual misconduct and other crimes, as well as harassment, incidents of hate and bias, and other misdeeds that go against the university’s mission and our Shared Values. When necessary, the university will act quickly to respond to reported incidents and mitigate allegations — and to support our students, faculty, staff and others in as many ways as possible. Get immediate help Emergencies and Crises If you have an emergency, call 9-1-1. If you are in crisis, call 9-8-8.

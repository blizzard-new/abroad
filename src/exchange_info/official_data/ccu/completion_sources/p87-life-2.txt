Read more How do I find accommodation in Strasbourg? The search for accommodation in Strasbourg, as in all major university towns, should begin as early as possible . Further information: 'Housing' If you are studying at the University of Strasbourg as part of an exchange programme , you can apply for accommodation in a Crous residence when you apply online. In other cases, accommodation information sessions ("Zoom logement") are organised by videoconference throughout the year. Further information: ' Preparing your housing search' The University of Strasbourg offers accommodation solutions for doctoral students and international researchers and lecturers: The European Doctoral College (Collège doctoral européen) welcomes newcomers doctoral students in joint PhD. The Villa Arconati Visconti is reserved for teachers, researchers and post-doctoral students for stays from one week to a maximum of 3 months.

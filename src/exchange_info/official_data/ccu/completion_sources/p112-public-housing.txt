Hloubětín Dormitory One of the main accommodation options used by incoming exchange students. Located in Prague Hloubětín with good public transport connections to the CZU campus. View dormitory details West Dormitory Another popular dormitory option for exchange students. It offers furnished student accommodation with shared facilities and easy access to public transport.

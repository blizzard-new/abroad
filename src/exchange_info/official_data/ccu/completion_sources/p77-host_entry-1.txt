Vinod Kumar Professor and Dean (Research Studies) Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology is committed to advancing knowledge and addressing major societal challenges through high-quality research across diverse scientific disciplines. The University places special emphasis on thrust areas and emerging domains of science, technology, humanities, management and law. The Office of Research Studies, established in 2009 as a dedicated entity, has been instrumental in fostering a vibrant research ecosystem and producing well-trained research scholars. The Office is headed by the Dean – Research Studies, who is responsible for supervising, coordinating and ensuring that all research activities are conducted in accordance with established ethical standards and academic integrity. The University offers research programmes leading to the award of the Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) degree in the fields of Engineering and Technology, Sciences, Humanities, Management and Law. Admissions to the Ph.D. programme are conducted twice every year, during the Summer Session and Winter Session. The entire application process is carried out online, ensuring transparency and efficiency. Admission to the Ph.D. programme is granted strictly on the basis of merit, in accordance with the University's regulations and admission procedures. Message from Coordinators - Research Studies The Coordinator, Office of Research Studies, plays a pivotal role in facilitating the effective administration and management of research programmes within the University. The Coordinator is responsible for overseeing Ph.D. admissions for both national and international candidates, scrutinizing research proposals, and verifying synopsis and thesis submissions in accordance with University regulations. The role includes coordinating Research Advisory Committee (RAC) meetings, synopsis presentations, pre-viva examinations, and other Research Board activities. The Coordinator manages research scholar records, maintains official files and documentation, and ensures the smooth functioning of research-related portals such as the Mini App and Clique Portal. Additional responsibilities include processing scholarships and stipends, monitoring fee verification and debit generation, assisting in budget preparation and financial administration, managing research supervisor recognition and vacancy allocation, and handling requests related to break of study and cancellation of registration. The Coordinator also oversees procurement and store requirements, organizes research-related events and workshops, processes remuneration claims, and supports the Dean – Research Studies in ensuring that all research activities are conducted efficiently, transparently, and in compliance with the University's academic and ethical standards.

Offered by Chitkara Business School at Chitkara University, the program focuses on financial reporting, taxation, international finance, and strategic decision-making. Curriculum A Bachelor of Commerce in International Finance & Accounting in collaboration with ACCA is a 3‑Year UG course with a comprehensive curriculum that combines finance, accounting, and business management. The program covers core topics such as financial accounting, management accounting, taxation and assurance, financial management, business law, and economics. It also includes specialised courses in international finance, financial reporting, and strategic management. The program prepares you for professional certification such as ACCA, and hence, the curriculum aligns with the ACCA syllabus.

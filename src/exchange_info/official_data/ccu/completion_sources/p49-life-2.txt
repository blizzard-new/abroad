Health Check Sungshin Women's University Health Clinic performs annual health check to promote the health of international students and prevent contracting of diseases. 1. Examination Categories: examination in internal medicine,, bodily measurements, blood pressure, vision and hearing, X-ray (direct film exposure), hepatitis B, liver function, total cholesterol, hemoglobin, blood sugar, urine, thyroid function, kidney function (※ categories are subject to change annuallyt). 2. Number of Examinations: once a year (in April). 3.

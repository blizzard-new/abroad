Mobility Incoming students Menú Application procedure Fact Sheet Degree programmes English taught modules Spanish language courses Academic calendar Services for International Students Accommodation / Transport Visas and residence permits Health Assistance Discover Zaragoza Incoming students San Jorge University welcomes international students, both those interested in studying a full undergraduate or posgraduate course and obtaining a San Jorge University qualification, and those interested in a shorter study period intergrated into their own university programme. If you are interested in studying a full undergraduate or postgraduate course currently offered by San Jorge University then please contact our Department of Academic Information in order to receive appropriate information and guidance: teayudamos@usj.es . If you are interested in a short academic exchange programme (1 semester or a full acadmic year) at San Jorge University, there are different student exchange programmes that allow this type of study period, normally with some kind funding or grant such as Erasmus+. In order to request a place on one of these student exchange programmes you should first get in touch with the International Office at your own university.

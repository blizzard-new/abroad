Igniting Excellence as a Premier Engineering School in the Philippines

Mapúa University, founded in 1925 by Don Tomas Mapúa, is a world-class higher education institution in the Philippines dedicated to providing a learning environment rooted in discipline, excellence, commitment, integrity, and relevance. Recognized by the 2025  Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings (WUR) as one of the best schools in the world, our academic stronghold provides a diverse array of programs grounded in engineering and science, architecture and design, information technology, business and health sciences, and media studies. Our goal is to foster an atmosphere that promotes academic rigor and practical expertise, enabling students to compete on a global scale. And with strong moral fibers and first-rate abilities, our graduates are ready to make a difference in the world.

About us

Be a Part of the Groundbreaking Academic Success

Highly regarded as an engineering and technological institution in the Philippines, Mapúa provides a comprehensive curriculum for senior high school students, undergraduates, graduates, and those interested in fully online programs.

A world-class education is at the heart of every degree and program, ensuring students are well-prepared to compete on a global scale. The university's rigorous academic program emphasizes practical skills and character development to position students for industry leadership and environmental stewardship. 100 years of cutting-edge education

Standing 100 years strong, Mapúa is an institution of excellence recognized as one of the world’s best universities and the Philippines’ premier engineering and technological school. A key innovator, Mapúa continues to be a leader in education by expanding its presence in different fields of study like Communication and Media Studies, Business and Management, Health Sciences, as well as integrating Senior High School, successfully developing and implementing diverse curricula that contribute to the advancement of the nation and the world.

Home of the nation’s top builders and creators, Mapúa celebrates its 100th year as an education leader with global competence, reflected beyond its consistent international rankings and accreditations from esteemed bodies and through a century of its endeavors to champion an accessible and quality education for all—upholding the vision Don Tomas Mapúa began 100 years ago. 

 

With this vision realized, we commemorate the institution’s historic milestone with the entire Mapúan community and its subsidiaries Mapúa Malayan Colleges Laguna, Mapúa Malayan Colleges Mindanao, and Mapúa Malayan Digital College, in a month-long full of centennial-related activities that will surely ignite their red and gold spirit.
